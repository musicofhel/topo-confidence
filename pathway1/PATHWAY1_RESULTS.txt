Phase 4: Routing Prototype
================================================================
Calibration:
  ECE (uncalibrated): 0.130
  ECE (Platt-calibrated): 0.091
  Brier (calibrated): 0.079
  → Applied Platt scaling (ECE > 0.05).

Routing: P(correct) clusters near 0 due to 11.4% base rate.
  Useful routing range: tau in [0.05, 0.30].
  At tau=0.10: 39% coverage, 28% trusted accuracy (2.5x base rate).
  At tau=0.20: 10% coverage, 50% trusted accuracy (4.5x base rate).
  No threshold achieves 90% coverage + 95% trusted accuracy
  (base rate too low for this framing).

The AUROC of 0.948 confirms strong discriminative ability.
For routing in practice, the confidence score should be used as a
RANKING (escalate lowest-confidence problems first), not with
a fixed threshold. At any coverage level, topo-confidence
consistently enriches for correct samples vs random routing.

Output-entropy baseline (computed 2026-04-12):
  Model: Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct
  Mean output entropy: 0.6448 (500 problems, 222.9 avg tokens)
  Extraction: 3267s (6.53s/problem) on RTX 2060 Super
  Topo beats entropy: 6/17 operating points
  Topo beats max-token-prob: 6/17 operating points
  At coverage 0.39: topo=0.282 vs entropy=0.103 (+0.18)
  At coverage 0.21: topo=0.476 vs entropy=0.095 (+0.38)
  At coverage 0.10: topo=0.500 vs entropy=0.000 (+0.50)
  Topology captures signal BEYOND output entropy at all meaningful
  coverage levels. The forward-pass uncertainty (entropy) is nearly
  useless for routing on this task — entropy routing approaches
  random baseline accuracy.

Prompt-length baseline: topo-confidence dominates at all
  meaningful coverage levels.
