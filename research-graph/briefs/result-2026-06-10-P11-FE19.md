# Result brief — P11-FE19 (Verification routing C_exact vs prefill-gating C_infer, + verify-then-correct)

Post-FE269, the surviving applied story for the L19 prefill DoM was selective
prediction / compute routing (not steering). FE19 tested whether a **post-hoc
verification** signal (Kumaran 2604.22271's post-answer-newline "PANL" activation)
routes the recoverable B-bucket better than the **pre-hoc prefill** signal did —
i.e. whether C_exact (verify-then-route) beats C_infer (prefill-gating) at matched
compute. It also tested Kumaran's verify-then-correct mechanism as a third arm.

**Verdict: C_exact LOSES.** No non-degenerate verification-routed policy beats the
achievable uniform-K frontier beyond noise; the H-19 bet is refuted at 1.5B. The
applied story stays where FE269 left it — prefill-DoM selective prediction (F-8,
71.6%@0.5-cov), not verify-and-route.

## What was run (all local, 2060 Super, Qwen-2.5-1.5B-Instruct, MATH-500 n=500)

- **S1 gate:** K=1 greedy reproduced at 0.486 (243/500) from the stage2 cache.
- **S2 verification pass:** model self-grades its own K=1 answer (Yes/No); all-layer
  residual activation captured at the post-answer (PANL-equivalent) token, (500,29,1536).
- **S3 verify-then-correct:** "this may be wrong, reconsider" on all 500, max_new=1024.
- **S4 PANL probe:** 5-fold OOF DoM + logistic probe per layer, predicting "K=1 wrong".
- **S5 routing sim:** accuracy vs avg-K (charging verify ~0.1 + correct ~1.0), every
  router × arm; **uniform baseline = upper convex hull = random K=1/K=8 routing**.
- **S6 decision:** significance (≥1.96 SE, n=500) + non-degeneracy (frac_routed≤0.70) guards.

## Two comparator artifacts caught (fresh-eyes), both would have manufactured a false WIN

1. **Degenerate router:** the only policy clearing the loose pre-registered "≥55% at
   avg-K<8" was `B_resample|verbalized` at avg-K 7.04 / acc 0.556 — but it routes 85%
   of problems to K=8 (the verbalized verdict says "correct" only 21.8% of the time),
   so it is uniform-K8 in disguise and the +0.6pp margin is 0.27 SE (SE=0.022).
2. **Dominated-point interpolation:** linearly interpolating the uniform frontier
   through K=2 (0.413) and K=4 (0.495) inflates apparent wins, because both points are
   *below* the K=1→K=8 line. The honest baseline is the **upper convex hull** (= random
   routing between K=1 and K=8), which dominates uniform K=4 (0.513 vs 0.495 at avg-K 4).
   Against the hull, every routed point is +0.1–0.67 SE — nothing significant.
