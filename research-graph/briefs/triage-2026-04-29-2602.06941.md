# Triage brief — 2602.06941 — Endogenous Resistance to Activation Steering in Language Models

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06941
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy) — unfalsifiable-null risk.**
   *Mechanism*: The paper documents Endogenous Steering Resistance (ESR), a circuit-level mechanism in which off-target activation perturbations are detected by 26 SAE-identified consistency-checking latents and partially counteracted downstream. If ESR exists in Qwen-2.5-1.5B at L19, a per-position DoM steering bank that moves a problem from "incorrect" toward "correct" geometry will be partly snapped back by the consistency circuits, yielding a smaller-than-predicted accuracy gain or a null. A null result for H-1 would then be misattributed to "DoM is correlational" when the correct interpretation is "DoM is causal but resisted."
   *Falsifiable test*: Run H-1 with and without zero-ablation of the (currently undiscovered) Qwen-1.5B analogues of the 26 ESR latents. If ablation amplifies the steering effect by ≥25% (consistent with the paper's recovery-rate reduction), H-1's null is an ESR artifact, not a refutation of F-2's causality.

2. **Refutes F-3 (prefill ⊥ final-token DoM, cos = 0.046) interpretation — orthogonality may be a generic ESR artifact, not a correctness-pathway signal.**
   *Mechanism*: ESR works by re-orienting late-layer state away from injected (off-task) directions toward a "default" trajectory. If the prefill DoM direction is being passively re-orthogonalized by the same consistency machinery on every problem (correct or incorrect), the 0.046 cosine is a baseline of the consistency circuit's standing-orthogonal projection — not evidence that prefill and final-token encode different correctness-relevant information.
   *Falsifiable test*: Compute cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) separately on correct vs incorrect problems on the cached Pathway 10 NPZs. If both groups show cos ≈ 0.046 (uniform orthogonality), F-3's "two-channel" framing collapses into a single fact about the consistency circuit. If correct problems show systematically higher or lower cosine, F-3's correctness interpretation survives.

3. **Refutes F-7 (D-bucket has a collective geometric signature) — the signature could be low-ESR rather than intrinsic difficulty.**
   *Mechanism*: D-bucket = K=1 right, K=8 wrong (sampling-fragile). ESR predicts that such problems are precisely the ones where the consistency-checking circuit fails to fire on perturbed/sampled trajectories. If D-bucket's "distinctive geometric signature" (F-7) is the SAE-latent activation profile of the ESR circuit operating at low intensity, F-7 is reframed: the signal is not intrinsic to problem difficulty, but reflects the model's self-monitoring weakness on those problems.
   *Falsifiable test*: Train a small SAE on Qwen-1.5B L19 prefill activations (or use an off-the-shelf one if available), identify candidate ESR-correlated latents by activation pattern on perturbed off-topic prompts, then check whether D-bucket vs A-bucket loadings on those latents differ. If yes, F-7 is partly an ESR-deficiency signature; if no, F-7's intrinsic-difficulty framing survives.

4. **Refutes H-13 (head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token circuits) — circuits may be mis-identified if ESR heads share the prefill DoM channel.**
   *Mechanism*: The paper claims dedicated attention heads carry consistency-checking. If those heads also carry a substantial fraction of the prefill DoM signal that drives F-2's AUROC 0.7731, head-level ablations meant to isolate "correctness-prediction circuits" will instead identify "consistency-checking circuits" — and the attribution conclusions will mistake one mechanism for the other.
   *Falsifiable test*: Before running H-13, screen heads for ESR-like behavior (do they activate differentially on off-topic prompts vs on-topic prompts). Heads showing both ESR signatures and high prefill-DoM contribution must be flagged; their attribution is ambiguous and any "this head encodes correctness" claim is unsafe.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering)** — the headline 71.6% selective-prediction accuracy at coverage 0.5 (selective_pred = current canonical) is a *passive* readout; H-1 is the *active* test. ESR predicts H-1's effect size is bounded above by (steering magnitude) − (ESR resistance magnitude). For Qwen-1.5B, the paper says ESR is weaker than in Llama-3.3-70B, so H-1 should still observe a lift, but smaller than naive predictions.
- **F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731 OOF)** — paper provides an alternative non-trivial mechanism: if the prefill state already encodes the consistency-circuit's pre-firing pattern, AUROC 0.7731 partly measures the consistency circuit's anticipation of off-task drift. This connects F-2 to a steering-resistance channel rather than (or in addition to) a confidence channel.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046)** — explicit alternative explanation (see refutation 2).
- **H-13 (head-level attribution)** — paper provides a concrete circuit decomposition (26 SAE latents) that should be cross-referenced before claiming head ownership of the prefill-DoM signal.

## Methodologies extracted

- **SAE-latent zero-ablation for circuit causality** — train (or load) a sparse autoencoder on a target layer's residual stream, identify latents with differential activation on a contrast (e.g., off-topic vs on-topic), zero-ablate them at inference, measure behavioral change.
  *Replication cost*: H100 day if no off-the-shelf SAE for Qwen-1.5B L19 exists; ~CPU hours if one does (Goodfire / Apollo / EleutherAI release pace makes this plausible by late 2026).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — SAE-based latent identification is a natural extension of F-2's "L19 prefill DoM is a direction" toward "L19 prefill DoM is a sparse-feature combination," and gives us a refutation handle for H-13.
- **Off-topic activation steering protocol** — inject a steering vector that pulls the model toward task-irrelevant generation, then measure recovery rate over multiple completions.
  *Replication cost*: 1h H100 for a small Qwen-1.5B sweep (cheap because Qwen-1.5B is tiny).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct test of whether ESR exists at our scale; if absent, simplifies interpretation of H-1 nulls.
- **Meta-prompting for self-correction induction** — prepend an instruction asking the model to monitor and correct its own off-topic drift; measure recovery-rate amplification.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — only if ESR is detected at our scale; it's a confound for H-1 evaluation if user prompts inadvertently trigger it.
- **Self-correction fine-tuning to induce ESR in smaller models** — supervised fine-tune on examples where the model recovers from off-topic drift mid-generation.
  *Replication cost*: 1 day H100 + dataset creation overhead.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — out of scope for the topo-confidence project's MATH-500 focus; flagged for completeness.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Endogenous resistance" as a model-internal counterforce to external interventions** — reframes interpretability work that perturbs activations: the observed effect is (perturbation) − (resistance), not just (perturbation). This generalizes to any project (including ours) that infers causal structure from activation manipulation. Direct intersection with H-1 and any future causal-claim experiment on F-2.
- **SAE latents as the granularity of consistency-checking circuits** — sparse-feature decomposition is the right unit for this kind of circuit, not raw heads or layers. Suggests our DoM "single direction" framing (F-2) may be a coarse summary of a sparse-feature combination, dovetailing with P11-FE7 (low-dim manifold test).
- **Recovery-rate as a behavioral readout for circuit health** — gives us an alternative to AUROC for evaluating internal correctness signals, where "the model corrects itself across multiple attempts" is the metric. Loosely relates to the K=1 vs K=8 contrast that defines F-7's D-bucket.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- **Model scale matters**: Llama-3.3-70B shows substantial ESR; Llama-3 family and Gemma-2 family show less. Qwen-2.5-1.5B is much smaller than even Llama-3 — expect ESR to be weak or absent natively. Our null hypothesis for H-1's ESR-resistance term should be small.
- **26 SAE latents are the causal core** — when an SAE is available for our model+layer, this gives a concrete ablation target list size order-of-magnitude (tens, not thousands).
- **Recovery rate amplification factors**: 25% reduction from latent ablation; 4× amplification from meta-prompting on Llama-3.3-70B. These set effect-size expectations for any causal manipulation.
- **No code or data release URL surfaced** — paper available on arxiv abstract + PDF; check abstract page for any GitHub repo before relying on replication.

## 2602.06941 — Endogenous Resistance to Activation Steering in Language Models (McKenzie et al., 2026)

**Relevance:** Documents an internal consistency-checking mechanism ("ESR") in which models partially counteract task-misaligned activation steering during generation. Identifies 26 SAE latents on Llama-3.3-70B causally linked to recovery behavior; zero-ablation of these latents drops the multi-attempt recovery rate by 25%. Directly relevant to H-1 (per-position DoM steering on MATH-500), where ESR-like resistance is a confound for null/partial steering effects, and to F-3's prefill/final-token orthogonality, where ESR offers a non-correctness-pathway explanation for the 0.046 cosine. Also intersects H-13 (head-level circuit attribution) by exposing a circuit-decomposition resolution finer than heads.

**Key claim we tested:** Activation steering effects are bounded by an internal resistance term mediated by a small number of SAE-identified consistency-checking latents.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — three cheap CPU-only refutation tests (P11-FE269, P11-FE270, P11-FE271) target whether ESR confounds F-3, F-7, and H-1 at our 1.5B scale. ESR is documented primarily on Llama-3.3-70B; whether it exists in Qwen-2.5-1.5B is open. Resolution of these FEs governs whether H-1's null-hypothesis must be widened to include a resistance term.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE269, P11-FE270, P11-FE271, H-69, H-70, H-1, H-13
