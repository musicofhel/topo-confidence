# Triage brief — 2606.12397 — Redesign Mixture-of-Experts Routers with Manifold Power Iteration

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12397
Triaged: 2026-06-14

Authors: Songhao Wu, Ang Lv, Ruobing Xie, Yankai Lin (Renmin University / Tencent), 2026.

One-line: MoE router redesign. Each router row is driven toward the **principal singular direction** of its expert weight matrix via a "Power-then-Retract" update — a single power-iteration step `R̂ = R·Wᵍ·Wᵍᵀ` followed by an L2 retraction `R' = C·R̂/‖R̂‖₂`. Pretrained 1B–11B MoE; faster convergence, +1–2pt downstream, better load balance, 0.2% overhead.

This is a **weight-space / training-time** paper about MoE routers — not about hidden states, correctness, or probing. Its value to us is purely **methodological**: it hands us a cheap, principled tool (single-step power iteration + a normalized projection metric λ) to test the long-standing weight-space question — *is our L19 prefill DoM correctness direction a principal singular read of the L19 MLP weights, or an off-principal tail read?* That question sits underneath F-2 and the admission note's H-740/H-744/H-256.

## Refutations

The paper's load-bearing premise is **"the principal singular direction provides the most expressive / highest-information description of a weight matrix; a vector that reads from that matrix should align with it."** Our findings make several falsifiable predictions that this premise can break.

1. **Refutes F-2's implicit "DoM = principal weight read" framing.** F-2 says a single L19 prefill direction (DoM ≈ PC1, cos 0.9216) predicts correctness at AUROC 0.7731. If the paper's premise held for our setting, the correctness-informative direction should align with the leading right singular vector `v₁` of the L19 SwiGLU weights (`W_gate`/`W_up`/`W_down`). **Mechanism:** compute `v₁` by power iteration on cached/downloaded `W_L19`, then the paper's own alignment metric λ (Eq. 11) between DoM and `W_L19`. If λ(DoM, W_L19) is near the random-vector baseline (≈ √(rank⁻¹), i.e. ~0.02–0.05 for a 1536-d read), then the correctness direction is an **off-principal, low-spectral-mass read** — directly refuting the paper's claim that the principal direction carries the functionally relevant information, *and* refuting H-740/H-744 (DoM = leading singular vector). **Falsifiable:** seconds-of-CPU SVD/power-iteration on the L19 MLP weight + one dot product against the cached DoM vector.

2. **Refutes the single-principal-direction sufficiency claim via F-9 / cov-spectrum (0.7928).** The paper asserts a single principal direction is "the optimal compressed representation." But FE881 shows correctness AUROC *rises* from PC1-alone toward **0.7928** using the top-20 log-eigenvalues of the L19 covariance **after PC1 is residualized out** — i.e., correctness signal survives precisely in the subspace orthogonal to the dominant direction. **Mechanism:** if a single principal-direction read (whether weight-`v₁` or activation-PC1) captured the information the way the paper claims, the PC1-residualized cov-spectrum could not add measurable AUROC over PC1. The +0.0072 residualized lift is a standing counterexample to "principal direction = most expressive, others are noise." **Falsifiable:** already in `pc1_resid_cov_spectrum_results.json`; reinforce by building a power-iteration single-`v₁` read and showing it ceilings below the residualized-spectrum 0.7928 and the full-1536-d 0.7847.

3. **Refutes a "both reads share the principal axis" reading of F-3 (cos 0.046).** F-3 establishes prefill-DoM and final-token-DoM are *structurally* orthogonal (cos 0.046) within the same L19 residual space. The paper's premise predicts that any functionally important read of a matrix gravitates to its single dominant singular direction. If that were true here, both the prefill and final correctness reads would project heavily onto the *same* `v₁` of `W_L19` and therefore could not be near-orthogonal to each other. **Mechanism:** compute λ(prefill_DoM, W_L19) and λ(final_DoM, W_L19); if both are high *and* the two DoMs are orthogonal, the "one principal direction dominates all useful reads" premise fails — there must be ≥2 functionally distinct, non-principal read axes. **Falsifiable:** two projection computations on cached prefill/final DoM vectors.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Manifold Power Iteration (MPI), "Power-then-Retract"** — recover a weight matrix's principal singular direction online with one matrix-vector step `R̂ = R·W·Wᵀ`, then L2-retract `R' = C·R̂/‖R̂‖₂`. No SVD needed; converges to `v₁` over steps.
  *Replication cost*: 5–15min CPU on cached/downloaded L19 MLP weights (1536×8960 SwiGLU; even exact SVD is seconds).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — direct tool to test DoM-as-principal-weight-read (Refutations 1, 3).
- **λ normalized-projection alignment metric (Eq. 11)** — `λ = ‖R'·W‖₂ / (‖R'‖₂·‖W‖₂) ∈ [0,1]`, the cosine-like alignment of a read vector with a matrix's dominant row-space. Their Table 5 reports per-layer λ ≈ 0.22–0.37 (vanilla) vs 0.62–0.70 (MPI).
  *Replication cost*: seconds CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — the headline quantity for our weight-read test; gives an interpretable baseline (random ≈ low, principal ≈ 1).
- **Rayleigh-quotient / squared-projection objective (Eq. 3)** — `max ‖R W‖²/‖R‖²`, framing vector-matrix alignment as steepest ascent on `W Wᵀ`. Pure theory lens.
  *Replication cost*: n/a (analytical).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **no** as an experiment, but useful framing for interpreting λ results.
- **L2 retraction with `C = C'·N^(-1/2)` scale law** — norm-constrained gating-logit boundedness for training stability/load balance.
  *Replication cost*: n/a — training-time only.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **no** — irrelevant to read-out probing on a frozen model.

## Approaches & framings

- **"A read vector should encode the principal singular direction of the matrix it reads."** Reframes our supervised DoM as a candidate (possibly *failed*) principal weight read — turns "what is the DoM geometrically?" into a concrete, testable weight-space question. Intersects F-2 / H-740 / H-744 / H-256.
- **Power-then-Retract as steepest ascent on the Rayleigh quotient under minimal update.** An optimization lens that could reframe how *any* learned probe direction relates to the weights it reads from — i.e., is a logistic-regression DoM implicitly doing power iteration on `W_L19`, or pulling orthogonal to it? Intersects F-2/F-3.
- **Per-layer alignment profile (Table 5).** The idea of plotting a single alignment scalar across all layers is a cheap diagnostic we could borrow to ask *which* layer's MLP weights the correctness direction best aligns with — independent test of the L19 choice. Intersects F-2 layer-selection and H-921 (Fisher-J layer choice).

## Implementation details worth capturing

- PyTorch pseudo-code (Fig. 1): `R_hat = (R.unsqueeze(1) @ wg.transpose(1,2) @ wg).squeeze(); R_prime = normalize(R_hat, p=2, dim=-1); C = C_prime * N**-0.5; logits = C * (x @ R_prime.T)`.
- **Single** power iteration per training step is optimal; 10 iterations gave *worse* downstream (-1.39pt) and 5% lower throughput — "aggressive alignment disrupts stability." (Useful caution: full convergence to `v₁` is not always desirable.)
- `C := C'/√N` keeps gating-logit L∞ at O(1) independent of expert count N (Eq. 7).
- Built on `megablocks.layers.moe.MoE`; 0.2% training slowdown at 11B; router weights pre-computable at load → zero inference overhead.
- Post-hoc λ verification (Table 5) confirms MPI roughly doubles router↔expert principal alignment (≈0.25 → ≈0.65).

## Replicable intermediates

- **λ(DoM, W_L19) sanity check.** Load Qwen-2.5-1.5B L19 MLP weights (`mlp.gate_proj`/`up_proj`/`down_proj`; downloadable from HF, ~seconds), power-iterate (or SVD) for `v₁`, and compute Eq. 11 λ against the cached DoM vector used in FE291 / `pathway11_h100`. Compare to a random-vector baseline. This is the smallest possible test of the admission-note hypothesis and Refutation 1.
- **λ(PC1, W_L19) cross-check.** Same against the cached PC1 (cov-spectrum / `pca_covariance/results.json`, FE291). Since cos(DoM,PC1)=0.9216, λ(PC1,·) should track λ(DoM,·) — a consistency check.
- **Single-`v₁` read AUROC ceiling.** Project the 500×1536 cached L19 prefill activations onto `v₁`, score correctness AUROC, and compare against PC1-residualized cov-spectrum (0.7928) and full ceiling (0.7847) to test Refutation 2's sufficiency claim.
