# Triage brief — 2406.11717 — Refusal in Language Models Is Mediated by a Single Direction

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11717
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **The "best" single direction is selected by sweeping `|I| × L` candidates and validating against held-out behavior — not picked by intuition at one layer.** Arditi et al. extract a difference-in-means (DoM) vector at every (post-instruction position × layer) pair, then choose the single most effective vector by validation on `D_harmful^val ∪ D_harmless^val`. **Refutes F-2 implicitly**: our claim that "L19 prefill is special" is based on a layer choice we made, not on a position×layer sweep optimized against held-out correctness AUROC. If we ran their selection algorithm on Qwen-2.5-1.5B activations against MATH-500 correctness, the winning (l\*, i\*) might be (a) at a different layer (their refusal-direction attention heads cluster at L10-L14 of a 24-layer model, ≈40-58% depth, whereas our L19/L28 sits at 68% depth), or (b) at a different post-prompt token position. **Falsifiable**: re-extract DoM at every (layer, post-instruction-template-position) on Qwen-2.5-1.5B-Instruct, score each via OOF correctness AUROC on MATH-500. If peak AUROC > 0.7731 at a layer ≠ L19, the "L19 is canonical" claim is wrong.

2. **One direction works *across all post-instruction positions*; F-3's prefill ⊥ final-token orthogonality may be an artifact of our position choice, not a real two-mechanism story.** Arditi et al. ablate the *single* direction `r̂` at *all* positions and *all* layers and successfully erase refusal — i.e. the "behavior direction" is shared across the entire post-instruction trajectory in their setting. F-3 reports cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046, framing prefill and final-token as encoding qualitatively different things. **Refutes F-3 if** their methodology applied to correctness shows that the prefill DoM, when ablated at all positions including the final-answer token, behaves identically to the final-token DoM ablation — indicating both vectors are noisy estimators of the same underlying direction. **Falsifiable**: do the symmetric pair of directional ablations (ablate `prefill_DoM_unit` at ALL positions vs. ablate `final_DoM_unit` at ALL positions) on Qwen-2.5-1.5B-Instruct prefilled on MATH-500 problems; measure the change in MATH-500 K=1 accuracy. If both ablations move accuracy similarly, prefill and final-token are not "orthogonal mechanisms" in any causal sense.

3. **Causal sufficiency is the test, and we have not run it for correctness.** Arditi et al. demonstrate that for each model the single DoM direction is *both necessary and sufficient*: ablation removes refusal, addition induces refusal on harmless prompts. Our F-2 / F-8 are pure correlation — DoM AUROC 0.7731, selective-prediction at coverage 0.5. We have no analogue of "add `r` to a wrong-trajectory's residual stream and observe MATH-500 correctness flip." **Refutes F-2's "encodes correctness" framing if** activation-addition of prefill DoM to a known-incorrect trajectory does *not* flip the model toward correctness, and if directional ablation of prefill DoM does *not* degrade MATH-500 accuracy. Their paper sets the bar: any "direction encodes X" claim must show both add→induce-X and ablate→remove-X with minimal collateral damage on capability benchmarks. **Falsifiable**: this is essentially H-1 promoted to a refutation test, with weight orthogonalization (eq. 5 of the paper) as the cleaner intervention than per-position addition.

4. **Rank-1 weight orthogonalization preserves MMLU/ARC/GSM8K within ~1pp.** Arditi et al. show that orthogonalizing every output-writing weight matrix `W_out ← W_out − r̂r̂ᵀW_out` removes refusal while leaving MMLU/ARC/GSM8K within 99% confidence intervals (Table 3). **Refutes F-9 ("CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM")** in a stronger direction than F-9 itself goes: if a single rank-1 weight edit can completely rewire a behavior while preserving GSM8K within 1pp, the "60 features collapse to 1 direction" finding is even more conservative than stated — *all of CoE-60 plus L19 DoM may collapse to a single rank-1 weight direction.* **Falsifiable**: weight-orthogonalize Qwen-2.5-1.5B w.r.t. our prefill_L19_DoM and measure (a) MATH-500 accuracy delta, (b) CoE-60 trajectory feature distribution shift. If MATH-500 accuracy collapses to ~0% AND CoE-60 distribution becomes degenerate, the "rank-1 mechanism" hypothesis is supported and F-9 should be re-stated.

5. **Their direction lives in *post-instruction template tokens*, not the final prompt token.** Arditi et al. specifically restrict their candidate set to `i ∈ I` where `I` is "all template tokens after the instruction" — i.e. the chat-template scaffold tokens (`<|im_start|>assistant\n` for Qwen). Our prefill DoM is extracted at the end-of-prompt position. **Refutes F-2's positional choice** if the highest-AUROC position turns out to be one of the chat-template scaffold tokens (e.g. the `assistant` role token), not the user-prompt-final token. This would also reframe F-3: prefill (user-prompt-final) and final-token (post-CoT) being orthogonal is unsurprising if the *real* signal lives at neither — both at the chat-template "assistant" marker and at intermediate post-instruction positions. **Falsifiable**: extract DoM at every chat-template token between end-of-user-instruction and start-of-CoT, plus the user-prompt-final token, score each by AUROC against MATH-500 correctness.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM, AUROC 0.7731)** — strongest precedent: same difference-in-means probe, same residual-stream linear-direction story, validated across 13 models from 1.8B to 72B. Promotes our F-2 from "interesting on one model" toward "instance of a general phenomenon."
- **F-3 (cos(prefill, final) = 0.046)** — challenged. Arditi et al. claim a single direction works across all post-instruction positions; if so, prefill and final-token DoMs should be parallel, not orthogonal.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with L19 DoM)** — supported and extended. Their rank-1 weight orthogonalization preserving MMLU/ARC/GSM8K within ~1pp is consistent with "60 features collapse to 1 direction" but goes further: maybe everything collapses to 1 weight-space direction.
- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy)** — direct methodological precedent. Their activation-addition (eq. 3) and directional ablation (eq. 4) are the exact interventions H-1 needs. Their weight-orthogonalization (eq. 5) is a cleaner one-shot version.
- **H-13 (head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token circuits)** — direct methodological transfer. Their direct feature attribution (DFA) on attention heads (Kissane et al. 2024; Makelov et al. 2024) projecting head outputs onto the refusal direction is exactly the recipe for H-13.
- **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes replace supervised DoM)** — orthogonal: their work is fully supervised. They explicitly note "the methodology we used to extract the refusal direction is likely not optimal" — leaves the door open for label-free alternatives.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Difference-in-means (DoM) probe** — `r = µ_harmful − ν_harmless` per-(layer, position).
  *Replication cost*: already what we do; ~10min CPU on cached NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — already running.

- **Position × layer sweep validation selection** — compute |I|·L candidate vectors, score each by ablation+addition behavior on a held-out validation set, pick the single best.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached Qwen-2.5-1.5B activations IF we have all-layer all-post-instruction-position activations cached (we likely have only L19 cached — needs re-extraction, ~1h H100).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly tests whether L19 is actually special vs. an arbitrary choice.

- **Directional ablation `x' = x − r̂r̂ᵀx`** — projection out at every layer, every position, in-flight.
  *Replication cost*: small inference patch on Qwen forward pass, ~2h H100 for MATH-500 K=1 sweep.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives causal evidence for F-2 and addresses Refutation 3.

- **Activation addition at single layer, all positions** — `x^(l)' ← x^(l) + r^(l)` at the layer the vector was extracted from.
  *Replication cost*: same as above.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — the H-1 experiment.

- **Weight orthogonalization `W_out ← W_out − r̂r̂ᵀW_out`** — applied to embedding, positional embedding, attention out, MLP out matrices, plus output biases. Equivalent to inference-time directional ablation but free at inference and exposed as a one-shot weight edit.
  *Replication cost*: ~30min H100 (one weight edit + MATH-500 eval).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — cleanest one-shot test of "is the prefill direction causally load-bearing for correctness?"

- **Direct feature attribution (DFA) on attention heads** — project each head's output onto `r̂` to attribute "how much does this head write to the direction?"
  *Replication cost*: 1h on cached activations + 1h H100 for fresh per-head outputs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — backbone of H-13.

- **Adversarial-suffix vs. random-suffix cosine-similarity comparison** — control for "did appending any suffix change cos(activation, direction)" with a length-matched random-suffix baseline.
  *Replication cost*: ~1h H100.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes if we ever investigate prompt-injection robustness; not core to current pathway.

- **LM Eval Harness capability sweep (MMLU/ARC/GSM8K/TruthfulQA)** — standard battery to verify intervention does not destroy the model.
  *Replication cost*: ~3-4h H100 for the full sweep.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — necessary for any weight-modifying intervention we propose.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Necessary and sufficient" framing for a single direction**: behavioral interpretation of a probe direction must show both ablation removes the behavior and addition induces it on out-of-distribution prompts. Direct intersection: F-2 and F-8 are correlation-only; we have no sufficiency demonstration.
- **Reframing of "direction" from "probe target" to "rank-1 weight subspace"**: the same direction can be implemented as an inference-time activation hook, an MLP-only patch, or a weight-space edit. Implies F-2 should be tested at the weight-space level, not just as a probe.
- **"Behavior lives in mid-depth, attention heads write to it from layers 10-14 of 24"**: contrasts our late-layer (L19/28 ≈ 68% depth) framing. Suggests F-2 should sweep all layers before claiming L19 is canonical.
- **Mechanistic-suppression framing for adversarial prompts**: adversarial suffixes work by *hijacking attention* away from instruction-region tokens, not by adding a competing signal. Reframes any "prompt sensitivity" finding (e.g. F-7 D-bucket fragility, H-21 D-bucket attention entropy) toward attention-pattern analysis rather than activation-magnitude analysis.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Train/val splits: 128 train + 32 val for both harmful and harmless. Light-touch — same scale as our DoM training.
- Greedy decoding, max 512 tokens for refusal-evaluation generations.
- LlamaGuard 2 as the safety classifier (not used by us, but interesting as a "downstream classifier on completions" pattern).
- Open-source code: `https://github.com/andyrdt/refusal_direction` — directly importable for the position×layer sweep selection algorithm.
- TransformerLens for exploratory analysis; vLLM for production inference.
- Weight orthogonalization in eq. (5) applies to *all* matrices that write to the residual stream: embedding, positional embedding, attention-out (per-head per-layer), MLP-out, and any output biases. Easy to miss the bias terms.
- Their candidate-vector selection over `|I| × L` uses behavioral validation, not probe AUROC — they pick the direction that *causes* the most refusal flip on validation, not the direction that *predicts* refusal best. Worth noting: this is a different objective than our AUROC-based ranking.
- Qwen 1.8B has 24 layers; the top-8 attention heads for refusal cluster at L10-L14 (heads H12.10, H12.8, H14.2, H14.12, H14.6, H14.7, H13.2, H10.10). For Qwen-2.5-1.5B (28 layers), the analogous range would be roughly L12-L16, *not* L19.
- "Selecting a single vector" returns one (l\*, i\*) per model; activation addition is applied at l\* across all positions; directional ablation is applied across all layers and positions.

## 2406.11717 — Refusal in Language Models Is Mediated by a Single Direction (Arditi, Obeso, Syed, Paleka, Panickssery, Gurnee, Nanda; NeurIPS 2024)

**Relevance:** Direct precedent for the single-residual-stream-direction story underlying F-2 (prefill L19 DoM, AUROC 0.7731). Replicates the "one direction mediates a behavior" claim across 13 chat models from 1.8B to 72B (Qwen, Yi, Gemma, Llama-2, Llama-3 families) using difference-in-means probes on contrastive harmful/harmless instruction pairs. Establishes a methodological gold standard for any "direction X encodes behavior Y" claim: ablation must remove Y *and* addition must induce Y, with capability benchmarks (MMLU/ARC/GSM8K) preserved within ~1pp. Our F-2 / F-8 are correlation-only and have not met this bar. Also introduces weight orthogonalization (W ← W − r̂r̂ᵀW) as a one-shot equivalent to inference-time directional ablation — directly applicable to test whether prefill_L19_DoM is causally load-bearing. The paper also exposes two methodological gaps in our pipeline: (1) we choose L19 by intuition while they sweep all (layer, post-instruction-position) candidates and validate behaviorally; (2) we extract DoM at the user-prompt-final position while they extract from chat-template post-instruction tokens.

**Key claim we tested:** A single one-dimensional residual-stream direction extracted via DoM is necessary AND sufficient for a target behavior (refusal in their setting; we are testing for correctness).

**Our result:** **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED (necessary side: F-2 AUROC 0.7731; sufficiency side: untested).** Our F-2 is the necessary side — DoM at L19 prefill predicts correctness at AUROC 0.7731. The sufficient side (ablate → degrade MATH-500 accuracy; add → flip incorrect to correct) is the gap, addressed by H-73 / FE-72 / FE-73. Their layer choice (mid-depth, L10-L14 of 24) raises a refutation against our L19/28 ≈ 68% choice (H-72 / FE-71). Their "single direction across all post-instruction positions" challenges our F-3 orthogonality finding (Refutation 2 / FE-71).

**Related experiments:** P11-FE71, P11-FE72, P11-FE73, P11-FE74; H-72, H-73, H-74, H-75; H-1 (existing per-position DoM steering); H-13 (existing head-level attribution).
