# Triage brief — 2106.00012 — Persistent Homology Captures the Generalization of Neural Networks Without A Validation Set

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00012
Triaged: 2026-04-29

Authors: Gutiérrez-Fandiño, Pérez-Fernández, Armengol-Estapé, Villegas (BSC, 2021)

**One-line summary.** PH-diagram Heat distance between consecutive *training-state* MLP weight-graphs Pearson-correlates 0.62–0.89 with validation accuracy across MNIST/CIFAR/Reuters; the authors propose this "homological convergence" as a holdout-free generalization monitor. The object is a *directed flag complex on weighted MLP edges*, not residual-stream point clouds at inference.

## Refutations

1. **F-10 ("PH at Gaussian null on residual streams") — possible scope-error refutation via between-state PH.**
   F-10 evaluated PH features on *single-state* L19 residual point clouds and found they hit the matched-covariance Gaussian null (0.690 vs 0.693). This paper computes PH on a *fundamentally different object*: the weighted directed graph of MLP edges, with the filtration threshold sweeping normalized edge weight from 0 to 1 (Eq. 1), and looks at *Heat-distance between consecutive states* rather than within-state features. Mechanism: the discriminative signal in their setup lives in PD *evolution*, not in any single PD's static structure. Falsifiable: take ≥5 fine-tuning checkpoints of Qwen-2.5-1.5B on MATH-500 (or train an MLP probe head from scratch on cached L19 features), compute their Heat distance between consecutive checkpoint weight-graphs, check Pearson correlation with K=1 MATH-500 accuracy. If r > 0.7, F-10's null is scope-correct ("PH on inference residuals = covariance") but mis-generalized as "PH ⊥ generalization signal" — between-state PH on weights would carry signal that within-state PH on activations doesn't.

2. **F-9 ("CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM") — paper's between-state framing reframes "trajectory features".**
   F-9's "trajectory" was Wang's Chain-of-Embedding 60-d hand-engineered descriptor across layers within one forward pass. The paper here defines a *between-state distance* (per-batch persistence-diagram Heat distance) that explicitly captures step-to-step changes — a different "trajectory" axis (training time, not depth). Mechanism: per-token L19 residual evolution during MATH-500 generation has a natural between-state analogue — Heat-distance between PD(token_t) and PD(token_{t+1}) over the answer prefix. F-9 didn't test this; CoE-60 collapses depth and is silent on temporal between-state structure. Falsifiable: on the cached `pathway11_h100/extracted_states/` 1024-tok L19 sequences for the 500 MATH problems, build a Vietoris-Rips PD per token window (say 16-token sliding), compute Heat distance between consecutive PDs, and average per-problem; check whether this scalar adds AUROC over the 0.7731 DoM baseline. If it lifts AUROC > 0.79, F-9 was over-generalized to "all PH-flavoured trajectory features are redundant."

3. **F-1 ("breathing is universal across architectures and scales") — paper exposes an architecture-specific blind spot of PH-on-weights.**
   The paper finds CNN correlations drop sharply (CIFAR-10 CNN r=0.42 vs CIFAR-10 MLP r=0.82) because their MLP-only graph representation can't capture convolutional weight structure (Sec. 6 note). Mechanism: if PH-on-weights is architecture-coupled (works on MLPs, fails on conv) while *dimensional breathing in residuals* is architecture-universal (per F-1, replicated across Qwen, Llama, Gemma, scales 1.5B/7B), then breathing and PH-on-weights cannot be the same phenomenon — PH is the architectural-failure mode that F-1 claims to escape. Falsifiable: re-run Pathway 11's breathing extraction on a CNN-on-CIFAR setup AND apply the paper's Heat-PD method on the same training checkpoints. If breathing replicates with comparable PR-amplitude on the CNN but PH-Heat correlation drops to ~0.4 (matching the paper's CIFAR-10 CNN r=0.42), F-1 gains methodological independence from PH-on-weights and the failure-mode boundary is established. If breathing also degrades on CNN, F-1's "universal" claim narrows to transformer-specific.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Directed flag complex on weighted MLP edge-graph.** Each neuron is a 0-simplex; edges are weighted by `max(1 − |w|/max(|W|), ζ)` with ζ=1e-6 (Eq. 1). Negative weights become reverse edges, biases become edges to isolate vertices. *Replication cost*: 20–30 min CPU for a Qwen-1.5B head module per checkpoint; full model is intractable per their 7-day-on-2×V100 estimate. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes for *probe-head* MLPs only (cheap), no for full Qwen weights (memory-bound, paper authors flag scalability as the headline limitation).
- **Edge-weight–thresholded filtration with normalized weight ∈ [0,1].** ε sweeps 0→1 with η=0.01 minimum interval to suppress noise; persistence intervals computed up to H3 over Z2. *Replication cost*: 1–2h CPU per persistence diagram on a small MLP. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes for ablation against Pathway 9's H0+H1-only setup.
- **Heat and Silhouette PD vectorizations (giotto-tda).** Discretized form of PD permits efficient distance computation across millions of intervals; paper picks Heat over Silhouette empirically (Heat plots cleaner). *Replication cost*: trivial wrapper around existing giotto-tda calls. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct upgrade for the EXP-026 PH features (currently uses raw H0_max_lifetime, H0_entropy, H1_pers_entropy, etc., not vectorizations).
- **Between-consecutive-state PD distance ("homological convergence").** Compute PD at state t and t+1, take Heat-distance. *Replication cost*: 1–2h CPU per consecutive pair. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — analogue for between-token residual streams is the most novel reusable framing.
- **Cumulative-vs-non-cumulative distance summary.** Cumulative integration of step-wise distances stabilizes the curve enough to compare with val-acc. *Replication cost*: trivial. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes if we adopt the between-state framing.
- **Persistence diagram filtering by interval lifespan η.** Drop intervals with `death − birth < 0.01`. *Replication cost*: trivial. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly applicable to EXP-026 features.

## Approaches & framings

- **Homological convergence as a holdout-free generalization monitor.** Reframes generalization tracking as a *self-similarity* problem (compare model to itself across training) rather than an *external-data* problem (compare model output to held-out labels). Intersects F-10 (which tested *within-state* PH and got null) by suggesting we tested the wrong object.
- **Between-state vs within-state TDA dichotomy.** Most TDA-on-NN papers measure within-state structure (one PD per network); this paper measures between-state evolution (Δ-PD per step). Reframes F-9's "trajectory features are redundant" — depth-trajectory ≠ time-trajectory.
- **Architecture-coupling of PH-on-weights.** Their CNN failure mode (r=0.42) makes architecture-dependence explicit. Intersects F-1's universality framing by carving out a regime (conv weights) where their method fails but breathing presumably wouldn't.
- **PH as a scale-up wall.** Authors flag 7 days on 2×V100 for tiny MLPs (4–256 unit layers) on MNIST/CIFAR. This is a hard scaling cliff, and means "PH on Qwen weights" is methodologically blocked, motivating activation-space alternatives (which is what F-10 already explored).

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Code: https://github.com/asier-gutierrez/nn-evolution (MIT)
- Library: giotto-tda (PD vectorizations, distance computations)
- Filtration normalization: `max(1 − |w|/max(|max(W)|, |min(W)|), ζ)`, ζ=1e-6
- Persistence interval filter: η=0.01 minimum lifespan, infinity replaced by 1.0
- Field: Z₂ for persistence
- Homology dimensions: H0–H3
- Optimizer for MLPs: RMSProp, batch 256
- Architectures: base MLP `{Input, Linear(512), Dropout(0.2), Linear(512), Dropout(0.2), Output}`; CNN feature extractor frozen (3 conv blocks, k=3, starting 32 channels, max-pool interleaved)
- Hyperparameter sweeps: layer size {4,16,32,128,256}, label count {2,4,6,8,10}, lr {1e-5..0.1}, dropout {0,0.2,0.4,0.5,0.8}; 5 seeds each
- Hardware: 2×V100 32GB + 1.5TB RAM, 7 days
