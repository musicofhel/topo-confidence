# Triage brief — 2505.23224 — MMBoundary: Advancing MLLM Knowledge Boundary Awareness through Reasoning Step Confidence Calibration

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23224
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-2 ("Prefill L19 DoM is a strong correctness predictor, *better than final-token*") in the per-step granularity regime.** MMBoundary builds its calibration signal from sentence-level *generation-time* token statistics (length-normalized log-prob, mean token entropy, TokenSAR) and shows MECE 0.304–0.392 — i.e. confidence quality at sentence granularity *without any hidden-state probe*. Mechanism: sentence-level token-entropy aggregation captures variance the single prefill projection cannot, because prefill is computed before any of the reasoning chain has been generated. **Falsifying experiment**: on cached MATH-500 K=1 generations from `pathway11_h100`, compute per-sentence U_LNLP / U_MTE / U_TSAR; train a logistic regressor on (per-sentence-correctness vs. per-sentence-uncertainty) and a parallel one on (per-problem-correctness vs. prefill-DoM). If the per-sentence aggregate beats prefill DoM AUROC 0.7731, F-2's "*better*" claim is restricted to question-only prompts and falls in the step-granularity regime.

2. **Refutes F-9 ("CoE-60 is redundant with single-layer L19 DoM").** MMBoundary's ablation shows that *removing any one* of {U_LNLP, U_MTE, U_TSAR, U_CLIPScore} hurts MECE — i.e. the four uncertainty signals are *not* mutually redundant in their setting. Mechanism: their TokenSAR signal weights tokens by relevance-to-context, which is orthogonal to mean-pooled hidden geometry. **Falsifying experiment**: on cached pathway11 NPZs, augment the L19 DoM probe with a per-question feature vector (mean U_TSAR, mean U_MTE) and re-fit the logistic head. If AUROC moves materially above 0.7731, then "single-layer L19 DoM is sufficient" (F-9's central claim that CoE-60's gain is illusory) does not generalize — the redundancy was specific to within-CoE features, not to all generation-time signals.

3. **Refutes F-8 ("Selective-prediction via prefill DoM works at 50% coverage = 71.6% on answered").** MMBoundary delivers per-sentence rather than per-problem confidence, enabling *partial-answer abstention* (skip/regenerate flagged steps inside a chain) — a strictly richer policy than F-8's per-problem refuse-and-spend. Mechanism: hallucination-snowballing means that one bad sentence near the start dominates K=1 correctness; a per-step policy short-circuits before the cascade. **Falsifying experiment**: on the same MATH-500 1024-tok generations, reproduce F-8 numbers, then compare with a per-sentence policy that regenerates the lowest-confidence sentence (using their U_TSAR proxy) on a 50% sentence-coverage budget at matched compute. If sentence-level outperforms problem-level at matched K, F-8's framing ("prefill direction is the right granularity") is wrong even if the *direction itself* survives.

4. **Refutes H-12 ("Semantic-entropy probes (label-free) replace supervised DoM for refusal").** Stronger than corroboration: MMBoundary's Table 3 reports 93–96% agreement between their Internal Confidence Estimation and self-consistency-derived confidence at U-difference < 0.1 — but their *combined* signal (with reward-shaped RL) crushes self-consistency-only baselines by 5.7% MECE. Mechanism: pure label-free uncertainty is calibration-poor without RL alignment to a knowledge-accuracy reward. **Falsifying experiment**: on MATH-500, compute label-free U_TSAR and compare to F-2's supervised prefill DoM; then add a small PPO step using K=8 majority correctness as RKA. If RL-aligned label-free already matches supervised DoM, H-12's "replace" claim is *too weak* — the right framing is "label-free + RL alignment can match"; if RL-aligned label-free still trails, H-12 itself is refuted.

5. **Refutes H-5 ("Prefill signal encodes problem familiarity vs decomposability").** MMBoundary's per-sentence calibration shows that confidence is highly *non-uniform within a single chain* (their MECE drops 7.5% over per-response baselines because the *dispersion* matters). Mechanism: if prefill encoded "decomposability" as a problem-level scalar, you would not expect within-chain confidence variance to dominate calibration error. **Falsifying experiment**: on cached generations, compute within-question variance of token-entropy across sentences, and check correlation with prefill-DoM residual after regressing out problem-level accuracy. If within-chain variance is substantial *and* uncorrelated with prefill DoM, then prefill cannot be encoding a holistic decomposability signal — at best it's a problem-level prior.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 / F-8** (AUROC 0.7731 on Qwen-2.5-1.5B; 71.6% selective at 50% coverage) — MMBoundary attacks the same "predict correctness from internal state" problem but at *sentence* granularity, with MECE 0.304/0.392/0.361 across A-OKVQA/ScienceVQA/CulturalVQA, and Reasoning-Chain F1 up to 0.665. Direct comparator: their best ECE 0.316 on A-OKVQA is the multimodal analogue of our 0.7731 AUROC frame.
- **F-9** (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM; cos(prefill, final) = 0.046) — MMBoundary's four-signal ablation contradicts the "one signal suffices" intuition in their setting; matters for whether CoE-60's gain over L19-DoM was a math-only artifact.
- **F-3** (prefill ⊥ final-token DoM, cos 0.046) — MMBoundary uses *only* generation-time token statistics, never decomposed by position-class. If sentence-level signals subsume both prefill and final-token information, F-3's orthogonality is informationally moot for the calibration use case.
- **H-12** (label-free semantic-entropy probes) — Their U_MTE / U_TSAR are exactly the H-12 family; they validate the family at scale and report 93–96% self-consistency agreement, which strengthens the *prior* that H-12 will replicate, even if their "replace supervised DoM" framing is too strong.
- **H-19** (C_exact verification routing beats C_infer scaling at matched compute, CRITICAL) — MMBoundary's per-sentence reward `R_KA` (knowledge-accuracy alignment vs. annotated reference chain) is a *step-level* form of C_exact; their 8.3% accuracy lift over self-consistency baselines is direct evidence that step-level verification beats response-level sampling.
- **H-9** (breathing amplitude predicts problem difficulty) — Their MECE breakdown by dataset (ICE Std ~0.039 across all three) suggests confidence-dispersion is comparable across domains, weakly arguing breathing amplitude as a problem-level rather than dataset-level signal.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Length-normalized log probability** — exp(−(1/L) log P(y|x,θ)). One-line: average sequence neg-log-prob, length-debiased.
  *Replication cost*: 5min CPU on cached generation logits (we have these from pathway11_h100).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — already a baseline anyone would compute; useful as the "no-probe" floor against prefill-DoM AUROC 0.7731.

- **Mean Token Entropy (U_MTE)** — average per-token entropy of the next-token distribution (Fomicheva et al., 2020).
  *Replication cost*: 5min CPU on cached logits.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly compatible with H-12; would let us answer whether label-free entropy probes can match supervised prefill-DoM on MATH-500.

- **TokenSAR (Duan et al., 2024)** — relevance-weighted neg-log-prob, where token relevance R_T(y_k, y, x) = 1 − g(x∪y, x∪y\y_k) for a sentence-similarity g.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached logits + a sentence-similarity model (e.g. all-MiniLM-L6-v2).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — a refined variant of MTE that the ablation singles out as having the *largest* contribution to MECE; high value-per-hour if we want to challenge F-9's redundancy claim.

- **CLIPScore as cross-modal uncertainty (Hessel et al., 2021)** — max(cos(visual, text), 0).
  *Replication cost*: not applicable to our pipeline.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — our setting is text-only MATH-500.

- **Confidence statement pool + SBERT cosine reverse-mapping** — five-bin natural-language confidence statements; sentence-encoder cosine similarity maps generated language back to a numeric score for reward shaping.
  *Replication cost*: 1h on existing models; we'd need MATH-500 with ground-truth per-step correctness annotations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — adds capability we don't currently exercise; orthogonal to the geometry program.

- **MECE — Multi-granularity ECE** — per-sentence ECE: (1/|A|) Σ_a (1/|a|) Σ_{(z,c)∈a} |I(z) − conf(c)|.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU once we have per-sentence labels.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — finer-grained than ECE; relevant if we ever add per-step supervision to MATH-500.

- **PPO with three additive rewards** (R_KA = step-level knowledge accuracy vs. reference chain; R_EC = sentence-level expected calibration; R_CS = consistency between expressed-confidence and internal-confidence).
  *Replication cost*: 1–2 days H100 on a 1.5B model.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — too expensive vs. the geometry-only program; mention as a baseline if we ever pivot to RL-calibration.

- **GPT-4o reference-chain annotation pipeline** — perception-level + reasoning-level decomposition; per-sentence single-knowledge constraint.
  *Replication cost*: ~$0.001 per problem × 500 = ~$0.50 for MATH-500 with quality filtering.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes, cheaply — would unlock per-step labels for any sentence-level analysis on MATH-500.

## Approaches & framings

- **Sentence-level confidence as the natural granularity for hallucination prevention** — frames hallucination as a *cascade* (snowballing) rather than a final-answer phenomenon. Intersects F-4 (asymmetric collapse at the *final* token) directly: if the cascade view is right, F-4's final-token signal is *too late* — the damage is done several sentences earlier.
- **Aligning expressed (verbal) and internal (entropy) confidence as a reward signal** — confidence as a property of *language* the model emits, not just a hidden-state property. Cleanly contrary to our pure-geometry frame; suggests geometry might be necessary but insufficient for actionable calibration.
- **Cross-modal grounding as a confidence prior** (CLIPScore) — encodes a constraint that the answer must be *connected* to the input — analogous to a "groundedness probe" we don't currently have on MATH-500 (where the analogue would be "answer must be connected to the problem statement, not memorized").

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Backbone: **LLaVA-NeXT 7B** primary, **Qwen2VL 7B** generalization check (Appendix E).
- Confidence binning: U_Final percentile bins at [0, μ−σ, μ+σ, μ+2σ, μ+3σ, 1] mapping to confidence levels 5..1.
- Sentence encoder for reverse-mapping statements → scores: `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`.
- Reward weights α, β, γ on R_KA, R_EC, R_CS — values not in main text (likely Appendix B); cite as a tuning gotcha if replicating.
- Inter-annotator Kappa = 0.79 on faithfulness — concrete benchmark for our own human-eval if we ever do one.
- Significance: paired t-tests p < 0.05 against all baselines.
- Self-consistency vs. internal-confidence agreement: |C_ICE − C_SCE| < 0.1 for 93–96% of samples — useful number for future H-12 work.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2505.23224 — MMBoundary: Advancing MLLM Knowledge Boundary Awareness through Reasoning Step Confidence Calibration (He et al., ACL 2025)

**Relevance:** Step-level confidence calibration on multimodal reasoning chains, using a four-signal label-free uncertainty estimator (U_LNLP, U_MTE, U_TSAR, U_CLIPScore) followed by SFT + PPO with three reward functions (knowledge-accuracy, expected-calibration, internal/external consistency). Primary connection to our work is the methodology stack — TokenSAR and Mean Token Entropy are directly applicable to MATH-500 — and the *framing* shift from per-response to per-step calibration, which directly tests F-8's granularity assumption and weakens H-12's "replace supervised DoM" claim. The multimodal benchmarks (A-OKVQA / ScienceVQA / CulturalVQA) are not transferable, but the per-token machinery is.

**Key claim we tested:** Label-free, generation-time per-sentence uncertainty signals are sufficient to drive substantial calibration improvements (MECE −7.5%, accuracy +8.3%) without any supervised hidden-state probe.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** Pending FE-73 / FE-74 / FE-75 on cached MATH-500 logits. If U_TSAR alone matches prefill-DoM AUROC 0.7731, F-2 must be re-stated with a label-free generation-time baseline. If per-sentence variance is informative beyond prefill, H-5 needs revision. Their *multimodal* claims (CLIPScore-driven calibration) are not testable in our pipeline.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE73, P11-FE74, P11-FE75, H-73, H-74, H-75; relates to F-2, F-3, F-8, F-9, H-5, H-12, H-19.

**Status:** TO TEST
