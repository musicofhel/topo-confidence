# Triage brief — 2602.01992 — Emergent Analogical Reasoning in Transformers

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01992
Triaged: 2026-05-26

**Authors:** Minegishi, Feng, Furuta, Kojima, Iwasawa, Matsuo (ICML 2026 spotlight)

**One-line:** Formalizes analogical reasoning as functor application between categories; shows geometric alignment in embedding space + additive vector arithmetic in residual stream implement analogy in both synthetic transformers and pretrained LLMs (Gemma2-2B/9B, LLaMA), with non-monotonic scaling.

## Refutations

**R1 — Against F-2 (single L19 prefill direction, AUROC 0.7731):** The paper demonstrates that what appears as a single discriminative direction in embedding space can be a *functor vector* implementing a structured relational mapping between categories. If the DoM direction is functioning as a functor between "correct-class" and "incorrect-class" embedding categories, then AUROC 0.7731 measures the quality of learned category alignment rather than intrinsic problem-difficulty geometry. **Mechanism:** Compute Dirichlet Energy E(ℰ) = Σ ||h_correct_i − h_incorrect_j||² over L19 prefill activations for correct/incorrect problem pairs matched by difficulty. If Dirichlet Energy is low (indicating functor-like geometric alignment), the DoM direction is a functor artifact, not a geometric predictor. **Falsifiable by:** Compare Dirichlet Energy between (a) correct/incorrect groups matched by MATH-500 topic, and (b) random correct/correct pairs. If (a) ≪ (b), the functor interpretation is supported and F-2's "direction" framing is misleading.

**R2 — Against F-3 (prefill/final DoM orthogonality, cos=0.046):** The paper's layer-wise analysis shows Dirichlet Energy decreasing progressively across layers while target probability increases — i.e., geometric alignment is *built up* through successive transformer layers. Their functor arithmetic `e_target ≈ e_source + f` naturally produces near-orthogonality when the functor vector f is large relative to the source embedding. **Mechanism:** If the prefill→final-token transformation is interpretable as additive functor application (h_final ≈ h_prefill + f_transform), then cos(DoM_prefill, DoM_final) ≈ 0 is an expected consequence of the functor being orthogonal to the source, not a surprising structural finding. **Falsifiable by:** Fit the functor model h_final = h_prefill + f for the 500 MATH-500 samples, compute residual ‖h_final − (h_prefill + f)‖. If residual is small relative to ‖f‖, the functor model explains the orthogonality and F-3 loses its distinctiveness as a finding.

**R3 — Against F-1 (dimensional breathing universality across scales):** The paper's central scaling result is that analogical reasoning shows *non-monotonic* scaling: geometric alignment in embedding space improves from d_model=64→128→256 but *degrades* at 512. Compositional reasoning scales monotonically. If breathing is an instance of geometric alignment (which it must be if it reflects correct/incorrect category structure), then F-1's "universality across architectures and scales" is at risk: breathing could follow the same non-monotonic pattern, appearing at 1.5B and 7B but potentially vanishing or inverting at different widths. **Mechanism:** The residual stream width changes between 1.5B (1536-dim) and 7B (3584-dim), and we already see qualitative differences (F-6: 7B prefill PR inversion). The paper predicts that geometric structure should degrade at wider residual streams. **Falsifiable by:** Extract breathing metrics at a third scale (e.g., 0.5B Qwen-2.5 with narrower residual stream ~896-dim). If breathing is *stronger* at the narrower model, the non-monotonic pattern holds and F-1's universality claim needs qualification.

**R4 — Against F-10 (PH null on residual streams):** The paper shows that weight decay regime critically determines whether structured geometric representations emerge at all. Moderate weight decay (0.01–0.1) accelerates structured representation formation; strong weight decay (1.0) prevents it entirely while still permitting compositional reasoning. If Qwen-2.5's training used weight decay in a regime that suppresses relational geometric structure (which is plausible for standard large-scale pretraining), then PH appearing indistinguishable from Gaussian is a training artifact, not a statement about the representational capacity. **Mechanism:** PH measures global topological features of the point cloud; the paper shows these features are regime-dependent. **Falsifiable by:** Compare PH of L19 activations from models trained with different weight decay (e.g., fine-tune a small model with WD=0.01 vs WD=1.0 on the same data, then compute PH). If PH is non-null only under moderate WD, F-10's null is regime-specific, not fundamental.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (DoM AUROC 0.7731):** The paper's functor vector `f` that maps between categories is mechanistically parallel to the DoM direction. Both are single vectors in the residual stream that separate two classes. The paper's "parallelism measure" (cosine similarity between (e_target − e_source) and f) directly parallels our cos(DoM, PC1) = 0.9216. If the DoM direction is interpretable as a functor, it reframes F-2 from "geometric predictor" to "emergent category alignment."

- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046):** The paper's layer-wise Dirichlet Energy analysis (decreasing across later layers) combined with additive functor arithmetic provides an alternative explanation for the near-orthogonality. Rather than being a surprising structural finding, it may be the expected signature of a structured transformation.

- **F-1 (breathing universality):** The paper's non-monotonic scaling of geometric structure (optimal at intermediate width, degrading at large width) directly bears on whether breathing is universal or width-dependent. The 1.5B→7B comparison (F-6 shows qualitative inversion) already hints at non-monotonicity.

- **F-4 (asymmetric collapse):** The paper's Dirichlet Energy framework could quantify whether "correct trajectories collapse harder" is equivalent to "correct-category embeddings achieve tighter geometric alignment" — i.e., correct answers have lower Dirichlet Energy because they map more cleanly to a structured category.

- **F-10 (PH null):** The paper's finding that geometric structure is training-regime-dependent (weight decay sensitivity) provides a potential explanation for why PH is null — not because topology is absent, but because the training regularization prevents it from forming.

- **H-4 (breathing ↔ Sharpness Dimension correspondence):** The paper's category-theoretic formalization of geometric alignment could provide the formal bridge between breathing and sharpness. If breathing = geometric alignment of functor categories, and sharpness = eigenspectrum of the loss landscape, the functor framework might connect them.

- **H-5 (prefill signal encodes problem familiarity vs decomposability):** The paper explicitly distinguishes compositional reasoning (decomposable) from analogical reasoning (relational). If the prefill signal differentially encodes these two types, H-5's "familiarity vs decomposability" maps onto the paper's "compositional vs analogical" distinction.

## Methodologies extracted

  *Replication cost*: 10min CPU — we have 500 MATH-500 L19 prefill activations cached; define correct/incorrect "correspondence" matrix and compute.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — directly applicable to cached NPZ data as a new metric on the 500×1536 activation matrix.

- **Parallelism measure (functor vector arithmetic)** — Cosine similarity between (h_target − h_source) and the functor vector f, measuring whether inter-category mapping is implemented as additive vector arithmetic.
  *Replication cost*: 15min CPU — compute as cos((h_correct_mean − h_incorrect_mean), DoM_direction) for matched problem pairs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — tests whether DoM acts as a functor.

- **Layer-wise Dirichlet Energy progression** — Track geometric alignment metric across all 28 layers of Qwen-2.5-1.5B to see if alignment builds progressively (as in the paper's LLM experiments).
  *Replication cost*: 2h H100 — requires fresh extraction at all layers (currently only L19 cached).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **no** for now — requires multi-layer extraction, which is an H100 job.

- **Logit lens + Dirichlet Energy coupling** — The paper measures both Dirichlet Energy decrease and target logit probability increase across layers, showing they are temporally coupled. Could be replicated to check if L19's DoM signal coincides with a Dirichlet Energy minimum.
  *Replication cost*: 2h H100 — same multi-layer requirement.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **no** for now — same blocker as above.

- **PCA-based structural alignment visualization** — PCA on entity embeddings before and after training, colored by category, to visualize geometric alignment emergence. Directly analogous to our PCA on correct/incorrect activations.

## Approaches & framings

- **Analogy as functor, not composition.** The paper's core distinction: analogical reasoning (identifying relational correspondence across domains) is categorically different from compositional reasoning (chaining known facts). This reframes the DoM direction: rather than measuring "how hard is this problem" (a compositional fact), it might measure "how well does this problem's representation align with the correct-answer category" (a relational/analogical mapping). Intersects H-5's familiarity-vs-decomposability distinction.

- **Two-component decomposition of reasoning.** Geometric alignment (passive, in embedding space) + functor application (active, via attention/residual arithmetic). Maps onto our prefill (passive geometry) vs final-token (active computation) distinction in F-3. Provides a theoretical frame for why these should be orthogonal: they are different components of the same mechanism.

- **Non-monotonic scaling of structured representations.** Compositional capabilities scale monotonically; analogical/geometric capabilities don't. This is a conceptual shift from "bigger is better" and may explain the qualitative 1.5B↔7B differences (F-6).

- **Training dynamics as diagnostic.** The paper uses the three-stage learning progression (memorization → composition → analogy) to identify when geometric structure emerges. Applied to our context: the *order* in which breathing/DoM/PCA structure appears during training could reveal whether these are memorization artifacts, compositional side-effects, or genuine geometric structure.
