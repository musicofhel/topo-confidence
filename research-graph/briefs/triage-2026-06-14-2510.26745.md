# Triage brief — 2510.26745 — Deep sequence models tend to memorize geometrically; it is unclear why.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26745
Triaged: 2026-06-14

> Noroozizadeh, Nagarajan, Rosenfeld, Kumar (CMU + Google Research, 2025). A theory/mechanistic
> paper. Core claim: deep sequence models store atomic facts not only as *associative memory*
> (a rotated adjacency-matrix lookup over near-orthogonal embeddings) but as *geometric memory* —
> embeddings arranged so that `Φ(u)ᵀΦ(v)` encodes the **global multi-hop distance** between
> entities, even for pairs that never co-occur. This is a low-rank factorization of the adjacency
> matrix whose top directions are the **top eigenvectors of the (negative) graph Laplacian**
> (Fiedler vectors). They show it via in-weights path-star path-finding, reduce it to a weight-tied
> 2-layer linear (1-hop Node2Vec) model, and argue the geometry arises from a **spectral bias that
> emerges naturally from cross-entropy minimization** without rank/bottleneck/multi-hop-supervision
> pressures. Transformers show an "adulterated" version (global geometry competing with associative
> memory + a "bipartite geometric memory" living in the most-*negative* eigendirections that the
> SVD of weight-untied models folds up).

## Refutations

**R1 — F-2 / H-5: the L19 prefill DoM may be a memorization-proximity coordinate, not a reasoning-correctness signal.**
F-2 claims a single L19 prefill direction *predicts correctness* at AUROC 0.7731, and the project's
working interpretation is that it reads out something about the model's confidence/computation. This
paper's geometric-memory thesis says the dominant low-rank direction of a model's internal embedding
geometry is the **top Laplacian/Fiedler eigenvector encoding global multi-hop distance** — i.e. how
*geometrically close* a query's entities are to densely-memorized regions. Mechanism: if MATH-500
problems whose tokens/entities sit in well-organized (strongly geometric) regions of L19's spectral
embedding are the ones answered correctly, then DoM ≈ PC1 (cos 0.9216) is reading *memorization
familiarity*, and "correctness" is confounded with proximity-to-memorized-facts. This refutes F-2's
*interpretation* (reasoning/confidence) even while preserving its number. Falsifiable: stratify the
500 problems by an independent memorization-proximity proxy (n-gram/entity overlap with a training
corpus, or nearest-neighbor density in the L19 prefill cloud). If DoM AUROC collapses toward chance
*within* the high-familiarity stratum but the high-vs-low-familiarity split itself carries most of
the 0.7731, DoM is a familiarity detector, not a correctness detector.

**R2 — F-3: prefill/final-token DoM orthogonality (cos 0.046) may be the global-vs-bipartite eigendirection split, not "two structurally distinct circuits".**
F-3 asserts the prefill and final-token DoM directions are structurally orthogonal and reads this as
two separate circuits. The paper explicitly identifies *two co-existing geometric memories sharing
one spectral structure*: a **"global" geometry on the top (most-positive) eigenvectors** and a
**"bipartite" geometry on the most-negative, zig-zagging eigendirections** that the SVD of a
weight-untied model folds into separate top singular directions. Mechanism: prefill DoM could load on
the top-positive (global navigation) eigenvectors of the L19 covariance while final-token DoM loads
on the most-negative (bipartite-cut) eigendirections of the *same* operator — orthogonal by spectral
construction, not by being independent circuits. Falsifiable: eigendecompose the L19 covariance (or
the pooled prefill∪final Gram matrix), project both DoM vectors onto the eigenbasis. If prefill loads
on top-positive eigvecs and final loads on the most-negative/alternating-sign eigvecs, F-3's
"two circuits" framing is a single-spectrum SVD-folding artifact.

**R3 — F-7: D-bucket's "distinctive collective geometric signature" may be a spectral memorization-failure region, not a difficulty signature.**
F-7 says the hardest (D-bucket) problems share a *collective, not per-problem* geometric signature.
The paper's "adulterated spectral bias" remark predicts exactly such a collective structure: a
Transformer's geometry has *headroom* — regions where global geometric memory failed to form and the
model fell back on associative (local-lookup) memory. Mechanism: D-bucket = problems sitting in the
poorly-organized / low-top-eigenvector-projection region of L19's spectral embedding (associative,
not geometric). Their shared signature is *where geometric memory broke down*, which is a
memorization-coverage property, not an intrinsic-difficulty property. Falsifiable: compute each
problem's projection energy onto the top-k L19 covariance eigenvectors; if D-bucket problems
collectively occupy the low-projection ("ungeometrized") tail, the distinctive signature is a
spectral-coverage region, reframing F-7.

**R4 — geometry-portable-signal (REFUTED premise) / F-10: we may have refuted the wrong geometry.**
The premise `geometry-portable-signal` was refuted (P11-FE-EDGEGEN) using token-cloud spectra,
layer-depth profiles, prompt clouds, and PH summaries; F-10 separately found PH indistinguishable
from a Gaussian null. This paper argues the *informative* geometry is none of those — it is the
**top Laplacian eigenvectors of the model's in-weights entity graph**, a global multi-hop structure
that is *not* a per-cloud topological summary and *not* a covariance-of-token-positions statistic.
Mechanism: if global portable signal lives in the Fiedler/spectral-embedding readout rather than the
geometry features we actually tested, our refutation of `geometry-portable-signal` over-generalizes
from a non-representative readout family. Falsifiable: build the spectral (Laplacian-Fiedler)
embedding of the 500 prefill vectors and test whether *that* readout carries correctness signal
out-of-family where token-cloud/PH geometry did not. (Note: this also *corroborates* F-10's
spectral-beats-topology direction — the cov-spectrum probe at 0.7928 already beats topology — so R4
is the weakest of the four and is as much an extension as a refutation.)

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (AUROC 0.7731) + FE291 cos(DoM, PC1) = 0.9216.** The paper says geometric memory's
  dominant directions are the *top eigenvectors* of the model's internal adjacency/Laplacian. PC1 is
  literally the top eigenvector of the L19 activation covariance, and DoM ≈ PC1 (0.9216). The paper
  supplies a *mechanistic story* for why a single top-eigenvector direction would carry correctness
  signal: it is the model's global geometric-memory coordinate.
- **Cov-spectrum probe, top-20 PC1-residualized log-eigvals AUROC 0.7928 (FE881).** This is exactly
  a spectral readout of the L19 geometry, and it is the project's *best* probe. The paper's central
  claim — informative structure is spectral/low-rank, concentrated in the leading eigendirections —
  predicts precisely this win, and predicts that residualizing PC1 (the global Fiedler direction)
  and reading the *remaining* spectrum captures the "adulteration" structure.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046).** See R2 — directly reinterpretable as the
  global-vs-bipartite (top-positive vs most-negative) eigendirection split.
- **F-7 (D-bucket collective signature).** See R3 — directly reinterpretable as the model's
  "adulterated"/ungeometrized spectral region.
- **F-10 (PH ≈ Gaussian null).** Corroborated: the paper locates the real structure in the *linear*
  graph spectrum, not in topology, consistent with PH adding nothing beyond covariance.
- **H-5 (prefill signal encodes problem familiarity vs decomposability).** The geometric-memory
  framing is a concrete mechanism for H-5: "familiarity" = proximity in the spectral entity
  embedding; "decomposability" = whether the answer is reachable by short geometric navigation vs
  requiring ℓ-fold associative composition. This is the closest conceptual match in the brief.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Graph-Laplacian / Fiedler spectral embedding of an entity set** — embed items by the top
  eigenvectors of the (negative) graph Laplacian of a similarity/adjacency graph; these encode global
  multi-hop distance.
  *Replication cost*: ~20–40 min CPU on the cached 500×1536 L19 prefill matrix (build kNN graph →
  Laplacian → eigsh top-k).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — it is a different dimensionality reduction to compare head-to-head
  with PCA/DoM on the same cached activations (R4 / FE-A).
- **EVD-vs-SVD eigendirection decomposition (spectral folding)** — diagnose whether two directions
  are top-positive vs most-negative eigendirections of one operator; weight-untied SVD "folds" the
  negative spectrum into top singular directions.
  *Replication cost*: ~10 min CPU (one eigendecomposition of cached L19 covariance + two projections).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct test of R2 / F-3 (FE-B).
- **Top-k-eigenvector projection-energy diagnostic** — measure how much of an item's representation
  lives in the leading vs trailing eigenspace ("geometric" vs "associative/adulterated" coverage).
  *Replication cost*: ~15 min CPU on cached covariance + per-problem prefill vectors.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct test of R3 / F-7 (FE-C).
- **Multi-hop cosine-similarity heatmaps** — visualize node-node cosine structure to read off cluster
  geometry; here applied to per-problem prefill vectors stratified by correctness.
  *Replication cost*: ~10 min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes (cheap visualization, low ROI on its own).
- **Eigenvector-projection-norm training dynamics** — track ‖projection of embedding onto each
  eigenvector‖ over training to see the spectral contraction.
  *Replication cost*: needs training-time checkpoints / multiple model snapshots — H100/new-data.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — we don't train; only relevant if H-8 (checkpoint stability) is run.
- **Weight-tied 2-layer linear Node2Vec sandbox** — minimal model isolating spectral bias.
  *Replication cost*: needs a new toy training setup.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — off-topic for the MATH-500 hidden-state pipeline.

## Approaches & framings

- **Associative vs geometric memory dichotomy** — reframes "what a model stores" as lookup-table vs
  low-rank-factorized-with-global-structure. Intersects H-5 (familiarity vs decomposability) and the
  DoM-as-readout interpretation in F-2: gives a name to "what the prefill direction is reading."
- **Spectral bias as the source of representation geometry** — top Laplacian eigenvectors emerge
  naturally from cross-entropy minimization, no rank/bottleneck/multi-hop pressure required.
  Intersects the cov-spectrum result (FE881, 0.7928) and PC1≈DoM (FE291): supplies a "why" for the
  spectral-readout findings being the strongest probes.
- **"Adulterated" geometry / measurable headroom** — Transformer geometry = clean spectral geometry
  contaminated by associative memory. Intersects F-7 (D-bucket = the adulterated region) and the
  PC1-residualization move in FE881.
- **Bipartite geometric memory (negative eigendirections)** — a second, zig-zagging geometry on the
  most-negative spectrum. Intersects F-3: a concrete candidate identity for the final-token DoM
  direction.
- **Implicit in-weights reasoning replaces ℓ-fold composition with 1-step navigation** — reframes
  "hard reasoning" as geometric reachability. Intersects H-5's "decomposability" axis.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Spectral bias claim is for the **negative graph Laplacian** `≈ A − D`; learned embeddings align
  with its **top (Fiedler) eigenvectors**. For us: the analogue is the top eigenvectors of the L19
  activation covariance (PC1 = global/Fiedler analogue).
- **Weight tying matters**: untied two-layer models factorize via SVD (folds negative eigenvalues
  into top singular directions → bipartite geometry); tied models factorize via EVD (clean global
  geometry). Relevant when interpreting prefill vs final DoM (R2).
- Geometry emerges **without** low-rank constraint — embedding dimension can exceed the structure's
  rank; the dynamics self-stabilize by contracting `Φ` onto the Fiedler eigenspace and pushing the
  rest to the coefficient matrix's null space.
- "Associative first, geometry later" training dynamics — geometric memory appears only after many
  more gradient steps than associative memory (relevant to H-8 checkpoint-stability framing).
- No code link surfaced in the HTML body; appendix §B.2.2 specifies the Transformer architecture for
  the Fig. 1 visualization.

the top Laplacian/Fiedler eigenvector encoding global structure — i.e. the correctness-predictive
prefill direction should *be* the top eigenvector of the L19 covariance, and the informative readout
