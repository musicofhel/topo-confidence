# Triage brief — 2402.18048 — Characterizing Truthfulness in Large Language Model Generations with Local Intrinsic Dimension

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18048
Triaged: 2026-04-29

Authors: Fan Yin, Jayanth Srinivasa, Kai-Wei Chang (UCLA + Cisco Research, 2024).
Code: https://github.com/fanyin3639/LID-HallucinationDetection (the
graph's `repo_url` points at a renamed mirror `Truthfulness-LID`).

## Refutations

1. **Refutes (or sharply narrows) F-10 — "PH on trained residual streams is at the
   Gaussian null."**  Yin et al. report AUROC 0.737 (LID-MLE) and 0.746
   (LID-GeoMLE) on Llama-2-7B QA truthfulness using a *local* kNN-MLE
   intrinsic-dimension estimator on the same kind of residual-stream
   activations where our PH/TwoNN pipelines collapsed (TwoNN scored 0.407 in
   EXP-021; layerwise PH was at the Gaussian null in F-10 / EXP-026).  The
   refutation mechanism: PH and TwoNN are *global / scale-invariant*
   summaries that average over all neighbour distances; LID-GeoMLE conditions
   on a fixed neighbour count (k=500) and its anisotropy-aware correction
   (Gomtsyan et al. 2019) preserves directional structure that PH discards.
   If true, F-10's "no geometric signal in residuals" claim is overbroad —
   what is null is *coordinate-free global topology*, not *local
   neighbour-pool dimension*.  Falsifiable: run LID-GeoMLE (k=500, k=200)
   on the cached `pathway11_h100/` Qwen-2.5-1.5B per-token L19 activations
   over the 500 MATH-500 problems, predict K=1 correctness; if AUROC ≥ 0.65
   the F-10 framing has to be split into PH-null and LID-positive halves.

2. **Refutes F-3 — "Prefill and final-token DoM directions are orthogonal
   (cos = 0.046), and final-token carries less correctness signal."**
   Yin et al. measure their LID at the *last token of the generated
   sequence* (`X_{-1}^i` in their notation) and reach 0.746 AUROC on
   Llama-2-7B — *higher* than our final-token L19 DoM AUROC of 0.7186.
   If a coordinate-free invariant of the same final-token activations
   beats a linear direction on those activations by ~3 AUROC points, then
   either (a) our final-token signal is being projected onto the wrong
   axis (DoM under-utilises the final token), or (b) Yin's gain is
   model/task specific.  Mechanism for (a): DoM picks one rank-1
   direction in a ~3500-dim residual stream; LID integrates over a 500-pt
   neighbourhood and is sensitive to whatever rank that neighbourhood
   spans.  Falsifiable: extract LID-GeoMLE at our L19 *final-token*
   activations on MATH-500 K=1 generations; if AUROC ≥ 0.74 then F-3's
   "final-token is information-poor" inference is wrong — final-token
   is information-rich along an axis we are not reading.

3. **Refutes (the layer-selection logic of) F-2 / F-9 — "L19 is the right
   layer; CoE-60 cross-layer features are redundant with single-layer
   L19 DoM."**  Yin et al. report a "hunchback" of LID across layers
   AND — crucially — that the *peak detection layer is 1–2 layers behind
   the peak LID layer* (their Figure 3, Llama-2-7B).  If detection has
   a layer-lag against the underlying geometric signal, then a
   single-layer L19 probe is generically suboptimal — there is a
   `(LID_{l-1}, LID_l)` derivative carrying signal that DoM at L19
   alone cannot see.  Falsifiable: compute LID-GeoMLE at L17, L18, L19,
   L20, L21 on cached H100 activations; if `LID_{L18} - LID_{L19}` adds
   ≥ +0.01 AUROC over L19 DoM via a 2-feature logistic, F-9 needs to
   weaken from "redundant" to "redundant-only-at-peak-layer".

4. **Refutes H-12 — "Semantic-entropy probes (label-free) replace
   supervised DoM for refusal."**  Yin et al. report LID-GeoMLE 0.746 vs
   Semantic Entropy 0.694 (7B avg) and 0.764 vs 0.736 (13B avg) — a
   consistent +5 to +5.5 AUROC margin for LID over SE, on the *exact
   benchmarks* that motivate H-12 (free-form QA).  Mechanism: SE
   requires K samples to estimate, exposing it to the K-budget vs
   probe-quality tradeoff; LID is a one-pass invariant of a single
   forward pass.  H-12's premise (SEPs as label-free comparator) is
   empirically dominated by LID on the same evaluation regime.
   Falsifiable: run SE and LID-GeoMLE head-to-head on Qwen-2.5-1.5B
   MATH-500; if LID > SE, replace H-12 with "LID is the label-free
   probe of record".

5. **Refutes H-9 — "Breathing amplitude predicts problem difficulty."**
   Yin's controlled mixing experiment (their Table 8: prepend
   ground-truth prefix, then let the model continue, measure LID of the
   mixed sequence) supports a *signal-mixing* mechanism for raised LID:
   when human-distribution and model-distribution segments meet in the
   same activation pool, kNN distances inflate and LID rises.  Our
   "breathing-amplitude predicts difficulty" framing attributes
   amplitude to compute-cost-of-reasoning.  If Yin is right, then on
   D-bucket problems (F-7) we should see elevated LID at *prefill*
   (before any token has been generated) — a difficulty signature
   from prompt geometry alone, not from generation dynamics.
   Falsifiable: split MATH-500 into difficulty buckets, plot LID at the
   prefill-final token; if D-bucket LID > A-bucket LID *at prefill*,
   H-9's compute-cost story is at minimum incomplete.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2** (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731): Yin reports LID-GeoMLE at
  AUROC 0.746–0.764 on Llama-2 free-form QA — a parallel result with a
  different invariant (local ID vs linear direction) on a different
  generation regime (free-form QA vs MATH-500 CoT). Same layer-anchor
  philosophy (best of mid-late layers).
- **F-3** (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046): Yin probes only the
  final token, which is exactly the position where our DoM AUROC drops
  to 0.7186; their last-token AUROC of 0.746 implies signal we are
  losing.
- **F-4** (correct trajectories collapse harder than incorrect at the
  final token): Yin's mechanism — truthful continuations live closer
  to the natural-language manifold (smaller LID), incorrect
  continuations mix distributions (larger LID) — predicts the *same*
  asymmetry but in dimensional terms.  This is the closest thing to a
  competing mechanistic story for F-4 that we have seen.
- **F-7** (D-bucket has a distinctive collective geometric signature):
  Yin's mixing experiment is a near-direct test of whether D-bucket
  fragility shows up as LID elevation; their result that mixed
  prefix+continuation has higher LID is a hypothesis-generating signal.
- **F-9** (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM): Yin's
  layer-detection-lag finding (peak detection ≠ peak LID, lag of 1–2
  layers) is direct evidence that single-layer signals leave
  cross-layer derivative information on the table.
- **F-10** (PH = Gaussian null): The graph already records this paper
  with `EXTENDED_BY` and `CONTRADICTED_BY` edges to F-10 (note:
  GeoMLE not TwoNN).  This brief upgrades both edges from the
  admission filter's read to explicit refutation logic with a concrete
  replication plan.
- **H-12** (Semantic-entropy probes label-free): Yin shows LID > SE on
  Llama-2 free-form QA; H-12's premise is dominated.

## Methodologies extracted

- **LID-MLE** — Levina & Bickel (2004) maximum-likelihood estimator of
  intrinsic dimension from kNN distances:
  `m̂(X^i) = ((1/(T-1)) · Σ_{j=1..T-1} log(Q_T(X^i)/Q_j(X^i)))^{-1}`
  where `Q_j` is the distance to the j-th nearest neighbour of `X^i`
  in a fixed pool of T candidate activations (T=500).
  *Replication cost*: 20 min CPU on cached `pathway11_h100/`
  per-token L19 NPZs (kNN over 500 problems × 7 stage markers).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — it directly tests F-10 and is the
  cheapest thing in the queue.
- **LID-GeoMLE** — Gomtsyan, Mokrov, Panov, Yanovich (2019) variant
  with a quadratic distance correction that handles boundary /
  anisotropic neighbourhoods.  Yin reports +0.01 AUROC over plain MLE.
  *Replication cost*: 30 min CPU; same data, slightly more
  arithmetic.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — drop-in replacement once LID-MLE
  is wired up.
- **Layer-selection by cumulative LID argmax** — pick layer
  `l* = argmax_l Σ_i m̂(X_l^i)` over a held-out batch.
  *Replication cost*: 5 min CPU once LID-MLE exists.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives a non-DoM justification for
  L19 (or moves the anchor).
- **Cross-dataset neighbour pool** — when probing dataset A, draw the
  500-NN pool from dataset B; Yin reports a small drop (~2 AUROC)
  showing the LID signal is mostly local-anisotropy, not
  dataset-specific neighbour geometry.
  *Replication cost*: 1 h CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct probe of F-1 universality
  (does LID transfer across MATH-500/HumanEval/etc.?).
- **Mixing experiment** — prepend a true-prefix prefix, let the model
  continue, then measure LID of the merged sequence.  Mechanistic
  test for whether high LID reflects distributional mixing.
  *Replication cost*: 2 h H100 (need new generations).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct mechanistic counterpart to
  F-7 (D-bucket = distributional mixing?).
- **kNN robustness sweep** — Yin tests k = 50, 100, 200, 500, 1000,
  1500; performance plateaus above ~200.
  *Replication cost*: trivial once LID-MLE exists.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes, as part of the basic ablation.

## Approaches & framings

- **Truthfulness-as-low-dimensional-manifold.**  Truthful continuations
  live on a low-dimensional human-language submanifold; untruthful
  continuations leave it.  Concretely intersects F-4 (asymmetric
  collapse) with a non-PR, non-DoM language: collapse manifests as
  reduced *neighbour-pool* dimension, not reduced participation
  ratio.
- **Signal-mixing hypothesis for elevated LID.**  When prompt-style
  and continuation-style activations co-exist in a neighbour pool,
  pairwise distances inflate and LID rises.  Direct mechanistic
  alternative for the F-7 D-bucket signature (currently described
  collectively but without a generative mechanism).
- **Local-vs-global ID separation.**  The paper's quiet but strong
  point: "PH/TwoNN at null" is a finding *about a global invariant*;
  conditioning on a fixed-k neighbour pool changes the answer.  This
  reframes F-10 from "geometry is null" to "scale-invariant geometry
  is null; scale-conditional geometry is informative."
- **Detection-vs-mechanism layer separation.**  The peak-LID layer
  (where the geometric signature is strongest) and the
  peak-detection layer (where you should probe to predict
  truthfulness) are not the same.  This is a framing that we have
  not internalised — our L19 anchor was chosen by detection
  performance, not by where the signal originates.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Repo: https://github.com/fanyin3639/LID-HallucinationDetection
  (graph stores it under `Truthfulness-LID`; check both).
- Models tested: Llama-2-7B and Llama-2-13B; some ablations on
  earlier Llama / OPT mentioned but not in main tables.
- Default k = 500; stable above k = 200.
- Activations are sampled at the *last token of the generated
  sequence* (post-EOS or end-of-generation), single forward pass per
  problem.
- Layer selection: per-model `argmax_l` over summed LID; for
  Llama-2-7B the "hunchback" peaks around layers 14–18 of 32, with
  detection peaking 1–2 layers later.
- Distance metric: L2 in residual-stream space (no whitening).
- Neighbour pool: drawn from the *same* dataset's training split
  (their default); cross-pool ablation in their Table 3.
- Calibration: AUROC against a ground-truth-correctness label
  (rouge-L vs gold answer thresholded at 0.3 in their setup).
- Compute: numbers reported on a single A100; the kNN step is the
  dominant cost.
- Sensitivity: GeoMLE adds ~+0.01 AUROC over MLE on average; the
  paper recommends GeoMLE as default.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2402.18048 — Characterizing Truthfulness in Large Language Model Generations with Local Intrinsic Dimension (Yin, Srinivasa, Chang, 2024)

**Relevance:** A direct competitor / collaborator to our DoM-based
correctness probe.  Yin et al. show that local kNN-MLE intrinsic
dimension at the last token of an LLM's generation predicts
truthfulness on free-form QA at AUROC 0.737–0.764, *beating*
Semantic Entropy and SAPLMA, on Llama-2-7B and -13B.  Two facts make
this a high-leverage paper for us: (i) it uses a *local* ID estimator
(MLE / GeoMLE with k=500) rather than the global TwoNN we tried in
EXP-021 (which scored 0.407) or the layerwise PH that gave F-10 its
null result, and (ii) its empirical regime (last-token activations on
chain-style generation) is the regime where our final-token DoM
underperforms our prefill DoM (F-3).  Together these suggest F-10 is a
result about *coordinate-free global topology* rather than about
"residual geometry is uninformative", and that final-token activations
carry a signal our linear probes are missing.

**Key claim we tested:** Local kNN intrinsic dimension at the last
token of LLM generation predicts correctness at AUROC ≈ 0.74–0.76 on
free-form QA — a regime where our equivalent (final-token L19 DoM)
scores 0.7186, and our TwoNN benchmark scored 0.407.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — H-94 / P11-FE71 will run LID-GeoMLE
