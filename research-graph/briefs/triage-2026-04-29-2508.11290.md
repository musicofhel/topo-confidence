# Triage brief — 2508.11290 — SafeConstellations: Mitigating Over-Refusals in LLMs Through Task-Aware Representation Steering

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11290
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-2 may be a task-identity confound, not a pure correctness signal.**
   SafeConstellations (Table 5, Fig 5) shows that mid-to-late residual-stream
   layers (L14–L18, peak L16) carry strong **task-conditioned** geometric
   structure — per-task silhouette 0.119–0.158 vs combined-pool silhouette
   0.077–0.102, a ~2× gap. The "single-layer DoM" framing of F-2 (AUROC 0.7731
   on Qwen-2.5-1.5B at L19) is computed pooled across all 500 MATH-500 problems,
   which mixes algebra / geometry / number-theory / counting subtypes. If the
   prefill L19 DoM is partly *which-MATH-subtype* rather than pure
   correctness, AUROC should drop substantially when computed within
   problem-type. **Falsifiable**: stratify F-2's 5-fold OOF AUROC by MATH-500
   subject tag. If within-subject AUROC drops below ~0.70 while pooled
   AUROC remains 0.77, F-2's signal is partly subject-confound, not
   correctness-pure.

2. **H-1 risks utility-degradation rather than reasoning-improvement.**
   SafeConstellations Table 2 is a direct precedent for the "what could go
   wrong with DoM steering" failure mode: their *fixed-layer intense*
   ablation hits 60.42% refusal reduction but drops MMLU 6.91 points
   (46.57 → 39.20) and Table 3 shows the steered outputs are literally
   "assistantassistantassistant…" repetition or garbled multilingual tokens.
   If H-1 applies a fixed L19 DoM coefficient α and reports a MATH-500
   accuracy bump, the bump may be a parser-side artifact (broken decode
   producing different — sometimes correct-by-chance — answer strings)
   rather than genuine answer-direction improvement. **Falsifiable**: H-1
   must report (a) MMLU side-channel under the same α, (b) GPT-4o
   coherence ratings on the steered traces. If MMLU drops or coherence
   drops while MATH-500 accuracy "improves," H-1 is degeneration-driven.

3. **F-3 orthogonality (cos 0.046) may be a pooling artifact.**
   The paper's central empirical claim is that pooling across tasks
   collapses within-task structure (per-task silhouette ≈ 2× combined).
   F-3's cos 0.046 is computed across all 500 MATH-500 problems pooled.
   If MATH-500 has multiple latent "task subtypes" (multi-step proof vs
   one-step arithmetic vs symbolic manipulation), within-subtype
   prefill_DoM and final_DoM may be substantially less orthogonal — i.e.,
   the orthogonality is a heterogeneity-mixing artifact, not a structural
   property of the residual stream. **Falsifiable**: stratify
   cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) by MATH-500 subject tag. If any subset
   shows |cos| > 0.20, the "orthogonal directions" framing is
   partial-pooling artifact rather than structural.

4. **H-12 single-direction probes will fail on the same multi-task confound.**
   SafeConstellations is direct evidence against task-agnostic refusal
   probing: their *single-direction* fixed-layer baseline (16.66% OR
   rate) barely beats the no-intervention baseline (17.77%), while their
   task-conditioned dynamic-layer config crushes it (4.81% on LLaMA).
   Semantic-entropy / EigenScore / DoM probes that don't condition on
   task-identity should inherit the same task-mixing degradation. H-12
   would replace supervised DoM with an unsupervised probe — if that
   probe is evaluated only on math, it may look strong; on mixed
   math+sentiment+translation it will collapse. **Falsifiable**: evaluate
   H-12's probe on a multi-task held-out set (math + general QA +
   sentiment). SafeConstellations predicts a task-conditioned baseline
   will beat the unconditioned probe by ≥3× refusal/abstention F1.

5. **F-7's "collective geometric signature" may dissolve under task-stratification.**
   F-7 reports D-bucket (incorrect, low-PR) has a distinctive *collective*
   geometric signature on pooled MATH-500 data. SafeConstellations'
   per-task-vs-combined silhouette gap is direct evidence that any
   collective geometric structure found on heterogeneous data may
   dilute under task-stratification. If D-bucket is partly the union of
   per-subtype geometries (e.g., geometry problems happen to live in a
   different residual-stream region than algebra), the "distinctive
   signature" may shrink toward null when computed within MATH-500
   subject tag. **Falsifiable**: rerun F-7's density-based D-bucket
   detector within each MATH-500 subject. If D-bucket precision/recall
   drops > 30% within-subject, F-7 is a task-mixture artifact.

  L14–L18 (peak L16) as the strongest task-conditioned separator. L19 is
  one layer above their peak; the geometry is plausibly the same family.
  Their effectiveness ratio Eff(ℓ) = ‖v‖/(σ_tar+σ_ref+ε) is a more

- **F-3 (cos prefill_DoM, final_DoM = 0.046)** — Reframed as a

  claim is testable via per-subtype cos.
- **F-8 (Selective prediction acc 71.6% at coverage 0.5)** — Their
  Pot(ℓ) per-sample dynamic layer score (Eq 4) is a plug-in upgrade
  for picking the routing layer per problem.
- **H-1 (Per-position DoM steering)** — Direct precedent. Paper
  provides confidence-gated, dynamic-layer-conditioned recipe that we
  do not currently use.
- **H-12 (Semantic-entropy / label-free probes for refusal)** — Direct
  contrast. Paper's task-agnostic baseline collapses. Suggests H-12
  must be benchmarked on multi-task data.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Per-layer effectiveness score** Eff_t(ℓ) = ‖v_t(ℓ)‖ / (σ_tar + σ_ref + ε)
  for picking top-K steering layers per task.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached pathway11 NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct upgrade for picking the
  optimal prefill-DoM layer instead of fixing L19.

- **Per-sample steering potential** Pot(ℓ) = ‖h(ℓ) − c_tar‖ / (‖h(ℓ) − c_ref‖ + ε).
  Top-K' layers with highest Pot(ℓ) are selected per-prompt.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached prefill+final NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — per-sample dynamic layer selection
  could change F-8 selective-prediction accuracy.

- **Adaptive steering coefficient** λ(ℓ) = λ_0 · (1 − LAlign(ℓ)) · Confidence · κ(ℓ),
  where LAlign uses cosine separation and κ(ℓ) is a depth-dependent
  multiplier (early/mid/late = 0.8/1.0/1.2).
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU (build, eval on holdout).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — adaptive λ is currently missing
  from H-1 / P10-FE1.

- **Task-conditional centroid construction** From a small labeled set
  (4–8 samples per class suffice), build per-task target/refusal
  centroids at every layer.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU after MATH-500 subject-tagging.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — needed to test refutations 1, 3, 5.

- **Cluster-separation metric battery** Silhouette score, Davies-Bouldin
  Index, centroid distance per layer.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — replicable on pathway11_h100 NPZs
  to compute per-task-vs-combined silhouette gap.

- **GPT-4o LLM-as-judge with three-way refusal × three-way safety
  taxonomy** {direct answer, direct refusal, indirect refusal} ×
  {benign, cautious, harmful}.
  *Replication cost*: ~$50 API budget for 500 MATH-500 traces.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — project scope is mathematical
  correctness, not refusal/safety classification.

- **Top-K layer compression for activation memory bank** retain only
  K=5 most-effective layers per task to reduce 32-layer footprint
  by 6.4×.
  *Replication cost*: trivial.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — sparsifies our prefill-direction
  store if/when we extend to multi-task.

## Approaches & framings

- **Constellation framing.** Replaces Arditi's "single residual-stream
  direction" with a per-task multi-layer trajectory. This generalizes
  F-2 / F-3: the prefill-DoM and final-DoM may be two waypoints on a
  task-specific trajectory rather than two static directions. Could
  reframe F-3's orthogonality as task-pooling artifact.

- **Conditional intervention via task-identity gating.** Only intervene
  when (i) task confidence ≥ 0.85, (ii) task is in benign-intent set.
  Reframes H-1: per-position DoM steering may need a confidence gate
  to avoid degenerate decodes on out-of-distribution problems.

- **Effectiveness ratio as steering-vector quality.** Eff = ‖v‖/spread is
  a reformulation of the linear-probe SNR concept that is more directly
  tied to steering performance than to AUROC. Suggests the "best DoM
  layer" by AUROC may not be the "best DoM layer" by steering-effect.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Models: LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen-1.5-7B-Chat. (Note: Qwen-1.5-7B
  is one generation behind our Qwen-2.5-7B but architecturally
  similar — 32 layers, residual-stream MLP+attn, hidden 4096.)
- Confidence threshold τ = 0.85 (selected from {0.5, 0.75, 0.8, 0.9}).
- K = 5 memory-bank layers, K' = 4 dynamically-steered layers.
- λ_0 = 0.3 base steering intensity.
- Depth multipliers: κ_early = 0.8 (L0–10), κ_mid = 1.0 (L11–20),
  κ_late = 1.2 (L21–31).
- Top selected layers and effectiveness scores (LLaMA-3.1-8B):
  - Translate: L20 (4.756), L14 (4.711), L15 (4.700), L18 (4.635), L-1 (16.822)
  - Sentiment: L14 (5.225), L15 (5.221), L16 (5.059), L11 (4.911), L-1 (19.323)
  - Cryptanalysis (Qwen-1.5-7B): L18 (1.577), L16 (1.528), L14 (1.514), L15 (1.506), L-1 (5.010)
  - **Note**: the L-1 (final-norm) score is dramatically higher than mid-layer scores —
    suggests the residual stream's *last* normalized layer is where target/refusal
    separation is widest, even though steering there may be too late to affect decode.
- Memory: 847MB FP32 for 5 tasks × 5 layers × 4096-dim on LLaMA-3.1-8B. 424MB at FP16.
- 4–8 samples per class sufficient for new-task centroid construction.
- A100 40GB or L4 22.5GB tested. Batch steering not supported.
- Latency: +0.2s for short outputs, decode-bound for long.
- Code: `https://github.com/Sakonii/SafeConstellations/`
- Dataset: `https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sakonii/task-over-refusal-dataset`

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2508.11290 — SafeConstellations: Mitigating Over-Refusals in LLMs Through Task-Aware Representation Steering (Maskey, Yadav, Dras, Naseem; ACL 2026)

**Relevance:** Task-aware activation steering with per-task layer-wise centroids (target vs refusal). Directly relevant to our prefill-DoM line (F-2, F-3) because their core methodological move — per-task silhouette ~2× combined-pool silhouette — is a refutation template for any pooled-across-MATH-500 finding. Their effectiveness ratio Eff(ℓ) and per-prompt steering potential Pot(ℓ) are direct upgrades for our layer-selection (currently fixed at L19). Their adaptive coefficient λ(ℓ) and confidence-gating recipe is direct precedent for how H-1 / P10-FE1 should be instrumented to avoid utility-degradation. The fixed-vs-dynamic-vs-task-conditional ablation (Table 2) is a published instance of the failure mode our P10 v1 already saw with fixed steering vectors. Models tested: LLaMA-3.1-8B + Qwen-1.5-7B-Chat (one Qwen generation behind our Qwen-2.5).

**Key claim we tested:** Refusal directions in LLM residual streams are task-specific, not global; task-conditioned dynamic-layer steering dominates fixed-direction steering on the safety/utility frontier (4.81% OR rate vs 16.66% with no MMLU loss vs 6.91-pt MMLU drop).

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — claims have not been replicated on our MATH-500 / Qwen-2.5 data. Specifically: (a) per-MATH-subject silhouette gap (refutation #1 + H-90), (b) effectiveness-ratio peak layer (H-91), (c) within-subject cos (refutation #3 + H-92), (d) Pot(ℓ) per-prompt selective-prediction (P10-FE71), (e) adaptive-coefficient steering (P10-FE72). All five tests are direct ports of the paper's methodology to our cached NPZs.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE71, P11-FE72, P11-FE73, P10-FE71, P10-FE72, H-90, H-91, H-92.

**Status:** TO TEST
