# Triage brief — 2410.13640 — Latent Space Chain-of-Embedding Enables Output-free LLM Self-Evaluation

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13640
Triaged: 2026-04-29

This is the *original* CoE paper (Wang et al., ICLR 2025). It is already
seeded into the graph at `status='pending_triage'` and into PAPER_INDEX
(line 151: "PARTIALLY CONFIRMED within-domain, CONTRADICTED on the 'CoE >
single-layer' cross-domain claim"). This deep pass is therefore not a
fresh-paper sweep — it's a re-triage that audits whether our existing
EXP-020 / EXP-030 / EXP-036 actually tested the paper's *headline* score
or only a downstream 60-dim feature surrogate. Refutations focus on that
distinction.

## Refutations

1. **F-9 ("CoE-60 trajectory features are redundant with single-layer L19
   DoM") may be tested against the wrong CoE variant.** The paper's
   headline score is **CoE-C**, a closed-form *complex-space* aggregate
   `sqrt((Σ Re(C))² + (Σ Im(C))²) / L` where
   `C(h_l, h_{l+1}) = ||h_{l+1}−h_l||·exp(i·angle(h_l, h_{l+1}))`. Our
   EXP-030 and EXP-036 used a *60-dim feature vector* fed to XGBoost /
   LR. XGBoost with row-wise feature scaling cannot reproduce the
   coherent complex-sum-then-magnitude cancellation that CoE-C performs.
   Mechanism: CoE-C accumulates phase-aligned magnitudes; an XGB tree on
   per-layer-pair magnitudes/angles treats them as independent features
   and discards the relative-phase structure. **Falsifiable experiment:**
   compute closed-form CoE-C from the cached Qwen-2.5-1.5B P11 1024-tok
   activations on MATH-500; report scalar AUROC. If CoE-C ≥ 0.80 vs L19
   DoM 0.7731, F-9 weakens from "redundant" to "redundant only when
   reduced to 60 features but not as a closed-form score" — and H-6
   becomes the active contest.

2. **F-2 ("Prefill L19 DoM is a strong correctness predictor") is
   challenged by a *label-free* baseline that may match or exceed it on
   the same model family.** The paper reports CoE-C label-free AUROC =
   75.22% averaged across 7 models on Mathematics (GSM8K + MATH).
   Qwen1.5-7B and Qwen2-7B sit inside that average, so Qwen-family
   numbers are accessible. F-2 (0.7731) is supervised, OOF, 5-fold,
   single layer. Mechanism: if a label-free closed-form score
   approximates a supervised single-layer probe, the supervision is
   adding ≤ noise — the geometry alone carries the signal. **Falsifiable
   experiment:** compute the paper's CoE-C on our exact P11 setup (Qwen-
   2.5-1.5B, MATH-500, 1024-tok) without any training. If CoE-C AUROC ≥
   0.77, F-2's "L19 prefill direction is the headline mechanism" framing
   is structurally weakened; the headline becomes "trajectory geometry".
   If CoE-C ≤ 0.70, F-2 holds and the paper's label-free claim is shown
   to be model/domain-specific.

3. **F-8 (selective prediction at 71.6% acc@coverage 0.5) is at risk of
   being re-attributable to CoE-C.** F-8 uses supervised prefill DoM as
   the gating signal. The paper's CoE-C is millisecond-level, label-
   free, computed on the same hidden states the gating already touches.
   If we re-run the F-8 selective-prediction protocol with CoE-C as
   gating instead of L19 DoM and reach acc@coverage 0.5 ≥ 71.6%, F-8's
   mechanism shifts: it's not "prefill direction picks the right
   problems" — it's "trajectory shape picks them, and prefill direction
   is one rough proxy." Mechanism: gating at coverage 0.5 only requires
   *ranking* answers by confidence; both DoM and CoE-C are 1-D scores,
   so they will succeed or fail on identical splits modulo monotone
   transforms. **Falsifiable experiment:** reuse `pathway11_h100/
   prefill_gated_compute/results.json`'s 500 problem K=2.5 schedule but
   replace the L19 DoM gating with CoE-C; report acc@coverage 0.5.

4. **H-6 ("CoE-60 still beats single-layer DoM at 1024-tok labels") has
   not actually been tested with the right CoE variant.** Our prior
   evidence (EXP-030 cross-domain transfer = 0.716 vs DoM 0.720) used
   60-dim XGB. The paper offers four scores: CoE-Mag, CoE-Ang, CoE-R,
   CoE-C. Within-domain at 1024 tokens, the within-domain test uses
   their *closed-form* CoE-C — which has not been computed on our
   cached features. **Falsifiable experiment:** compute all four closed-
   form scores from cached P11 1024-tok activations and report each
   AUROC vs L19 DoM (0.7731). If any closed-form score ≥ 0.80 within-
   domain, H-6 flips from "CONFIRMED on 256-tok, untested on 1024-tok"
   to "CONFIRMED with caveat: only specific closed-form variants beat
   DoM, not the 60-dim trained classifier."

## Methodologies extracted

- **CoE-Mag (per-layer L2 step magnitude, normalized by total).**
  `Mag(H) = (1/L)·Σ ||h_{l+1}−h_l|| / ||h_L−h_0||`. Closed-form scalar.
  *Replication cost*: 20 min CPU on cached P11 activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct refutation experiment for F-9.

- **CoE-Ang (per-layer angle step, normalized).**
  `Ang(H) = (1/L)·Σ arccos(h_l·h_{l+1}/||h_l||||h_{l+1}||) / arccos(h_0·h_L/...)`
  Closed-form scalar.
  *Replication cost*: 20 min CPU on cached P11 activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct refutation experiment for F-9.

- **CoE-R (real-space combination).** `(1/L)·Σ [Mag/Z_M − Ang/Z_A]`.
  *Replication cost*: 20 min CPU on cached P11.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — pairs naturally with CoE-C.

- **CoE-C (complex-space magnitude of mean phasor).**
  `sqrt((Σ Re(C))² + (Σ Im(C))²)/L` where `C = M·exp(i·A)`. The paper's
  headline score; combines magnitude and angle as a phase-coherent sum.
  *Replication cost*: 20 min CPU on cached P11.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — this is the variant we have not yet
  tested.

- **All-layer hidden state extraction at L+1 positions (input, every
  intermediate, output).** Paper uses every layer including embedding
  and unembedding. Our P11 cache only stores L0, L7, L14, L19, L21, L24,
  L27 (selected layers). We'd need to re-extract all 28 layers if we
  want bit-exact replication. *Replication cost*: ~2 h H100 if we re-
  extract; otherwise we approximate with 7-layer sub-sample.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — the 7-layer subsample probably
  preserves the closed-form's qualitative behavior, and saves the H100
  time.

- **Domain-bucketing for AUROC variance reporting (4 domains × 7
  models).** Methodology for cross-domain robustness reporting.
  *Replication cost*: free (we already do something similar).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — our finding-set is already
  organized cross-domain.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Latent thinking path" framing** — treats the layer-axis trajectory
  of a single hidden state as a path through embedding space. This is a
  layer-axis dual of our token-axis "breathing" frame (F-1). Cross-
  intersection: their notion of step magnitude per layer is what
  EXP-031/032 measures across tokens. The two trajectories live in
  orthogonal axes (layer vs. position) but use identical primitives.
- **Output-free / label-free as an architectural commitment** — the
  paper rejects probing classifiers explicitly because they require
  labels. This frames F-2 / F-8 as supervision-locked, and motivates
  H-12-style label-free alternatives.
- **Phase-coherent aggregation via complex space** — instead of
  concatenating magnitude and angle as features, treats each layer-step
  as a phasor and sums. This is a non-trivial geometric move we have
  not used: it is a very simple form of the "trajectory winding"
  invariants persistent-homology people gesture at, but realized as a
  closed form.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Paper uses **all L+1 hidden states** including embedding (h_0) and
  unembedding (h_L). We'd want to confirm whether to include or skip
  the embedding layer when replicating; their formulation requires h_0
  for the normalizer Z_Mag = ||h_L − h_0||.
- Normalization choice: dividing each per-layer step by the total
  start-to-end step. This creates a closed-form scalar invariant to
  uniform layer-norm scaling; useful sanity check for F-9 closed-form
  variant.
- The CoE-C formula `sqrt((1/L)·Σ Re(C))² + ((1/L)·Σ Im(C))²)` admits
  cancellation: if angles flip layer-to-layer, Re(C) sums to ~0 and
  CoE-C reflects only the asymmetric component. Worth knowing because
  in our cached activations the per-layer angle changes flip sign
  around L14–L19; CoE-C may be measuring exactly this transition.
- Code: paper repo at `https://github.com/Alsace08/Chain-of-Embedding`
  (per the graph's `repo_url` field). We have not pulled it; for a
  bit-exact replication this is the canonical reference.
