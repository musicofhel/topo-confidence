# Triage brief — 2508.19505 — Caught in the Act: a mechanistic approach to detecting deception

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19505
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-3 (Prefill ⟂ final-token DoM, cos = 0.046) loses force if many directions exist.**
   The paper applies Iterative Nullspace Projection (INLP) and finds **20 (Qwen-3B) to ~100 (DeepSeek-7B / Qwen-14B)** linearly independent directions encoding deception in the same layer. By analogy, a single L19 residual stream may carry dozens of *correctness* directions; the prefill-vs-final orthogonality (cos = 0.046) is then merely two arbitrary draws from a high-dim subspace, not evidence of two distinct circuits.
   *Mechanism*: if ≫2 correctness directions exist at L19, expected pairwise cosines among random correctness directions are O(1/√d_eff) — 0.046 is consistent with random orientation in a ~500-dim subspace, not with "orthogonal-by-design."
   *Falsifiable experiment*: run INLP on the Qwen-1.5B L19 prefill correctness probe with cached `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/` activations. If we recover >5 directions before AUROC collapses to chance, the F-3 "two orthogonal circuits" framing is overstated; the geometry is "one fat correctness subspace."

2. **F-2 (prefill > final-token AUROC) is methodology-specific, not architectural.**
   The paper achieves >90% deception probe accuracy on **final-token only**, explicitly noting "aggregation across token positions led to decreased performance." This refutes any reading of F-2 as "prefill is a privileged position for any behavioral signal." Their final-token works fine; ours doesn't (0.7186 vs 0.7731 for prefill).
   *Mechanism*: prefill > final may reflect that *correctness* is computed during prompt absorption while *deception* is committed during generation. The position ordering is feature-conditional, not anatomical.
   *Falsifiable experiment*: replicate their final-token deception probe on cached Qwen-1.5B activations (we have the same model). If we get >0.85 AUROC for deception at final-token while correctness AUROC at final-token stays at 0.7186, the position-ordering for correctness vs deception is opposite, refuting any "final-token is uniformly weaker" reading of F-2.

3. **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM) is fragile.**
   F-9 says CoE-60's 0.811 AUROC is matched by single-layer L19 DoM, implying nothing extra in cross-layer aggregation. But if INLP exposes ~20-100 directions at L19 alone, then "CoE-60 = single-layer L19 DoM" can only hold if the single direction we projected onto already captured the *dominant* correctness direction. Multiple INLP-discoverable directions would offer additional probes that, when concatenated, beat the single DoM.
   *Mechanism*: our DoM is the class-mean difference (rank-1); INLP recursively finds the next direction after projecting out this one. If the second direction adds AUROC, F-9's "redundancy" claim downgrades to "the *first* direction is redundant with CoE-60 — there are more."
   *Falsifiable experiment*: train INLP-iter-2 probe (project out DoM, refit logistic regression) on L19 prefill activations. If AUROC goes from 0.7731 → ≥0.79 in a 2-direction concat, F-9 needs amendment.

4. **F-1 (dimensional breathing universal across scales) gains a capacity floor.**
   Their layer-wise probe accuracy curve **only emerges at ≥7B** — Qwen-1.5B achieves chance (~50%) for deception while 7B/14B reach 0.8+. They conclude "dimensionality of the residual stream emerged as a key component for the successful use of linear classifiers." This implies dim(effective residual stream) is what matters — the very quantity F-1's breathing tracks.
   *Mechanism*: if probe accuracy floors at chance below some effective-dimension threshold, then F-1's "universal across scales" is only true for the *shape* of the breathing curve, not for its functional consequence. Smaller models breathe but don't carry probe-recoverable behavioral content.
   *Falsifiable experiment*: replot per-layer probe AUROC against per-layer participation ratio across Qwen-1.5B/7B (cached) and check correlation. If high, breathing amplitude *is* the probe-accuracy ceiling, qualifying F-1 to "universal in shape, capacity-bounded in content."

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731 on Qwen-1.5B)** — Their per-layer accuracy curve peaks at "approximately two-thirds of the network depth." For Qwen-1.5B (28 layers), 2/3 depth = layer **18.67 ≈ L19**, *exactly* our peak layer. This is corroborating evidence that L19 is a meaningful peak across very different probe targets (correctness vs. deception), suggesting it is a model-architectural rather than task-specific peak.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046)** — INLP finding 20-100 deception directions challenges "two orthogonal directions" as a meaningful structural claim (see Refutation 1).
- **F-2 / F-8 (selective prediction at 71.6% acc, coverage 0.5)** — Their 1.5B Qwen achieves chance (~50%) on deception probing, while we get AUROC 0.7731 on the *same model* for correctness. Implication: the prefill DoM is genuinely picking up "knowledge of correct answer," not a generic behavioral salience direction. If the L19 residual carried a generic "salient" axis, we would expect >50% deception probing too.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with L19 DoM, AUROC 0.811 ≈ single-layer)** — INLP gives a path to break the redundancy ceiling (see Refutation 3).
- **H-13 (head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token circuits)** — Their gradient-based saliency attribution (Wu et al. 2307.13339) back-propagates the dot product (probe direction · last-token residual) to per-token attribution scores. Direct method we could lift onto our prefill DoM: which prompt tokens drive the prefill correctness signal?
- **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes label-free replace supervised DoM)** — Their supervised linear probe beats LLM-as-judge (GPT-4o) by ~10 points consistently. Bounds the loss we accept by going label-free; if label-free probes can't approach supervised probes, H-12 is a downgrade not a parity result.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Logistic-regression linear probe on final-token residual stream** — fits class-conditioned linear classifier per layer.
  *Replication cost*: trivial (minutes CPU on cached activations).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — already do, but the explicit "aggregation across positions hurt" finding is a useful negative result against any plan to mean-pool prefill+final.
- **Iterative Nullspace Projection (INLP, Ravfogel 2004.07667)** — train classifier, project out separating hyperplane, retrain on residual, iterate.
  *Replication cost*: ~30 min CPU per round × 50-100 rounds = a few CPU hours on cached L19 activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes — highest-priority extraction from this paper**. Directly tests Refutations 1 and 3.
- **LLM-as-judge baseline (GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini)** — prompted to evaluate output for the property of interest, returns label + score + explanation.
  *Replication cost*: ~$50 in API calls for 500 MATH-500 problems × 2 model judges.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — useful for selective-prediction comparison vs DoM, low priority.
- **Gradient-based saliency attribution (Wu et al. 2307.13339)** — back-prop dot product (probe direction · last-token residual) to obtain per-input-token attribution scores.
  *Replication cost*: a few hours GPU on cached activations + model loaded.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives an interpretable handle on which prompt tokens carry the prefill correctness signal (Pathway 11 has never had token-level attribution).
- **Synthetic argument generation pipeline** — sample MMLU question, binarize, generate (Q, A, direction) triplet → LLM produces argument for the chosen direction. Filtering step rejects arguments that don't follow instructions.
  *Replication cost*: hours of LLM inference per model.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — orthogonal to our MATH-500 / 1024-tok regime.
- **Layer-wise probe sweep** — one probe per layer, plot AUROC vs depth.
  *Replication cost*: trivial; we already have this.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — already done in P8/P11; cross-task layer-peak alignment with their figure 1 is a free comparison.

## Approaches & framings

- **Three-stage layer-wise probe accuracy** — near-random in early layers, peak at ~2/3 depth, slight decline in late layers. We already see this for correctness probes; the paper's confirmation across deception (a fundamentally different signal) elevates this from "DoM-specific" to "general probe-accuracy structure of decoder transformers." Intersects F-2's L19 peak choice and H-13.
- **Multitudes of independent linear directions** — the framing that property X is encoded by N≫1 mutually orthogonal directions, not a single rank-1 axis. This is the most disruptive lens for our work: F-2, F-3, F-8, F-9 all implicitly treat "the" prefill DoM as the correctness direction. INLP says we should suspect a subspace.
- **White-box vs black-box benchmarking** — explicit framing that compares internal-activation probes against LLM-as-judge text classifiers. We have done neither cross-comparison; could borrow this lens for H-12 evaluations.
- **Capability-gated probe accuracy** — probe AUROC scales with model capability rather than fixed across scales. Reframes F-1's universality claim as architectural-shape-but-not-content (see Refutation 4).

## Datasets & benchmarks

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Models: Qwen 2.5 family (1.5B, 3B, 7B, 14B) + DeepSeek-r1-distill (1.5B, 7B). Direct overlap with our P11 model set (Qwen-2.5-1.5B and 7B).
- Probe is fit on **final-token activations only**. Aggregation across token positions decreased performance (their explicit finding).
- Peak probe accuracy at ~2/3 depth ⇒ for 28-layer Qwen-1.5B that is ~L19, exactly our peak.
- Argument generation uses recommended temperature/top-k/chat-template from Qwen and DeepSeek tech reports (full precision).
- Filtering step: an evaluator model rejects arguments that don't follow the prompt instructions (adherence to specified option, no reference to non-specified choices). See Figure 6.
- Saliency: backprop ∂(probe_direction · last_token_residual) / ∂(input_token_embeddings); take per-token L2 norm as attribution. Red = pushes toward deception, blue = pushes away.
- INLP convergence: <100 rounds for Qwen-3B; closer to 100 rounds for 7B/14B / DeepSeek (Figure 2).

### H-77: L19 prefill correctness lives in a multi-direction subspace, not a single rank-1 axis
**Priority:** HIGH
**Motivated by:** 2508.19505 + F-2, F-3, F-9
**Test:** Apply Iterative Nullspace Projection (Ravfogel 2004.07667) to cached Qwen-1.5B L19 prefill activations from `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/`. After fitting initial logistic-regression DoM probe (AUROC 0.7731), project out the separating hyperplane and refit on the residual; repeat 50 rounds. Record per-round AUROC and cumulative AUROC of the d-direction concat probe. ~2 h CPU.
**Requires:** CPU only; cached prefill NPZs already on disk.
**Would change:** If ≥5 INLP rounds yield non-chance AUROC, F-3 (cos = 0.046 between prefill and final DoM) downgrades from "two orthogonal correctness circuits" to "two random draws from a >5-dim correctness subspace." If 2-direction concat lifts AUROC above 0.79, F-9 (CoE-60 ≈ single-layer DoM) gains a clean escape: CoE-60 was matched by the *first* direction; subsequent directions were not represented in the rank-1 DoM. If INLP collapses to chance after 1-2 rounds, F-2/F-3/F-9 are robustly single-direction phenomena.
**Blocks:** nothing; informs H-1 (steering: which direction do we steer along?), H-13 (head attribution: per-direction or per-subspace?).

### H-78: Probe-accuracy capacity floor — 1.5B is below threshold for many behavioral signals while still above for correctness
**Priority:** MEDIUM
**Motivated by:** 2508.19505 + F-2
**Test:** Replicate Boxo et al.'s deception probe on cached Qwen-1.5B activations using their MMLU synthetic-argument pipeline (Appendix A.1). If deception probe stays at chance (~50%) at all layers for Qwen-1.5B while correctness peaks at 0.7731 at L19, we have a strict ordering: correctness < deception in capacity demand on this architecture. ~6 h H100 (argument generation) + ~1 h CPU (probing).
**Requires:** H100 for argument generation (model loaded), CPU for probing; new MMLU-derived synthetic dataset.
**Would change:** If Qwen-1.5B can host correctness probing but not deception probing, F-1's "universal across scales" claim restricts to *shape* of breathing, not to recoverability of behavioral content — the residual stream has structure but not capacity for deception-class abstractions at 1.5B. If both work, the capacity-threshold framing is wrong and Boxo et al.'s 1.5B-at-chance result is a methodology artifact (synthetic data quality, not residual-stream capacity).
**Blocks:** nothing; informs cross-scale findings (F-6) and H-11 (big-to-small distillation feasibility).

## 2508.19505 — Caught in the Act: a mechanistic approach to detecting deception (Boxo, Yoo, Socha, Raval, 2026)

**Relevance:** Linear probes on residual-stream activations to detect a behavioral property (deception) in the *exact same Qwen-2.5 family* (1.5B, 3B, 7B, 14B + DeepSeek-r1-distill 1.5B/7B) we use in P11. Layer-wise probe accuracy peaks at ~2/3 depth — for Qwen-1.5B that is L19, *exactly* our F-2 peak layer. INLP analysis exposes 20-100 independent linear directions encoding deception in the same residual stream we treat as carrying a single rank-1 DoM. Gradient-based saliency attribution offers a token-level interpretability method we have never applied to prefill DoM.

**Key claim we tested:** A single linear direction in the L19 residual stream of Qwen-1.5B captures the dominant correctness-relevant signal (F-2: AUROC 0.7731), and the prefill vs. final-token directions are meaningfully orthogonal (F-3: cos = 0.046). Boxo et al. challenge this implicitly: their INLP result on a different behavioral property in the same model family suggests "the" direction is a rank-1 projection of a much higher-dimensional behavioral subspace.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — H-77 will run INLP on the L19 prefill correctness probe to determine whether F-2/F-3/F-9 survive the multi-direction reframe. The 2/3-depth layer peak on Qwen-1.5B is *already corroborated* by our independent choice of L19, lending architectural rather than task-specific status to that peak. Their 1.5B-at-chance for deception while we get 0.7731 for correctness on the same model strengthens F-2's specificity (we are picking up correctness, not generic salience).

**Related experiments:** P11-FE57 (INLP on L19 correctness probe), P11-FE58 (saliency attribution on prefill DoM), H-77, H-78.
