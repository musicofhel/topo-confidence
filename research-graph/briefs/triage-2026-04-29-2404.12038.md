Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12038
Triaged: 2026-04-29

Code: https://github.com/SproutNan/AI-Safety_SCAV

## Refutations

1. **Against F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM, AUROC 0.7731 ceiling).** SCAV trains a sigmoid linear probe on residual-stream embeddings at *every* layer of LLaMA-2-7B-Chat, Vicuna, Mistral, etc. and reports >95% test accuracy from layer 10–11 onward, climbing to >98% by the last layers — using only 140+140 samples. Our equivalent supervised probe at L19 saturates at 0.7731 OOF AUROC despite ~250 correct + ~250 incorrect MATH-500 problems. *Mechanism*: if `correctness` is encoded as linearly as `malicious` (same probe family, same architecture class), the gap should not be 20+ AUROC points. Either (a) we are anchored on the wrong layer (L19 by Pathway 1 selection ≠ optimal for OOF) or (b) correctness is not as cleanly linearly encoded as safety, in which case the "single-direction predictor" framing of F-2 is an artifact of *the layer we picked*, not of geometry. *Falsifiable test*: redo the SCAV probe sweep on cached Qwen-1.5B prefill activations across all 28 layers; if peak ≤ 0.78 anywhere, F-2's ceiling is intrinsic, not a layer-choice artifact.

2. **Against F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046 → orthogonal concepts).** Figure 4(b) of SCAV shows that perturbing only an early layer (where the probe is linearly separable) is *cancelled* by later layers — the network actively undoes the perturbation unless multiple layers are perturbed jointly. *Mechanism*: late-layer circuits actively oppose early-layer signals. If the same applies to correctness, the prefill ↔ final orthogonality we attribute to "different concepts" might instead be *active cancellation* — the final-token direction is the negation, geometrically orthogonal because it precisely undoes the prefill projection. *Falsifiable test*: patch the final-token L19 activation with the *negation* of the prefill DoM projection on a held-out subset; if accuracy drops measurably more than under random-direction ablation of equal magnitude, F-3's "different concepts" story is wrong and the directions are an opposing pair.

3. **Against H-1 (Per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy).** SCAV's closed-form attack is `ε = [sigmoid⁻¹(P₀) − b − wᵀe] / ‖w‖`, `v = w / ‖w‖` — i.e., the steering direction is the *probe weight* normalized, not the raw difference-of-means. The current `topo_confidence` v0.2 reference impl uses raw DoM. If SCAV is right that the probe weight (Fisher-like direction) is what causes the boundary crossing while DoM only happens to correlate, then E1 with raw DoM will plateau and probe-weight-direction will succeed. *Mechanism*: raw DoM is confounded by within-class variance; the probe normal is the discriminating axis. *Falsifiable test*: P10-FE1 already plans the (probe-weight) vs (mass-mean) vs (Sinii bias) sweep — predict probe-weight wins; if mass-mean ties or beats it, SCAV's framing is wrong for correctness or H-1 succeeds equally with either.

4. **Against F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM).** SCAV explicitly requires multi-layer perturbation: layers with TestAcc > 90% are perturbed sequentially because single-layer attacks succeed only on linearly-separable layers and get corrected by deeper layers. By analogy, the *aggregation across layers* in CoE-60 may not be redundant with L19; it may be capturing late-layer correction signal that L19 alone misses. *Mechanism*: F-9's "redundant" finding may have been measured on a regime where L19 already had near-peak signal; if we tested at a layer where signal was weaker or where late-layer correction matters, CoE-60 would dominate. *Falsifiable test*: train a multi-layer probe ensemble on prefill activations at every layer where single-layer AUROC > 0.65; compare ensemble OOF AUROC to L19 alone. If ensemble > L19 + 2σ noise, F-9's redundancy claim is over-stated.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (Prefill DoM AUROC 0.7731)** — SCAV is the *direct precedent* in supervised linear probes on residual streams; their >95% on safety provides an upper-bound expectation for what linear probes can achieve when the target is well-encoded, sharpening the question of why correctness saturates at 0.7731.
- **F-3 (cos = 0.046, prefill ⊥ final)** — SCAV Fig 4(b) provides a non-trivial alternative explanation (active cross-layer cancellation, not orthogonal concepts).
- **F-7 (D-bucket geometric signature)** — SCAV's per-layer linear-separability map is a tool for asking *which layer* exposes D-bucket membership most cleanly; the L19 anchor was chosen pre-D-bucket.
- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering)** — SCAV provides the closed-form perturbation magnitude (`ε = [sigmoid⁻¹(P₀) − b − wᵀe] / ‖w‖`) and the binary-search hyperparameter selection that we currently lack in our E1 formulation.
- **H-12 (label-free probes for refusal)** — SCAV is the supervised gold-standard; whatever H-12 produces should be benchmarked against SCAV-style supervised probe accuracy.
- **H-17 (RoPE de-rotation)** — SCAV gets a static linear direction that works across positions without de-rotation, which is mild evidence *against* H-17's "rotation is mechanical" prediction (or evidence that safety probes operate before/around RoPE in a way correctness probes don't).

## Methodologies extracted

- **Per-layer linear safety probe** — `Pm(e) = sigmoid(wᵀe + b)` trained with cross-entropy + L2 reg on 140 malicious + 140 safe instruction embeddings; reported per-layer test accuracy.
  *Replication cost*: 10 min CPU on cached prefill NPZs (per layer × 28 layers).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — we already do single-layer probes; this just sweeps every layer with the same machinery.

- **Closed-form embedding-level attack** — `ε = [sigmoid⁻¹(P₀) − b − wᵀe] / ‖w‖`, `v = w / ‖w‖`, with magnitude minimization subject to `Pm(e + ε·v) ≤ P₀`.
  *Replication cost*: 1 day H100 (needs full generation rerun with intervened activations).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct H-1/E1 method.

- **Multi-layer sequential perturbation (early → late)** — perturb only on layers with TestAcc > 90%, in increasing-layer order, single closed-form step per layer.
  *Replication cost*: 1–2 day H100 (variable depending on perturbation count).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — refutation arm for F-9.

- **t-SNE on probe-layer embeddings** — qualitative separability check before AUROC reporting.
  *Replication cost*: 5 min CPU per layer on cached prefill NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — fast sanity step before any AUROC claim.

- **Auto-selected perturbation hyperparameters** — binary search on `P₀` threshold (0.01% in the paper) until ASR-useful is maximized.
  *Replication cost*: free (logic only) once embedding-level attack is implemented.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — eliminates a manual α sweep in P10-FE1.

- **Probe-weight direction `w/‖w‖` as the canonical steering vector** (vs mass-mean / DoM).
  *Replication cost*: free (already extracting both).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — comparison arm in P10-FE1.

- **Prompt-level attack via AutoDAN genetic algorithm + dual loss `Pm(eˢ) + ‖eˢ − e‖`** — not applicable to our work; we don't do prompt optimization.

## Approaches & framings

- **Linear separability as a function of alignment training, not architecture.** SCAV shows aligned LLaMA gets >95% probe accuracy while unaligned Alpaca gets near-chance. Reframes our F-2 result: the prefill correctness direction may be a side-effect of Qwen-1.5B-Instruct's SFT/RLHF rather than fundamental computation. Cross-tests against base-model Qwen would settle this.

- **Embedding-level attack as inverse steering.** Behavior change framed as crossing a learned decision boundary by minimum-norm perturbation. Re-frames our H-1 (per-position DoM steering) — instead of "add α·v" the question becomes "find ε that crosses the probe's threshold" — a quantity, not just a direction.

- **Late-layer correction circuits.** SCAV demonstrates that residual-stream perturbations are not additive across layers; later layers actively reverse earlier-layer modifications. Directly bears on F-3 interpretation and on any P10/P11 patching experiment.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Probe form: `Pm(e) = sigmoid(wᵀe + b)`, L2-regularized cross-entropy.
- Layer-inclusion threshold for attack: TestAcc > 90% (otherwise excluded from perturbation set).
- Perturbation threshold: `P₀ = 0.01%` on the probe output post-attack.
- Sequential perturbation order: early → late layer.
- Embeddings extracted at all L layers; aligned LLMs cross 95% TestAcc at layer 10–11.
- Training data minimum: ~5 instruction pairs already gives near-100% ASR-keyword (Figure 3); 140+140 is comfortably above the saturation point.
- Models tested: LLaMA-2-7B-Chat, LLaMA-2-13B-Chat, LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B, Qwen-1.5-7B-Chat, Vicuna-v1.5-7B, WizardLM-2; black-box GPT-4 transfer.
- Code reference: https://github.com/SproutNan/AI-Safety_SCAV

## Replicable intermediates

- **Per-layer prefill probe AUROC sweep on Qwen-1.5B** — we have or can cheaply re-extract per-layer L0..L27 prefill activations from `pathway11_h100/`; train SCAV-style probes at each layer; compare to L19 = 0.7731. Script: adapt `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/` probe code to loop over layers. Cost ~30 min CPU.
- **t-SNE on L19 prefill — correct vs incorrect** — direct visualization; if separability looks like SCAV's aligned-LLaMA t-SNE we have geometric corroboration of F-2; if it looks like unaligned-Alpaca we have a problem. Cost ~5 min CPU.
- **Cosine angle: probe weight `w` vs raw DoM at L19** — cheap (one cached NPZ + sklearn LR fit). If `cos(w, DoM) > 0.95`, the probe-weight-vs-DoM debate is moot for L19 and SCAV's closed-form formula is interchangeable with our DoM scaffolding; if low, probe-weight steering and DoM steering will diverge in P10-FE1.
- **Single-step closed-form steering magnitude prediction** — for any held-out problem we have prefill `e`, probe `(w, b)`, threshold `P₀`; compute ε via SCAV's formula and check whether the predicted magnitude lies in the same range our manual α-sweep explored.

## 2404.12038 — Uncovering Safety Risks of Large Language Models through Concept Activation Vector (Xu, Huang, Chen, Wang, 2024)

**Relevance:** SCAV is the closest published methodological precedent to F-2 / H-1: a supervised linear probe (`Pm(e) = sigmoid(w·e + b)`, L2 reg, ~140 examples) on residual-stream embeddings, used both as a correctness/safety predictor and as a closed-form steering direction (`v = w/‖w‖`, `ε = [sigmoid⁻¹(P₀) − b − w·e] / ‖w‖`). It reports >95% probe TestAcc starting at layer 10–11 in aligned LLaMA-2/Vicuna/Mistral, sharply higher than our 0.7731 OOF AUROC at L19 — a gap that licenses two cheap experiments: a per-layer probe sweep (P11-FE2038) and a closed-form-magnitude steering arm in E1 (P10-FE2038). The paper also surfaces a multi-layer-correction mechanism (Fig 4b) that provides a concrete alternative reading of F-3's prefill ⊥ final orthogonality, namely active cross-layer cancellation rather than encoding-of-different-concepts.

**Key claim we tested:** Linear probes on aligned-LLM residual streams achieve near-perfect class separation from layer 10–11 onward, and the probe-normal direction (not the difference-of-means) is the canonical steering vector.
