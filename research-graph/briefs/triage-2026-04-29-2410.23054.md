# Triage brief — 2410.23054 — Controlling Language and Diffusion Models by Transporting Activations

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23054
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-3 orthogonality is not a steering blocker.**
   Our F-3 narrative treats `cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046` as evidence
   that a fixed-vector steering direction cannot be the right object for H-1.
   AcT's TruthfulQA results (Linear-AcT @ λ=1 yields MC1 +4.95% on Gemma2-2B
   and +7.76% on Llama3-8B) are produced by a *fixed* per-layer affine map
   estimated once on n≈700 truthful/untruthful sentence pairs and applied
   identically at every token position. **Mechanism**: AcT generalizes
   ITI-M / CAA / ActAdd as the same `T(a) = ωa + β` family with mean-pooling
   across tokens; mean-pooling makes per-token direction differences
   (including the prefill/final orthogonality we observe) largely irrelevant
   because the intervention is fit to a token-averaged distribution.
   **Falsifiable test**: fit Linear-AcT on cached pathway11_h100 L19 prefill
   activations using correct/incorrect MATH-500 K=1 outcomes as the two
   distributions, apply at every generation step at λ=1, measure ΔAccuracy.
   If Δ ≥ +3pp, F-3's "orthogonality is the problem" framing is wrong and
   H-1's per-position-DoM design is over-engineered.

2. **F-2's L19 DoM AUROC = 0.7731 may be a floor, not a ceiling, due to
   variance asymmetry.**
   Paper Figure 3 documents that on real Gemma2-2B activations the source
   and target standard deviations differ substantially (σ_a ≠ σ_b) for
   toxic/non-toxic sentences. Our F-2 probe (DoM = mean-difference only) is
   isomorphic to Mean-AcT, which the paper repeatedly shows is dominated by
   Linear-AcT (which adds the σ-ratio term `ω`). **Mechanism**: if
   `σ_correct ≠ σ_incorrect` at L19 prefill, a simple ω-scaled coordinate
   transform should beat the mean-only DoM probe. **Falsifiable test**:
   train a coordinate-wise affine probe `T(a) = ωa + β` with closed-form
   (ω, β) on the same 5-fold splits used for EXP-037; compute AUROC. If
   AUROC > 0.7731 by ≥ 0.02 OOF, the F-2 framing of "DoM is a strong
   correctness predictor" is incomplete — `σ`-aware probes do meaningfully
   better, and our 256-token-truncation post-mortem may have understated
   what the geometry actually carries.

3. **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer DoM) under-estimates causal
   inter-layer signal.**
   AcT's causal sequential estimation (Section 3.2) shows that estimating
   each layer's transport map *after* applying earlier layers' maps is
   strictly better than simultaneous (independent) estimation (Table 4).
   That says: the intervention at layer ℓ depends on the residual-stream
   state already shaped by layers 1…ℓ-1 — a multi-layer signal that a
   single-layer probe cannot recover. F-9 claims CoE-60 trajectory features
   are "redundant" with single-layer L19 DoM. **Mechanism**: if causal
   Linear-AcT applied to all post-LN layers strictly outperforms
   Linear-AcT applied at L19 alone (toxicity reductions 7.5× post-LN-all
   vs 5.6× attention-single, Table 2 implies a non-trivial multi-layer
   gain), then multi-layer features carry signal beyond L19 — contradicting
   F-9's redundancy claim. **Falsifiable test**: fit Linear-AcT
   independently at L1, L10, L19, L25 on cached prefill activations and at
   the full causal {L1…L27} stack; if causal-stack AUROC > L19-alone AUROC
   by ≥ 0.02, F-9 needs reframing.

4. **H-1's α-sweep is the wrong lever; λ ∈ [0,1] is the right one.**
   H-1 proposes injecting `α · w_t_norm` and sweeping α ∈ {-4,-2,-1,0,1,2,4}.
   AcT shows (Section 3.3, Figure 4) that bias-multiplier α for ITI-C is
   unbounded, non-interpretable, and changes optimum across model/layer/task
   (best α=8 for Gemma toxicity, α=3 for Llama toxicity, α=2 for
   truthfulness). The OT-grounded knob `λ ∈ [0,1]` peaks consistently at
   λ=1. **Mechanism**: the additive-shift parameterization H-1 uses inherits
   the same OOD pathology AcT highlights (transports activations outside the
   target distribution support). **Falsifiable test**: re-formulate H-1's
   intervention as the AcT λ-knob with transport support; if the AcT-knob
   sweep finds a consistent λ≈1 peak across our 4 model/layer pairs while
   H-1's α-sweep does not, then H-1 is testing the wrong steering primitive
   and the project's "good selective predictor, not a lever" closure clause
   should not fire prematurely.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering of MATH-500 accuracy)**: AcT is the
  literature's best-engineered version of what H-1 prescribes (extract
  contrastive activations, fit a per-layer map, intervene at inference
  time). Our headline anchor (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM)=0.046) was the
  *reason* H-1 went to per-position; AcT's mean-pooled λ=1 result on
  TruthfulQA suggests a fixed-vector formulation may suffice. Linear-AcT
  on cached prefill activations is essentially a free baseline for H-1.
- **F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)**: AcT's σ-ratio argument predicts
  that a coordinate-wise affine probe should beat the mean-only DoM probe;
  the gap is bounded above by the variance asymmetry between
  correct/incorrect activation distributions. Direct, cheap to test on
  cached data.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM)=0.046)**: AcT's per-layer mean-pooling
  argument means token-position-level direction differences are partially
  artifacts of pooling choice. Re-computing F-3 with mean-pooled
  generation-token activations (not last-token only) is a same-data check.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer DoM)**: AcT's causal sequential
  estimation (Definition 3.2) is a principled way to fit the multi-layer
  signal that F-9 claims is redundant with L19. If causal Linear-AcT > L19
  Linear-AcT, F-9 needs to be tightened to "L19 captures most but not all
  of the inter-layer signal."
- **H-12 (Semantic-entropy probes label-free replace supervised DoM for
  refusal)**: AcT also requires labels (paired contrastive distributions),
  so H-12's label-free advantage over DoM extends to AcT.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Linear-AcT (affine optimal-transport map per neuron)** — closed-form
  `T(a) = ωa + β` fit on sorted source/target samples; ω, β have analytic
  expressions in terms of recentered sorted observations.
  *Replication cost*: 20min CPU on cached pathway11_h100 prefill activations
  (sorting + closed form).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — it's a cheap superset of our existing DoM
  probe and directly tests F-2's ceiling.

- **Mean-AcT (mean-shift, ω=1)** — special case where σ_a = σ_b assumed;
  equivalent to ITI-M / CAA / ActAdd's mean-difference intervention.
  *Replication cost*: 5min CPU on cached prefill activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — it's our existing DoM, included for
  apples-to-apples comparison with Linear-AcT.

- **Causal sequential layer estimation** — fit layer-ℓ map after applying
  layers 1…ℓ-1 maps in a forward pass; iterate.
  *Replication cost*: 1h H100 (two forward passes per intervened layer).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes for a small layer subset {L10, L19, L25}
  on Qwen-2.5-1.5B. Only worth running if single-layer Linear-AcT shows
  meaningful effect first.

- **Transport support clipping** — only transport samples in observed
  source range `[min A, max A]`; bypass intervention for OOD samples.
  *Replication cost*: trivial, ~20 lines of code on top of any probe.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — relevant if we ever steer at inference
  (H-1) and want to avoid OOD activation injection.

- **λ-interpolation knob** — `T(a, λ) = (1-λ)a + λT(a)` provides bounded,
  interpretable conditioning strength.
  *Replication cost*: trivial.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — replaces H-1's unbounded α-sweep.

- **Mean-pooling across tokens for activation pooling (vs last-token)** —
  paper finds mean-pooling more robust across models and tasks (Appendix D).
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU re-running F-3 with mean-pooled generation
  tokens instead of last-token.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — sanity check on F-3's 0.046 cosine.

- **Layer-name probing protocol** — paper systematically reports best layer
  in {Pre-LN, Post-LN, Attention, MLP} per task per model; finds Post-LN
  most reliable for AcT (Section 4.2).
  *Replication cost*: 2h CPU sweeping our 4 layer-types at L19 for cached
  prefill activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — only matters if we end up running
  Linear-AcT at inference; for the AUROC sanity check we already know L19
  Pre-LN is what F-2 used.

- **LLM-as-a-judge concept presence** — query Llama3-8B-instruct with
  yes/no on whether a generation contains a target concept (Appendix I).
  *Replication cost*: 2h on a small GPU per 500 generations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — orthogonal to our correctness pipeline,
  unless we ever validate steering qualitative outcomes.

## Approaches & framings

- **Steering as optimal transport between activation distributions** — the
  paper's central reframe: every existing activation-steering method is a
  special case of transporting an activation distribution µ to ν. This
  intersects our F-2 / F-3 / H-1 framing by shifting the question from
  "what direction in activation space encodes correctness?" (DoM-style) to
  "what map between correct- and incorrect-activation distributions
  preserves the in-distribution support?" (AcT-style). The latter is
  σ-aware; the former is mean-only.
- **Source/target distributions instead of binary labels** — recasts
  steering as distribution matching rather than classification, which
  legitimizes σ-aware probes and motivates support clipping. Direct
  parallel for our correctness probes: "correct trajectories" and
  "incorrect trajectories" are two distributions, not just two clusters
  of points.
- **Bounded, interpretable strength λ ∈ [0,1]** — replaces unbounded
  bias multipliers (ActAdd α, ITI-C α) with a probability-of-transport
  knob. Direct parallel for H-1's α-sweep design.
- **Modality-agnostic intervention** — same recipe for LLM activations
  and diffusion-model activations. Not directly relevant to our
  math-correctness pipeline but flags that AcT recipes from T2I might
  later port to multi-modal LLM correctness work.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Code: `https://github.com/apple/ml-act` (Apple, public).
- Linear-AcT memory cost: `2 floats × n_layers × n_activations × 4 bytes` —
  e.g., 0.91 MB for Gemma2-2B post-LN. Can be **fused into linear layers
  for zero inference overhead** if λ is fixed.
- Estimation cost: dominated by sorting activations, `O(NLD log(NLD))`,
  cheaper than a forward pass.
- Sample budget: n≈700 paired sentences sufficient for stable maps —
  matches our 5-fold prefill probe sample size (500 MATH-500 problems
  → ≈400 train).
- Best layer-type for AcT: Post-LN consistently across models/tasks
  (Llama3-8B has no Post-LN, so Attention layers are used instead).
- Pooling: `ϕ(z) = z.mean(-1)` across tokens — important across-method
  contrast vs last-token (`z[..., -1]`).
- Causal estimation > simultaneous (Tables 4–5).
- No hyperparameter tuning needed for AcT — λ=1 is the default and
  consistently best.
- Linear-AcT closed form:
  `ω = Σ ã^(i) b̃^(i) / Σ (ã^(i))²`,
  `β = m_b - ω · m_a`,
  where `(i)` superscripts are sorted observations, m_a/m_b are means,
  `ã/b̃` are mean-recentered sorted samples.

- **2306.03341** — Inference-Time Intervention (Li et al.) — already in
  graph (status: graphed, CONTRADICTED partial). AcT explicitly subsumes
  ITI-C and ITI-M as special cases of Linear-AcT, and demonstrates ITI-C
  requires task-specific α tuning (α=8 toxicity Gemma, α=3 toxicity Llama,
  α=2 truthfulness) while AcT's λ=1 transfers. Strengthens our existing
  CONTRADICTED partial classification.
- **2308.10248** — ActAdd (Turner et al.) — already in graph (status:
  graphed). Subsumed by Mean-AcT in the AcT generalization.
- **2312.06681** — CAA (Rimsky et al.) — already in graph (status:
  graphed). Subsumed by Mean-AcT.
- **2310.01405** — REPE / Representation Engineering (Zou et al., 2023)
  — NOT in graph; recommend admission. AcT positions itself relative to
  REPE as the "principled OT generalization" of REPE-style contrastive
  steering vectors. REPE is also the only prior method to use a form of
  causal layer-by-layer estimation, which AcT formalizes. Worth admitting
  as the canonical pre-AcT baseline beyond ITI/CAA/ActAdd.
- **2407.12824 / Whispering Experts (Suau et al., 2024 ICML)** — AURA —
  NOT in graph; recommend admission. AURA dampens activations
  proportional to per-neuron classification ability rather than additive
  shift; AcT shows AURA has no λ-knob (uncontrollable strength) but is
  competitive on toxicity. Relevant alternative methodology if H-1 ever
  needs a non-additive baseline.
- **2404.03592** — ReFT (Wu et al., 2024) — NOT in graph; not strongly
  recommended for admission. Uses low-rank projection / counterfactual
  representation; orthogonal to our additive-DoM design. Cite-only at most.
