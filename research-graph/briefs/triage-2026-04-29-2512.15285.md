# Triage brief — 2512.15285 — Topological Metric for Unsupervised Embedding Quality Evaluation

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15285
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-10 ("PH on trained residual streams = Gaussian null") by counter-example.**
   F-10 concluded that VR persistent homology summaries on Qwen-2.5-1.5B trained
   residual streams sit at the Gaussian null. This paper applies VR persistent
   homology (H₀ + H₁, total-persistence summary, eq. 1) to *trained* embedding
   point clouds and reports mean Spearman 0.84 (UBM) and 0.76 (collaborative
   filtering) with downstream task performance — i.e., PH on trained
   representations is *not* at the null in their setting.
   *Mechanism by which the refutation could fire*: their summary normalizes by
   max pairwise distance (eq. 1's denominator); ours did not. The Gaussian null
   for *unnormalized* total persistence is well-known to scale with diameter,
   so a diameter-normalized total persistence may carry signal that the raw
   summary loses to scale-noise.
   *Falsifiable test*: implement eq. 1 (gudhi/ripser, H₀ + H₁) on the P11 prefill
   pooled-embedding point cloud (500 problems × d=1536 for Qwen-2.5-1.5B L19),
   compare Persistence values for correct vs. incorrect partitions against a
   Gaussian-with-matched-covariance null (10⁴ permutations). If diameter-normalized
   PH separates classes at p<0.01 where the unnormalized version did not, F-10
   needs to be reframed: "raw total persistence is Gaussian-like; normalized
   total persistence is not."

2. **Refutes H-7 ("density-based outlier detection finds D-bucket") on
   mechanism.**
   H-7 hypothesizes that D-bucket (F-7) is found by density estimators
   (kNN density, LOF, etc.). The paper's eq. 2 says expected H₀ persistence
   scales as n^(1−1/d) · Vol(M)^(1/d) / Diam(M) — i.e., total persistence is a
   monotone proxy for intrinsic dimension d (Yukich 2006), not local density.
   If D-bucket is an *intrinsic-dim* outlier (collapsed manifold) rather than
   a *density* outlier, density-based detectors would fail where Persistence
   succeeds.
   *Mechanism*: low intrinsic dim ⇒ low expected total persistence (eq. 2);
   D-bucket may correspond to dimension-collapsed problems where the prefill
   geometry has degenerated even though local density is unremarkable.
   *Falsifiable test*: compute Persistence_0 + Persistence_1 on the prefill
   point clouds restricted to A-bucket (n=207) vs. D-bucket (n=36) from F-7.
   If D-bucket Persistence is significantly lower than A-bucket but
   density-based outlier scores are not, the H-7 mechanism (density) is wrong
   and the true mechanism is intrinsic-dim collapse — pointing the project
   at a different probe family.

3. **Refutes F-1 ("dimensional breathing is universal") as a covariance-spectrum
   artifact.**
   F-1 measures breathing via participation ratio on the per-token residual
   covariance — a spectral statistic. Eq. 2 of this paper provides an
   *independent* intrinsic-dim proxy from total persistence that does not
   touch covariance eigenvalues at all. If per-token Persistence_0 across
   layers does *not* exhibit a breathing-shaped curve where PR does, then
   "breathing" reflects only the leading-eigenvalue redistribution in PR, not
   a true manifold-dimensionality oscillation.
   *Mechanism*: PR is dominated by the top few eigenvalues of the
   centered-residuals covariance; topological persistence is sensitive to the
   *full* metric structure. They can disagree when an embedding's covariance
   inflates without the underlying manifold's topological richness changing
   (e.g., anisotropic stretching).
   *Falsifiable test*: on cached P11 NPZ (28 layers × ~1024 tokens × d=1536),
   for each token build the layer-stack point cloud (28 points in ℝ^d) — or,
   more interpretably, for each layer build the across-token point cloud and
   compute Persistence per token-window. Compare the resulting curve to the
   PR breathing curve from F-1. If correlation r < 0.5, F-1's "dimensional"
   framing is over-stated — it's a covariance-spectral phenomenon, not a
   manifold one.

4. **Refutes F-2 ("L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731 is the prefill correctness signal")
   on completeness.**
   F-2 frames the prefill correctness signal as a *single linear direction*
   (DoM). Persistence is a fully unsupervised, label-free, geometry-only
   scalar. If a Persistence-derived per-problem score (e.g., Persistence on
   the *neighborhood* point cloud around each problem's prefill embedding)
   hits AUROC ≥ 0.65 on MATH-500, then a substantial fraction of the F-2
   signal is recoverable without labels at all — meaning the L19 DoM mostly
   re-reads a topological-complexity signal that exists in the geometry alone.
   *Mechanism*: correct and incorrect problems may sit on differently-organized
   submanifolds (different intrinsic dim / topological richness, eq. 2);
   supervised DoM finds the linear projection that best separates them, but
   the underlying signal is a non-linear geometric one.
   *Falsifiable test*: for each MATH-500 problem, compute Persistence on its
   k-nearest-neighbor point cloud (k=50) in prefill-L19 space; rank-correlate
   the resulting score with binary correctness; report AUROC with 5-fold OOF.
   If AUROC ≥ 0.65 (within 10 points of DoM's 0.7731), F-2's "single direction"
   framing is incomplete — and H-12 (label-free probes) gets a strong
   candidate metric.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-10 (PH on trained residual streams = Gaussian null)** — the paper is the
  most direct foreign challenge to F-10 in our graph. Their VR-H₀-and-H₁
  total-persistence (eq. 1) reports correlation Spearman 0.84 on trained
  embeddings; F-10 said *some* PH summary is at the null. The disagreement is
  most likely about *which* summary (raw vs. diameter-normalized) and *which*
  point cloud (per-token trajectory vs. across-problem corpus). Headline anchor
  to compare: F-10's null vs. their UBM Spearman 0.840.
- **F-1 (dimensional breathing universal)** — eq. 2 (Yukich 2006 asymptotic)
  gives a closed form for E[Persistence_0] in terms of intrinsic dim d. Our
  breathing curve uses PR (peak ~67/88 at the inflection token); Persistence
  is an independent estimator of the same quantity.
- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731, OOF 5-fold)** — Persistence is a
  candidate label-free competitor. Anchor: DoM's 0.7731 is the threshold a
  Persistence-derived score has to clear to matter.
- **F-7 (D-bucket has distinctive collective signature)** — eq. 2's
  intrinsic-dim interpretation gives a direct mechanism for what makes D-bucket
  collective rather than per-problem: dimension-collapse is a population
  statistic.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer DoM)** — Persistence is yet
  another global-geometry summary that could either replicate or refute the
  CoE-60 ≈ DoM equivalence by being orthogonal to both.
- **H-7 (density-based outlier detection finds D-bucket)** — Persistence-based
  detection is the natural alternative if H-7's density assumption is wrong.
- **H-12 (label-free probes replace supervised DoM)** — most direct fit; the
  paper's metric *is* a label-free probe family member.
- **H-16 (Marchenko-Pastur bias correction preserves breathing magnitude)** —
  eq. 2's intrinsic-dim asymptotic and our MP correction target the same
  quantity (effective d) by different routes; cross-validating them would
  strengthen H-16.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Vietoris-Rips filtration of an embedding point cloud, H₀ and H₁** — build
  the VR complex over the embedding cloud and track persistence pairs in
  homology dimensions 0 and 1.
  *Replication cost*: minutes-to-hours CPU with gudhi or ripser for n ≤ 1000;
  scales as O(n³) worst-case, but H₁ on 500 points is tractable.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — directly applicable to our cached P11
  prefill embeddings (n=500 problems).
- **Diameter-normalized total persistence (eq. 1)** —
  Persistence_k(X_n) = Σ_{(b,d)∈H_k}(d−b) / max_{i,j} ‖x_i − x_j‖.
  *Replication cost*: trivial after PH is computed.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — possibly the missing ingredient for
  F-10's null result.
- **Yukich-2006 asymptotic for E[Persistence_0]** (eq. 2) — gives an
  intrinsic-dim estimator from a single PH calculation.
  *Replication cost*: closed-form post PH.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — cross-check against MP-corrected
  participation ratio for H-16.
- **Cross-domain unsupervised-vs-downstream Pearson/Spearman correlation
  protocol** — for each unsupervised metric, sweep hyperparameters, compute
  metric and downstream score, report (Pearson, Spearman, top-k pick quality).
  *Replication cost*: low; protocol is the same regardless of underlying
  metric.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — this is the right framework to evaluate
  whether Persistence beats DoM-AUROC on label-free correctness ranking.
- **Per-epoch model selection benchmark** — across training epochs of a single
  model, score each checkpoint by the unsupervised metric and pick the best.
  *Replication cost*: requires a training run with checkpoints saved; we don't
  have these for Qwen-2.5-1.5B.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **no** — out of scope; we are not training models.
- **Baseline metric suite: RankMe, α-ReQ, NESum, μ₀-incoherence, StableRank,
  P-C number, SelfCluster.**
  *Replication cost*: low (most are spectral; <1 hour CPU each).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **partly** — RankMe and NESum are worth adding
  as baselines for any future label-free correctness-probe experiment.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Embedding quality = topological richness, measured by lifetime of
  persistence features."** Reframes representation evaluation away from
  spectral / linear-separability assumptions. Intersects F-1 (which is purely
  spectral) and F-7 (collective vs. per-problem signature) by offering a
  population-level, geometry-respecting alternative.
- **Diameter normalization as the discriminator between "PH carries signal"
  and "PH is null."** This is a methodological framing the F-10 conclusion
  did not consider — our null result may be specific to unnormalized
  summaries.
- **Eq. 2's intrinsic-dim interpretation of total persistence.** Treats total
  persistence as a *parameter estimate* for manifold dimension, not a feature.
  This intersects H-16 (MP correction for breathing) by providing an
  independent estimator.
- **Population-level vs. trajectory-level PH.** The paper builds the point
  cloud across a population of *examples*; our F-10 work built it across
  tokens *within* an example. These are different topological objects, and
  the paper's success doesn't automatically translate to our trajectory
  setting — but it points at where signal might exist that we haven't
  measured.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Author code: https://anonymous.4open.science/r/topo_metrics-94D6/ (anonymous,
  pre-publication) — should clone and snapshot before the anonymous link
  expires.
- VR PH up to dim 1 only; H₂ and higher not computed (consistent with their
  eq. 2 derivation focused on H₀).
- Diameter normalization is critical (eq. 1 denominator).
- For UBM the embedding sweep covers embedding size 64–512, dropout {0.1, 0.3},
  layers 1–4, sequence length 64–256 — i.e., they do not need huge n to see
  the correlation; ~tens of configurations suffice.
- For collaborative filtering they take **absolute** correlation values
  because lower metric ↔ better model in that setup — a sign-direction
  caveat we should mirror if we adopt the metric.
- No explicit minimum-n analysis — risk that their numbers are sample-size
  contingent.

  same token. Look at correlation across the 1024-token trajectory.
  ~1 day CPU.

  whichever cached PH outputs were used in F-10 (likely P7/P8 layer-wise

  CPU; if it only cached summary statistics, it requires re-running the PH

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2512.15285 — Topological Metric for Unsupervised Embedding Quality Evaluation (Shestov, Klenitskiy, Denisova, Dzagkoev, Petrovich, Savchenko, Makarenko, Sber AI Lab, 2025)

**Relevance:** Proposes "Persistence," an unsupervised, label-free metric defined as the diameter-normalized total persistence (H₀ + H₁) of a Vietoris–Rips filtration on an embedding point cloud (eq. 1). Reports mean Spearman correlations 0.84 (user-behavioral modeling), 0.76 (collaborative filtering), 0.61 (per-epoch financial-analytics model selection) with downstream task performance, outperforming RankMe / α-ReQ / NESum / μ₀-incoherence on those benchmarks. Eq. 2 (Yukich 2006) gives an asymptotic interpretation of expected H₀ persistence as a monotone proxy for intrinsic manifold dimension. The paper does not test on language-model hidden states or transformer residual streams, but every component (VR filtration, total persistence, intrinsic-dim asymptotic) is directly applicable to our cached P11 prefill activations.

**Key claim we tested:** Diameter-normalized total persistence on trained embedding point clouds carries non-trivial signal about representation quality — i.e., persistent homology is *not* at the Gaussian null on trained representations.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — F-10 says PH on trained residual streams is at the Gaussian null; this paper says PH (with eq. 1's normalization) on trained embeddings strongly correlates with downstream quality. The disagreement may be entirely about diameter normalization (eq. 1 denominator) and/or the choice of point cloud (per-token trajectory vs. across-problem corpus). P11-FE75 + P8-FE55 will resolve.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE75 (Persistence as label-free correctness probe), P11-FE76 (per-token Persistence breathing curve), P8-FE55 (re-evaluate F-10 with eq. 1 normalization), H-85, H-86.

**Status:** TO TEST
