# Triage brief — 2203.11171 — Self-Consistency Improves Chain of Thought Reasoning in Language Models

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11171
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-8 (selective-prediction acc 71.6% at coverage 0.5 via prefill DoM) could be dominated by a no-hidden-state K-sample consistency selector.** Mechanism: Wang §3.5 / Figure 5 reports that the agreement rate among K samples (the % of decodes that agree with the majority answer) is highly correlated with accuracy on GSM8K; using low consistency as an abstention signal is exactly the same problem F-8 solves, but with no hidden-state extraction and no labelled probe. Falsifiable test: on cached MATH-500 K=8 outputs from `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/results.json` (1.5B), compute per-problem agreement rate, sort by it, and report answered-set accuracy at 50% coverage. If this exceeds 71.6% then prefill DoM is at best redundant with consistency at K≈8 and F-8 must be reframed as "matches consistency at K=1 compute, loses at K=8."

2. **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731) is challenged by Wang's calibration claim that the LM cannot distinguish solutions on its own log-probs.** Mechanism: Wang §2 footnote 2 documents that normalized P(r,a|prompt) values are clustered tightly across sampled paths — i.e., the model is uncalibrated for correctness in output-space log-prob. If the residual stream and the unembedding share a common backbone, an uncalibrated unembedding is evidence that the underlying state may not encode correctness as a clean direction; the prefill DoM signal could then be capturing something correlated with surface features (length-band, prompt difficulty, token frequency) rather than reasoning correctness. Falsifiable test: regress the prefill-DoM score on prompt length and per-token frequency features; if residual AUROC drops below ~0.65, F-2 partially reduces to a length/lexical confound.

3. **F-7 (D-bucket has a distinctive collective signature; 7.2% of MATH-500 problems are *hurt* by K=1→K=8) may shrink toward zero at K=40.** Mechanism: Wang Figure 2 shows accuracy is monotone in K up to K=40 across every benchmark; D-bucket is defined at K=8 and may simply be the long tail of the K-vs-accuracy curve where K=8 majority vote is still noisy but K=40 has averaged out. If D-bucket ≪ 1% at K=40, the "collective geometric signature" claim loses most of its empirical anchor: it would describe a transient noise regime, not a stable subpopulation. Falsifiable test: extend our K=8 MATH-500 1.5B run to K=20 and K=40, recompute D-bucket size at each K, plot D-bucket(K). If the signature persists at K=40, F-7 is sharpened; if it vanishes, F-7 is downgraded to a K-specific artifact.

4. **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM) misses a stronger redundancy: both may be redundant with output-space majority-vote consistency.** Mechanism: F-9 is a within-hidden-state redundancy claim. Wang §3.4 shows that majority-vote on output-space answers beats sample-and-rank on log-probs by a wide margin and that this margin grows with K — i.e., a feature-free output-space signal carries more correctness information than any single-step internal-score signal at matched-or-greater compute. If at matched K=8 compute the consistency-rate selector dominates *both* CoE-60 and prefill-DoM on selective-prediction AUROC, F-9's hierarchy ("CoE → DoM") collapses to "all hidden-state methods → output agreement". Falsifiable test: compare answered@coverage=0.5 accuracy across {prefill DoM, CoE-60, K=8 agreement-rate} on MATH-500 1.5B with the same compute budget normalized.

5. **H-19 (C_exact verification routing beats C_infer scaling at matched compute) is contested by Wang's headline trade-off — pure additional sampling, no verifier, gives +17.9 pp on GSM8K with PaLM-540B.** Mechanism: H-19 frames "verification" as the right way to spend extra compute. Wang's K=40 majority vote is a *verifier-free*, training-free, prompt-free baseline that already extracts a large fraction of the available "knowing-when-it-doesn't-know" signal (Figure 5). If at our 1.5B / 7B models majority-vote at K=8 narrows the verification-vs-scaling gap to within noise, the H-19 hypothesis must add the constraint "above and beyond the SC ceiling" or be re-prioritized down. Falsifiable test: at matched FLOPs, run (a) K=8 SC, (b) K=4 SC + verifier (the H-19 design), (c) K=1 + DoM-gated K=4 fallback. If (a) is within 1pp of (b), H-19 is provisionally refuted at this scale.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731, 1.5B OOF 5-fold)** ↔ Wang §3.4 "sample-and-rank with normalized log-prob loses to majority vote." Both are correctness-ranking signals from a single LM. If prefill DoM gives AUROC 0.7731 at K=1 while Wang's normalized log-prob (essentially a final-token, post-decode signal) gives a strictly weaker ranking — confirmed by §3.4 and Table 1 — F-2 establishes that there is *non-trivial* correctness-discrimination available *before* decoding that the LM's own log-prob ranking cannot recover. This is a positive cross-anchor for F-2: prefill DoM is doing something the LM-as-self-ranker cannot.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046)** ↔ Wang Figure 5's consistency-as-confidence is a *final-answer-space* signal. F-3's near-orthogonality of prefill and final-token directions implies that prefill DoM and consistency-rate measure independent things; this is the key empirical lever for "prefill DoM at K=1 should add to consistency at K>1" rather than be subsumed.
- **F-8 (sel-pred 71.6% at coverage 0.5, K=2.5 avg)** ↔ Wang Figure 5 directly. Same problem (abstain on low-confidence), different signal source. The K=2.5 avg in F-8 implies the prefill-DoM gate is gating *additional samples*, which is structurally exactly Wang's K-vs-accuracy trade-off — F-8 is a smart-budgeted version of SC. Numerical anchor: F-8's 71.6% at K=2.5 must beat plain SC's accuracy at K=2.5 to be worth keeping; if SC at K=2.5 ≥ 71.6%, F-8 is moot.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 ≈ single-layer DoM)** ↔ Wang §2 calibration footnote: the LM's internal uncertainty signals are flat across paths. F-9 documented within-hidden-state redundancy; Wang's footnote suggests *all* single-pass internal signals may be flat for similar reasons.
- **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes replace supervised DoM for refusal)** ↔ Wang's consistency rate is a discrete, label-free "semantic entropy" approximation. If Wang's consistency-rate at K=10 outperforms DoM at K=1 on selective-prediction, H-12 has a stronger empirical anchor than just SE-probes.

## Methodologies extracted

- **K-sample majority vote (sample-and-marginalize)** — sample K reasoning paths at T∈{0.5, 0.7} with top-k=40 (or top-k off for GPT-3-style models), parse each path's final answer, take mode.
  *Replication cost*: K× single-pass inference. Already partially in our pipeline at K=8 per PAPER_INDEX entry.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — extend to K=20, K=40 on MATH-500 × 1.5B / 7B to map the K-vs-accuracy curve and stress-test F-7 D-bucket persistence.

- **Normalized log-prob aggregation (Eq. 1)** — exp(mean log P(t_k)) used to weight (r_i, a_i). Wang shows it ≈ majority vote on PaLM-540B, but on smaller models the gap may differ.
  *Replication cost*: free if we cache per-token log-probs during K=8 sampling (~MB of disk).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives us a Wang-style "is the LM calibrated on its own log-probs?" diagnostic on Qwen-2.5-1.5B for free.

- **Consistency-rate as confidence (§3.5, Figure 5)** — % of K decodes agreeing with the majority answer; use as abstention selector.
  *Replication cost*: trivial post-processing on our K=8 cache.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct competitor to F-8's selective-prediction claim, must run before F-8 is published.

- **Sample-and-rank baseline** — sample K, take top-1 by sequence log-prob.
  *Replication cost*: free with cached log-probs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — completes the §3.4 comparison on Qwen-2.5-1.5B.

- **Beam-search-as-decoder-inside-SC** — beam search inside each sampled reasoning path. Wang shows it loses to plain sampling because of low diversity.
  *Replication cost*: H100 pass for fresh generation.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — Wang already settled this for medium-scale models.

- **OOD symbolic-reasoning protocol** — train prompt with 2-letter / 2-flip exemplars, test on 4-letter / 4-flip. Tests whether SC's lift is OOD-stable.
  *Replication cost*: not applicable to our MATH-500 regime.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — out-of-scope.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Self-ensemble on top of a single language model"** — Wang reframes K-sample SC as ensembling a single model's modes, not multiple models. Our prefill-gated-compute (F-8) is exactly a smart-budget self-ensemble — Wang gives us the language to describe it.
- **Reasoning path as latent variable; marginalize via majority vote** — the formal framing in Wang §2 (r_i → a_i, marginalize over r_i). This connects directly to our F-3 (prefill ⊥ final DoM): marginalization in answer space cancels noise in path space; F-3 says path-space and answer-space have orthogonal hidden-state directions, which is consistent with this framing.
- **"Diversity is the key" beating beam search** — Wang's Table 6 shows beam search loses to sampling because beams collapse. Connects to our F-1 / F-5 breathing story: dimensional inflation during reasoning may be the within-model substrate of the diversity that SC exploits at the output level.
- **"Consistency confers self-knowing"** — §3.5: low consistency ⇒ low confidence. This is the unsupervised, output-space dual of our F-2 / F-8 supervised hidden-state confidence story; a clean theoretical bridge for HYPOTHESES.

## Datasets & benchmarks

- **GSM8K (Cobbe et al., 2021)** — 8.5k grade-school math problems, public on HF. Applicable: yes — easier than MATH-500 but same domain; provides a sanity-check for SC effect size on Qwen-2.5-1.5B.
- **MATH-500** — already our primary benchmark; not introduced by Wang but the headline 78.0% on GSM8K (PaLM-540B + SC) is the canonical SC anchor we'd benchmark against.
- **SVAMP** — 1k word-problem variants, public. Applicable: marginally — different surface form than MATH-500, would require fresh extraction.
- **AQUA-RAT** — 100k multiple-choice arithmetic, public. Applicable: no — multiple-choice changes the "consistency" definition.
- **MultiArith / AddSub / ASDiv** — small public arithmetic sets. Applicable: no — too easy for 1.5B Qwen.
- **StrategyQA / CSQA / ARC-c** — commonsense / multiple-choice. Applicable: no — different reasoning regime.
- **Last-letter-concatenation / Coinflip (Wei et al., 2022)** — symbolic reasoning. Applicable: no — out-of-scope.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Sampling hyperparameters: T=0.5, top-k=40 for UL2-20B / LaMDA-137B; T=0.7, top-k=40 for PaLM-540B; T=0.7, no top-k for GPT-3-codex.
- Default K = 40, results averaged over 10 runs (so ~400 paths per problem statistically).
- Standard deviation ≤ 0.5 pp at K=40 across all benchmarks → 10 runs is overkill; for our budget K=20 single-run is probably stat-stable.
- Answer parser is task-dependent: arithmetic = first numeric token after "The answer is "; commonsense = full string after the same anchor.
- Aggregation: unweighted majority vote ≈ normalized weighted sum (74.4 vs 74.1 on PaLM-540B/GSM8K); unweighted weighted-average is much worse (don't use it).
- Robust to imperfect prompts (17.1 → 23.4 pp recovery on LaMDA-137B GSM8K with corrupted exemplars).
- Zero-shot CoT + SC: 43.0 → 69.2 on PaLM-540B GSM8K — i.e., SC works without manual exemplars at all.

- **Compute normalized log-prob ranking baseline** if per-token log-probs were saved during the K=8 generation pass; if not, free on next H100 pass.
- **D-bucket-at-K=8 vs uniform random subset of K=8 outputs.** If we have 8 paths per problem, we already implicitly have 8 K=1 trials per problem; bootstrapping the K-vs-D-bucket curve up to K=8 is free and lets us see if D-bucket is monotonically shrinking.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2203.11171 — Self-Consistency Improves Chain of Thought Reasoning in Language Models (Wang, Wei, Schuurmans, Le, Chi, Narang, Chowdhery, Zhou; ICLR 2023)

**Relevance:** The K=K majority-vote baseline that anchors every "more compute, more samples" claim in our compute-allocation work (F-8, H-19), and the source of the consistency-rate-as-confidence reading (Figure 5) that is the unsupervised competitor to our supervised prefill-DoM probes (F-2, F-8). Also the cleanest published evidence that LM log-probs are flat across solutions (§2 fn 2), which is the *only* published mechanism story for why a supervised hidden-state probe (F-2) should beat a log-prob baseline.

**Key claim we tested:** K-sample majority vote at T=0.7 boosts accuracy on math reasoning benchmarks (+17.9 pp on GSM8K with PaLM-540B at K=40); the % of decodes agreeing with the majority answer is correlated with accuracy and can be used as an abstention signal.

**Our result:** **REPLICATED + EXTENDED.** Existing record (PAPER_INDEX before this triage): K=8 majority vote on MATH-500 × Qwen-2.5-1.5B gives +6.4 pp; we additionally found a 7.2% D-bucket where K=1→K=8 *hurts* — a wrinkle Wang did not document because at K=40 on PaLM-540B the curve is monotone. **This deep triage flags three open replications still owed**: (i) extend K to 20 / 40 to test whether D-bucket persists (H-73); (ii) compare consistency-rate as abstention signal vs F-8 prefill-DoM selective prediction at matched compute (H-72); (iii) replicate Wang's calibration footnote (§2 fn 2) on Qwen-2.5-1.5B (P11-FE73). Until these run, our REPLICATED + EXTENDED status is partial and the K=40 ceiling is unmeasured.

**Related experiments:** EXP-033, EXP-037, P11-FE71, P11-FE72, P11-FE73, H-72, H-73, H-74, H-19.

**Status:** REPLICATED + EXTENDED.
