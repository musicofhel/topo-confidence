# Triage brief — 2601.09269 — RISER: Orchestrating Latent Reasoning Skills for Adaptive Activation Steering

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09269
Triaged: 2026-04-29

Authors: Wencheng Ye, Xiaoyang Yuan, Yi Bin, Pengpeng Zeng, Hengyu Jin, Liang
Peng, Heng Tao Shen.

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-3 (prefill ⊥ final-token DoM, cos 0.046).** RISER injects
   *one* composite vector at the **prefill last-token activation** at a
   middle layer, then lets it propagate "as a sustained cognitive priming
   during decoding," and reports MATH gains of +1.8 to +6.2 over CoT and
   +6.2 over static CAA on Qwen2.5-7B. If a *single* prefill-position
   intervention shifts *answer-token* correctness, the prefill-steering
   subspace and the final-token correctness subspace cannot be functionally
   independent — they must communicate through residual-stream propagation.
   F-3's near-orthogonality (cos 0.046) is then a measurement artifact of
   our linear DoM probes, not a property of the actual influence channels.
   *Falsification:* Train a 6-cluster K-means on our cached prefill-L19 1.5B
   activations (the RISER recipe) → use top centroid as an injection vector
   at prefill-only → measure ΔK=1 accuracy on MATH-500. If accuracy moves
   by ≥1pp, F-3 needs a propagation-channel asterisk.

2. **Refutes F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM).** RISER
   builds a **library of K=6 distinct, low-cosine reasoning vectors**
   (avg pairwise <0.1) and shows that the *learned router-mediated
   composition* outperforms any single-vector (Top-1 Vector Only) by
   1.7–3.4pp on MATH/GPQA/TruthfulQA. This is direct evidence that the
   useful steering signal at a single mid-layer is *not* a single
   direction; there are at least 6 functionally-distinguishable
   sub-directions and the optimal one is input-dependent. F-9's claim
   that one L19 DoM captures the same information as a 60-dim
   CoE-trajectory feature is exactly the structural prediction RISER
   refutes from the steering side. *Falsification:* On our 5-fold
   prefill-L19 features, fit a K=6 mixture-of-linear-probes
   (router-style: gating MLP picks 1-of-6 directions per problem). If
   OOF AUROC > 0.7731 by ≥0.01 with K=6 directions, single-direction
   L19 DoM is not sufficient and F-9's redundancy claim is wrong-side
   of the conclusion.

3. **Refutes H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy)** in
   its strong form. H-1 motivates a *per-token, per-position* DoM steering
   bank — RISER demonstrates that a **single-shot prefill injection**
   (zero per-position adaptation) is sufficient for the gains. If the
   simpler one-shot variant is on the Pareto frontier, the
   per-position-bank engineering investment in H-1 has no headroom to
   justify it. *Falsification:* Apply the simpler RISER-style one-shot
   injection on Qwen2.5-1.5B with our cached L19 prefill-DoM as the
   sole vector. If ΔMATH-500 accuracy ≥ +1pp at K=1 from one-shot alone,
   H-1's per-position justification collapses into "single-shot prefill
   injection is enough" — H-1 should be downgraded or merged with a new
   simpler hypothesis.

4. **Refutes F-2 layer-locality (prefill L19 is *the* predictive layer).**
   RISER reports that **L20 + L25 + L40** are the optimal injection layers
   for Qwen2.5 (with L19–L21 the lower bound), not a single layer.
   On Qwen2.5-7B (28 layers), L25 is *deeper* than our L19 probe. If the
   strongest steering direction lives at L25 rather than L19, our L19 DoM
   probe is reading a *propagated reflection* of an earlier, more
   structured signal, and AUROC 0.7731 is a lower bound left on the
   table by sub-optimal layer choice. *Falsification:* Re-extract L25
   prefill activations on Qwen2.5-1.5B (proportional layer ≈ L25 of 28),
   compute DoM, OOF 5-fold AUROC. If L25 AUROC > L19 AUROC + 0.01, F-2's
   "L19 is the layer" framing is a measurement-artifact of where we
   chose to probe.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-3** (prefill/final cos 0.046). RISER's single-injection-at-prefill
  recipe is a *steering-side* test of whether prefill and final-token
  channels are independent. Our orthogonality is a probe-direction
  geometry; RISER tests the influence-channel topology and (if their
  gains hold) the channels are causally connected.
- **F-9** (CoE-60 ≈ single L19 DoM). The 6-vector clustering directly
  says "single direction is not enough at this layer."
- **H-1** (per-position steering). RISER's success without per-position
  variation is the cheapest possible steering Pareto-frontier point we
  can put H-1 against. Compare against H-1's per-position bank.
- **F-2** (prefill L19 = best correctness predictor, AUROC 0.7731).
  RISER's layer ablation favoring L20/L25/L40 questions whether L19 is
  optimal. We picked L19 from a single ablation early in P11; this
  paper is a prompt to redo that.
- **F-5** (breathing is content-dependent). RISER's K=6 input-dependent
  router is a direct mechanism for *why* breathing should be content-
  dependent: different reasoning skills are activated for different
  problems, so dimensional inflation per-problem reflects different
  vectors being recruited.
- **H-12** (semantic-entropy probes replace supervised DoM). RISER's
  vector elicitation step is *unsupervised* (CAA difference + K-means);
  only the router needs labels. This is partial confirmation that
  label-free vector extraction works, with the caveat that selection
  among extracted vectors needs a label signal.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA) + K-means clustering (K=6) on
  difference vectors** — extract a *library* of reasoning vectors rather
  than a single direction. Centroid of each cluster, L2-normalized.
  Average pairwise cosine <0.1.
  *Replication cost:* 30 min CPU on cached MMLU/MATH paired prompts;
  we already have all activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — directly enables the Refutation 2
  experiment and is the cheapest way to test whether L19 DoM is single-
  direction or multi-cluster.
- **Lightweight bottleneck MLP router (~5M params, <0.1% of base)** with
  two heads: sigmoid selection mask (Gumbel-Sigmoid in train,
  threshold 0.7 in inference) + strength head bounded αmax=2.0.
  *Replication cost:* 1 day with cached probe features.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes if** Refutation 2 lands — gives a
  per-input multi-direction probe to compare against single-DoM AUROC.
- **Two-stage training: SFT with oracle labels (200 examples) → GRPO RL
  refinement** with accuracy reward (1/0) + KL regularizer (coef 0.001).
  *Replication cost:* 1–2 H100 days for Qwen2.5-7B; out of budget for
  1.5B until Refutation 2 motivates it.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **no, not yet** — only if a static
  multi-vector ensemble already shows AUROC lift; otherwise GRPO is
  premature.
- **Single-shot prefill last-token injection at a middle layer**, vector
  added once via element-wise addition, no per-token re-injection.
  *Replication cost:* 30 min H100 forward-pass on MATH-500 with one
  intervention vector.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — Refutation 3 directly. Trivial
  ablation of H-1's per-position assumption.
- **LLM-Judge filtering of difference vectors before clustering** —
  noise reduction step; throws out CAA pairs the judge can't separate.
  *Replication cost:* trivial (Claude/GPT-4 calls).
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **maybe** — gates Refutation 2 quality.
- **Layer sensitivity sweep** — middle layers optimal, edges degrade.
  *Replication cost:* 1 h CPU on cached features.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — Refutation 4 directly.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Latent skill orchestration" framing** — model has a finite library
  of reusable reasoning primitives, problem-solving = picking and
  composing the right ones. Directly compatible with our F-5
  (content-dependent breathing) and reframes F-7 (D-bucket) as
  "router picked the wrong primitive for these problems."
- **"Steering as router" rather than "steering as fixed vector"** —
  generalizes ITI (2306.03341, status: CONTRADICTED) and CAA
  (2312.06681, pending) into an input-conditional dispatch. Our P10v2
  direction-rotation finding (DoM rotates during generation) is
  consistent with this: the rotation may be the *router* selecting
  different primitives at different generation positions.
- **Strength-bounded intervention (αmax=2.0)** — explicit
  conservatism on activation perturbation; complements our (un-tested)
  assumption that DoM-direction steering is well-behaved at moderate
  scale.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Vectors: K=6 K-means centroids, L2-normalized, avg cos <0.1.
- Router: bottleneck MLP, 5M params (<0.1% of base).
- Selection: Gumbel-Sigmoid (train) → threshold 0.7 (inference).
- Strength: bounded head, αmax=2.0.
- Injection layer (Qwen2.5): 20, 25, 40; middle layers > edges.
- Injection layer (Llama-3-8B): 13.
- Injection point: **last-token activation after prefill, layer-l only,
  single shot**, propagates through subsequent layers via residual
  stream.
- Training: SFT (200 oracle examples) → GRPO RL.
- Reward: accuracy 1/0; KL coefficient 0.001.
- Headline gains: 3.4–6.5% avg zero-shot accuracy lift; 2–3× token
  efficiency vs CoT; MATH (Qwen2.5-7B) 53.3% (RISER) vs 51.5% (CoT)
  vs 47.1% (CAA) vs 49.8% (CAST).
- No code link visible from abstract/HTML scrape (would need full PDF).

### H-269: L19 prefill DoM is one direction in a K≈6 input-conditional library, not the single correctness axis
**Priority:** HIGH
**Motivated by:** 2601.09269 + F-9 (CoE redundancy claim)
**Test:** K=6 K-means on cached P11-H100 1.5B prefill-L19 activations.
Fit a 6-component mixture-of-linear-probes (router MLP picks 1-of-6 per
input). Compute OOF 5-fold AUROC. Compare against single-direction L19
DoM (0.7731). Threshold for confirmation: AUROC > 0.7831 (≥ +0.01).
~30 min CPU on cached NPZs.
**Requires:** CPU, sklearn K-means + a small PyTorch gating MLP, no new
data.
**Would change:** Confirm → F-9 wrong-side; CoE-60 is *not* redundant
with single-direction L19 DoM, and the right framing is "L19 contains a
small library of correctness primitives." Also strengthens F-5
(content-dependent breathing) by giving it a vector-library mechanism.
Reject → F-9 stands; the L19 signal is genuinely one-dimensional.
**Blocks:** P11-FE269; if confirmed, gates a per-cluster steering
experiment (a follow-on H-N tied to H-1).

## 2601.09269 — RISER: Orchestrating Latent Reasoning Skills for Adaptive Activation Steering (Ye et al., 2026)

**Relevance:** RISER builds a K=6 library of CAA-extracted reasoning
vectors and trains a lightweight (~5M-param) router MLP via SFT then
GRPO to compose them per-input, injecting once at the prefill last-token
activation at a middle layer (L20/25/40 on Qwen2.5; L13 on Llama-3-8B).
Reports +3.4-6.5pp avg zero-shot lift across MATH/GSM8K/GPQA/MMLU-Pro/
ARC-C/Ethics/TruthfulQA/BBH and 2-3× token efficiency vs CoT. This
overlaps three of our anchors at once: (a) the *layer*  question — they
favor layers deeper than our L19, (b) the *single-direction* question —
their K=6 library directly probes whether single-direction L19 DoM
captures the full input-conditional structure (F-9), and (c) the
*single-shot prefill* question — their one-shot injection at prefill-
last-token is the cheapest possible point against H-1's per-position
bank.

**Key claim we tested:** A composed library of 6 reasoning vectors
applied at a single middle-layer prefill position outperforms any
single fixed vector by 1.7-3.4pp.

support all three of P11-FE269 (K=6 mixture probe AUROC vs 0.7731), P11-
FE270 (L25 vs L19 AUROC), and P11-FE271 (single-shot prefill DoM
injection on MATH-500). Each is <1 day with cached data. Until those
land, the comparison is pre-registered, not adjudicated.
