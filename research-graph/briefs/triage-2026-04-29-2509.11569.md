# Triage brief — 2509.11569 — D²HScore: Reasoning-Aware Hallucination Detection via Semantic Breadth and Depth Analysis in LLMs

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11569
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM) at 7B scale.**
   F-9 says CoE-60's all-layer trajectory features collapse to a single-layer L19 DoM signal at 1.5B. D²HScore is *also* an all-layers construct (mean dispersion over L layers + drift between consecutive layer centroids), but the paper reports it **beats CoE-R / CoE-C baselines** on GSM8K with Qwen1.5-7B-Instruct (74.48 AUROC) and on TheoremQA (75.88).
   *Mechanism*: D²HScore swaps CoE's full per-layer trajectory geometry for two scalar summaries (centroid-distance dispersion, consecutive-layer drift) — a strictly weaker representation. If a *weaker* multi-layer summary still beats CoE on math reasoning at 7B, the F-9 collapse story (single layer ≈ all layers) cannot generalize beyond Qwen-2.5-1.5B.
   *Falsifiable test*: extract D²HScore on our P11 H100 cached 7B activations (Qwen-2.5-7B, 1024-tok labels) and compute MATH-500 AUROC. If D²HScore_AUROC > L19-DoM_AUROC at 7B, F-9 is contradicted at scale.

2. **Refutes F-2 (supervised prefill L19 DoM is the winning correctness predictor) on a label-free axis.**
   F-2's headline AUROC 0.7731 is *supervised* (labels needed to fit the DoM direction). D²HScore is *training-free and label-free* yet reaches 74.48 AUROC on GSM8K (Qwen1.5-7B). At matched architecture and a comparable math benchmark, a label-free score in the same numerical neighborhood weakens F-2's "the prefill direction is special" narrative.
   *Mechanism*: if attention-weighted layer-centroid dispersion captures most of the discriminative signal, the L19-specific DoM may just be a supervised collapse of the same all-layer geometric variance, not a privileged direction.
   *Falsifiable test*: replicate D²HScore on our cached P11 1.5B per-layer NPZs at the prefill position; compare AUROC to F-2's 0.7731. If D²HScore ≥ 0.75 with no labels, F-2 needs a "supervised lift over geometry" subclaim or it's just relabeling label-free dispersion.

3. **Refutes F-4 (asymmetric collapse — correct trajectories collapse harder) on directional sign.**
   F-4 claims correct trajectories *collapse* (low PR) more aggressively than incorrect at the final token. D²HScore's headline finding implies the opposite directionality at the population level: **higher intra-layer dispersion correlates with hallucination**, i.e. wrong outputs spread *more* in representation space. Their dispersion is the mean L2 distance of attention-selected token reps from the layer centroid — closely analogous to PR-style spread.
   *Mechanism*: if D²HScore's dispersion sign is opposite to the F-4 PR-collapse sign, our P11 collapse story is either (a) layer-position confounded (final-token vs averaged) or (b) sample-orientation confounded (per-trajectory dispersion vs per-problem PR).
   *Falsifiable test*: compute D²HScore-style intra-layer dispersion on cached P11 1.5B NPZs at final token, separately for correct vs incorrect K=1 generations on MATH-500. If correct trajectories have *higher* dispersion (matching D²HScore), F-4's sign is reversed and the asymmetric-collapse claim flips to "asymmetric spread."

4. **Refutes F-7 (D-bucket has distinctive single-layer collective signature).**
   F-7 attributes the D-bucket pathology (K=1 right, K=8 wrong) to a layer-localized geometric signature. D²HScore averages dispersion + drift across **all layers** with no layer-specificity ablation that recovers single-layer dominance, yet still discriminates on math. If a uniformly-averaged signal works, the L19 single-layer privilege in F-7 may be a bias-variance artifact of small-N D-bucket fitting.
   *Falsifiable test*: compute D²HScore on cached P11 1.5B NPZs and check whether it discriminates B-bucket vs D-bucket as well as the L19 prefill DoM does. If yes, single-layer specificity is not load-bearing.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Intra-Layer Dispersion** — mean L2 distance from per-layer centroid over attention-selected key tokens; averaged across layers.
  *Replication cost*: ~1h CPU on cached P11 per-layer NPZs (1.5B and 7B).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — direct test of F-4 sign and F-9 redundancy at 7B.

- **Inter-Layer Drift** — L2 distance between consecutive-layer centroids of attention-selected tokens; averaged across layer pairs.
  *Replication cost*: ~1h CPU on cached per-layer NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — first measurement of "trajectory speed" through depth on our data; complements CoE-60.

- **Attention-guided key-token selection** — top-k tokens by mean over heads of the final token's attention weights to each preceding position; optimal k≈0.4–0.5 (Fig 5 ablation).
  *Replication cost*: ~30min CPU per checkpoint; we already cache attention rollouts in P11 Stage 4a.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — this is a cleaner alternative to the post-answer-newline (PANL) heuristic in H-18 and may directly substitute for it.

- **Joint dispersion+drift score** — sum or normalized combination of the two; simple linear / per-feature scaling.
  *Replication cost*: trivial once both are computed.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — head-to-head against single-layer L19 DoM.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Breadth × Depth" decomposition.** Within-layer (horizontal, breadth) vs across-layer (vertical, depth) representational dynamics as orthogonal axes of correctness signal. Intersects F-1 (breathing — temporal/horizontal) + F-3 (prefill/final orthogonality — vertical) but reframes them along *layer* axis rather than *token-position* axis, suggesting a 2D layer×position grid we have not formally explored.
- **Reasoning-aware hallucination detection.** They explicitly target reasoning benchmarks (GSM8K, TheoremQA, MGSM) rather than QA factuality, claiming the reasoning chain itself produces detectable geometric distortions. Aligns with our MATH-500 emphasis but argues the signal is in *all layers averaged*, not in one privileged layer.
- **Training-free + label-free as a hard constraint.** Deliberately excludes all probes — sets a methodological bar D²HScore must clear without supervised lift. Our F-2/F-8 framework is supervised-lift-positive; D²HScore is the relevant null model.

## Datasets & benchmarks

- **GSM8K** — 8.5k grade-school math problems; HF (`gsm8k`); MIT-equivalent; **applicable: yes** — closer to MATH-500 than any benchmark we already use, and the only benchmark where their headline includes Qwen1.5-7B (74.48 AUROC).
- **TheoremQA** — ~800 STEM theorem-application problems; HF (`TIGER-Lab/TheoremQA`); CC-BY; **applicable: yes** — plausible OOD-math probe for F-2 generalization.
- **MMLU** — 57-task knowledge benchmark; HF; permissive; **applicable: marginal** — knowledge factuality, not reasoning trajectory.
- **Belebele** — multilingual reading comprehension, 122 languages; HF; CC-BY-SA; **applicable: no** — language coverage outside our scope.
- **MGSM** — multilingual GSM8K, 11 languages; HF; **applicable: marginal** — only useful if we ever run multilingual breathing.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- All-layer averaging (no per-layer ablation that selects a winning layer) — a direct contrast to our L19-priviledged story; worth recording as a baseline.
- Attention top-k optimal at k≈0.4–0.5 of tokens (Fig 5).
- Five models tested include **Qwen1.5-7B-Instruct** and **DeepSeek-distilled-Qwen-7B** — the latter overlaps with our P11-FE5 (Qwen-1.5B R1-Distill); they offer a 7B distilled point we don't have.
- Method is single-pass — no sampling, no temperature variation, no multiple decodes; cheaper than CoE-60 in wall-clock if we already cache per-layer activations.
- No code link surfaced from arxiv abs/html. Search GitHub by author "Yue Ding D2HScore" if running implementation.
