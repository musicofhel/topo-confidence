# Triage brief — 2605.05566 — Nonsense Helps: Prompt Space Perturbation Broadens Reasoning Exploration

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05566
Triaged: 2026-05-17

## Refutations

1. **Refutes H-553 (prefill DoM fragile to surface-form perturbations):** LoPE shows that prepending 100–300 random Lorem Ipsum tokens to MATH-500 prompts does NOT destroy the model's question comprehension — their t-SNE analysis (Figure 6b) demonstrates that near-natural perturbations cluster tightly with unperturbed question representations. If the same holds for L19 prefill activations in Qwen-2.5-1.5B, the DoM direction may be *robust* to such perturbations rather than fragile. **Falsifiable by:** extracting L19 prefill activations from Lorem-perturbed MATH-500 prompts and measuring cos(DoM_perturbed, DoM_original) and AUROC shift.

2. **Refutes F-7 / H-708 (D-bucket geometric signature caused by GRPO gradient void):** The paper identifies the zero-advantage problem — when all G=8 rollouts fail, GRPO produces zero training signal. LoPE resolves 50 uniquely-solvable problems that pure resampling cannot. If D-bucket problems are exactly the zero-advantage set, F-7's "distinctive collective geometric signature" may be a trivially-explained GRPO training artifact (never-updated parameters) rather than a genuine geometric structure of the representation space. **Falsifiable by:** checking overlap between MATH-500 problems that Qwen-2.5-1.5B always gets wrong (K=8 pass@8=0) and the D-bucket membership set; if overlap >80%, the D-bucket signature is likely a training-void artifact.

3. **Refutes F-5 (breathing is content-dependent, not AR-mechanics):** LoPE demonstrates that content-irrelevant prefix tokens (pure Lorem Ipsum noise) shift the model's exploration enough to solve previously unsolvable problems — implying that AR-mechanical effects of prefix context (entropy shift, attention redistribution) matter independently of semantic content. If Lorem prefixes shift dimensional breathing patterns despite being semantically void, F-5's "content-dependent not AR-mechanics" conclusion is too strong. **Falsifiable by:** extracting L19 dimensional breathing (participation ratio trajectories) for identical problems with vs without Lorem prefix; if PR curves differ significantly for the nonsense-prefix condition, AR-mechanics do drive breathing.

4. **Refutes H-759 (DoM robust to synonym-level perturbations):** H-759 hypothesizes robustness to *synonym-level* perturbations. LoPE uses much stronger perturbations (100-300 random Latin tokens) that still preserve model comprehension (Figure 6a). If DoM is robust to synonyms but NOT robust to Lorem prefixing, the robustness has a narrow boundary. Conversely, if DoM is robust to BOTH, then H-759 is confirmed but the signal's information-theoretic grounding becomes harder to explain (how can a direction be simultaneously predictive and insensitive to input distribution shifts?). **Falsifiable by:** measuring AUROC of L19 DoM on Lorem-perturbed vs original prompts.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (AUROC 0.7731):** LoPE trains on Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen2.5-Math-7B — both close relatives of our Qwen-2.5-1.5B target. Their training-time exploration metrics quantify per-question success rates that directly map onto DoM's correctness prediction. If LoPE-trained checkpoints have a different DoM direction than standard-GRPO checkpoints, this tests H-707/H-774 (DoM direction rotates under different RL objectives).

- **F-7 (D-bucket collective signature):** The zero-advantage analysis is a direct operational definition of what we call D-bucket — problems the model systematically fails on. Their Venn diagram (Figure 2) showing 50 problems uniquely solvable via Lorem perturbation suggests D-bucket membership is mutable under distribution shift, which would update F-7's framing from "stable geometric signature" to "training-regime artifact."

- **F-5 (breathing is content-dependent):** The token-level entropy analysis (Figure 3) quantifies exactly how Lorem prefixing shifts the output distribution: it eliminates the near-zero entropy spike and right-shifts the distribution. This is a measurable AR-mechanical effect of semantically-null content, bearing directly on F-5's claim.

- **H-708 (D-bucket from GRPO gradient void):** This paper essentially provides the training-dynamics mechanism that H-708 hypothesizes — zero-advantage ⟹ zero gradient ⟹ never-updated representation ⟹ stable but artifactual geometric cluster.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Lorem Perturbation for Exploration (LoPE)** — Prepend 100–300 randomly assembled Latin-vocabulary tokens to prompts during GRPO resampling, triggering alternate reasoning pathways when all original rollouts fail.
  *Replication cost*: Training requires 4×A100 for days. But the *inference-time perturbation* (just prepending Lorem tokens and re-running inference) is ~30min on our 2060 Super for 500 MATH-500 problems.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — the inference-time perturbation experiment (not the full GRPO training) is directly applicable to cached activations and fresh forward passes.

- **Perplexity-filtered perturbation selection** — Filter random Latin sequences to perplexity band 20–30 for optimal exploration without comprehension corruption.
  *Replication cost*: 10min CPU (just compute perplexity of candidate prefixes with our frozen model).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — trivial preprocessing step.

- **t-SNE representation clustering for perturbation impact** — Embed question representations with and without perturbation, verify tight clustering = preserved comprehension.
  *Replication cost*: 20min CPU on cached activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — we already have the activation extraction pipeline.

- **Token-level entropy/perplexity distribution analysis** — Measure per-token entropy and perplexity across full generated responses to characterize exploration vs exploitation.
  *Replication cost*: 1–2h GPU (requires fresh forward passes during generation).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — requires generation, not just prefill extraction.

- **Off-policy importance-weighted advantage shaping** — f(ρ)=ρ/(ρ+γ) with γ=0.1 to down-weight high-ratio off-policy samples in GRPO.
  *Replication cost*: requires GRPO training infrastructure.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — we don't do GRPO training locally.

## Approaches & framings

- **Zero-advantage as a diagnostic category:** Problems where pass@G=0 form a structurally distinct training-dynamics class. This reframes our D-bucket (F-7) from a geometric observation to a training-dynamics consequence — the "gradient void" framing.

- **Prompt-space vs parameter-space exploration:** The paper argues that input perturbation is cheaper and more targeted than temperature increases or parameter noise for exploration. This frames our prefill geometry work: if the representation space is smooth enough that Lorem prefix ≈ small perturbation in activation space (their Figure 6b), then prefill DoM might be insensitive to input noise — a testable prediction.

- **Near-natural perturbation optimality:** There's a sweet spot (perplexity 20–100) where perturbations are out-of-distribution enough to trigger exploration but not so extreme as to corrupt comprehension. This gives a principled way to titrate perturbation strength for robustness testing of our DoM probe (H-553, H-759).

## Implementation details worth capturing

- G=8 rollouts per question, G'=24 resampling rollouts — operational definition of "when to declare zero-advantage"
- Lorem vocabulary: 63 Latin words from python-lorem package
- Perturbation length: uniform(100, 300) tokens
- Perplexity sweet spot: 20–30 for filtered Latin
- Training framework: EasyR1 (Zheng et al., 2025)
- Temperature 1.0 for rollout, 0.6 for evaluation
- Policy shaping: f(ρ)=ρ/(ρ+γ), γ=0.1
