# Triage brief — 2602.10346 — Geometry-Aware Decoding with Wasserstein-Regularized Truncation and Mass Penalties for Large Language Models

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10346
Triaged: 2026-05-06

## Refutations

**1. Refutation of F-8 (Selective-prediction via prefill DoM at 50% coverage):**
Top-W decoding lifts Qwen2.5-3B GSM8K accuracy from 72.55% (Top-H, T=1.0) to 75.99% without any abstention mechanism — a gain of ~3.4pp by decoding alone. If the problems on which our DoM-based abstainer declines to answer are precisely those that Top-W can recover via better sampling, then selective prediction's value proposition shrinks: the model was capable all along, just poorly sampled. This would fire if DoM-abstained problems have disproportionately high Top-W recovery rates.
- **Mechanism:** DoM identifies problems where the model "hesitates" in hidden-state space; Top-W shows that hesitation can be resolved at decode time without abstention.
- **Falsifiable test:** Re-generate answers to MATH-500 under Top-W (λ=2.2, β=2.8, T=1.0) on Qwen-2.5-1.5B. Measure accuracy on the subset of problems where DoM < threshold (i.e., problems the selective predictor would abstain on). If Top-W accuracy on that subset exceeds greedy accuracy by >5pp, the selective-prediction mechanism is weaker than claimed.

**2. Refutation of F-2 (L19 prefill DoM AUROC 0.7731):**
The paper demonstrates that Qwen2.5-3B accuracy ranges from 9.10% (Top-p, T=2.0) to 75.99% (Top-W, T=1.0) — an 8.5× range — purely from decoding strategy. Since DoM is extracted at prefill (before generation), it cannot account for this decode-time variance. This means DoM is predicting something like "ease of correct sampling" rather than "presence of model knowledge." If a problem has correct knowledge in its prefill representation but fails under greedy/top-p because the correct continuation is geometrically isolated in logit space (exactly the scenario Top-W addresses), then DoM's predictive power may be conflated with output-distribution geometry rather than reflecting deep "understanding."
- **Mechanism:** DoM conflates model-knowledge with decode-tractability; Top-W deconfounds them by making decode-tractability uniformly high.
- **Falsifiable test:** Extract DoM on MATH-500 (Qwen-2.5-1.5B) and generate with both greedy and Top-W. Compute AUROC of DoM predicting correctness under each decoding regime. If AUROC drops substantially (>0.03) under Top-W decoding, DoM was partly tracking decode-difficulty rather than knowledge.

**3. Refutation of F-5 (Breathing is content-dependent, not AR-mechanics):**
Top-W demonstrates that output-distribution geometry (token-embedding distances in logit space) is causally relevant to generation quality. If the geometry that matters most sits in the output distribution rather than in the residual stream, then our residual-stream breathing pattern (F-1, F-5) may be an epiphenomenon of output-logit-space structure leaking backward through LayerNorm. The causal arrow would run: output-distribution shape → residual-stream geometry, not vice versa.
- **Mechanism:** Residual-stream geometry at L19 is an artifact of the model "pre-shaping" its logit-space distribution; the real signal is in output space.
- **Falsifiable test:** Compute correlation between L19 breathing amplitude and the Wasserstein distance W_1(p_greedy, p_TopW) at the first generated token. If breathing amplitude is strongly predicted by logit-space distortion (r > 0.5), this supports the epiphenomenon interpretation.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (DoM AUROC 0.7731):** Top-W uses diagonal-whitened Mahalanobis distance in token-embedding space. Our DoM operates in residual-stream space without explicit whitening. The paper's whitening procedure (Appendix A: normalize embeddings, compute per-coordinate variance, scale by (Φ_ℓ + ε)^{-1/2}) is directly applicable to our 1536-d L19 activations as a preprocessing step before computing DoM.

- **F-8 (Selective prediction at 50% coverage, 71.6% answered accuracy):** The paper's results on GSM8K show that geometry-aware decoding achieves similar accuracy gains to abstention. At T=1.0 on Qwen2.5-3B, Top-W reaches 75.99% vs. 75.97% for Top-H. Our selective predictor achieves 71.6% at 50% coverage — but it was measured under greedy decoding. The relevant comparison is whether DoM-based abstention stacks with Top-W decoding or is largely redundant with it.

- **F-10 (PH-null: topology not distinguishable from Gaussian null):** The paper uses Wasserstein-1 distance (earth mover's) which is a 1st-order distributional measure — exactly the kind that F-10 shows works (covariance spectrum gives 0.7928 AUROC), while higher-order topological summaries fail. Top-W's success with W_1 aligns with F-10's conclusion that first-order geometry suffices.

- **H-5 (Prefill signal encodes problem familiarity vs decomposability):** If Top-W disproportionately helps on decomposable-but-hard-to-sample problems vs. truly unfamiliar problems, this could help disambiguate what prefill DoM actually tracks.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Diagonal-whitened Mahalanobis distance** — normalize embeddings to unit norm, subtract mean, scale each coordinate by inverse std. Yields an isotropic ground metric for OT computations.
  *Replication cost*: 5min CPU on cached activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly applicable as a preprocessing step before computing DoM/PC1 on our cached L19 activations.

- **Distance-to-set potential (f(i) = -dist(i, S))** — for a reference set S, compute the 1-Lipschitz potential as negative distance to nearest point in S. Combined with log-probability gives a joint score.
  *Replication cost*: 10min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — set S = centroids of correct-sample activations, compute distance-to-set for each sample, test as a correctness predictor.

- **Alternating f-step/S-step optimization** — iterate between updating a feasible potential (geometry) and optimizing the subset (prefix scan). Converges in 2-3 iterations.
  *Replication cost*: 15min CPU for analogous procedure on 500 samples.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — the alternating optimization is interesting as a way to select an "informative subset" of activation dimensions, but the vocabulary-scale framing doesn't directly transfer.

- **Wasserstein-1 (Earth Mover's Distance) under learned metric** — W_1 between two distributions over a metric space defined by learned embeddings. Used here for output distributions but applicable to any pair of distributions over a metric space.
  *Replication cost*: 20min CPU (exact W_1 via linear programming on 500 samples with 1536-d Euclidean ground metric).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — compute W_1 between correct and incorrect activation distributions as an alternative separability measure to AUROC.

## Approaches & framings

- **Distribution shaping as truncation optimization:** Viewing decoding as selecting an optimal subset S of a distribution to retain, subject to faithfulness + entropy + mass constraints. This framing maps to our "which dimensions of the hidden state carry the signal" question — we're also implicitly selecting a subspace (PC1, top eigenvectors) that preserves the correctness signal while discarding noise.

- **Unification of heuristics via metric specialization:** Top-W reduces to Top-k (uniform metric, cardinality budget) and Top-H (uniform metric, entropy constraint). Analogously, our DoM (project onto one direction) vs. cov-spectrum (project onto multiple eigenvectors) vs. full-1536-d logistic regression might be unifiable as different "metric" choices in an optimal-subspace framework.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Default hyperparameters: λ=2.2, β=2.8, topm=1200, altiters=3.
- Diagonal whitening: ε=small (not specified, likely 1e-8) for numerical stability.
- Distance computation restricted to top-m=1200 candidates for efficiency (Theorem I.1 proves this is exact under mild conditions).
- Code released: https://github.com/arashgholami/top-w-decoding
- ~5.4% slower than Top-p/Min-p/Top-H in wall-clock time.
- Models tested include Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct — same family as our Qwen-2.5-1.5B.
