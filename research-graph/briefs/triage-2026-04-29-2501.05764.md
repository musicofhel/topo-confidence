# Triage brief — 2501.05764 — Controlling Large Language Models Through Concept Activation Vectors

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05764
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes the strong form of H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy) if their per-input adaptive epsilon recipe fails on correctness.**
   GCAV demonstrates that *per-input adaptive* steering strength (Eq 6: `ε = (s₀ − b − wᵀe) / ||w||`) is what makes activation steering work — fixed-c steering (ActAdd) is insufficient. Mechanism: their Llama-2-7b-chat sentiment success rate of 0.5566 vs ActAdd's 0.4240 isolates the per-input-strength term as the lever. Falsifiable: implement GCAV's exact closed-form ε formula on our prefill L19 DoM (which IS a logistic-regression CAV with `w = w_DoM`, `b = b_DoM`, `s₀ = sigmoid⁻¹(0.5)`) and steer Qwen-2.5-1.5B at the prefill last-token position on MATH-500. If MATH-500 K=1 accuracy does not exceed the 48.6% baseline by ≥2pp, the failure mode is *not* "steering needs adaptive strength" but something deeper — refuting H-1's working assumption that the prefill DoM is a causal correctness vector.

2. **Refutes F-9's redundancy claim (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM) via multi-layer steering surfacing additional signal.**
   GCAV's layer-selection experiment (Fig 3) shows control success rate peaks across a *band* of layers (10-15, 15-20) and that classifier test accuracy is non-trivially distributed across layers, not collapsed to one. Mechanism: if multi-layer CAV stacking adds independent control variance over single-layer L19, then the residual stream encodes correctness signal at multiple layers in non-trivially different subspaces — which would contradict F-9's "single-layer L19 DoM is sufficient" framing for *causal* (not just predictive) intervention. Falsifiable: train CAV-style classifiers at L17, L19, L21, L23 on our prefill activations; compare AUROC of the L19-only probe (0.7731) against an SLSQP-weighted ensemble across these four layers. If ensemble AUROC ≥ 0.79 and the marginal gain over L19-alone exceeds the bootstrap CI of L19, F-9's redundancy claim is wrong.

3. **Refutes the interpretation of F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046 → orthogonal directions encode "different things").**
   GCAV constructs the same logistic-regression-normal CAV at every layer and finds they all encode *the same concept* despite occupying nominally different subspaces — geometric orthogonality across positions/layers does not imply semantic disjointness. Mechanism: the CAV training procedure projects out a single linear direction; whether it is "the" toxicity direction or one of many is not constrained, so two near-orthogonal CAVs can represent the same concept up to noise/redundancy. Falsifiable: compute the fraction of correctness-prediction AUROC explained by the prefill_DoM ⊕ final_DoM joint subspace vs each individually; if joint AUROC is *not* materially higher than max(prefill, final), then the orthogonality is geometric-not-semantic and F-3's "different things" reading is over-strong.

4. **Refutes H-1's implicit assumption that correctness-as-a-concept is on equal footing with surface concepts (toxicity, sentiment) for activation steering.**
   GCAV explicitly only validates on Llama-2-7b/-chat for *surface* attributes (toxicity, sentiment, topic, formality) — concepts that are token-distribution-shifting, not problem-decomposing. Mechanism: math correctness is not a property of the token distribution given a fixed input, but of the latent multi-step computation. Surface-attribute steering can succeed by tilting per-token logits; correctness steering would have to alter chain-of-thought structure. Falsifiable: run GCAV's exact protocol with "correct" vs "incorrect" prompt pairs (CAV-Output style — only training on prompts whose outputs were verified correct/wrong) on Qwen-2.5-1.5B and check K=1 MATH-500 accuracy. If ≤ baseline + 1pp, this refutes the implicit "correctness is a concept like toxicity" assumption underlying H-1, and reframes F-2's 0.7731 AUROC as *prediction-only*, not *intervention-amenable*.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)**: GCAV's CAV is mathematically identical to our DoM — logistic regression weight normal direction (Eq 2: `v = w / ||w||`). The CAV classifier test accuracy plays the same role as our 0.7731 AUROC. F-2 therefore *is* a CAV result for the concept "correctness", reframed.
- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy)**: GCAV provides the missing piece — a closed-form per-input adaptive ε (Eq 6/7). The current H-1 plan can be sharpened to use GCAV's adaptive-ε recipe rather than fixed steering, which prior work (ActAdd, 2308.10248) shows is the weaker variant.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046)**: GCAV's multi-layer construction shows that *layer-different CAVs can encode the same concept*. Position-different CAVs (prefill vs final) may be analogous; F-3's orthogonality should be re-tested for *semantic* disjointness via joint-subspace classifier AUROC, not just geometric cos.
- **H-12 (Semantic-entropy probes label-free)**: GCAV-Input vs GCAV-Output (filter by output-realized concept vs prompt-class) is the same axis as label-free vs supervised probes — GCAV-Output requires verified outputs (= our correctness labels), GCAV-Input is more like SEPs. The 0.0879 vs 0.1231 toxicity gap on Llama-2-7b-chat anchors how much label quality matters.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with L19 DoM)**: GCAV layer-selection (Fig 3) provides a methodologically clean test — train CAV at every Qwen-2.5-1.5B layer, measure both probe AUROC *and* causal control success, see if causal lift is strictly L19-localized.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Logistic-regression CAV (Eq 1, 2)** — train binary classifier `P_d(e) = sigmoid(wᵀe + b)` on activations from contrastive prompt pairs; CAV `v = w / ||w||`.
  *Replication cost*: trivial. We already have this — our prefill L19 DoM is a CAV.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — already running.

- **Closed-form per-input adaptive steering strength (Eq 6, 7)** — `ε = I(P_d(e) < p_0) · (s₀ − b − wᵀe) / ||w||` where `s₀ = sigmoid⁻¹(p_0)`. Steering only fires when the probe says the input is on the wrong side, and strength scales with distance-to-threshold.
  *Replication cost*: 1 evening on cached prefill activations; the formula uses only existing classifier params (`w`, `b`) plus a target `p_0`.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — primary FE below (P11-FE83). This is the missing piece in H-1.

- **GCAV-Output filtering** — train CAV only on prompts whose generations *actually* contained the target concept, not just by prompt-class label.
  *Replication cost*: free for us — our F-2 already trains on correctness *outcome* labels (was the answer right?), which is the GCAV-Output equivalent.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: already done; explicitly note the alignment in PAPER_INDEX.

- **Multi-concept SLSQP simultaneous steering (Eq 8, 9)** — solve constrained LP for `{ε_i, δ_j}` over multiple concept vectors, using SciPy SLSQP.
  *Replication cost*: 30 min once we have multiple correctness-related CAVs (e.g., per-bucket A/B/C/D direction).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — interesting for combining a "correctness" CAV with a "non-D-bucket" CAV (F-7).

- **Layer-group steering** — apply the CAV simultaneously across a contiguous band of layers (e.g., 10-15) rather than one.
  *Replication cost*: trivial; just stack scaled-vector additions.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes, but only if single-layer L19 fails — to avoid over-fitting to GCAV's framing.

- **Layer-selection by classifier test accuracy** — pick steering layers where the per-layer CAV classifier has highest held-out accuracy (Fig 3, peak after L10 on Llama-2-7b-chat).
  *Replication cost*: 2h on cached pathway11_h100 activations (we already have all 28 layers' L-only DoM AUROCs from P8).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes, useful confirmation of L19 choice for Qwen.

- **Perspective API for toxicity, SiEBERT for sentiment** — out-of-domain for our work but useful template for using *external* classifiers as evaluators.
  *Replication cost*: not applicable.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — out of scope.

## Approaches & framings

- **"CAV as classifier normal direction"** — recasts our DoM as a member of a method family with established theoretical grounding (Kim et al. 2018 TCAV). Useful for positioning F-2/F-3 in publication: "DoM is a CAV; F-2 is a CAV result; F-3 is a multi-position CAV finding."
- **"Steering strength as distance-to-decision-boundary"** — geometrically clean reframing: ε is the projection of (decision threshold − current activation logit) onto the CAV. Maps directly onto our problem of steering correctness probes.
- **"CTG via activation engineering vs prompt-engineering vs decoding-side"** — three-way taxonomy (their Related Work). Our work sits on the activation-engineering arm but for *correctness* not *style/safety*; intersects with H-1.
- **"Granular vs coarse control"** — they emphasize per-input granularity as the differentiator vs ActAdd's fixed-c. Mirrors our F-2-vs-F-7 distinction (population-level AUROC vs per-bucket structure).

## Datasets & benchmarks

- **RealToxicityPrompts** (Gehman 2020) — 1000 toxic + 1000 random prompts, public HF, accessible. Applicable? **no** — not math correctness; useful only as a comparison anchor for "how much steering moved things on a *surface* concept" relative to our correctness target.
- **IMDB negative reviews** (Maas 2011) — 1000 negative reviews truncated to 32 tokens, public HF, accessible. Applicable? **no** — sentiment domain, but the per-input ε protocol can be ported.
- **GPT-4o-generated topic/style prompts** — 100 prompt pairs per concept, generated on-demand, free. Applicable? **no** — but the recipe (use a strong external LLM to generate contrastive prompt pairs for a concept) is portable to "correct vs incorrect MATH-500 problem framings."

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Base models: Llama-2-7b and Llama-2-7b-chat (32-layer architecture)
- Classifier: logistic regression (no regularization specified; assume sklearn default)
- CAV training set: 100 contrastive prompt pairs (small!)
- Steering strength range in figures: `c = 2 × 10^k` for k=0..3 (ActAdd) and `p₀ = 1·10^k` for k=1..6 (GCAV target probability)
- Multi-concept solver: `scipy.optimize.minimize` with SLSQP
- Perplexity computed via Llama-2-13b-chat (their evaluation model)
- Toxicity: Perspective API (gated by Google Cloud)
- Sentiment: SiEBERT (HF: `siebert/sentiment-roberta-large-english`)
- Formality: `s-nlp/roberta-base-formality-ranker` (Babakov 2023)
- Layer-group steering reported: 0-5, 5-10, 10-15, 15-20, 20-25, 25-30 (six groups)
- No public code link given in the AAAI 2025 PDF; would need to email authors
