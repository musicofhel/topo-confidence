# Triage brief — 2312.06681 — Steering Llama 2 via Contrastive Activation Addition

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06681
Triaged: 2026-04-29

Authors: Panickssery, Gabrieli, Schulz, Tong, Hubinger, Turner. arXiv 2023 (v1
Dec 2023, latest July 2024). Anthropic / Harvard / Göttingen / CHAI.

## Refutations

1. **CAA's "single static vector applied at every post-prompt token position
   works" finding contradicts F-3's claim that prefill and final-token DoMs are
   geometrically distinct (cos 0.046).** Mechanism: CAA generates a single MD
   vector at the answer-letter token and adds it uniformly at every generated
   position from instruction-end onward — and reports robust effects across
   seven Llama-2 behaviors plus generalisation to free-form generation. If F-3's
   orthogonality is a *general* property of the residual stream, the steering
   vector should partially cancel its own effect across positions; that CAA
   does not see this collapse implies prefill/final orthogonality is either
   correctness-specific or an artefact of how F-3 sampled "prefill" vs "final"
   tokens. Falsifiable test: replicate CAA's *position-uniform* application of
   the prefill-DoM correctness vector on Qwen-2.5-1.5B MATH-500 (multiplier ±1
   at L19, all post-prompt positions). If the resulting accuracy delta matches
   prefill-only application, F-3's geometric significance is overstated.

2. **CAA finds optimal steering layer at ~⅓ depth (L13/32 = 41% on Llama-2 7B,
   L14-15/40 = 36% on 13B) and reports a sharp drop-off in steering effect at
   layer 17.** F-2 elects L19/28 (≈68% depth) on Qwen-2.5-1.5B as the canonical
   prefill DoM probe layer. Mechanism: CAA's "concept emerges around ⅓ of
   layers, gets consumed for downstream computation by ~50% depth" trajectory
   predicts a *correctness* MD-vector AUROC peak well below L19 on Qwen-2.5-1.5B
   too. If a clean layer sweep shows AUROC peaks at L9-L12 and tails by L17-L19,
   F-2's L19 choice is post-hoc / Qwen-quirky and the headline 0.7731 number is
   not at the optimal layer. Falsifiable test: full per-layer prefill DoM AUROC
   sweep on cached pathway11_h100 1024-tok activations across all 28 Qwen-2.5
   layers — if max is at L≤12 or L17-18 and L19 is not the argmax within ±0.005,
   the layer choice loses provenance.

3. **CAA reports MMLU is essentially unchanged (0.59-0.65 across all behaviors
   and multipliers) under multiplier ±1 steering at the optimal layer — i.e.
   the steering vector does *not* perturb general capability/correctness.** This
   is a strong null against H-1 ("per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500
   accuracy"). Mechanism: behavioural steering vectors and a putative correctness
   vector live at the same depth (~⅓-⅔ of the network); if CAA's behavioural
   vectors at this depth do not move MMLU, an MD-extracted "correct answer
   letter vs incorrect answer letter" steering vector applied to Qwen-2.5-1.5B
   may equally fail to move MATH-500 accuracy — making the prefill DoM a *probe
   target* (diagnostic) rather than a *cause* (intervenable). Falsifiable test:
   construct an MD steering vector from the correct/incorrect Qwen MATH-500
   labels at L19, apply it CAA-style at multiplier ±1 to a held-out 100-problem
   subset, measure MATH-500 K=1 accuracy delta. Null delta refutes H-1 and
   downgrades F-2 to "diagnostic-only".

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)** — CAA *is* the canonical Mean
  Difference (MD) extraction protocol our prefill DoM implements. Their
  validation that 50-100 contrast pairs suffice for behavioural directions
  upper-bounds the variance budget for our 500-problem MD on MATH-500 — i.e.,
  the 0.7731 cannot easily be blamed on under-sampling.

- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046)** — CAA explicitly applies *one*
  vector at *every* token position post-prompt, contrary to a strict reading
  of F-3 that demands position-specific vectors. See Refutation 1.

- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM)** — CAA's finding that
  single-layer MD captures the entire behaviour signal corroborates F-9's
  redundancy claim: stacking activations across layers gives no extra
  steering control beyond the optimal-layer MD.

- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500)** — CAA is the canonical
  baseline against which "per-position" must be measured. If CAA-style uniform
  application of the correctness MD vector moves MATH-500 K=1 accuracy by ≥1pp,
  H-1 is partially confirmed; if it does not, H-1 must demonstrate that
  *position-specific* application beats *uniform* application — a strictly
  higher bar. Connects to P10-FE1 (steering arms a/b/c).

- **H-8 (prefill direction stable across training checkpoints)** — CAA Section
  8.3 finds steering vectors transfer between Llama-2 7B base and chat models
  (cosine peaks 0.6-0.8 in layers 7-15). Direct evidence that MD steering
  directions survive RLHF; predicts H-8 will replicate on Qwen-2.5-Math-1.5B
  vs Qwen-2.5-1.5B-Instruct.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Mean Difference (MD) steering vector** — average residual-stream
  activation difference between (positive, negative) prompt pairs at the
  answer-letter token, extracted at a single layer.
  *Replication cost*: 5min/vector on a single GPU; we already do this for
  prefill DoM.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — already implemented; CAA gives us the
  canonical protocol to compare against.

- **Letter-clustering vs behaviour-clustering PCA diagnostic** — project
  contrastive-pair activations to 2D PCA; if both letter axis and behaviour
  axis separate cleanly, the dataset can yield a steering vector. Diagnoses
  whether a dataset is degenerate (only letter signal, no behaviour signal).
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached prefill activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — quick sanity check that "correct vs
  incorrect" on MATH-500 has both letter and behaviour clustering at L19.

- **Layer sweep with ±1 multiplier to find optimal steering layer** — CAA's
  recipe for choosing the steering layer is "fix multiplier at ±1, sweep
  layers, take the one with largest behavioural delta". Different from our
  current "L19 picked by AUROC".
  *Replication cost*: 8h H100 (sweep 28 layers × ±1 multiplier × 100 MATH-500
  problems).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct test for Refutation 2.

- **Inter-layer steering-vector cosine matrix** — measure cos(MD_layer_i,
  MD_layer_j) to check whether the direction "converges" through depth. CAA
  finds slow decline in late layers, fast in early.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached prefill activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — informs H-17 (RoPE-mechanical rotation).

- **Token-wise dot-product visualisation** — colour each generated token by
  cos(activation_t, steering_vector) to see *where* in the response the
  concept is "active". Reveals semantically-meaningful tokens (e.g. "I cannot
  help" lights up the refusal direction).
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU per problem on cached generation NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly probes H-13 (head-level
  attribution); could reveal whether prefill DoM lights up at the
  answer-newline token (relevant to H-18).

- **Base-vs-chat steering vector cosine comparison** — extract MD vector from
  base model and chat model at same layer, plot inter-layer cosine matrix.
  *Replication cost*: 4h H100 (extract from Qwen-2.5-1.5B base + Instruct).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct test of H-8.

- **CAA + prompting / + finetuning composability test** — apply steering on
  top of system prompts and supervised fine-tuning, measure incremental effect.
  *Replication cost*: 1 day H100.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: low priority for current pathway; useful if H-11
  (big-to-small distillation) ever runs.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Behaviour vector" as a position-stationary residual-stream object.**
  CAA presupposes that a single direction captures a behaviour at all
  post-prompt positions. Our F-3 disputes this for correctness specifically;
  their paper is the strongest single piece of evidence that the assumption
  *does* hold for behavioural axes (sycophancy, refusal, corrigibility).

- **MD vs PCA equivalence (citing Tigges et al. 2310.15154).** CAA cites that
  Mean Difference yields steering vectors statistically similar to PCA-based
  ones. Relevant when we compare prefill DoM (currently MD) to PCA-based
  alternatives — likely no major improvement to be expected from PCA.

- **Steering as diagnostic ↔ steering as causal intervention.** CAA's MMLU
  null lands them squarely in the "diagnostic" reading (CAA reports a property
  of internal representations, but routinely cannot perturb downstream
  capability). Our H-1 frames steering as causal; CAA's MMLU result is the
  closest published prior on this distinction.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Llama-2-7B-Chat optimal layer **13** (out of 32, 41%); Llama-2-13B-Chat
  optimal **14 or 15** (of 40, 35-37%).
- Steering effect drops sharply at **layer 17** and beyond — late-layer
  representations are "consumed".
- Multipliers in {−2, −1, 0, +1, +2} are the practical range; >|2| degrades
  generation quality (manual + GPT-4 rated).
- 50 held-out multiple-choice questions per behaviour for evaluation.
- Generation set sizes per behaviour given in Appendix E (typically 100-500
  contrastive pairs).
- Steering vectors are normalised across behaviours but **not across layers**
  (residual norm grows exponentially per Heimersheim & Turner; they do not
  rescale per layer to preserve the natural norm).
- Single 1×10⁻⁴ SGD lr, 1 epoch finetune baseline on 1000 examples = 10min on
  2× L40.
- Codebase: https://github.com/nrimsky/CAA (see Appendix A).
- HuggingFace `transformers` + raw PyTorch hooks for activation save/inject.

  Contrasts with CAA Figure 7 to test their "convergence in late layers"
  claim on a different model family.

- **Letter-vs-behaviour PCA diagnostic on MATH-500 prefill activations** —
  PCA(prefill_L19_activations) coloured by (a) correct-letter A/B/C/D and (b)
  correct/incorrect. If both axes are visible, our MD direction is on solid
  ground; if only the letter axis separates, the prefill DoM is a letter
  signal in disguise (refutation candidate).

- **Token-wise dot-product visualisation on a few MATH-500 generations** —
  for each step in a sampled generation, plot cos(activation_t, prefill_DoM).
  Look for spikes at answer-emission tokens; corroborates F-2 mechanism
  story or reveals it's diffuse.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2312.06681 — Steering Llama 2 via Contrastive Activation Addition (Panickssery, Gabrieli, Schulz, Tong, Hubinger, Turner, 2023)

**Relevance:** CAA is the canonical Mean Difference (MD) steering protocol that
the project's prefill-DoM extraction implements. It supplies (a) the dataset-
quality PCA diagnostic we currently lack, (b) the layer-sweep recipe for
choosing the steering layer, (c) the MMLU-style capability-preservation null
test, and (d) the strongest published prior on whether MD-extracted directions
can intervene on capability vs only diagnose it. CAA's design assumption — one
vector applied at every post-prompt token works — is directly at odds with
F-3's prefill/final orthogonality, making this paper the cleanest available
falsification target for F-3.

**Key claim we tested:** "A single MD-extracted residual-stream vector
applied uniformly at all post-prompt token positions modulates a target
behaviour while leaving general capability (MMLU) intact."

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — we have not yet (a) replicated the layer sweep
on Qwen-2.5-1.5B, (b) constructed a CAA-style correctness MD vector and
applied it position-uniformly, or (c) measured MATH-500 K=1 delta under such
an intervention. The paper is the trigger for P11-FE73, P11-FE74, P11-FE75,
P11-FE76, P11-FE77, P11-FE78 (above) which together would let us classify CAA
as REPLICATED (uniform CAA works on correctness too), CONTRADICTED (CAA-uniform
fails on correctness, supporting F-3), or PARTIALLY CONFIRMED (works on
behaviour-style probes but not on MATH-500 K=1 correctness).

**Related experiments:** P11-FE73, P11-FE74, P11-FE75, P11-FE76, P11-FE77,
P11-FE78, P10-FE1 (existing TRIGGERED MD-vector arm), P2-FE1 (existing
prefill-DoM re-derivation), H-1, H-67, H-68, H-69.

**Status:** TO TEST
