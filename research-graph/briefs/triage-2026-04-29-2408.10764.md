# Triage brief — 2408.10764 — Predicting Rewards Alongside Tokens: Non-disruptive Parameter Insertion for Efficient Inference Intervention in Large Language Model

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10764
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM is *better than* final-token, AUROC 0.7731 vs 0.7186) may be a 1-D probe-capacity artifact.** Otter trains a *multi-dimensional* expanded hidden subspace `h'_i` at every block whose final-layer projection predicts a calibration signal. The DoM is a 1-direction linear probe; if the prefill > final asymmetry collapses or even inverts when matched-capacity probes (Otter task head with several inserted dims) are trained at every position, then F-2's "prefill is uniquely strong" reading is a 1-D-projection effect, not a property of where correctness is encoded. **Mechanism:** higher-rank readout at the final token recovers signal that 1-D DoM throws away. **Falsifiable:** train Otter's MHA-only insertion (e.g. 4 added heads) on Qwen-2.5-1.5B with K=1 MATH-500 correctness as the regression target, then evaluate AUROC at prefill vs final token from the same trained head; if final-token AUROC ≥ prefill AUROC, F-2 reframes.

2. **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046, "directions are orthogonal") may be 1-D shadows of overlapping multi-D subspaces.** Otter's per-block extension means the *useful subspace* for correctness is by construction multi-dimensional. Two random low-D projections of overlapping high-D subspaces can have arbitrarily small cosine while their span overlap is large. **Mechanism:** principal-angles between the prefill and final-token correctness subspaces (rank ≥ 2) could be small even though their dominant 1-D directions are near-orthogonal. **Falsifiable:** SVD the per-position activations within correctness classes (P11-FE7 already proposes this) and measure subspace principal angles, not 1-D cosines. If the top-3 components from prefill and final share span ≥ 0.5, F-3's orthogonality interpretation is misleading.

3. **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM) implies a single layer carries all the linearly-decodable correctness signal — Otter's per-block insertion challenges this.** Otter inserts at every transformer layer, and ablations show non-trivial contribution from multiple layers (the per-block hidden-state extension is fed forward through all blocks). **Mechanism:** if Otter's *layer-budget* ablations on a correctness-prediction variant show monotone gains as more layers are made trainable past L19, single-layer L19 sufficiency is contradicted. **Falsifiable:** compare three Otter variants on MATH-500 K=1 correctness: (a) parameters inserted only at L19, (b) inserted at L0…L19, (c) inserted at all layers. If (b) or (c) ≥ (a) + 2 AUROC points at matched parameter count, F-9 is contradicted.

4. **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy) presupposes the correctness-relevant axis lies inside the original frozen hidden state.** Otter's central design move is the opposite: leave the original hidden state untouched and learn the calibration signal in an *appended* subspace. Their FFN-vs-MHA ablation (Table 4) shows that adding parameters into entirely new attention heads (which the original model has no access to) outperforms FFN expansion. **Mechanism:** if accurate calibration / correctness prediction primarily lives in subspaces that *don't exist* in the frozen LLM, then steering along a DoM that lies inside the frozen subspace can only modify behavior, not improve correctness. **Falsifiable:** train Otter on K=1 correctness for Qwen-2.5-1.5B; use the trained reward head to gate sampling at decode time (top-k re-rank by predicted reward); compare accuracy lift to a per-position DoM steering implementation of H-1 on the same prompts at matched compute. If Otter-gated decoding improves accuracy and DoM-steering does not, H-1's premise is wrong.

5. **F-8 (selective-prediction 71.6% at coverage 0.5 with prefill DoM) is a 1-D-probe upper bound, not a feature-richness upper bound.** Otter's "Task Head Only" baseline (a single linear layer on top of frozen LLM) is a closer analog to our final-token probe; the *full* Otter (with inserted parameters at every layer) substantially exceeds Task Head Only on every reported task (e.g. Greedy: reward 4.916 vs 4.215; DEXP: toxicity 0.295 vs 0.389). **Mechanism:** the same gap likely exists for correctness selective-prediction — a frozen-LLM linear probe (which is what prefill DoM is) leaves capacity on the table that a small-overhead trainable insertion would close. **Falsifiable:** at matched H100 inference cost, build a selective-prediction curve from a 1.5B Otter trained on MATH-500 correctness; if its accuracy at 50% coverage exceeds 71.6% by ≥ 3 points, F-8 ceases to be the headline selective-prediction result.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)** — Otter's "Task Head Only" baseline is the methodological isomorph of a final-token linear probe. Comparing full-Otter vs Task-Head-Only on MATH-500 correctness directly probes the ceiling that prefill DoM (AUROC 0.7731) is operating against.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill, final) = 0.046)** — Otter's expanded hidden state `[h_i, h'_i]` doubles available correctness-encoding capacity at every position; a multi-D readout from this expanded subspace at prefill and final challenges the 1-D-orthogonal interpretation.
- **F-8 (selective-prediction 71.6% at coverage 0.5)** — Otter's reward-prediction architecture is a drop-in replacement for the prefill DoM scorer used to select the answered 50%. Re-doing selective prediction with an Otter-style head is the natural "richer probe" comparison.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM)** — Otter's per-block insertion design is the strongest a priori counterexample to single-layer sufficiency available in our reading queue. If matched-capacity multi-layer Otter beats single-layer Otter on MATH-500 correctness, F-9 weakens.
- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering)** — Otter is an alternative *non-disruptive* intervention paradigm. The H-1 protocol can be benchmarked against an Otter-gated decoding baseline; conversely, Otter's existence questions whether the useful intervention axis lives in the original hidden subspace at all.
- **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes — label-free replacement for supervised DoM for refusal)** — Otter is *the supervised extreme*: it requires labeled rewards / preferences. The prior admission note flagged this connection; substantively, Otter is not a label-free alternative — it is a more powerful supervised one. It frames H-12's question as a Pareto trade-off (label-free SEPs vs supervised Otter at matched cost), not a replacement.
- **H-13 (Head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token circuits)** — Otter's Table 4 ablation finds MHA insertion outperforms FFN insertion at matched time overhead (speedup 2.69x vs 2.41x). This directly supports the H-13 framing that *attention heads* (not MLPs) carry the prefill-vs-final mechanism.
- **H-14, H-15 (length-band and figure work)** — no connection.
- **H-19 (C_exact verification routing)** — Otter's reward head is the architectural building block of an inline C_exact verifier; its per-token forward-pass cost (~3% extra time) makes inline verification essentially free, which strengthens the case for H-19 over inference-time C_infer scaling at matched compute.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Non-disruptive hidden-state expansion** — concatenate trainable rows/cols to `W_FFN`, `W_Q/K/V/O` such that original `h_i` is preserved exactly; expanded `h'_i` carries the calibration signal. Implemented by zero-padding the off-block of the weight matrix so original outputs are bit-identical.
  *Replication cost*: 1–2 days engineering on Qwen-2.5-1.5B + ~12h H100 training on MATH-500 correctness labels.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly tests F-2 / F-3 / F-9 / F-8 ceilings, and reuses our existing Pathway 11 H100 pipeline.

- **Layer-norm restriction to original-only mean/variance** — strictly enforce RMSNorm uses only `mean(h_i^2)`, never the expanded part, with a regularizer minimizing `(rms(h_i) − rms([h_i, h'_i]))^2` summed over blocks.
  *Replication cost*: ~1 day implementation on top of HF transformers Qwen modeling code.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — required for any Otter-on-Qwen replication.

- **Parameter Copying initialization** — initialize new FFN dims by copying random columns from `W_FFN`; initialize new attention heads by copying existing heads. Authors show this dominates Normal and Random init on validation generalization.
  *Replication cost*: trivial (init code).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — free upgrade, low risk.

- **Reward-guided greedy / top-k / top-p decode** with per-token reward correction (ARGS-style baseline). Token logits adjusted by `+ λ · reward(prefix + token)` at every step.
  *Replication cost*: ~1 day to wire into a vLLM / HF generate loop.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes if H-1 (per-position steering) is run — same harness works.

- **MHA vs FFN parameter-budget controlled comparison** — match time overhead, vary insertion site between attention heads and FFN inner dim; measure task quality. Conclusion: MHA insertion wins at matched compute.
  *Replication cost*: 2× the base Otter run.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly probes H-13.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Calibration signal alongside tokens"** reframes the verifier-vs-generator split. Instead of two networks (LLM + reward model), one network with two output heads. This aligns with Pathway 11's gated-compute paradigm where prefill DoM gates K, but elevates the gate from a frozen probe to a trainable head sharing all 32 transformer layers with the LLM. Intersects F-8 directly: gated compute is currently a frozen prefill probe; Otter would make it a per-layer trained head.
- **"Original output preserved exactly"** as a design constraint distinguishes Otter from LoRA. Frames steering / intervention as *additive in capacity* rather than *additive in direction*. Intersects H-1: per-position DoM steering is direction-additive; Otter is capacity-additive — these are categorically different intervention paradigms.
- **MHA > FFN for adapter-style calibration heads** challenges De Cao et al.'s "FFN is the knowledge layer" prior. Intersects H-13's attention-head attribution framing — argues the attention component is where calibration / correctness routing lives.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Per-block expansion factor in main results: FFN inner dim +256, FFN inp dim +128, +4 inserted attention heads on Llama-7b → 8.51B total params (vs base 6.74B).
- **Code**: https://github.com/chenhan97/Otter
- **Single line of code change** required to existing inference engines because zero-padding makes the expansion an `nn.Linear` shape change, not a forward-pass change.
- **Layer-norm regularizer weight** `λ` for `L_reg` (variance/mean drift between `h_i` and `[h_i, h'_i]`) is a critical hyperparameter not exhaustively swept in the paper.
- Trained with Huggingface transformers on a single A100; speculative-decoding training uses 4 epochs ShareGPT.
- Initialization choice (Parameter Copying) materially affects validation generalization but not training-set fit — this is the kind of subtle reproducibility hazard worth recording before any replication.
- Speculative-decoding speedup: 2.72× vs base, 14.8% over Medusa, 45.5% over Vicuna-draft on MT-Bench.
- Layer norm modification is the *only* code edit required to inference engine.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2408.10764 — Predicting Rewards Alongside Tokens: Non-disruptive Parameter Insertion for Efficient Inference Intervention in Large Language Model (Yuan et al., 2024)

**Relevance:** Otter inserts trainable rows/cols into transformer FFN and MHA weights such that the original hidden state `h_i` is preserved bit-identically, while an appended `h'_i` carries a calibration signal (reward, draft tokens, anti-expert logits). For our project, Otter is the strongest a priori counterfactual to two of our central 1-D-probe-based findings: F-2 (prefill > final asymmetry, AUROC 0.7731 vs 0.7186) and F-9 (single-layer L19 sufficiency). It also provides a direct architectural template for inline correctness probes that would replace the frozen prefill DoM gate currently used in P11 selective prediction (F-8, 71.6% at coverage 0.5). The paper's MHA-vs-FFN ablation (Table 4) further bears directly on H-13 head-level attribution.

**Key claim we tested:** A multi-dimensional, per-layer trainable readout matched in compute to a frozen 1-D linear probe will materially improve calibration-signal recovery — i.e., the Task-Head-Only baseline (a single linear layer on top of the frozen LLM) leaves substantial accuracy on the table that distributed per-block insertion recovers.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — three FE-graphed experiments (P11-FE82/83/84) and a fast pre-flight check (P11-FE85) cover the relevant claims for our MATH-500 / Qwen-2.5-1.5B regime. The paper's three reported tasks (preference alignment, detoxification, speculative decoding) do not include math correctness, so direct replication of headline numbers is N/A; the testable claim is the *gap* between Task-Head-Only and full Otter, transposed to correctness prediction.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE82, P11-FE83, P11-FE84, P11-FE85, H-83, H-84, H-85.

**Status:** TO TEST
