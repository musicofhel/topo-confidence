# Triage brief — 2310.06824 — The Geometry of Truth: Emergent Linear Structure in LLM Representations of True/False Datasets

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06824
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-3 (prefill ⊥ final-token DoM, cos = 0.046) may be a snapshot of a continuous layer-rotation rather than a true geometric fact.**
   *Mechanism*: Marks & Tegmark §4 + App. C document that, across the layers of LLaMA-2-13B, `cities` and `neg_cities` truth axes start *antipodal*, rotate to *orthogonal*, and finally *align* in late layers. They explicitly use layer 12 (not 15) for the cities+neg_cities visualization because the rotation is mid-flight. Our F-3 reports cos = 0.046 between prefill_DoM and final_DoM at L19 only — the two directions could still be the *same* truth axis sampled at two different positions in a similar rotation. Marks & Tegmark predicts this rotation should be visible in our data.
   *Falsifiable test*: Recompute cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) at every layer 0…27 of Qwen-2.5-1.5B. If the curve passes through ±1 (alignment) anywhere, F-3 is a layer-15-specific artefact, not a robust orthogonality finding. Cached prefill + final activations already exist at L19 only; we would need a cheap pass over other layers' final hidden states.

2. **F-2's AUROC 0.7731 may be leaving substantial accuracy on the table because we use raw difference-in-mean, not the IID-corrected mass-mean probe (Σ⁻¹ θ_mm).**
   *Mechanism*: §5.1 introduces the distinction between raw difference-in-mean (their `p_mm`) and the *covariance-corrected* version `p_mm^iid` = Σ⁻¹·(μ⁺−μ⁻), which equals Fisher LDA. They show MM (raw) lags LR/CCS for LLaMA-2-7B — i.e. *exactly the small-model regime* where Qwen-2.5-1.5B sits. Our pipeline reports raw DoM, not the LDA-projected variant. If the residual covariance Σ has anisotropy along non-truth directions (likely, given F-1's dimensional breathing), the IID correction could noticeably boost classification AUROC.
   *Falsifiable test*: Compute Σ from cached L19 prefill activations on the train fold; project the existing DoM through Σ⁻¹; rerun 5-fold OOF AUROC. If lift > 0.02, F-2's headline number is a methodology artefact, not a ceiling.

3. **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy) likely fails because we have no "negated MATH" paired training set, and §5/§6 show single-distribution probes have low NIE.**
   *Mechanism*: Table 2 shows raw `cities`-only training gives NIE ≈ 0.13/0.19 for false→true / true→false, while paired `cities+neg_cities` gives ≈ 0.85/0.97 — a ~5× causal effect from including the matched negation. Our DoM is trained on a single problem distribution (correct vs incorrect MATH-500) with no structurally-paired contrast set. Paper predicts: even with high classification AUROC, single-distribution DoM directions will steer poorly. This refutes the implicit assumption underlying H-1 that AUROC = causal validity.
   *Falsifiable test*: Run a steering experiment with magnitude calibrated as in §6 (normalize so p(μ⁻ + θ) = p(μ⁺)), measure NIE on a held-out 100-problem MATH-500 split. If NIE < 0.20, H-1 should be downgraded to PARKED until we have a paired-contrast training set (e.g. correct/incorrect rewrites of the same problem).

4. **F-2 / F-8 may be mostly capturing token-identity at the prefill[-1] position, not truth.**
   *Mechanism*: Footnote 3 of §4 explicitly warns that, at the *final token* (rather than the EOS punctuation summarization token), top PCs reflect token identity (e.g. "China" clusters regardless of truth value). Our prefill[-1] is *whatever token ends the prompt template* — depending on the template, this can be a numeric token, "Solution:", a colon, etc. — not a stable summarization position. AUROC 0.7731 could be partially carried by template-token leakage rather than truth.
   *Falsifiable test*: Re-extract DoM at the EOS-punctuation token of the problem statement (period before "Solution:") and rerun 5-fold AUROC. Compare against the prefill[-1] AUROC. If AUROC differs by > 0.03, the F-2 effect is position-specific and our position choice is suboptimal — Marks & Tegmark would predict the punctuation token is the right one.

5. **F-1 universality is in tension with the paper's "linear structure emerges only with scale" finding.**
   *Mechanism*: §4.1 + Fig. 5 establish that linear truth probing fails for LLaMA-2-7B and only emerges at 13B/70B. We claim "dimensional breathing is universal across architectures and scales," with the smallest model in our suite being Qwen-2.5-1.5B. Marks & Tegmark predicts that whatever we are observing at 1.5B is *not* the same linear-truth direction we infer for 7B; if so, our breathing claim is a pre-linearization phenomenon at small scales and a post-linearization phenomenon at large scales — they are not the same thing.
   *Falsifiable test*: Compute, at 1.5B, 7B, and (if available) a 0.5B model, both (a) DoM AUROC for correctness, and (b) PCA's leading-component truth-separation accuracy. If (b) collapses to chance for 1.5B but DoM AUROC > 0.7, breathing is uncorrelated with linear truth representation at small scale, and F-1 needs a scale-stratified restatement.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)** ↔ this paper's MM probes match LR for classification. *Corroborates* that simple difference-in-mean captures most of the linear-truth signal — but the paper's MM is *covariance-corrected* and we are not, which makes 0.7731 a likely lower bound.
- **F-3 (cos = 0.046, prefill vs final DoM)** ↔ paper's layer-rotation finding (App. C: cities/neg_cities go antipodal → orthogonal → aligned across layers). Predicts our orthogonality is layer-local, not global.
- **F-4 (asymmetric collapse at final token)** ↔ paper's "summarization at clause-ending punctuation" (§3, group b). Suggests the asymmetric-collapse signature could be the same summarization machinery active at the end-of-CoT punctuation.
- **F-7 (D-bucket collective signature)** ↔ paper's "non-truth features can dominate the probe direction even when they are merely correlated with truth on training data" (§4.1). D-bucket may correspond to a non-truth feature that aligns with correctness on most of our training distribution but flips sign on a minority.
- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering)** ↔ paper's intervention experiments (§6, Table 2) — directly informative, with concrete NIE numbers (0.85/0.97 paired, 0.13/0.19 unpaired) we can use as a sanity-check target.
- **H-8 (DoM stability across checkpoints)** ↔ §4.1 hierarchical-emergence framing — predicts late-checkpoint DoMs should be more *cross-dataset-aligned* than early-checkpoint DoMs.
- **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes replace supervised DoM)** ↔ paper's CCS comparison — CCS is unsupervised but underperforms MM on causal interventions (Table 2). Suggests label-free probes may classify well but steer poorly.
- **H-17 (DoM rotation during generation is RoPE-mechanical)** ↔ paper's *layerwise* rotation provides a layerwise analogue to our *positional* rotation. Both rotations might share a common cause (e.g. the model rotating features into more abstract bases).

## Methodologies extracted

- **Mass-mean probe (raw `p_mm`)** — direction θ = μ⁺ − μ⁻ where μ are class-conditional means; classification via σ(θᵀx) with bias chosen so probe value matches mean labels.
  *Replication cost*: 10 min CPU on cached L19 prefill activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — this is essentially what F-2 already does, but worth checking our pipeline matches the paper's normalization (Marks & Tegmark center activations and use unbiased probes).

- **IID mass-mean probe (`p_mm^iid` = Σ⁻¹·(μ⁺−μ⁻))** — covariance-corrected difference-in-mean, equivalent to Fisher LDA under homoscedasticity (App. F proof).
  *Replication cost*: 20 min CPU (need Σ from full training fold; small numerical-stability work for high-dim Σ inversion).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly addresses Refutation 2.

- **Activation patching for causal localization** — cache activations on `pT`; replay model on `pF` swapping `h[i,ℓ](pF) → h[i,ℓ](pT)` at one (position, layer) pair; measure log P(TRUE) − log P(FALSE) shift.
  *Replication cost*: ~2h H100 per (model, layer-grid, position-grid) on 20 prompts. Already partially implemented for our few-shot setup.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — would let us localize where in the residual stream MATH-500 correctness is *causally* encoded vs merely *predictable*. Currently F-2 only does the prediction half.

- **PCA visualization of class-conditional clusters** — center activations per dataset; plot top-2 PCs colored by truth label.
  *Replication cost*: 5 min CPU per layer.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — sanity check on whether L19 actually has a linear truth axis (vs DoM AUROC working via cumulative non-linear effects).

- **Causal NIE protocol** — normalize θ so probe assigns θ a "δ-mean shift" worth one class swap; intervene additively at all group-(b) positions; measure log-prob difference shift.
  *Replication cost*: 30 min H100 per probe per dataset.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — our H-1 currently lacks a NIE-style metric, only behavioural accuracy. Adopting NIE makes us comparable to this paper.

- **Contrastive dataset construction** — paired (s, ¬s) statements with `not` insertion; logical conjunctions/disjunctions of true/false elements; structurally controlled negation as a non-truth feature.
  *Replication cost*: 2h to draft a "negated MATH" or "wrong-answer-paired-with-right-answer" dataset.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — key gap; addresses Refutation 3 directly.

- **`likely` distractor dataset** — non-factual generations whose final token is the most-or-100th-most-likely completion; tests whether truth probes are picking up text plausibility instead.
  *Replication cost*: 4h for MATH-style analogue (e.g. plausible-but-wrong final answer tokens vs correct).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — interesting control for "does prefill DoM track *answer plausibility* rather than *correctness*?"

## Approaches & framings

- **Truth as direction, not as classifier** — emphasis on the *direction* identified, with classification accuracy treated as one of two complementary metrics (the other being causal NIE). Reframes our F-2 result: AUROC is the cheap half; the causal half is missing.
- **Hierarchical layer-wise abstraction** — features become "more abstract" with depth; small-model truth probes pick up surface features while large-model probes pick up abstract truth. Implies our F-1 dimensional-breathing may be a small-model phenomenon at 1.5B that is qualitatively different from what 7B is doing.
- **Group-(b) summarization** — clause-ending punctuation tokens are the canonical residence of full-statement features, not the final content token. Reframes our prefill[-1] choice — we should examine whether the prefill template ends in punctuation or not.
- **Salience-based mis-alignment model** — when a non-truth feature `f⁻` is salient on D but anti-correlates with truth on D′, PCA shows orthogonal/antipodal separation; this is a *prediction* about which datasets will mis-generalize. Useful framing for understanding F-7's D-bucket: D-bucket may be a region where a feature `f⁻` (correlated with correctness on our train fold) flips sign.
- **Calibrated few-shot as a baseline** — they use 1-shot prompts and a probability-difference threshold (calibrated so 50% of statements are labelled true); we should adopt this as our verifier-prompt baseline rather than uncalibrated zero-shot.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Models: LLaMA-2 (7B, 13B, 70B). All open-weight.
- Layer choice: most-downstream group-(b) hidden state — for 13B, this is layer 15 over the EOS punctuation token; for 70B, the rule is more context-dependent (App. B). For Qwen-2.5-1.5B (28 layers), the analogue is roughly layer 17–20 — overlaps with our L19 choice.
- Train/test split: random 80/20 within each dataset; if test-set ≠ train-set, full test-set is used.
- Activations are *centered* (subtract per-dataset mean) before probing; probes are *unbiased*.
- For mass-mean probing, paper uses `p_mm` for OOD test sets but `p_mm^iid` for IID test sets — pipeline-level distinction we currently elide.
- NIE normalization: choose θ scale such that `p(μ⁻ + θ) = p(μ⁺)`. Important for H-1 — without this, magnitude is arbitrary.
- Code/data: https://github.com/saprmarks/geometry-of-truth (MIT-style license inferred from public release).
- Calibrated few-shot: pick threshold so half of statements get labelled true, "improves performance by a few percentage points" — methodology gotcha for verifier baselines.
- Footnote 3 warning: representations *over the final content token* show token-identity clusters in PCA, not truth — implies our current "prefill[-1]" extraction is not necessarily the right token position.

## 2310.06824 — The Geometry of Truth: Emergent Linear Structure in LLM Representations of True/False Datasets (Marks & Tegmark, COLM 2024)

**Relevance:** Foundational reference for the "linear truth direction" framing that motivates F-2 and F-8. The paper introduces mass-mean (difference-in-mean) probing as a stronger alternative to logistic regression for *causal* purposes — at scale, MM probes match LR/CCS for classification (≥95% AUROC on transfer in some conditions for LLaMA-2-13B/70B) but identify directions that are 3–5× more causally implicated under the §6 NIE protocol (e.g. 0.85 vs 0.18 for cities+neg_cities at LLaMA-2-13B). It also documents two phenomena directly relevant to our pipeline: (a) layer-wise rotation of paired truth axes (antipodal → orthogonal → aligned across layers of LLaMA-2-13B) which provides an alternative interpretation of our F-3 cos(·)=0.046 finding, and (b) token-position contamination at non-summarization tokens, which casts doubt on our prefill[-1] extraction choice.

**Key claim we tested:** "Difference-in-mean (mass-mean) is a strong probe for linear truth representations and identifies directions that are causally implicated, not merely predictive." Our F-2 already operationalizes the predictive half on MATH-500 correctness (AUROC 0.7731). The causal half — the part Marks & Tegmark argue is the load-bearing claim — is currently untested in our pipeline.

**Our result:** **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED** Predictive half: F-2 corroborates that raw difference-in-mean is a useful probe at small scale (Qwen-2.5-1.5B, AUROC 0.7731). Causal half: untested; FE75 / H-75 are queued to measure NIE on MATH-500.
