# Triage brief — 2505.15442 — On the Generalization vs Fidelity Paradox in Knowledge Distillation

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15442
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **H-11 (big-to-small distillation of 7B self-prediction into 1.5B) is structurally undermined by the generalization-vs-fidelity paradox.** The paper shows in Table 5 that the Spearman correlation between teacher-student reasoning fidelity (BLEU on CoT) and student math accuracy is ≈0 (e.g., AddSub 0.01, GSM8K 0.01). KD raises 0.5B–1.5B accuracy by up to 10–22% but does **not** force the student's intermediate reasoning to mirror the teacher's. **Mechanism that fires the refutation:** if reasoning trajectories diverge while accuracy converges, then the *internal* prefill direction the 7B teacher uses (our F-2 anchor: AUROC 0.7731 for prefill L19 DoM on 1.5B) almost certainly will not be transferred — the 1.5B student will recover the 7B's correctness label distribution via its own internal mechanisms, leaving cos(student_prefill_DoM, teacher_prefill_DoM) ≈ 0 even when label-AUROC rises. **Concrete falsifier:** distill Qwen-2.5-7B into Qwen-2.5-1.5B with RevKD on Math10K (their best method, 6.3% avg gain), then re-extract our standard L19 prefill activations on MATH-500 and measure cos(distilled_1.5B_prefill_DoM, original_7B_prefill_DoM). If <0.3 with student MATH-500 accuracy raised from 48.6% → ≥55%, H-11's premise that the 7B *signal* is what gets distilled is refuted; what's distilled is the *output behavior*, and the geometry has to be re-discovered (or doesn't exist).

2. **F-1 (dimensional breathing is universal across architectures and scales) collides with the paper's "students develop their own reasoning strategies" finding.** The paper's Appendix B reports that Qwen-2.5 self-fidelity (KD-trained vs SFT-trained same student) drops to ~2% lower than KD-teacher fidelity in GSM8K and AQuA, indicating that distillation *reshapes* the student's internal reasoning relative to its SFT counterpart. **Mechanism:** if a small change in training objective (SFT → RevKD) produces measurable shifts in reasoning patterns, then the breathing signature on Qwen-2.5-1.5B-SFT and Qwen-2.5-1.5B-RevKD-from-7B should differ in peak PR, inflation rate, or temporal location — even though both are "Qwen-2.5-1.5B" architecturally. **Falsifier:** extract per-token L19 traces on MATH-500 for both checkpoints, compute the temporal participation-ratio curve from `pathway11_h100/`, and measure ΔPR_peak. If |ΔPR_peak| > 5 between SFT and KD versions of the *same architecture and scale*, F-1's universality claim narrows from "architecture & scale" to "architecture, scale, AND training objective" — a non-trivial weakening.

3. **F-2 / F-9 (single-direction L19 DoM and CoE-60 redundancy) collide with KD's accuracy-fidelity decoupling under the noise-injection ablation.** The paper shows (Figure 5) that injecting Gaussian noise into teacher logits with σ=2 drops Qwen-3B → Qwen-1.5B math by 3.6%, and σ=5 collapses Qwen-3B → Qwen-0.5B from 55.87% → 5.85%. The teacher-student gap result (Mirzadeh-style) shows Qwen-1.5B does *better* from Qwen-7B than from Qwen-14B. **Mechanism:** if the bandwidth of useful KD signal is so narrow that σ=2 already corrupts it and a 14B teacher is *worse* than a 7B teacher for a 1.5B student, then the "single-layer DoM captures everything" framing (F-9: CoE-60 redundant with L19 DoM) likely doesn't survive distillation — the post-distill student probably needs a *different* probe (different layer, different direction) to recover the same 0.7731 AUROC. **Falsifier:** repeat the 28-layer DoM scan from EXP-???/scratch on Qwen-2.5-1.5B-RevKD-from-7B and report the layer-wise AUROC profile. If the peak shifts off L19 by ≥3 layers, F-2's "L19 is the canonical layer" framing is distillation-fragile, and F-9's redundancy claim has to be redone per-checkpoint.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **H-11 (big-to-small distillation of 7B self-prediction into 1.5B):** the paper is the first large-scale empirical baseline for what KD actually transfers (accuracy: yes; reasoning fidelity: weakly; teacher-student agreement on math: weakly). It does *not* test internal-state transfer. Anchored to the headline numbers: 1.5B MATH-500 accuracy is 48.6% (baseline). If RevKD-from-7B can lift that toward 7B's 73.2% even partially, that's the H-11 win condition; the paper predicts +5–10% on math reasoning, so 53–58% on MATH-500 is the realistic ceiling.
- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731 on 1.5B):** the paper provides the missing procedure (RevKD with τ=2, λ=0.5, β=0.5, LoRA r=8 α=16) and the empirical observation that KD reshapes reasoning. Direct experiment: re-extract prefill DoM after distillation; if AUROC stays ≥0.75, the direction survives KD; if it drops to ~chance, KD reshapes the geometry too.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with L19 DoM at AUROC parity):** if distillation breaks one but not the other, F-9's redundancy claim is no longer tight — it would mean CoE and DoM measure *different* things in the distilled student even if they're redundant in the SFT student.
- **F-7 (D-bucket collective signature, 36 problems on 1.5B):** distillation may erase or relocate D-bucket if it changes the per-problem reasoning strategy — the paper's noise-collapse result suggests the bucket signature is bandwidth-fragile.
- **H-5 (prefill encodes problem familiarity vs decomposability):** the paper's "task-unaware teacher degrades student up to 40%" is a strong indirect signal that *task-domain priors* are what get transmitted, not generic reasoning capability. If H-5 is right that prefill DoM = familiarity, then KD should preserve it (familiarity is data-shaped); if H-5 is wrong and prefill DoM = decomposability, KD likely won't.

## Methodologies extracted

- **RevKD (Reverse KL distillation, Gu et al. 2024 / MiniLLM-style)** — student minimizes D_KL(P_S || P_T), avoiding overestimation of teacher's low-probability tail.
  *Replication cost*: ~1 day H100 for Qwen-7B → Qwen-1.5B on Math10K (10K samples, 4 epochs, LoRA r=8).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — gives a cheap student checkpoint that should improve 1.5B MATH-500 accuracy and is the natural vehicle for testing H-11.
- **GKD with on-policy mixing (Agarwal et al. 2024)** — λ=0.5 mix of fixed teacher data + student self-generated data, β=0.5 JSD between forward/reverse KL.
  *Replication cost*: ~1.5× RevKD (extra inference passes for self-gen).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **maybe** — only worth it if RevKD shows promise; GKD beats RevKD on instruction-following but is comparable on math (Table 1 ANOVA on math is non-significant).
- **SeqKD (Kim & Rush 2016)** — sample teacher outputs and SFT the student on them.
  *Replication cost*: trivial — just generate teacher outputs once and SFT.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — cheapest baseline; gives a "behavior-cloned" student to compare against the logit-distilled one.
- **Teacher noise injection (logit-level Gaussian noise σ ∈ {0,1,2,5})** — diagnostic for teacher-signal bandwidth.
  *Replication cost*: 4× RevKD.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **probably not first** — interesting but secondary; only run if a baseline RevKD shows F-2 doesn't transfer.
- **Reasoning-fidelity metric (BLEU between teacher and student CoT generations)** — separate from final-answer agreement.
  *Replication cost*: trivial post-hoc on cached generations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — gives us "agreement on answer" vs "agreement on CoT" decomposition that maps neatly onto F-2 (prefill, problem-level) vs F-3 (final-token, answer-level) orthogonality.
- **Sentence-embedding fidelity (cosine on Reimers-Gurevych SBERT embeddings)** — Spearman 0.97 with BLEU per the paper. Could replace BLEU.
  *Replication cost*: trivial.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — better-behaved than BLEU on long CoT.
- **Task-aware vs task-unaware teacher comparison (Mirzadeh-style gap)** — fine-tune teacher on downstream domain *before* distillation; their result is up-to-40% degradation when you skip this.
  *Replication cost*: doubles teacher prep.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes if pursuing H-11 seriously** — Qwen-2.5-7B on MATH-500 isn't necessarily a "task-aware teacher" out-of-the-box at 1024-tok labels; we may need to SFT 7B on Math10K first.

## Approaches & framings

- **The accuracy/fidelity decoupling.** Paper frames distillation as transferring two distinct quantities: (a) end-task correctness and (b) reasoning-process structure, and shows they're statistically uncorrelated on math. This *exactly* mirrors our F-2/F-3 orthogonality framing (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046): different mechanisms produce the answer vs explain the answer. If our internal-state finding generalizes, the paper's external-behavior finding is its observable shadow.
- **Teacher-student gap as bandwidth limit.** The Qwen-1.5B-from-7B > Qwen-1.5B-from-14B result reframes "more teacher capacity = more transferred information" as a Goldilocks constraint — there's a per-student-size optimum for teacher capacity. This argues that for our purposes (transferring 7B prefill structure into 1.5B), we likely don't want a 14B teacher even if one were available; 7B is the right pairing.
- **Reasoning shifts under self-distillation/noise.** The self-fidelity-lower-than-teacher-fidelity finding is a useful framing for H-15 / H-22: the same architecture can adopt qualitatively different reasoning under different training objectives, which means our breathing/DoM findings on Qwen-2.5-1.5B-vanilla are *not* automatically true of Qwen-2.5-1.5B-instruct or Qwen-2.5-1.5B-RevKD.

- **GSM8K (8.8K train / 1319 test)** — grade-school math word problems. HF/Apache. **Applicable? partially** — easier than MATH-500, but useful as a sanity-check eval that pairs with the paper's headline numbers.
- **SVAMP, MultiArith, AddSub, AQuA, SingleEq** — small math reasoning sets (≤2K test). HF accessible. **Applicable? mostly no** — too easy and short-context for our 1024-tok regime; SVAMP at most as a quick sanity check.

- **Dolly-15K + SelfInst/Vicuna/SNI/UNI** — instruction-following; out of scope.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- LoRA adapters: r=8, α=16 for both SFT and KD on every model — clean, cheap-to-replicate.
- Single Nvidia A100 was sufficient for 0.5B–7B distillation runs.
- Batch size 16, lr 3e-4, **max-length 256**, 3–4 epochs (Commonsense / Math respectively).
- **CRITICAL CAVEAT for our project:** the paper trains and evaluates at max-length=256. This is exactly the truncation-confound length we documented in PROJECT_RECORD §1d. Their "KD improves math by 10%" headline could itself be a 256-tok artifact. Any replication on our side must use 1024-tok labels.
- Default τ=1 for zero-shot eval; ablation τ ∈ {1, 2, 5} shows τ=2 is best, τ=5 collapses. λ=0.5, β=0.5 for GKD.
- RevKD is the most consistent method (avg 6.3% gain) — start there.
- Code: https://github.com/LCS2-IIITD/KD_generalization

## Replicable intermediates

- **Score-only sanity check from cached 7B prefill activations.** Train an L19 DoM on cached Qwen-2.5-7B prefill activations (already in `pathway11_h100/`), use it to *re-rank* Qwen-2.5-1.5B's MATH-500 attempts. If 7B-DoM scoring of 1.5B prefill outperforms random selection on the 1.5B's K=2.5 selective-prediction (current 71.6% at coverage 0.5 from F-8), it's evidence that the 7B direction has *some* representation in the 1.5B activation space — and KD might be able to amplify it.
- **Cross-model cosine baseline before KD.** Compute cos(7B_prefill_DoM, 1.5B_prefill_DoM) using cross-model alignment (Procrustes or CCA) on shared MATH-500 problems. If cos < 0.3 *before any KD*, the 7B and 1.5B already use different directions and KD has further to go than H-11 implicitly assumes.
- **Reasoning-fidelity decomposition on existing 1.5B and 7B MATH-500 generations.** We have both models' completions cached; compute BLEU and SBERT-cosine between paired teacher/student CoTs on the 366 problems where 7B is correct. This is the SFT baseline fidelity and bounds how much improvement RevKD could deliver.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2505.15442 — On the Generalization vs Fidelity Paradox in Knowledge Distillation (Ramesh, Sengupta, Chakraborty, IIT Delhi, 2025)

**Relevance:** Provides the canonical empirical baseline for what knowledge distillation does and does not transfer between Qwen-2.5 model sizes (0.5B → 1.5B → 3B → 7B → 14B) on math reasoning. Directly bears on H-11 (big-to-small distillation of 7B prefill self-prediction into 1.5B): the paper's finding that RevKD reliably lifts smaller-student accuracy while *failing* to transfer reasoning fidelity (Spearman fidelity-vs-accuracy ≈0 for math) implies that any positive result for H-11 must be tested geometrically, not just behaviorally — student accuracy gains do not imply teacher-direction transfer. The paper's max-length=256 setup is directly analogous to our 256-tok truncation confound (PROJECT_RECORD §1d), so its accuracy claims should not be cited as 1024-tok-comparable; we should rerun at max-length=1024 if pursuing H-11. Provides exact recipe (LoRA r=8, α=16, τ=2, RevKD as the most consistent method) and Math10K + a single-A100 budget — making the H-11 experiment cheap to attempt.

**Key claim we tested:** For Qwen-2.5 sizes ≤7B, distillation from a larger teacher transfers correctness behavior to the student (10–22% peak gain on math; 1.3% on 7B), but does *not* transfer reasoning fidelity (BLEU correlation ≈0 with accuracy on math) and only weakly transfers teacher-student final-answer agreement.

**Our result:** **TO TEST.** We have not yet run RevKD on our Qwen-2.5-1.5B/7B pair. Pre-distillation prediction (P11-FE73 / H-72): cos(7B_prefill_DoM, 1.5B_prefill_DoM) is already low (≤0.3) on shared MATH-500 problems; post-RevKD, accuracy rises ~5–10% but cos(distilled_1.5B_DoM, 7B_DoM) likely stays ≤0.4 — i.e. the paper's behavior-without-fidelity result generalizes from CoT text to internal prefill geometry.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE72 (RevKD + DoM re-extraction), P11-FE73 (pre-KD cross-model cosine baseline), P11-FE74 (SFT-baseline reasoning-fidelity decomposition), P11-FE75 (post-KD per-layer DoM scan), P11-FE76 (task-aware vs task-unaware teacher ablation), H-72, H-73, H-11 (re-anchored).
