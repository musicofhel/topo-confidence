## EXPERIMENT_LOG entry

## EXP-45: P11-FE145 — Per-layer prefill DoM AUROC sweep (L0..L28)
**Date:** 2026-05-01
**Status:** COMPLETE
**Motivated by:** CAA (Panickssery et al.) reports the optimal steering
layer at ~⅓ depth with a sharp drop-off at L17 (53% depth) on Llama-2.
F-2 picks L19/28 (~68% depth) on Qwen-2.5-1.5B — well past CAA's
drop-off. Either Qwen has a different geometry, or L19 is post-hoc
lucky. A full per-layer sweep settles it.
**Hypothesis:** L19 is the argmax layer for prefill DoM AUROC (or within
±0.005 of it). If L19 is far from the peak (>0.005 below), the F-2
choice is post-hoc.
**What we actually tested:** Loaded all 29 per-layer prefill
activations (input embeds + 28 transformer blocks) from
`pathway8_layerwise/data/math500/problem_*.npz` (500 problems × 29
layers × 1536 dims). For each layer, ran 5-fold stratified OOF DoM
(seed=9999): train on 4 folds (mean(pos) − mean(neg)), score on
held-out fold, compute AUROC over all 500 OOF scores.
**Key result:** Peak at **L21 with AUROC 0.7718**. L19 at AUROC
**0.7705** (gap 0.0013 — within ±0.005). The curve rises monotonically
from L0 (0.50, embedding-output, no info) to L18 (0.7616), peaks at
L19–L21 (0.7705–0.7718), then declines slightly to L28 (0.7568). L17
(CAA drop-off layer) is at 0.7635 — *no sharp drop-off* on Qwen-2.5-1.5B.
**Verdict:** F-2 L19 choice CONFIRMED. The peak plateau is L19–L21,
spanning ~0.001 in AUROC; L19 is on the plateau. CAA's L17 drop-off
hypothesis does NOT generalise to Qwen-2.5-1.5B — Qwen's correctness
direction stays informative across the upper half of the network.
**Changed our understanding of:** F-2's geometric specificity. The
peak is not at one privileged layer — it's a 3-layer plateau (L19–L21).
"L19 is special" is too strong; "the upper-mid block (L19–L21) is the
DoM peak" is the right framing.
**Phase 1 decision branch status:** This is a *confirming* control,
not a refutation. Counter unchanged: 1 of 5 below 0.70 (FE448
residualized only). FE719+FE145 confirmed F-2 mostly survives;
FE448 demoted strength to MODERATE due to length confound.
**Files:** `pathway11_h100/per_layer_sweep/recompute_fe145.py` (regen,
deterministic), `pathway11_h100/per_layer_sweep/results.json` (output).
**Note:** The 0.7705 5-fold OOF AUROC at L19 differs by ~0.003 from the
0.7731 headline figure (EXP-037). Both are 5-fold OOF on the same data
but used different seeds and CV-split implementations; the difference
is well within seed-variance and doesn't change conclusions.
**Depends on:** EXP-037 (L19 baseline), EXP-43 (LOCO), EXP-44 (length).
**Enables:** Per-layer follow-ups (which layer carries length info, see
H-N: layer-resolved length artefacts), and a possible H-N about the
L19–L21 plateau.

### F-2: Prefill L19 DoM is a strong correctness predictor, *better than final-token*

```yaml

counterargument: "Length partial-correlation (FE448) is the dominant counterargument: ~⅔ of AUROC is length-explainable. Per-layer sweep (FE145) confirms L19 is on the peak plateau, but the plateau spans L19–L21, not a single layer."
overturned_by: "OOF residual AUROC ≤ 0.55 after length partialing (would push to INVALIDATED), OR a layer where length-residualized DoM AUROC matches raw within 0.02 (would restore STRONG)."
```

**Claim.** On Qwen-2.5-1.5B-Instruct, a 5-fold OOF logistic probe fit on
mean-pooled L19 activations at position 0 (last prompt token, pre-generation)
achieves **AUROC 0.7731**. Final-token DoM AUROC is **0.7186**. Per-layer
sweep (EXP-45) shows L19 sits on a 3-layer peak plateau (L19=0.7705,
L20=0.7706, L21=0.7718, this script's seed); L17 (CAA's drop-off layer)
is 0.7635 — Qwen-2.5-1.5B does NOT show CAA's sharp drop-off. Under
leave-one-subject-out CV across the 7 MATH-500 subjects (EXP-43), mean
AUROC is **0.7427**. After partialing out predicted-length from prefill
(EXP-44), in-sample DoM AUROC drops from 0.7834 to **0.6647** — gray zone
between length-artifact (≤0.55) and survives-partialing (>0.65). The 7B
shows the same prefill-pattern with a larger gap to final-token.

**Strength:** MODERATE (signal real but ~⅔ of AUROC is length-explainable;
plateau L19–L21, not single-layer specific).

**Evidence:** EXP-037 (`pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/results.json`
fields `prefill_DoM_auroc_oof` = 0.7731, `final_token_DoM_auroc_oof` =
0.7186). EXP-43 (`pathway11_h100/loco_subject/results.json`: LOCO mean
0.7427, worst 0.6032). EXP-44
(`pathway11_h100/length_partial/results.json`: length R² 1.0,
auroc_length_alone 0.7986, auroc_residualized 0.6647). EXP-45
(`pathway11_h100/per_layer_sweep/results.json`: peak_layer 21,
peak_auroc 0.7718, auroc_l19 0.7705, peak_minus_l19 0.0013).

**Controls passed:**
- 5-fold stratified OOF (seed 9999) — not a test/train leak.
- Leave-One-Subject-Out CV (EXP-43) — mean AUROC 0.7427, worst 0.6032
  (Number Theory). Subject-stratified leakage is small (~3pp).
- **Per-layer sweep (EXP-45, FE145)** — L19 is within ±0.005 of the
  L21 peak (gap 0.0013); the L19 choice is not post-hoc.

**Controls partially failed:**
- Length partial-correlation (EXP-44, FE448) — DoM AUROC drops 0.7834 →
  0.6647 after partialing predicted-length. Not refuted (0.6647 > 0.55)
  but does not survive partialing (0.6647 ≤ 0.65). Length is a major
  confound at L19, not just at the engineered-feature level (F-9).

**Strongest counterargument:** Sequence length alone (AUROC 0.7986)
predicts correctness *better* than raw DoM at L19. The DoM direction may
be largely re-discovering "this prompt will get a short / long
generation" — which itself proxies for problem familiarity. Pure
"correctness geometry" framing is too strong.
