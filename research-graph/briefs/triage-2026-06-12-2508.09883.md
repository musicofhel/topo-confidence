# Triage brief — 2508.09883 — Beyond Scaling Law: A Data-Efficient Distillation Framework for Reasoning (DED)

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.09883
Triaged: 2026-06-12

> Orientation: This is a **training-recipe / data-curation** paper (CoT distillation:
> teacher selection, corpus filtering, trajectory-diversity), not a hidden-state
> correctness-probing paper. It touches our program only through two analysis tools it
> borrows — **token-entropy analysis** and **PCA-shift (latent-centroid) analysis** — and
> through the shared MATH-500 benchmark. The refutations below are genuine but indirect:
> they use the paper's *findings about what latent geometry tracks* to interrogate whether
> our correctness geometry (F-2, F-4) is actually a domain/entropy axis in disguise.

## Refutations

**R1 — F-2 may be a domain/corpus-affinity axis, not a correctness axis.**
DED's PCA-shift analysis (Fig. 4, via Huan et al. 2507.00432) shows the **dominant PCA
direction of latent representations shifts systematically with the training corpus and the
evaluation task/domain** — the top latent axis tracks corpus affinity, not answer quality.
F-2 claims the top covariance axis (PC1 ≈ supervised DoM, cos 0.9216, FE291) predicts
*correctness* at AUROC 0.7731. **Mechanism:** if PC1 in our L19 prefill cloud is primarily a
subject/familiarity axis (algebra vs geometry vs number-theory), the 0.7731 AUROC could be
inflated by MATH-500's domain composition correlating with difficulty/correctness rather than
by a correctness-specific direction. **Falsifiable now:** stratify MATH-500 by subject (the
Hendrycks `subject` field) and recompute prefill-DoM/PC1 AUROC *within* each subject. If the
separation collapses within-subject (e.g. drops toward 0.5–0.6) it is a domain axis; the
existing worst-cell Number-Theory transfer of **0.603** (F-2's transfer note, FE-EDGEGEN) is a
hint this is partly real.

**R2 — F-4 "asymmetric collapse" may be a token-entropy proxy, not a correctness signature.**
DED's token-entropy analysis (Fig. 3) ties low per-step token entropy to a "more predictable,
structured" representation. F-4 claims correct trajectories collapse harder (lower effective
dimensionality / participation ratio) at the final token. **Mechanism:** confident generations
have low token entropy *and* more collapsed/structured residual geometry — the two co-move, so
"correct → collapses harder" could simply be "confident (low-entropy) → collapses harder," with
correctness as a downstream correlate of entropy, not of geometry. **Falsifiable now:** on cached
MATH-500, regress final-token PR / collapse against per-example mean token entropy (≈ −mean
logprob, already cached for the free baseline) and check whether the F-4 correct/incorrect collapse
gap survives partialling out entropy. If it vanishes, F-4's geometric framing is an entropy proxy
(and consistent with `free-baseline-strongest-readout` being CONFIRMED).

**R3 — F-8 portability: any frozen geometric correctness probe is teacher/corpus-specific.**
DED shows that two students distilled from different teachers (QwQ-32B vs DeepSeek-R1) on the
*same questions* end up with **measurably different latent geometry** (different PCA-offset
magnitudes, different token-entropy of the corpus) yet similar accuracy. **Mechanism:** if the
geometry of the correctness-bearing subspace is set by the *training corpus's* entropy/affinity
rather than by a universal correctness manifold, then a DoM/cov-spectrum probe fit on one model's
residuals (F-2/F-8 stack, AUROC 0.7731 / cov-spectrum 0.7928) should *not* transfer to a model
distilled from a different teacher — a sharper version of F-2's already-observed "hidden-dim-bound,
must refit per model" limitation. This directly re-tests the **already-REFUTED**
`geometry-portable-signal` premise on a new axis (teacher identity, not architecture). **Falsifiable
(GPU):** extract L19 prefill DoM on DS-32B-distilled-from-QwQ vs DS-32B-distilled-from-R1 and test
cross-model probe transfer — but see compute note in Tier 2.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2** (single L19 prefill direction, AUROC **0.7731**; PC1≈DoM cos **0.9216**, FE291; full-dim
  ceiling **0.7847**, FE882): DED's PCA-shift metric is the *same family* of analysis — Euclidean
  distance between centroids of latent representations — applied at the model-update level instead of
  the per-example level. Their finding that this top axis tracks corpus/domain is the engine of R1.
- **F-4** (asymmetric collapse): connects to DED's token-entropy analysis (R2) — their entropy
  framing offers a competing mechanism for the collapse asymmetry.
- **F-7** (D-bucket *collective*, not per-problem, geometric signature): DED's PCA-offset is also a
  *collective* (centroid-of-cloud) statistic, not per-example — methodological parallel; a positive
  R1 result would reinforce that the dominant collective axis is domain-collective, not correctness.
- **H-5** (prefill signal = problem familiarity vs decomposability): DED's "corpus affinity" framing
  (teacher–target consistency drives performance) is essentially a familiarity story; R1's
  subject-stratification is also a direct test of H-5.
- **`free-baseline-strongest-readout`** (CONFIRMED premise): R2 is a new piece of evidence in its
  favor if final-token collapse is fully mediated by logprob/entropy.

## Methodologies extracted

- **PCA-shift / latent-centroid-offset analysis** — compute the Euclidean distance between the
  centroids of a model's latent representations before vs after an intervention (here: training);
  smaller offset ⇒ more stable representation ⇒ better OOD. Source method: Huan et al. 2507.00432.
  *Replication cost*: ~20min CPU on cached NPZ (we already have the cloud; we just compute
  centroid distances between correct/incorrect or between-subject groups).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — it is a cheap, novel-to-us between-group geometric statistic on
  the 500×1536 matrix, directly feeding R1/R3.
- **Token-entropy distribution analysis** — per-step entropy of the model's next-token distribution,
  aggregated over a corpus/response; lower entropy ⇒ more structured, faster student convergence.
  *Replication cost*: ~10min CPU if per-token logprobs are cached; otherwise needs a GPU re-decode.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes (as a mean-logprob proxy) — feeds R2 directly.
- **Levenshtein-distance trajectory-diversity selection** — pick the P farthest-apart CoT trajectories
  (by edit distance) per question to maximise corpus diversity.
  *Replication cost*: trivial CPU, but needs raw generated CoT text we don't cache.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — operates on generated text, not on our cached activation matrices.
- **Teacher-selection smoke test** — sample one CoT per question from each candidate teacher, run a
  quick distill+eval, pick the teacher by downstream student score (not by teacher benchmark score).
  *Replication cost*: H100-days (full distillation loop).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — out of local-only compute scope; relevant only to SPEC-v8 framing.
- **Student-pass-rate hard-example compression** — drop questions whose student pass-rate exceeds a
  threshold, keeping only hard examples.
  *Replication cost*: needs per-question pass-rate from multi-sample decode (GPU).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — training-data-curation tool, not a residual-stream probe.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Beyond superficial factors" reframing** — token length and raw teacher capability are *not* the
  drivers; corpus token-entropy and latent-representation stability are. Intersects our program as a
  caution: it argues that surface/length features mislead and that latent-geometry stability is the
  real signal — orthogonally consistent with F-2's "hidden-dim-bound" finding and with our repeated
  result that length-based readouts are weak.
- **Corpus-affinity as the governing variable** — performance is set by consistency between the
  teacher-corpus characteristics and the target domain. Maps onto H-5's familiarity framing and
  motivates R1 (is our predictive axis a corpus/domain-affinity axis?).
- **Representation-stability ⇒ OOD generalization** — smaller PCA offset predicts better OOD. A
  framing we could borrow inversely: *per-example* distance-from-cloud-centroid as a difficulty/OOD
  proxy for selective prediction (F-8).

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Student: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B; teachers: DeepSeek-R1, QwQ-32B, Qwen3-32B, Qwen3-235B-A22B.
- SFT via LlamaFactory; context window 16,384; batch 48; lr 1e-5 AdamW; 10 epochs; 8×H800, ~9h / 1k examples.
- Corpus pipeline gotchas: drop responses len>16k; enforce strict `<think>…</think>` pairing;
  verify correctness with rule-based + LLM-as-judge; hard-filter by student pass-rate threshold.
- SOTA with **~800 curated examples** (math 200+→diversity-augmented; code 230→925). QwQ-corpus mean
  token entropy 0.477 (median 0.090) vs R1-corpus 0.705 (median 0.302); QwQ-corpus mean token length
  12,431 vs R1 9,877 — yet length did *not* drive performance.
- No code link surfaced in the HTML; model released as NTele-R1-32B / NTele-32B-V1 (HF).
