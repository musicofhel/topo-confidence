# Triage brief — 2602.04428 — Fine-Grained Activation Steering: Steering Less, Achieving More

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04428
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC = 0.7731 — a *single* L19 direction is the correctness predictor)** — AUSteer's central empirical claim is that block-level activations (which is exactly what an L19 residual-stream DoM is) are *heterogeneous*: each dimension `xi` is the coefficient of a different weight column `W:,i`, and different `W:,i` push different output token distributions, so a vector-mean pulls helpful and harmful dimensions together. **Mechanism**: if the L19 residual contains a small subset of "correctness-pushing" AUs and a large subset of irrelevant/anticorrelated AUs, the DoM (mean-of-correct − mean-of-incorrect) is a *coarse* projection. A sparse subset of dimensions, ranked by AUSteer's activation-momentum score on the same correct/incorrect contrast, should beat 0.7731 — and the full L19 DoM should be *demonstrably suboptimal* relative to its own AU-pruned version. **Falsifier**: on cached `pathway11_h100/extracted/qwen15b_*_l19_*.npz`, compute activation momentum `s_i = max(r_pos, r_neg)` per residual dimension over MATH-500 correct vs incorrect prefill activations (5-fold OOF), select top-k AUs (k ∈ {5, 20, 50, 100}), refit a logistic probe restricted to those k dimensions, report AUROC. If any k-AU probe AUROC ≥ 0.79 (Δ ≥ 0.02 over 0.7731 with k ≪ 1536), F-2's "L19 DoM is the signal" framing is incomplete — the signal lives in a sparse AU subset and the rest is noise the DoM mean is forced to average over.

2. **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy)** — H-1 plans a CAA-style steering experiment that injects the full L19 prefill DoM as a residual-stream bias. AUSteer reports +1.85 to +2.41 average accuracy gains on commonsense + math benchmarks while steering ≤ 100 dimensions, and shows that *introducing detrimental dimensions* (Mixed Combination on BoolQ Fig 2a/2b) **drops** accuracy below the positive-only subset baseline. **Mechanism**: if H-1's planned full-vector intervention shows null or negative effect on MATH-500, that null is consistent with two distinct hypotheses — (a) DoM steering doesn't transfer to long-form math, or (b) the DoM is a coarse aggregator that mixes helpful and harmful AUs. AUSteer predicts (b). **Falsifier**: run H-1 in three arms — (i) full L19 DoM as additive bias (CAA-style), (ii) AUSteer-FFN top-50 AUs in L19 with multiplicative `x̂_i = xi + γi*xi`, (iii) "Mixed Combination" arm that intentionally adds 10 detrimental AUs to arm (ii). If (ii) > (i) and (iii) < (ii) on MATH-500 K=1, the CAA-style block-level framing of H-1 is wrong — H-1 must be reformulated at AU level before any steering null can be interpreted as "DoM doesn't move correctness."

3. **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046 — orthogonality is the geometric finding)** — AUSteer's theoretical argument (§3.3) is that each AU drives the model toward a distinct output token distribution; Fig 3 shows the pairwise KL divergence between two AUs (44 vs 84) **grows** monotonically with steering strength up to s = 100,000, demonstrating the AUs converge to non-overlapping distributions. **Mechanism**: if the prefill and final-token "directions" are each averages over differently-weighted-but-partially-overlapping AU subsets, then a near-zero cosine between two such averages is the expected statistical outcome (random sums of high-dimensional sparse subsets have low cosine), not evidence of geometrically separated correctness sub-circuits. **Falsifier**: compute activation momentum of (a) prefill L19 → correctness contrast, (b) final-token L19 → correctness contrast, on the same MATH-500 cache. Take the top-100 AUs from each, measure (i) Jaccard overlap and (ii) sign correlation of their `s_i` scores. If Jaccard ≥ 0.5 with positive sign correlation but cos(DoM_prefill, DoM_final) ≈ 0.046, F-3's orthogonality is a magnitude-cancellation artifact of mean-pooling and the "two separate circuits" interpretation collapses. (If Jaccard < 0.1, F-3 is reaffirmed at AU level — strengthening it.)

4. **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM)** — AUSteer's "steering less achieves more" works *within a single block*; it does not address whether a sparse multi-layer set of AUs beats a sparse single-layer set. But the paper's principle ("the right granularity is dimensional, not block-level") directly extrapolates to layer-stacks. **Mechanism**: if F-9's redundancy result holds because L19 is the maximally-discriminative *block*, but multi-layer AU pruning would reveal that 5 high-momentum AUs at L17 + 5 at L19 + 5 at L21 beat 50 AUs at L19 alone, then F-9 was an artifact of comparing block-coarse CoE-60 against block-coarse L19 — a fairer AU-pruned comparison would put CoE back ahead. **Falsifier**: run AU-localization with activation momentum on each of layers L15–L23 separately; compare AUROC of (i) top-50 AUs at L19 only, (ii) top-10 AUs at each of {L15, L17, L19, L21, L23} concatenated (= 50 dims, matched budget), (iii) full CoE-60. If (ii) > (i) by ≥ 0.02 AUROC, F-9's redundancy claim must be reframed as "block-level redundancy" and a sparse cross-layer probe wins.

5. **F-10 (PH on trained residual streams = Gaussian null)** — AUSteer's heterogeneity argument is that single dimensions carry interpretable signal (token-distribution control) even when the *full vector* looks structureless. **Mechanism**: PH on a high-dimensional residual stream pools all AUs together, and most AUs are task-irrelevant (uniform/noisy). PH at the Gaussian null is the predicted outcome of pooling many heterogeneous-content dimensions, not evidence the residuals carry no topological structure. **Falsifier**: restrict PH analysis to the top-50 high-momentum AUs at L19 (subspace projection), recompute persistence diagrams, compare against Gaussian null in the same subspace. If projected PH departs from null while full-residual PH does not, F-10's "PH = null" finding is a dimensional-noise effect, not a property of the trained signal. (Cheap test on cached NPZs.)

## Methodologies extracted

- **Atomic-Unit (AU) decomposition** — `y = Wx = Σ_i xi · W:,i`, where each column `W:,i` is an "AU" and `xi` is its scalar coefficient. Steering one dimension = steering one AU. Applies to MHA Q/K/V/O and FFN up/down projections uniformly.
  *Replication cost*: trivial (numpy column index).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct lens on cached L19 residuals; no extraction needed.

- **Activation-momentum localization** — for `N` contrastive (positive, negative) pairs, compute per-AU `m_j_i = x_i^pos − x_i^neg`; `r_pos = (1/N) Σ 1[m_j_i > 0]`, `r_neg = (1/N) Σ 1[m_j_i < 0]`; discriminative score `s_i = max(r_pos, r_neg)`. Counting-based, magnitude-invariant — solves the "magnitudes grow with depth" problem that defeats raw activation differences.
  *Replication cost*: ~30 min CPU on cached prefill L19 (correct/incorrect MATH-500 pairs).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct alternative ranking to logistic-probe weights; cheap.

- **Multiplicative adaptive steering** — `x̂_i = xi + γi · xi`, where `γi = α · r_pos` (or `−α · r_neg`). Sign-preserving, magnitude-adaptive; replaces ITI's additive `x̂ = x + α·v`. Ablation Table 3 shows this beats fixed-vector by 1.91 and fixed-strength by 0.92 on Gemma2-9B.
  *Replication cost*: trivial — drop-in replacement for any planned ITI/CAA injection.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly applicable to P10-FE1 and P2-FE1 steering arms.

- **Dimension Sweep diagnostic** — sample one of every 4 dims in MHA outputs / one of every 100 in FFN, steer each individually, plot accuracy vs dim. Reveals heterogeneity directly (Fig 2a/2b).
  *Replication cost*: ~1h CPU per (head, layer) on cached forward passes; ~2h H100 for live forward passes.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — diagnostic for whether L19 DoM is "spread" or "concentrated" across dimensions.

- **Pairwise KL convergence diagnostic** — set steering strength to a saturation value (e.g. s = 100,000), measure normalized KL between intermediate-strength outputs and the saturation output. Demonstrates each AU converges to its own token distribution (Fig 3).
  *Replication cost*: ~1h H100 per AU swept (forward passes).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes if we do AU-level steering; it's the cleanest sanity check that an AU is "doing one thing" rather than degrading generation.

- **Promotion-vs-suppression sign convention** — Appendix K shows promoting positive-momentum AUs consistently outperforms suppressing negative-momentum ones at matched budget. Sign matters.
  *Replication cost*: trivial (sign of γ).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — required configuration choice for any AU-steering arm.

## Approaches & framings

- **AU-as-column-of-W reframing** — recasts every block activation as a sparse coefficient over a basis of weight columns. Bridges to Geva et al. 2022 (FFN as concept-promoter): if FFN columns promote vocabulary concepts, AUSteer says only a sparse column subset matters per task. For our work, this reframes L19 DoM not as a *direction* but as *a set of weight columns selected by activation momentum* — a small change with large interpretability consequences.
- **"Steering less achieves more" principle** — directly contradicts the additive-magnitude intuition behind CAA / ITI. Aligns with our empirical pattern that *single-layer* L19 DoM matches CoE-60 (F-9) — AUSteer extrapolates that *single-AU subset of single-layer L19 DoM* should do even better.
- **Token-distribution-control as the unit of analysis** — frames each dimension by *what it pushes the output to say*, not by its activation magnitude or probe weight. We have no current framing in these terms; gives a vocabulary for "what does the prefill DoM mean" that is grounded in vocab-space behavior, not residual-space geometry.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Code: `https://github.com/zijian678/AUSteer` (released).
- Hyperparameters: `k` (number of AUs steered, capped at 100) and `α` (global strength factor); paper sweeps both per task.
- Two variants: **AUSteer-Head** (intervenes on MHA output dimensions of selected heads) and **AUSteer-FFN** (intervenes on FFN output dimensions).
- Activation momentum is computed *globally across layers*: the counting-based score `s_i ∈ [0, 1]` gives a uniform scale that survives the "activation magnitude grows with depth" problem.
- Multiplicative scaling `x̂ = x + γ·x` (sign-preserving, magnitude-adaptive) outperforms additive ITI-style by 1.91 in ablation on Gemma2-9B.
- Promote useful AUs > suppress unhelpful ones (Appendix K).
- LLMs validated: LLaMA2-7B-Chat, Gemma2-9B-it, Qwen3-8B (primary); LLaMA3.1-8B, LLaMA2-13B, Gemma2-27B, Qwen3-30B-A3B (sparse MoE), Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct (4-bit) (scalability). **Qwen2.5-1.5B / 7B (our targets) are not in the paper's evaluation suite.**
- Headline number: AUSteer-FFN +1.85 average accuracy over SADI on LLaMA2-7B; +1.91 on Gemma2-9B; +0.7 on Qwen3-8B. Single-dim 84 of head-7-layer-27 alone hits 74.53% on BoolQ vs SADI 73.70% on LLaMA2-7B-Chat.

## Replicable intermediates

The smallest cross-check we could run right now against AUSteer's headline claim using existing assets:

- **AU-level activation momentum on cached L19 prefill residuals**. Cached artifact: `pathway11_h100/extracted/qwen15b_*l19*.npz` (the source of F-2's 0.7731 AUROC). Script (~30 lines numpy):
  1. For each dimension `i ∈ [0, 1536)` of the residual at L19, prefill position:
     - For each MATH-500 problem, label as positive (correct K=1) or negative (incorrect K=1).
     - Build matched random pairs (size N).
     - Compute `m_j_i = x_i^pos − x_i^neg`, then `r_pos`, `r_neg`, `s_i = max`.
  2. Take top-k AUs (k = 5, 20, 50, 100). Refit logistic regression restricted to those k dims, 5-fold OOF.
  3. Compare to F-2's full L19 DoM AUROC = 0.7731.
- **Dimension sweep on the same cache** — pick L19 head 7 (or whichever single head is most predictive), sweep one of every 4 output dims, report per-dim AUROC. Reveals whether L19 prefill DoM is "concentrated" (a few high-AUROC AUs) or "spread" (many moderate-AUROC AUs).
- Both tests are CPU-only, no pod needed, < 1h compute.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2602.04428 — Fine-Grained Activation Steering: Steering Less, Achieving More (Feng et al., ICLR 2026)

**Relevance:** Directly bears on F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC = 0.7731), F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046), F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM), and H-1 (per-position DoM steering on MATH-500). The paper decomposes block-level activations (MHA, FFN, residual streams) into atomic units (AUs = columns of the weight matrix), claims each AU drives the model toward a distinct output token distribution, and proposes AUSteer — activation-momentum AU localization + multiplicative adaptive steering (`x̂_i = xi + γi*xi`) restricted to ≤ 100 AUs. It outperforms ITI, CAA, SADI, STA on commonsense (BoolQ, COPA, WinoGrande), math (SVAMP, MAWPS), and open-ended generation (RealToxicPrompts, BPO) on LLaMA2-7B-Chat / Gemma2-9B-it / Qwen3-8B (and scales to Qwen3-30B-A3B and Llama-3.3-70B). Qwen2.5 (our target) is not in the eval suite, but the methodology applies to any cached residual stream.
