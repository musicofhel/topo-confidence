# Triage brief — 2602.10496 — Low-Dimensional Execution Manifolds in Transformer Learning Dynamics: Evidence from Modular Arithmetic Tasks

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10496
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **vs F-10 (PH on trained residual streams is at Gaussian null).** The paper applies a *random-K-dimensional-basis baseline* (`ρ_rand`) as a control for trivial dimensionality artifacts, then reports that PCA bases of training trajectories capture **9.7× more commutator energy than random early in training, decaying to 2.1× late** (Fig. 5C). This is the same control logic F-10 used (Gaussian null on point clouds), but applied to a different invariant: **trajectory PCA effective rank with paired projection-fraction comparison** instead of persistent homology of fixed-step point clouds. *Mechanism of the refutation*: F-10's null result may be invariant-specific — PH on point clouds is degenerate in high-D Gaussian-like data (Reani et al. 2023 result already cited there), but trajectory PCA + random-basis comparison can detect structure PH cannot. *Falsifiable test*: on cached P11 1024-tok L19 hidden-state trajectories (one trajectory per token position over the 1024-tok generation), compute (a) effective rank at 90% variance, (b) `ρ_exec` (projection of step-to-step deltas onto trajectory PCA top-K basis) vs `ρ_rand` (10 random K-dim orthonormal bases). If `ρ_exec/ρ_rand > 1` and the gap is comparable to the paper's 2-10× range, F-10's "geometry is at the null" framing scoped to PH must be downgraded — the residual-stream trajectory has structured geometry detectable by other diagnostics.

2. **vs F-1 (Dimensional breathing universal across transformer architectures and scales).** §4.5 of the paper reports a direct architecture-dependence: "Adding MLP layers fundamentally disrupts the low-dimensional geometric picture. Standard transformers with both attention and MLP components exhibit higher-dimensional training dynamics, with no clear collapse onto a 3-4 dimensional subspace." Qwen-2.5-1.5B and 7B both have MLPs in every block, so the paper predicts our breathing signature (PR=67/88 peak on 1024-tok labels) should *not* match the clean attention-only manifold; if our breathing signature *does* arise specifically from MLP residual contribution, F-1's "universal" framing is misscoped. *Mechanism*: MLP layers introduce non-attention computation that reshapes the residual stream geometry; attention-only collapse is special. *Falsifiable test*: re-extract Qwen-2.5-1.5B L19 hidden states at 1024-tok with MLP output of layer 19 zeroed (or run an attention-only forward pass from cached pre-MLP states), recompute temporal PR. If breathing collapses or flattens, F-1 is MLP-driven, not attention-driven, and the cross-architecture universality claim needs scoping to "transformers with MLPs."

3. **vs F-2 + F-9 ("single L19 prefill DoM AUROC 0.7731" / "CoE-60 redundant with single-layer DoM").** Paper's central claim: "execution remains distributed across the low-dimensional manifold" (3-4 dims) and SAE latents capture only "auxiliary routing structure." The implication for inference-time correctness probes is that the *correctness signal should also be distributed across a 3-4 dim subspace*, not concentrated on a single direction at a single layer. F-2 treats correctness as one direction (DoM at L19 prefill); F-9 says CoE-60 collapses to that single direction. If the paper's "distributed execution" picture transfers, AUROC should rise monotonically as we add directions up to ~K=4 then plateau, and the gap between K=1 and K=4 should be substantial. *Falsifiable test*: on cached prefill L19 NPZs, fit logistic regression on top-K PCA components of correct-vs-incorrect activation deltas for K ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 16}. Report 5-fold OOF AUROC at each K. If K=4 gives ≥+0.02 AUROC over K=1 with random-basis control, the F-2 single-direction story is incomplete and DoM is one coordinate of a small execution manifold rather than *the* correctness direction. If K=1 is within 0.005 of K=16, F-2 is vindicated and the paper's "distributed" claim doesn't transfer to inference geometry.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-1 (breathing universal across architectures/scales).** Paper's §4.5 contradicts at the architecture axis (MLP disruption); §4.6 supports the *scale-with-task-complexity* axis (manifold dim 3-4 for addition vs 4-6 for multiplication, consistent with F-1's layered scale claim). Numerical anchor: our 1.5B/7B PR peaks of 67/88 are matrix-rank quantities; theirs are parameter-trajectory ranks. Different observables but framing tension is real.
- **F-5 (breathing is content-dependent, not AR-mechanics).** Paper's task-complexity scaling (§4.1) is direct corroborating evidence: manifold dimensionality tracks computational complexity, not architecture or seed. Reinforces F-5.
- **F-7 (D-bucket has distinctive collective signature).** Paper's *curriculum-learning-induced fragmentation* into "fragmented solution regions" with catastrophic forgetting (§4.5) offers an alternative D-bucket mechanism: D-bucket may be problems sitting in pretraining curriculum's fragmented region, not problems with distinctive activation geometry per se. Speculative but testable.
- **F-10 (PH at Gaussian null).** Paper's `ρ_exec/ρ_rand` random-basis baseline is a methodological alternative to PH-vs-null. Its 9.7× → 2.1× pattern shows non-null geometric structure on a different invariant. F-10 may be invariant-specific.
- **H-4 (breathing ↔ EoS sharpness-dimension correspondence).** Paper provides a *third* framing — "execution manifold as attractor in learning dynamics" — that competes with both EoS and our breathing. The paper's Fig. 3 attention-entropy collapse curve resembles our temporal PR curve qualitatively; both reflect parameter→state geometric tightening. H-4's claim of formal correspondence between breathing and EoS now has a third candidate to formally connect with.
- **H-13 (head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token circuits).** Paper's per-matrix effective-rank decomposition (Fig. 1: W_V/W_O collapse to dim 3, W_Q/W_K to dim 4-5) is a direct methodology for H-13 — per-attention-matrix geometric attribution rather than per-head ablation.
- **H-16 (Marchenko-Pastur bias correction preserves breathing).** Paper's `ρ_rand` random-basis correction is a methodological cousin of MP — both ask "is the observed structure above the dimensionality-trivial baseline?" Worth implementing as a cross-check on top of the MP correction the H-16 plan already specifies.
- **H-22 (Abstract-CoT compressed breathing).** Paper's MLP-disruption finding implies verbalization-driven computation lives partly in MLPs; if Abstract-CoT short-circuits verbalization, the part of breathing that survives may be the attention-only execution-manifold component.

## Methodologies extracted

- **PCA effective rank at 90% variance** — `r_90 = min{k : Σ λ_i / Σ λ_total ≥ 0.90}` over flattened weight-matrix-trajectory snapshots `w_i ∈ ℝ^{d²}`. *Replication cost*: 10min CPU on cached NPZs. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly maps to our trajectory analysis on hidden-state sequences if we substitute hidden-state-over-tokens for weight-trajectory-over-steps.
- **Random-K-dim orthonormal-basis baseline (`ρ_rand`)** — average projection fraction over 10 random K-dim bases as a control for the trivial `√(K/P)` dimensionality artifact. *Replication cost*: 5min CPU. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — adds a control we don't currently use in PR or DoM analysis. Can detect spurious geometry attributable purely to dimensionality.
- **SGD commutator [∇_A, ∇_B] = θ_AB - θ_BA** — measures path-dependence of optimization across minibatches. *Replication cost*: requires retraining or fine-tuning Qwen with intermediate-checkpoint snapshots; H100 day at minimum. *We'd plausibly run this*: no — not aligned with our inference-time probing program; only relevant if we ever do continued pretraining or fine-tuning of Qwen.
- **Normalized commutator defect `D = ‖δ‖ / (‖η∇L_A‖·‖η∇L_B‖)`** — scale-invariant non-integrability metric. *Replication cost*: bundled with above. *We'd plausibly run this*: no, same reason.
- **PCA-trajectory-basis projection decomposition** — split commutator into in-execution-subspace `‖` and orthogonal-staging `⊥` components via `Π = BB^T`. *Replication cost*: 30min CPU once trajectory snapshots exist. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes for inference-time hidden-state trajectories; the per-step delta of the residual stream during generation is an analog of their parameter-step trajectory, and the same decomposition could distinguish "correctness-direction motion" from "noise" cleanly.
- **Sparse autoencoder + targeted single-latent ablation on intermediate activations** — train SAE with `L = ‖x̂ - x‖² + λ‖h‖₁`, then zero individual latents and measure accuracy delta at early/mid/late checkpoints. *Replication cost*: 4h H100 SAE training + 1d CPU ablation analysis (already partially scoped in P10-FE5). *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — already in queue as P10-FE5 for L19 prefill; this paper provides a published-method comparison and a prediction (small ablation impact, mostly mid-training).
- **Per-attention-matrix effective-rank tracking (W_Q, W_K, W_V, W_O separately)** — Fig. 1 methodology. *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached weights — but Qwen weights are 28 layers × 4 matrices, not a training trajectory. Requires either fine-tuning checkpoints or applying an analog (per-matrix activation contribution rank). *We'd plausibly run this*: yes for activation-side analog; no for weight-side without fine-tuning data.
- **Attention entropy at EOS token over training** — `H(A) = -Σ A_ij log A_ij`. *Replication cost*: 1h CPU on cached attention. *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — single-pass inference attention (no training trajectory) gives us only one snapshot; less informative than the temporal version.
- **Mixed-task vs curriculum training comparison** — train identical models with simultaneous mixed difficulties vs progressive curriculum, compare manifold dimensionality and forgetting. *Replication cost*: full retraining; H100 days. *We'd plausibly run this*: no — doesn't match our pretrained-Qwen-only program.

## Approaches & framings

- **Execution manifold as attractor in learning dynamics.** Reframes overparameterization: excess parameters serve as "buffer" / "staging space" absorbing non-commutativity, while computation lives in 3-4 directions. *Intersection*: directly competes with our F-2 (single-direction DoM) and F-9 (single-layer redundancy) framings — suggests correctness is a manifold, not a direction.
- **Parallel exploration interpretation of overparameterization (§5.1).** Wide parameter spaces let the model maintain *multiple candidate solutions* in orthogonal staging dimensions, then consolidate onto one execution manifold. *Intersection*: connects to H-19 (C_exact verification routing) — verification could be a consolidation signal between candidate executions in residual-stream staging space.
- **Grokking as discovery of integrable subspace (§2 + §5.1).** Grokking corresponds to finding the low-dim subspace where compositional operations commute. *Intersection*: gives us a mechanism for breathing-as-grokking-precursor (H-related; not currently in HYPOTHESES.md), if breathing is the inference-time analog of subspace consolidation.
- **Execution-routing separation via SAE.** SAE latents cleanly separate "auxiliary routing" (sparsifiable, interpretable, weakly causal) from "execution" (distributed, dense, causally critical). *Intersection*: predicts our DoM signal is *routing*, not *execution*, since DoM lives along a single sparse direction. If true, F-2 captures a routing correlate of correctness, not the computation itself — could explain why DoM is correlational (P11-FE3 patching test pending).
- **Architecture × geometry tradeoff (§5.3).** MLP disrupts low-dim geometric simplicity but presumably enables expressivity. *Intersection*: speaks directly to our cross-architecture universality claims (F-1) — clean breathing geometry may be inversely correlated with MLP load.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Embedding dim `d = 128`; sequence length `T = 32`.
- Attention-only transformer: `W_Q, W_K, W_V, W_O ∈ ℝ^{d×d}` per layer; 5 layers in Fig. 1.
- Optimizer: vanilla SGD, learning rate `η = 0.001`, batch size 64.
- Checkpoint cadence: every 100 steps; total 20k steps (loss converges by ~5k, accuracy 100% by ~3k).
- 20 random seeds per condition for variance reporting.
- `ρ_rand` averages over **10 random orthonormal bases** at each measurement step.
- Code/data: `https://github.com/skydancerosel/bubble-modadd` (acknowledged in §Conclusion — a reference to verify; commit history would tell us how reproducible the artifacts are).
- Effective rank uses 90% variance threshold (not 99%, not Marchenko-Pastur-corrected). Worth explicitly comparing if we adopt their methodology.

## Replicable intermediates

- **`ρ_exec/ρ_rand` analog on cached P11 L19 NPZs** — treat the per-token-position L19 hidden-state sequence as our "trajectory," compute PCA top-K basis on stacked deltas across the 1024-tok generation per problem, then compute projection-fraction of step-to-step delta vectors onto that basis vs onto 10 random K-dim bases. Cached artifact: `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/extraction_*.npz` (per-token L19 activations). Script: ~50 lines of numpy on top of the existing extraction loaders. CPU-only.
- **Per-matrix effective-rank on Qwen attention contributions** — for each layer's attention output, compute the rank-90 of the contribution rank across our 500 MATH-500 problems (rather than a training trajectory). This is the activation-side analog of the paper's weight-trajectory analysis. Cached artifact: same NPZs, but requires re-extraction of pre-output attention contributions (we don't currently cache W_O input separately). Cost: probably 30min CPU + 2h pre-extraction H100.
- **Multi-direction K-PCA probe sweep on prefill DoM** — fit logistic regression on top-K PCA features of correct-vs-incorrect prefill activations, K ∈ {1,2,4,8,16}. Cached artifact: `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/results.json` activation matrices. Cost: 1h CPU.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2602.10496 — Low-Dimensional Execution Manifolds in Transformer Learning Dynamics: Evidence from Modular Arithmetic Tasks (Xu, 2026)

**Relevance:** Studies attention-only transformer training dynamics on synthetic modular arithmetic and finds parameter trajectories collapse to 3-4 dim "execution manifolds" with a clean random-basis-controlled diagnostic (`ρ_exec/ρ_rand` 9.7× early → 2.1× late). The geometric framing parallels our breathing/DoM findings on a different observable (parameter-trajectory PCA vs hidden-state PR), and the paper introduces three methodological tools we can adapt: (a) random-K-dim-basis projection control, (b) per-matrix effective-rank decomposition, and (c) targeted SAE-latent ablation as a routing-vs-execution discriminator. Critically, the paper claims MLPs *disrupt* this clean low-dim collapse and that execution is *distributed* across the manifold rather than localized to a single direction — both claims directly tension F-1 (universality) and F-2 (single L19 DoM).

**Key claim we tested:** That attention-driven transformer geometry is intrinsically low-dimensional with structure detectable above random-basis baseline, and that this geometry is disrupted by MLPs and not localized to single SAE-recoverable directions.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — three concrete experiments (P11-FE81 random-basis control on residual-stream trajectories, P11-FE82 MLP-ablation breathing test, P11-FE83 multi-direction probe sweep) test whether the paper's claims transfer from attention-only modular-arithmetic training-dynamics to MLP-equipped Qwen inference geometry.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE81, P11-FE82, P11-FE83, P11-FE84; H-72, H-73, H-74; relates to P10-FE5 (SAE on L19) and to H-16 (MP correction, methodological cousin).

**Status:** TO TEST
