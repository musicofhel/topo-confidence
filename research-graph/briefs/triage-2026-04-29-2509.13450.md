# Triage brief — 2509.13450 — SteeringSafety: A Systematic Safety Evaluation Framework of Representation Steering in LLMs

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13450
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Predictive DoM ≠ causal/steerable DoM (refutes the implicit F-2→H-1 bridge).**
   F-2 reports prefill L19 DoM as a probe-AUROC-optimal direction (0.7731). H-1 then
   assumes that *the same* direction will move MATH-500 accuracy under per-position
   addition/ablation. SteeringSafety's pipeline explicitly separates **direction
   generation** (DiffInMeans → our DoM is exactly this) from **direction selection**
   (grid search across 25–80th percentile of layers, KL<0.1 on Alpaca filter). Their
   results show that across Gemma/Llama/Qwen the layer that survives the KL filter is
   *frequently not the layer that gives the cleanest DiffInMeans signal*, and that bias
   steering shows effectiveness <20% even when the underlying direction is well-formed.
   *Mechanism*: probe-optimal layers can require KL>0.1 perturbation magnitude to move
   behavior, in which case the direction is filtered out for steering use. *Test*: at
   L19 on Qwen-2.5-1.5B, compute the per-token KL-on-Alpaca induced by directional
   ablation along our prefill DoM; if it exceeds 0.1, then F-2's direction is excluded
   from any defensible steering protocol — i.e., H-1 must search a *different* layer.

2. **Entanglement makes "DoM steering raises MATH accuracy" an underspecified claim
   (refutes H-1's success criterion).**
   The paper shows every method × model × perspective combination tested produces
   substantial entanglement: social-behavior degradation up to 76%, hallucination
   steering shifts political views, jailbreaking compromises commonsense morality.
   H-1 is currently scored only on MATH-500 K=1 accuracy. *Mechanism*: even if DoM
   steering nudges 1.5B from 48.6%→55% on MATH-500, the same intervention is likely
   to wreck out-of-distribution capabilities (their reasoning entanglement is <2% on
   ARC-C/GPQA but >10% on social/normative axes). *Test*: any H-1 evaluation must
   add a SteeringSafety-style entanglement panel — at minimum ARC-C, GPQA, TruthfulQA
   — before the steering result is admissible.

3. **The fixed-vector framing (F-3 corollary) is incompatible with conditional
   steering's Pareto improvements.**
   F-3 establishes prefill_DoM ⊥ final_DoM (cos = 0.046). Pathway 10 v2 already
   noted that DoM rotates during generation. SteeringSafety shows that **conditional
   steering** (CAST, Lee et al. 2024) — apply intervention only when the input's
   similarity to the direction crosses a threshold — Pareto-dominates global steering
   on harmfulness and hallucination across all three models. *Mechanism*: if the
   "right" intervention requires gating on a learned input feature, then a single
   global DoM (whether prefill or final) cannot be the right object — the right
   object is a (direction, gate) tuple. *Test*: re-run H-1 with a CAST-style
   threshold derived from prefill activation similarity; if conditional H-1
   beats global H-1 by >1σ in MATH-500 accuracy at matched K, F-3's "fixed
   orthogonal directions" framing is incomplete.

4. **DIM-as-method works on small Qwen, but the layer is not 67% depth (refutes
   any "L19 is universal" reading of F-2).**
   They run Qwen-2.5-1.5B and Qwen-2.5-3B with the same 25–80th-percentile layer
   search and find the best layers vary by model and by perspective. Our L19 on a
   28-layer 1.5B model is at 68% depth — inside their range but not the typical
   middle-layers (~50%) optimum reported across their results. *Mechanism*: probe
   AUROC at L19 may be a model-and-task coincidence, not a structural fact about
   "where correctness lives". *Test*: replicate F-2's logistic-probe AUROC sweep
   across all 28 layers using *only* our cached prefill activations; if the AUROC
   curve is broadly flat from L14 to L22 (≥0.74), then F-2's "L19" specificity is
   noise. If sharply peaked at L19, F-2 stands.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)** — DIM in this paper is structurally
  identical to the DoM construction (mean of correct - mean of incorrect activations).
  The paper's empirical finding "DIM shows consistent effectiveness" is independent
  corroboration of the DiffInMeans construction at the *direction-extraction* level.
  But their effectiveness numbers for bias steering (<20%) suggest probe-AUROC and
  steering-effectiveness are weakly correlated, which constrains how F-2 should be
  read.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046)** — They find that direction
  application position matters: CAA uses POST_INSTRUCTION, DIM/PCA use ALL tokens,
  LAT applies cumulatively across layers. This is consistent with F-3's claim that
  the residual stream contains multiple non-aligned directions that must each be
  selected for the right token positions. The orthogonality finding becomes a
  *prediction* — different application positions should be optimal for prefill vs
  final-token directions.
- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy)** — Direct method
  precedent. The paper's exact pipeline (DIM direction → directional ablation at all
  tokens, all layers, with KL<0.1 Alpaca filter) is the canonical protocol H-1
  should use. Without the KL filter and conditional gating, H-1 is methodologically
  weaker than current state-of-the-art.
- **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes for refusal)** — They evaluate
  hallucination steering (FaithEval intrinsic + extrinsic, PreciseWikiQA) and
  observe that PCA + LAT do better than DIM/ACE on intrinsic hallucination. This
  matters for whether label-free directions (H-12's premise) can match supervised
  DoM at hallucination tasks.
- **H-13 (head-level attribution)** — They operate purely at the layer level
  (residual-stream additions). Their results imply head-level attribution would
  require an orthogonal experimental setup, and SteeringSafety provides the
  evaluation harness for that downstream work.

## Methodologies extracted

- **DiffInMeans (DIM) direction generation** — mean(activations | positive class) −
  mean(activations | negative class), one direction per layer.
  *Replication cost*: 20min CPU on cached prefill activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — already operationally identical to our DoM extraction.
- **Affine Concept Editing (ACE)** — DIM + DirectionalAblation + an affine offset to
  the residual stream (Marshall et al. 2024). One additional bias term per layer.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU on cached prefill activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — minimal cost, would test whether the affine
  addition improves selective-prediction beyond F-2's pure direction.
- **DirectionalAblation** — project residual stream onto direction's complement
  (`x_new = x - (x·d/d·d) d`), applied at every token.
  *Replication cost*: 30min on H100 with K=1 generation, MATH-500.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — this is the canonical "remove the direction"
  intervention and the right primitive for any H-1 protocol.
- **CAA-style ActAdd at POST_INSTRUCTION tokens** — add direction × scale to
  residual stream only at tokens after the user instruction.
  *Replication cost*: 30min on H100.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — competing protocol vs. ALL-tokens DIM ablation.
- **PCA-direction (top eigenvector of correctness-class activation covariance)**
  *Replication cost*: 20min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — sanity-checks whether DoM and PC1 are aligned.
- **LAT (Linear Artificial Tomography)** — RepE-format prompts, direction extraction
  via per-prompt activations, applied cumulatively across a sequence of layers.
  *Replication cost*: 2h H100 (needs RepE-format prompt rewrite).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: marginal — only if cumulative-layer steering is on the
  roadmap.
- **Conditional steering (CAST)** — apply intervention only when input activation
  similarity to a "trigger direction" exceeds learned threshold. Wraps any of the
  above.
  *Replication cost*: 1–2h CPU + 1h H100 for evaluation.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — ROI is high because it Pareto-dominates global
  steering in their results, and gives H-1 a defensible knob for entanglement.
- **KL-divergence check on Alpaca for direction-validity filtering** — drop any
  candidate (layer, direction, scale) tuple whose final-token KL on Alpaca prompts
  exceeds 0.1.
  *Replication cost*: 30min H100 across our existing direction candidates.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — cheap and gates whether F-2's direction is even
  *eligible* as a steering vector by current-best-practice criteria.
- **Effectiveness / Entanglement aggregate metrics** (Eq. 1 + 2 in §2.1) — normalized
  improvement on target perspective (effectiveness), L2-aggregated deviation across
  out-of-distribution perspectives (entanglement).
  *Replication cost*: trivial once we have multi-task evals.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — adopt as the success metric for any future H-1
  variant.
- **Grid search over 25–80th percentile of layers, step 2** — restrict candidate
  layers to mid-network.
  *Replication cost*: trivial; we already have all-layer activations cached.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — a sweep would directly test whether L19 is the
  AUROC-peak or just a single sample.

## Approaches & framings

- **Direction generation × selection × application as orthogonal axes.** The paper
  decomposes steering into three independent choices. Our project currently
  treats DoM as a monolith ("a direction at L19 used the way DoM was extracted"),
  which conflates extraction and application. Adopting this decomposition would
  let us write F-2/F-3/H-1 in terms that compose with the literature.
- **Effectiveness ⊥ Entanglement, both required for any steering claim.**
  Their Eq. 1 + Eq. 2 framing means that *every* future selective-prediction or
  steering claim in our project should report not only the in-domain metric (e.g.,
  MATH-500 K=1 accuracy or AUROC) but also a vector of out-of-domain deviations.
  This intersects all of F-2, F-8, H-1, H-19.
- **Conditional vs. global intervention as a Pareto axis.** Their conditional
  steering result is the strongest practical takeaway. If we want any of our
  steering hypotheses (H-1, H-19) to be competitive with current literature, the
  default protocol should be conditional, not global.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Code: <https://github.com/wang-research-lab/SteeringSafety>.
- Layer-search range: 25–80th percentile, step 2. For 28-layer Qwen-2.5-1.5B that
  maps to layers 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, 19, 21 — note L19 is *inside* the range but
  not centered.
- Direction extraction uses **input** to the transformer block (pre-LN, pre-attn).
  Confirm whether our cached activations are pre- or post-LN and reconcile before
  any cross-comparison.
- KL divergence threshold: 0.1 on the *last-token probabilities* over Alpaca
  prompts. Average across prompts; reject (layer, direction, scale) above 0.1.
- Application positions: ALL (every token), POST_INSTRUCTION (tokens after user
  prompt), or cumulative across a sequence of layers. Method-dependent.
- Models all use IT/Instruct variants; our P11 work is on Qwen-2.5-1.5B-Instruct
  + Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct, so format compatibility is good.
- Conditional steering implementation follows CAST (Lee et al. 2024); they use
  similarity-threshold gating computed from a held-out trigger set.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2509.13450 — SteeringSafety: A Systematic Safety Evaluation Framework of Representation Steering in LLMs (Siu, Crispino, Park, Henry, Wang, Liu, Song, Wang, 2025)

**Relevance:** Direct methodological precedent and benchmark for any P11 steering
experiment. The paper systematizes five training-free steering methods (DIM, ACE,
CAA, PCA, LAT) into a direction generation × selection × application decomposition,
runs them across three models (Gemma-2-2B, Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen-2.5-7B; plus
Qwen-2.5-1.5B/3B for scaling) on 17 datasets across seven safety perspectives, and
quantifies effectiveness (in-domain) vs. entanglement (out-of-domain side effects).
Core findings: DIM is consistent; all methods entangle substantially (social
behaviors degrade up to 76%, hallucination steering shifts political views,
jailbreaking compromises commonsense morality); KL<0.1 Alpaca filter is required to
keep directions steering-eligible; conditional steering (CAST) Pareto-dominates
global steering. Directly bears on F-2 (DIM is structurally our DoM construction),
F-3 (their direction-application axis is the right vocabulary for our orthogonality
finding), and H-1 (our planned per-position DoM steering must adopt their KL filter
and entanglement panel to be defensible).

**Key claim we tested:** Representation steering methods produce reliable
in-distribution effectiveness only when filtered by KL<0.1 on Alpaca, and even then
they entangle substantially across out-of-distribution safety axes.

**Our result:** **TO TEST.** Three concrete experiments are proposed: P11-FE3450
(layer-AUROC sweep), P11-FE3451 (KL-on-Alpaca check for our L19 prefill DoM), and
P11-FE3452 (smallest-possible H-1 ablation prototype). Until those run, we cannot
say whether F-2's direction is steering-eligible or whether H-1 is methodologically
viable.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE3450, P11-FE3451, P11-FE3452, P11-FE3453, P11-FE3454,
H-13450, H-13451, H-13452, H-1.

**Status:** TO TEST
