# Triage brief — 2406.15927 — Semantic Entropy Probes: Robust and Cheap Hallucination Detection in LLMs

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15927
Triaged: 2026-04-29

Authors: Jannik Kossen, Jiatong Han, Muhammed Razzak, Lisa Schut, Shreshth Malik, Yarin Gal (OATML, Oxford). Preprint, 2024.

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-3 (prefill/final-token DoM orthogonality, cos = 0.046).** Kossen et al. find that a *single* linear probe trained on hidden states at the **TBG** (token-before-generating, i.e. last input token) position and the same probe family trained at the **SLT** (second-last-token of generation) both achieve high AUROC for the *same* target — binarized semantic entropy. If their TBG and SLT probe weight vectors are not orthogonal but instead point in similar directions (i.e. a single "uncertainty" axis active at both ends of the sequence), this directly contradicts our cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046 finding. **Mechanism**: our orthogonality might be an artifact of supervising on *correctness* rather than *uncertainty* — semantic entropy is symmetric across positions, correctness is not. **Falsifying experiment**: train SEPs at TBG and SLT on Qwen-2.5-1.5B / MATH-500 (K=10 high-T samples for SE labels), compute cos(w_TBG, w_SLT). If cos > 0.5, F-3 is uncertainty-blind, not a property of the residual stream.

2. **Refutes F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731 framed as "L19 specifically matters").** Their Figure 2 shows AUROC monotonically rising across layers with peaks at mid-to-late layers (Llama-2-7B: layers ~17-25 of 32 are flat at AUROC ≈ 0.8 for TriviaQA), and a Llama-2-7B BioASQ outlier where *early* layers already saturate. **Mechanism**: their layer-AUROC curves are not narrowly peaked at one layer; the "L19 is special" claim may collapse if the AUROC vs layer curve on Qwen-2.5-1.5B is similarly broad. **Falsifying experiment**: re-extract DoM AUROC per layer for L0…L27 on Qwen-1.5B (we have caches), check whether the curve is sharply peaked at L19 or broadly flat across L15–L23. If the latter, F-2's specificity is overstated and the geometry is layer-generic, not layer-specific.

3. **Refutes F-8 (selective prediction via prefill DoM at 71.6% / 50% coverage).** Kossen et al. show that **SE-supervised probes generalize 7-10 AUROC points better OOD than accuracy-supervised probes** (Tab. 2). F-8's selective predictor is accuracy-supervised (DoM trained against MATH-500 correctness labels, OOF 5-fold). **Mechanism**: if we re-train a probe on the same Qwen / MATH-500 hidden states with binarized-SE targets instead of binary correctness, OOD generalization to GSM-8K or AIME-shape problems should improve under the SEP framing. If it does, F-8 needs to be re-scoped: "prefill DoM gives 71.6% on the *training distribution only*", with SEP-style supervision the canonical method for cross-domain selective prediction. **Falsifying experiment**: on Qwen-1.5B / MATH-500 prompts, sample K=10 high-T completions, NLI-cluster (DeBERTa or GPT-4 entailment), compute discrete SE per problem, binarize via Eq. (5) split, train logistic regression at L19. Test (a) in-distribution AUROC vs 0.7731, (b) train on MATH-500-by-category subset, evaluate held-out category vs accuracy-supervised baseline.

4. **Refutes the "DoM = correctness direction" reading of F-2 / F-8 entirely.** SEPs work *equivalently well* at TBG (no generated tokens at all) and SLT (full generation completed) — implying the residual stream encodes *semantic uncertainty* before the model commits to a particular output. If DoM is in fact aligned with the SEP direction (not with correctness), then we have been misinterpreting the geometry: it captures "model is unsure across semantic clusters" rather than "model will be wrong on this problem." **Falsifying experiment**: compute cos(prefill_DoM, w_SEP_TBG) on Qwen-1.5B / MATH-500 hidden states. If cos > 0.7, the prefill correctness signal is downstream of a more general semantic-uncertainty axis, and F-2 should be reframed as a corollary, not a primary finding.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2** (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731): SEPs at TBG are the direct semantic-entropy-supervised analog of our DoM. They report AUROC 0.7-0.95 across QA tasks at TBG (Figure 3). Our 0.7731 sits at the low end of their range — consistent with MATH-500 being harder than QA, but the *mechanism* (linear probe on last-input-token hidden state) is identical.
- **F-3** (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046): Kossen et al.'s near-equivalence of TBG and SLT probe AUROC is *suggestive* (not conclusive) that their effective directions overlap. We don't have their probe weights, so this is testable not refuted.
- **F-8** (selective-prediction at 71.6% / coverage 0.5): SEPs target hallucination detection / abstention — same use-case as F-8's selective predictor, with an SE-supervised twist that matters most OOD.
- **F-9** (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM): Kossen et al. ablate "Hidden State Alternatives" (Appendix, Fig. A.11): probing the residual stream or MLP outputs is *worse* than probing post-norm hidden states. This is a methodology nuance our P10 experiments did not control for — we always probe residual stream. Worth testing whether our DoM AUROC improves if we probe post-norm hidden state instead.
- **H-12** (Semantic-entropy probes (label-free) replace supervised DoM for refusal): This paper is *the* concrete realization of H-12. Promote H-12 from PARKED to HIGH if not already.
- **H-19** (C_exact verification routing beats C_infer scaling): SEPs at TBG are a candidate routing signal — at K=1 forward-pass cost they decide whether to spend more compute. If SEPs > prefill DoM as routing signal, P11-FE19 should switch supervision target.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Semantic entropy supervision via discrete SE (Farquhar et al. 2024)** — sample N=10 generations at T=1, NLI-cluster (DeBERTa-Large for entailment), compute discrete SE = -Σ p(C_k) log p(C_k) where p(C_k) = |cluster_k|/N. Use as continuous probe target, then binarize (see next).
  *Replication cost*: ~30min H100 for 500 MATH-500 problems × 10 generations on Qwen-1.5B (we already have K=8 sampling cached for some experiments — re-use if compatible).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — directly testable; produces SE labels that don't require ground truth.

- **"Best split" binarization of continuous SE (Eq. 5)** — for binarizing continuous semantic entropy into hi/lo classes for logistic-regression supervision, choose threshold γ* minimizing within-class variance (regression-tree splitting objective). Avoids forcing a 50/50 even split that may straddle the natural mode.
  *Replication cost*: ~5min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — small ablation, useful for any probe we train on continuous targets (PR amplitude, dimensional breathing magnitude, etc.).

- **TBG vs SLT probing positions** — train two probes, one at last token of input (no generation needed), one at last token of generated answer. TBG enables single-forward-pass refusal; SLT typically slightly better but requires full generation.
  *Replication cost*: ~20min CPU on cached NPZs (we have prefill activations; SLT activations may need re-extraction).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — direct test of F-3 orthogonality.

- **Counterfactual context-addition validation** — to confirm probe captures SE rather than spurious surface features, add the gold context to the prompt and check that p(high-SE) drops accordingly. Their TriviaQA result: p(high-SE) shifts from ~0.9 to a lower distribution when context is added (accuracy 26% → 78%, ground-truth SE 1.84 → 0.50).
  *Replication cost*: ~20min CPU; need a MATH-500 analog (e.g., add solution sketch or worked example to prompt).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes, but harder** — MATH-500 doesn't have a natural "context" field; we'd need to construct one (hint, partial solution).

- **Leave-one-out OOD evaluation across QA tasks** — train probe on N-1 tasks, evaluate on held-out task. Methodologically clean way to test generalization. We currently only do within-MATH-500 5-fold OOF.
  *Replication cost*: ~1h CPU per held-out partition. Requires us to define MATH-500 categories or use AIME / GSM-8K as held-outs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — directly addresses a known F-2 weakness.

## Approaches & framings

- **Predict uncertainty, not correctness, for OOD generalization.** Their core conceptual move: SE is intrinsic to the model's distribution over semantic meanings, while correctness depends on extrinsic ground truth that may be domain-specific. SE-supervised probes therefore capture a more transferable signal. This *directly* reframes F-2/F-8 as accuracy-supervised special cases and motivates a re-supervision experiment.
- **Single-pass uncertainty quantification.** Frame the practical contribution as eliminating the 5-10× cost of sampling-based SE. For our project, the analog is: prefill DoM is a single-forward-pass refusal signal — same cost story, different supervision.
- **Probes as model-state diagnostics, not classifiers.** They argue SEPs are evidence that hidden states *implicitly encode* semantic uncertainty. This framing supports our F-2 narrative if we adopt the SE-supervised version: hidden states encode uncertainty, prefill DoM is one correctness-projected slice of that.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- **Probe**: scikit-learn LogisticRegression, default L2 + LBFGS. No hyperparameter sweep claimed — a deliberately minimal probe.
- **Hidden state**: probe collected at every layer of every model; figures show AUROC-vs-layer curves. Probe operates on the *post-norm hidden state*, not the residual stream or MLP output (Appendix A.11 ablation prefers hidden state).
- **N=10 generations at T=1** for SE label computation; entailment via DeBERTa-Large for short-form, GPT-3.5 for long-form.
- **Binarization threshold γ*** chosen per dataset by Eq. (5) (within-class variance minimization). Soft labelling tried, did not improve (Appendix B).
- **Models**: Llama-2-7B/70B, Mistral-7B, Phi-3-3.8B (short-form); Llama-2-70B + Llama-3-70B (long-form). No Qwen, no math-specialized models — direct test on our setup is novel.
- **Code**: https://github.com/OATML/semantic-entropy-probes (per linkforge metadata).
- **Headline numbers** (from Tabs 1 & 2): in-distribution, SEP ≈ accuracy probe (∆AUROC ≈ ±2); OOD generalization (leave-one-task-out), SEP > accuracy probe by +2.2 to +10.5 AUROC points across all six (model, generation-length) configurations.
- **Counterfactual sanity check**: TriviaQA + context drops p(high-SE) (Fig. 5); accuracy 26% → 78%, SE 1.84 → 0.50.

## 2406.15927 — Semantic Entropy Probes: Robust and Cheap Hallucination Detection in LLMs (Kossen et al., 2024)

**Relevance:** Direct methodological comparator to F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731) and F-8 (selective prediction). Trains a logistic-regression probe on hidden states at the same TBG position we call "prefill" and at the SLT position we call "final-token." Crucial difference: their supervision target is *binarized semantic entropy* (computed via NLI-clustering of K=10 high-T samples), not correctness. Headline empirical claim: SE-supervised probes match accuracy-supervised probes in-distribution but beat them by 2-10 AUROC points OOD across 4 QA tasks × 5 models. The OOD generalization gap is the most consequential signal for our project, because F-2 / F-8 are accuracy-supervised and currently evaluated only within MATH-500 5-fold OOF — never tested under category-level domain shift.

**Key claim we tested:** "Semantic entropy supervision yields probes that generalize OOD better than correctness supervision, with no in-distribution penalty, while operating at single-forward-pass cost."

**Our result:** **TO TEST.** Their TriviaQA / SQuAD / BioASQ / NQ Open domain is QA (short factoid answers, 15-100 chars), not long-CoT math reasoning. The +7-10 OOD gap could be QA-specific (knowledge-domain correlations are easier to overfit than math-category correlations) or a general property of SE-supervision. Direct test on Qwen-1.5B / MATH-500 is in-budget (FE69, FE71) and would either reframe F-2 or confirm that math-reasoning DoM is supervision-invariant. Their TBG vs SLT near-equivalence also raises a refutation seam against F-3 (cos = 0.046).

**Related experiments:** P11-FE69 (replicate SEPs on Qwen / MATH-500), P11-FE70 (TBG vs SLT cosine), P11-FE71 (leave-one-category-out OOD), P11-FE72 (post-norm hidden state ablation), H-92, H-93, H-12 (semantic-entropy probes for refusal — promote from PARKED if currently parked).
