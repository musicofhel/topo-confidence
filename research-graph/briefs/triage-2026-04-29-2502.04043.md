## Refutations

1. **F-2 (single-direction L19 DoM is the right summary).** FLORAIN replaces the linear DoM translation used by ITI (Li 2024b) with an ellipsoid + Mahalanobis projection: `M_q = {x : (x − μ̂_q)^T Σ̂_q^{-1} (x − μ̂_q) ≤ ρ_q}`. The empirical wins over ITI on TruthfulQA imply the local covariance Σ̂_q carries signal beyond the mean-difference direction. Mechanism: F-2's headline (AUROC 0.7731 OOF on Qwen-2.5-1.5B at L19) comes from a *first-order* statistic (DoM = μ̂⁺ − μ̂⁻); a Mahalanobis distance on the same activations would weight residual directions by `Σ̂^{-1}` — if that lifts AUROC by more than ~1pp, F-2's "single direction" framing is reductive. Falsifiable test: build μ̂⁺ (correct, n=243) and μ̂⁻ (incorrect, n=257) at L19 prefill from existing P11 NPZs, fit Ledoit-Wolf-shrunk Σ̂, compute Mahalanobis-distance OOF AUROC, compare to 0.7731.

2. **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500).** H-1's implicit method is *linear additive* steering with a fixed direction. FLORAIN's intervention is *input-dependent* and nonlinear: `f(a) = (I + L(a)R^T) a + s` with `L(a) = tanh(W∘a + b)·R^T`. FLORAIN's improvement over additive ITI on every Llama variant predicts the same gap should appear if H-1 were extended to MATH-500: an input-dependent low-rank map should beat fixed-vector DoM steering. If it does, H-1 as currently specified (fixed vector) is the wrong null and its expected null effect is a method artefact, not evidence about MATH-500. Falsifiable test: train FLORAIN (single layer L19, Qwen-2.5-1.5B, MATH-500 correct/incorrect as desirable/undesirable) and measure Δ K=1 accuracy versus fixed-vector DoM steering on a held-out fold.

3. **F-3 (prefill ⊥ final-token DoM, cos 0.046).** F-3 reads orthogonality as evidence of *two distinct mechanisms* across positions. FLORAIN's framework collapses both positions into a single ellipsoid M_q over pooled activations and gets a clean separation. Mechanism: orthogonality of the *means* (first-order) does not imply distinct *regions* (second-order). If a single ellipsoid trained on activations from both positions classifies correctness as well as two separately-trained ellipsoids, F-3's "two mechanisms" interpretation is artefactual — the directions decouple but the regions don't. Falsifiable test: fit μ̂⁺_pre, μ̂⁺_fin, Σ̂_pre, Σ̂_fin at L19 from P11 NPZs and a single pooled (μ̂⁺_joint, Σ̂_joint); compare AUROCs of the three Mahalanobis classifiers.

4. **F-10 (PH on trained residual streams sits at the Gaussian null).** FLORAIN models the desirable region with a *parametric* ellipsoid (Gaussian-equivalent — first + second moments only) and gets state-of-the-art results on TruthfulQA. This corroborates F-10 to the extent that the relevant geometric structure is fully captured by mean+covariance. But the corollary is testable: if a *non-parametric* density model (kernel density, normalising flow) over the same activations fits no better than FLORAIN's ellipsoid on a held-out classification task, F-10's "null = no extra topology" claim survives a stronger test than PH. If it fits substantially better, F-10's null was an artefact of PH being insensitive to the actual non-Gaussianity rather than the activations being Gaussian. Falsifiable test: KDE / Gaussian-mixture log-likelihood vs single-Gaussian log-likelihood on L19 prefill activations of correct trajectories, on a held-out split.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **H-12 (semantic-entropy / label-free probes replace supervised DoM).** FLORAIN is *probe-free*: it eliminates the trained classifier head-selection step of ITI. The construction still uses correct/incorrect labels (`G(q)` desirable, `B(q)` undesirable), so it is *probe-free* but **not label-free**. This bounds H-12's scope: the FLORAIN improvement is achievable without a classifier but not without labels — H-12's hoped-for label-free regime is strictly stronger. PAPER_INDEX status of H-12-relevant predecessor 2406.15927 (Semantic Entropy Probes) currently CITED ONLY; FLORAIN strengthens the case that the *probe* is replaceable while leaving the label question open.

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731 OOF).** FLORAIN's `μ̂⁺ − μ̂⁻` "overall direction" in eq. (6) is literally the global DoM construct. Their extrapolation `μ̂_q = μ̂⁺_q + λ(α·d⃗_q + (1−α)·d⃗)` makes DoM the α=0 special case. This is the cleanest existing decomposition of "where DoM sits in the family of plausible intervention targets" — F-2's L19 DoM is the global / α=0 / linear / first-order corner.

- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering).** Direct METHOD_DIFFERS: FLORAIN intervenes at a *single layer* on *all heads* with an *input-dependent* map; H-1's per-position DoM is a *single-direction* additive bias. The headline AUROC anchor (0.7731 prefill, 0.7186 final-token) gives the floor we'd need FLORAIN-on-MATH-500 to match before claiming a real lift.

- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM).** FLORAIN's single-layer concentration argument is consistent with F-9: their experimental claim is that intervention concentrated in one layer beats interventions spread across many. Same direction, different evidence (truthfulness on TruthfulQA vs correctness on MATH-500 K=1=48.6%).

## Methodologies extracted

- **FLORAIN intervention map** — `f(a) = (I + L(a) R^T) a + s`, with `L_i(a) = tanh(W_i ∘ a + b_i)`, R constant, s bias. Sample-wise (input-dependent) nonlinear low-rank.
  *Replication cost*: ~1 day H100 to train + ~30 min eval. We have the activations cached.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — cleanest test of input-dependent vs fixed-vector steering for H-1.

- **Ellipsoid manifold + Mahalanobis projection** — `M_q = {x : (x − μ̂_q)^T Σ̂^{-1} (x − μ̂_q) ≤ ρ_q}`, distance `c_q(a, a') = (a − a')^T Σ̂^{-1} (a − a')`. Theorem 4.1 collapses Mahalanobis-projection objective to the closed-form `(√c_q(f(a_i), μ̂_q) − √ρ_q)_+^2`.
  *Replication cost*: 30 min CPU on cached NPZs (no FLORAIN training needed; just classification AUROC).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — first-order vs second-order test for F-2.

- **Extrapolation scheme for μ̂_q** — `μ̂_q = μ̂⁺_q + λ(α·(μ̂⁺_q − μ̂⁻_q)/‖·‖ + (1−α)·(μ̂⁺ − μ̂⁻)/‖·‖)`. Pulls per-question mean *away* from undesirable mass, blending local and global directions.
  *Replication cost*: trivial (CPU).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — only if we adapt to MATH-500's "no-per-question repetition" data structure (each problem unique, so `G(q)` has cardinality 1).

- **Linear shrinkage covariance** — `Σ̂^{-1} = (β·S + (1−β)·diag(S))^{-1}`, Ledoit-Wolf-style. Used to make the ill-conditioned empirical S invertible at small sample sizes.
  *Replication cost*: trivial (already in `sklearn.covariance.LedoitWolf`).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — exactly the regulariser we need for any second-order test on small per-bucket sample counts (D-bucket has only ~36 problems).

- **Preconditioned scaled gradient descent** — `W_{t+1} = W_t − η ∇_W F · (R_t^T R_t + εI)^{-1}` and analogous for R, b, s. Adaptive scaling, condition-number-free convergence rate (cites Tong et al. 2021).
  *Replication cost*: standard SGD-with-conditioning.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes if and only if we run FLORAIN itself.

- **Single-layer, all-heads intervention** — chooses one layer ℓ and intervenes on its full `D = d × H` activation. Contrasts with ITI's spread across multiple layers. The exact ℓ per model is in Appendix A (which I did not load).
  *Replication cost*: free — just a layer choice.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct check for our L19-special claim.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Probe-free" intervention** — bypass the detect-then-rectify two-stage pipeline; intervene unconditionally. Intersects H-12: probe-free ≠ label-free, narrows H-12's scope to specifically the *classifier* component being replaceable.
- **"Region modeling"** — desirable answers occupy a manifold, intervention = projection. Recasts our F-2 (DoM as a *direction*) into F-2′ (DoM as a *boundary normal* of a manifold). Compatible reframe; could regenerate our DoM via the special case `α=0`, k=0, ϕ linear.
- **"Single-layer concentration"** — consolidating intervention into one layer parallelises and reduces cumulative drift. Resonates with F-9 (single-layer L19 ≈ multi-layer CoE-60); orthogonal mechanism (computational vs informational).
- **"Sample-wise mapping"** — `L(a)` depends on the input. Reframes activation steering away from "find the universal direction" toward "learn the input-conditional projection". Strong METHOD_DIFFERS for H-1.
