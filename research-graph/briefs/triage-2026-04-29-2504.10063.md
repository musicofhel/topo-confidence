# Triage brief — 2504.10063 — Hallucination Detection in LLMs with Topological Divergence on Attention Graphs (TOHA)

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10063
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-10 ("PH on trained residual streams is at the Gaussian null") could be over-narrow.** TOHA computes 0-th order persistent homology on a *graph* (Vietoris–Rips of the attention map, edges 1−w_ij), not a residual-stream point cloud — and it gets AUROC ≥ 0.85 on multiple datasets (Mistral-7B SQuAD 0.89, XSum 0.96; Qwen2.5-7B XSum 0.69). F-10's null was demonstrated only against a Gaussian null on hidden-state activations. **Falsifiable test:** rerun our pathway11_h100 PH battery on attention graphs (rather than residual streams), 1.5B Qwen, MATH-500. If PH-on-attention beats Gaussian null even where PH-on-residuals didn't, F-10's scope must be restricted to "residual-stream point clouds" rather than "PH features in transformers."

2. **F-2 ("Prefill L19 DoM is the *strong* correctness predictor, AUROC 0.7731") could be dominated by an attention-graph signal at zero training cost.** TOHA reaches 0.77 on Qwen2.5-7B SQuAD with **just 50 annotated probe samples** — comparable AUROC, almost no annotation. **Falsifiable test:** on the cached Qwen2.5-1.5B / 7B MATH-500 generations, recompute MTop-Div on a small head subset and compare AUROC against the prefill DoM 0.7731 (1.5B) / 0.7186-equivalent (7B-final). If the attention-graph score matches or exceeds DoM with no per-problem labels, F-2's "strong" claim survives but its uniqueness/efficiency premium does not.

3. **F-9 ("CoE-60 trajectory features are redundant with single-layer L19 DoM") could be wrong about *attention-side* features.** TOHA's ablation (Table 8 / Sec 4.4) shows MSF/MTop-Div beats every conventional attention feature (sparsity, entropy, spectral norm) and beats prompt-internal MST length too. If attention-graph topology carries information *orthogonal* to L19 DoM, the F-9 redundancy claim becomes suspicious for the attention pathway. **Falsifiable test:** stack L19-DoM logit + MTop-Div as features in a 2D logistic regression on MATH-500 1.5B. If the joint AUROC > max of individual AUROCs by > 0.02 (well above 5-fold OOF noise), F-9's "single-layer DoM is sufficient" generalization is too aggressive.

4. **H-21 ("D-bucket has narrower prefill attention than A-bucket") may be testing the wrong feature of attention.** TOHA's discriminator is *not* attention concentration — it's the topological dissimilarity between prompt and response token clusters in attention-graph space. The "narrower attention" framing in H-21 is essentially attention entropy / participation — which TOHA's ablation (Table 8) shows is *worse* than MTop-Div for hallucination separation. **Falsifiable test:** on whatever 1.5B attention maps we have cached, compute *both* attention entropy *and* MTop-Div for D-bucket vs A-bucket; if MTop-Div separates them but entropy does not, H-21 is asking the wrong question and should be reformulated around topology rather than concentration.

5. **H-13 ("Head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token circuits") may be answered for free.** TOHA identifies just 4 (Mistral) / 3 (Llama-2-7B) "hallucination-aware" heads that transfer across datasets — and they overlap with the model's top-25 *induction/copying* heads (Elhage 2021, Feucht 2025). H-13's "prefill vs final-token circuits" hypothesis assumes circuits carve neatly along the prefill/final boundary, but if the actual locus is "induction heads that fire on copy-vs-novelty," the prefill/final cut is the wrong axis. **Falsifiable test:** rank Qwen2.5-1.5B heads by copying score; check whether the L19 DoM signal preferentially loads on copying-rank-low heads. If yes, H-13 should be rewritten around copying-head structure.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC = 0.7731 on 1.5B OOF):** TOHA hits AUROC 0.77 on Qwen2.5-7B SQuAD with 50 labels, 0.65 on CoQA. This is the closest cross-method benchmark anchor — both are "single forward pass" detectors that beat surface uncertainty. We should regard TOHA as the attention-side analogue of the F-2 DoM probe.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046, orthogonal):** TOHA's mechanism explicitly leverages prompt↔response *graph* divergence — the prompt/response separation is exactly the structure F-3 says exists at the residual-stream level. They observe it on attention; we observe it on residual directions. Both anchor a "prompt regime ≠ response regime" picture.
- **F-7 (D-bucket has a distinctive collective geometric signature):** TOHA's "hallucination-aware heads" generalize across datasets but vary by model — same flavor of "stable across content, unstable across architecture" claim as our D-bucket finding. Worth checking whether D-bucket items show elevated MTop-Div.
- **F-10 (PH on trained residual streams = Gaussian null):** TOHA *also* uses persistent homology, but on a different object (attention graph, 0-th order, MSF-equivalent). The contrast — null on residuals, signal on attention graphs — is itself a finding.
- **H-13 (Head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token):** Direct overlap; TOHA gives a head-selection algorithm (Δ_ij ranking + copying-rank cross-reference) we can borrow.
- **H-21 (D-bucket has narrower prefill attention than A-bucket):** Wrong feature; should be MTop-Div not entropy.

## Methodologies extracted

- **MTop-Div_G(R, P)** — Sum of 0-th order persistence intervals of the Vietoris–Rips complex of the attention graph after zeroing edge weights between prompt nodes. Equivalent to the length of the minimum spanning forest attaching response tokens R to the prompt cluster P (Proposition 3.1).
  *Replication cost*: ~hours on cached 1.5B attention maps if we re-extract them; CPU-only after extraction.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — it's the cheapest way to test refutations 1 and 2 against F-10 / F-2.

- **Hallucination-aware head selection (Δ_ij ranking).** For each head, compute mean MTop-Div on hallucinated minus grounded probe sets; sort descending; keep top-N up to N_max=10 by AUROC on validation. Probe set ≥ 50 samples.
  *Replication cost*: 50 labeled correct/incorrect MATH-500 problems (we have these); plus per-head MTop-Div.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — directly testable on Qwen2.5-1.5B with cached MATH-500 labels.

- **Copying-score head ranking** (Feucht et al. 2025, "dual-route model of induction"). Used as a cross-check that hallucination-aware heads ≈ induction heads.
  *Replication cost*: Code in the dual-route model paper; CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — would directly inform H-13 (head-level attribution).

- **Wasserstein distance between prompt/response persistence diagrams** (mentioned in §B.3 ablation as a baseline they tried; underperformed MTop-Div).
  *Replication cost*: gudhi or ripser.py + scipy.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **no** — paper already shows it underperforms; only run if we want to extend their ablation.

- **Information-theoretic bound MTop-Div ≥ α·PPL(P∪R) − β·PPL(R)** (§3.3). Provides a perplexity-difference interpretation; can be used to sanity-check whether our perplexity numbers explain MTop-Div.
  *Replication cost*: PPL is already computed in our pipeline.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — useful for the "novelty" framing of F-3.

## Approaches & framings

- **Attention graph as a non-metric pseudo-distance space.** Reframe attention scores w_ij as 1 − w_ij distances, treat the result as a weighted graph for TDA. This complements our hidden-state geometry framing (P10 directions, P11 DoM) — same "geometry of generation" research program but on a different object.
- **Prompt vs response *partition* as the discriminative structure.** Rather than treating tokens uniformly, the partition into P and R is itself the signal. This intersects F-3 (prefill/final orthogonality) and F-2 (prefill is enough) — both findings hinge on a similar partition.
- **Hallucination as topological *novelty* of response w.r.t. prompt.** MTop-Div is bounded below by perplexity-difference (PPL(P∪R) − PPL(R)). Reframes "the model is making things up" as "the response token cloud is far from the prompt token cloud in QK-induced geometry." Useful for the breathing/D-bucket narrative — D-bucket may be precisely the "high topological novelty" regime.
- **Head-as-circuit vs aggregate-head feature.** TOHA explicitly rejects "all attention heads equally important" (Sriramanan, Binkowski) — instead picks ~4 transferable heads. This intersects H-13 directly: their finding is a strong prior that head-level attribution will pay off.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- **Models tested**: LLaMA-2-7B-chat, LLaMA-2-13B-chat, LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.1, **Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct** (the latter overlaps directly with our pathway 11 verifier).
- **Probe set size**: 50–100 examples; performance plateaus ≥ 50 (their Figure 3a/b).
- **N_max = 10 heads** is enough; AUROC is already strong at N_max=1 for some datasets (Figure 4b/c).
- **Test set = 25%** of dataset, 5 random splits.
- **Anonymous code release**: https://anonymous.4open.science/r/tda4hallucinations-C449 — barcode computation likely ripser.py (Tralie 2018) per their references.
- **Compute**: roughly 7× SelfCheckGPT-with-1-gen, ~70× SelfCheckGPT-with-20-gens. CPU-feasible for the topology computation; the costly part is extracting attention.
- **Gotcha**: They explicitly *zero* the prompt-internal edge weights before computing the barcode — keeping them tanked AUROC by ~15-37 points on Mistral CoQA / MS MARCO (Table 4). This is non-trivial: an off-the-shelf "barcode of the attention graph" without this modification will not work.
- **Hallucination-aware heads (Mistral-7B)** reported in Figure 2 legend: (7,18), (12,15), (28,0), (8,15), (19,6), (12,27), (18,2), (18,3), (12,6), (12,7) and similar.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2504.10063 — Hallucination Detection in LLMs with Topological Divergence on Attention Graphs / TOHA (Bazarova, Yugay, Shulga, ..., Barannikov, Zaytsev, 2025)

**Relevance:** TOHA is the closest external method to the F-2 prefill-DoM probe in spirit ("single forward pass + small probe set → AUROC ~0.7-0.9 hallucination/correctness signal") but operates on the *attention graph* rather than the residual stream. The MTop-Div_G(R, P) score (sum of 0-th persistence intervals = MSF length attaching response to prompt after zeroing prompt-internal edges) is computed per-head; only ~4 heads are needed and they overlap with induction/copying heads (Elhage 2021, Feucht 2025). Tested on five LLMs including Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, our P11 verifier model. Provides a candidate refutation surface for F-10 (PH null claim) and a candidate orthogonal-signal companion for F-2.

**Key claim we tested:** Attention-graph topology, computed via 0-th order persistent homology of the response-vs-prompt subgraph partition, carries correctness/hallucination signal at AUROC competitive with consistency-based methods that need 10-20 generations. Specific attention heads ("hallucination-aware heads") transfer the signal across datasets and overlap with the model's induction/copying heads.

**Our result:** **TO TEST.** No replication on MATH-500 yet. Their reported numbers are on text RAG (CoQA, SQuAD, XSum, RAGTruth, HotpotQA) — overlapping with Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct architecture but not with our task. Replication on Qwen2.5-1.5B MATH-500 is the FE71 / H-50 experiment.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE71 (replicate on Qwen 1.5B MATH-500), P11-FE72 (joint with L19 DoM), P11-FE73 (induction-head overlap), H-50, H-51.

**Status:** TO TEST
