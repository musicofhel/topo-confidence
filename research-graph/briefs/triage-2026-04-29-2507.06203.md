# Triage brief — 2507.06203 — A Survey on Latent Reasoning

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06203
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Layer-Specialization circuit framing predicts circuit-level (not direction-level) dependence at L19 — would refute F-2 / F-3.**
   The survey (§4.2 "Intermediate Layers: Core of Latent CoT") asserts that intermediate layers "form specialized sub-circuits" with "causal influence on final reasoning outcomes." If correct in the strong form, ablating any reasoning-related directions at L19 should hurt MATH-500 accuracy comparably. Our F-2 / F-3 (single L19 prefill DoM at AUROC 0.7731; cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046) instead say correctness lives along a *specific 1-D subspace*, with prefill and final-token correctness signals in *orthogonal* directions of the same layer. The survey's framing predicts no such within-layer rank-1 structure.
   *Mechanism*: if reasoning is implemented by an entire intermediate-layer circuit, projecting out any high-variance direction at L19 should degrade accuracy by similar amounts; only DoM-aligned ablation should matter under our framing.
   *Falsifiable*: ablate top-K random orthonormal directions of equal norm at L19 vs the prefill DoM, measure MATH-500 K=1 accuracy delta. If random-direction ablation degrades accuracy by similar amounts to DoM ablation, F-2's 1-D framing is insufficient and the survey's circuit view wins.

2. **"Layer depth is the primary bottleneck" predicts D-bucket lifts under recurrent depth — would refute H-19 / F-7.**
   §4.1 cites Saunshi et al. and Merrill & Sabharwal: any K-layer transformer doing m-step CoT can be simulated by an (L+O(1))-layer transformer via m forward passes; "layer depth serves as the primary bottleneck for latent reasoning capacity." If true, the D-bucket (problems where neither extra K nor extra compute helps; F-7 collective signature) is just under-depth: applying recurrent-depth (Geiping 2502.05171) or Universal-Transformer-style adaptive recurrence should lift the D-bucket. Our H-19 / F-7 instead claim D-bucket failure is content-dependent and *not* a depth problem.
   *Mechanism*: depth-bottleneck theory implies adaptive vertical recurrence converts D-bucket → A/B/C-bucket as iteration count r → ∞.
   *Falsifiable*: run an adaptive-depth model (Recurrent-Depth at 2502.05171 has open weights) on the 36 D-bucket items at varying r ∈ {1, 4, 16, 64}, plot accuracy vs r. Monotonic lift would refute the content-not-depth interpretation of F-7.

3. **The Wang [113]/CoE citation in §4.2 frames CoE as a richer signal than single-layer probes — would refute F-9.**
   The survey uses CoE (2410.13640) as primary evidence that "the trajectory's geometric shape can distinguish correct from incorrect answers, enabling output-free self-evaluation." Our F-9 says CoE-60 is *redundant* with single-layer L19 DoM (0.811 ≈ 0.7731 within sampling noise), i.e. the trajectory-geometry framing is double-counting one direction. The survey implicitly elevates trajectory features above single-layer features.
   *Mechanism*: if CoE truly carries trajectory-distinct information beyond L19 DoM, the residual CoE features (after projecting out L19 DoM) should retain non-trivial AUROC; if not, F-9 is right and the survey's emphasis is misplaced.
   *Falsifiable*: on cached 1024-tok activations from `pathway11_h100/`, fit (a) full 60-feature CoE-60 logistic, (b) CoE-60 features with prefill_L19_DoM projection removed, (c) prefill_L19_DoM alone. If (b) ≈ (c), F-9 holds; if (b) > (c) by ≥0.03 AUROC, the survey's CoE-as-novel framing wins.

4. **The Wang [112] "Early Stabilization" / volatility OOD framing predicts F-4 collapses to OOD-detection — would refute F-4.**
   §4.2 also cites Wang [112] (NeurIPS 2024 "Embedding trajectory for OOD detection in mathematical reasoning"): incorrect answers have *higher* trajectory volatility, and models show "Early Stabilization" only on familiar tasks. Our F-4 says correct trajectories collapse harder than incorrect at the final token. These are surface-similar but structurally different: [112] attributes stabilization to *familiarity / OOD-ness*, F-4 attributes asymmetric collapse to *correctness*. If [112] is right and applies to MATH-500, F-4 is just OOD detection in disguise — and many MATH-500 incorrects are simply OOD problems.
   *Mechanism*: cross-tabulating per-problem OOD score (volatility) against correctness should show OOD ≈ incorrect; the asymmetric-collapse signal would then disappear after conditioning on OOD score.
   *Falsifiable*: re-extract trajectory volatility statistics over the 28-layer Qwen-2.5-1.5B residual stream for MATH-500, regress correctness ~ volatility + L19_DoM_score; if volatility drives F-4 to non-significance, F-4 is downstream of OOD.

5. **The "depth from optimization over time" thesis (§5.2) predicts breathing curves should grow with recurrence iteration count — would refute F-1 / F-5.**
   The survey's unifying view is that "the hidden state plays the role of a fast-weight layer whose parameters are refined" through more time steps (TTT/Titans/Atlas/fixed-point RNNs) — depth emerges from optimization. Under this lens, dimensional breathing (F-1) should be a *function of CoT length / iteration count*, scaling monotonically with recurrence depth. F-5 already says breathing is content-dependent and not AR-mechanics, but a recurrent-depth model gives a strictly stronger test: holding content fixed, vary recurrence count.
   *Mechanism*: if breathing is a recurrent-depth phenomenon, fixed-content prompts processed with r=1 vs r=64 in Recurrent-Depth should produce monotonically-deeper breathing peaks; F-5 predicts the curve is content-locked, recurrence-invariant.
   *Falsifiable*: extract residuals from Recurrent-Depth (2502.05171) on a held-out MATH-500 sample at r ∈ {1,4,16,64}, compute participation ratio per token; if peak-PR scales with r, F-1/F-5 need revision.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Coconut continuous-thought feedback (Hao et al., 2412.06769)** — Insert last-hidden-state of the previous decoding step as a continuous "thought" position before the next token.
  *Replication cost*: H100-day fine-tune at 1.5B; reuses Qwen tokenizer/architecture.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly tests whether breathing/DoM emerge in *latent* CoT vs explicit CoT (motivates new H-72 below).

- **Pre/Loop/Coda recurrent-depth (Geiping 2502.05171; AlgoFormer 29; Recursive Transformer 2)** — Three-stage architecture with shared Loop-block weights; iteration count r is dynamic.
  *Replication cost*: ~1 day H100 inference on a public Recurrent-Depth checkpoint + cached residual extraction.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly tests Refutation #2 and #5; high-leverage method.

- **CODI self-distillation in activation space (Shen et al. [91])** — Align hidden activations between teacher (full CoT) and student (compressed) via a single-step alignment loss.
  *Replication cost*: 1–2 H100 days; needs MATH-500 with both full and compressed traces.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: probably no — fine-tune-heavy and we already have a frozen extractor; could borrow the alignment-loss idea for cheaper distillation experiments.

- **Stepwise Internalization (Deng et al. [23])** — Curriculum that progressively removes CoT tokens during training, internalizing reasoning into hidden states.
  *Replication cost*: multi-day H100 fine-tune.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — too expensive vs return; track if Coconut/CODI fine-tunes become available.

- **Pause / filler tokens (Goyal et al. [37], Pfau et al. [79])** — Insert learnable or trivial tokens to provide more attention/computation steps.
  *Replication cost*: 30 min CPU to test the trivial-filler version (no fine-tuning needed) on cached prompts.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — cheap sanity test for H-2 (does inserting "..." into prompts shift prefill DoM AUROC?).

- **Back Attention (Yu et al. 2502.10835)** — Mechanism for transmitting hidden information from higher layers to lower layers, enhancing multi-hop reasoning.
  *Replication cost*: medium; requires modifying inference pass.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: probably no, but the *probing* methodology (neuron-level logit-flow analysis on multi-hop tasks) is borrowable for H-13.

- **Causal mediation analysis (Stolfo et al. 2305.15054)** — Quantify indirect contributions of MLP and attention modules in arithmetic reasoning.
  *Replication cost*: 2–4h CPU once activations are cached.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — clean fit for H-13 (head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token circuits).

- **Diffusion-based latent reasoning (LLaDA, DCoLT, MDM family)** — Bidirectional iterative denoising over masked sequences.
  *Replication cost*: very high; requires diffusion-trained checkpoints.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — orthogonal architecture family; not portable to our autoregressive pipeline.

- **Implicit fixed-point RNNs (Schöne et al. [87])** — Iterate a state-space block until convergence; small adaptive iteration count (≤16 for most NL tokens).
  *Replication cost*: medium; requires checkpoint or fine-tune.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — interesting but tangential.

## Approaches & framings

- **Vertical vs horizontal recurrence dichotomy** — vertical = activation-based depth iteration; horizontal = hidden-state propagation across long sequences. Cleanly separates Coconut-style methods from Mamba/RWKV-style. Intersects our finding registry: F-1/F-5 breathing is per-token (vertical-axis) phenomenon; F-2/F-3 prefill-vs-final-DoM is also vertical-axis (different positions, same layer). Our project does not yet engage with horizontal-recurrence models.

- **Layer Specialization (shallow / intermediate / deep)** — Survey codifies shallow=local/syntactic, intermediate=core reasoning circuits, deep=output refinement. Endorses our L19 (mid-depth on a 28-layer Qwen-1.5B) probe choice ex post.

- **Depth from optimization over time (§5.2)** — Hidden state as a fast-weight layer; longer sequences = more optimization steps = more effective depth. This framing is the strongest test bed for F-5 (content-not-mechanics breathing).

- **Curse of Depth (Sun et al., [99] = 2502.05795)** — Pre-LN variance growth and attention-matrix degeneration cause deep layers to underperform. Provides a *reason* L19 (mid-depth) outperforms deeper layers on probing — relevant to F-2 / EXP-018 layer-wise PH null.

- **"Pre/Loop/Coda" architectural template** — Modular separation of input-encoding / iterative-reasoning / output-decoding. Could re-frame our pathway: prefill = Pre+early-Loop, generation = remaining-Loop+Coda.

- **Turing-completeness of CoT** — CoT as a "dynamic computational tape" (Pérez 2019; Qiu et al. [81]; Li et al. [63]). Relevant to H-19 (C_exact verification) framing — verification is a CoT-tape sub-task.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- **Pre/Loop/Coda template (Table 1)** — explicit Prelude + shared-weight Loop block + Coda; dynamic-stop via early-exit on Δh < ε (Recursive Transformer) or fixed iterations (AlgoFormer); depth embeddings deprecated (Universal Transformer used sinusoidal d_t; later models drop them).
- **Coconut**: continuous thought = previous decoding step's last-layer hidden state, inserted as an extra position before the current token; same Transformer parameters reused.
- **CODI**: single-step activation alignment between teacher (full CoT) and student (compressed) achieves first parity with explicit CoT on GSM8K among latent methods.
- **System-1.5 Reasoning ([109])** — Depth + Step Shortcuts give 20× faster inference on GSM8K without modifying the Transformer backbone.
- **dKV-Cache / dLLM-Cache** — KV-cache strategies giving 2–10× / 9.1× speedups on masked-diffusion inference.
- **Curse of Depth (2502.05795)** — variance growth in Pre-LN; attention matrices in deep layers collapse to rank-1 single-column patterns.
- Survey's GitHub repo aggregates implementations: not loaded in this brief; flag for follow-up.

## 2507.06203 — A Survey on Latent Reasoning (Zhu et al., M-A-P, 2025)

**Relevance:** Comprehensive survey on latent CoT and reasoning that taxonomizes vertical (activation-based recurrence: Coconut, Universal Transformer, Recurrent-Depth) vs horizontal (hidden-state propagation: Mamba/RWKV, TTT/Titans) recurrence, plus a dedicated mechanistic-interpretability section (§4) on layer specialization. Indirectly endorses our L19 mid-depth probe choice via "intermediate layers are core of latent CoT." Most directly relevant: §4.2 cites Wang CoE ([113] = 2410.13640) and Wang embedding-trajectory-volatility ([112], NeurIPS 2024) — both intersect F-9 and F-4. Also surfaces five high-leverage cross-paper admissions (Coconut 2412.06769, Recurrent-Depth 2502.05171, Curse-of-Depth 2502.05795, Back-Attention 2502.10835, Yu-implicit-step-by-step 2411.15862, Stolfo-causal-mediation 2305.15054). The "depth from optimization over time" thesis (§5.2) gives a strong refutation handle on F-1/F-5 (content-not-mechanics breathing) — varying recurrence count r in Recurrent-Depth at fixed content is the cleanest available test.
