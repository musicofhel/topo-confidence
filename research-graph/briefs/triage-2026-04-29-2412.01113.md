# Triage brief — 2412.01113 — Think-to-Talk or Talk-to-Think? LLMs Faithfully and Iteratively Compute Answers During CoT (Kudo et al.)

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01113
Triaged: 2026-04-29

Authors: K. Kudo, Y. Aoki, T. Kuribayashi, S. Sone, M. Taniguchi, A. Brassard, K. Sakaguchi, K. Inui
Year: 2024 (v3 2026-03-19)
One-line thesis: For multi-hop arithmetic, LLMs do **not** pre-determine the final answer in the prefill state and then "talk" the reasoning; instead they iteratively compute (sub-)answers while emitting CoT tokens. CoT is therefore a *faithful* reflection of internal computation.

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-2 ("prefill L19 DoM predicts correctness AUROC 0.7731, *better than* final-token") — strongest refutation candidate.** Kudo et al. apply residual-stream patching at the prefill position and report that swapping prefill activations between a correct- and incorrect-bound problem does **not** flip the answer in their multi-hop arithmetic setting. If that result generalises to MATH-500, then the L19 prefill DoM correctness signal we report cannot be the model "already knowing the answer" — it has to be a *correlational* shadow of input-level features (problem familiarity, surface form, length, vocabulary). **Mechanism:** prefill geometry is causally upstream of nothing answer-relevant; the AUROC comes from `{problem features} → {DoM projection, correctness}` confounding, not from internal answer state. **Falsifiable experiment:** replicate Kudo's residual-stream patching at L19 prefill on 50 MATH-500 problems matched in length and topic; if patching does not move K=1 outcome despite moving the DoM projection by ≥1σ, F-2 is recharacterised from "predicts because the model has computed it" to "predicts because the input is easy." (Already partially scoped as `P11-FE3`.)

2. **Refutes F-3 ("prefill and final-token DoM directions are orthogonal, cos 0.046")**. Our F-3 narrative treats prefill and final DoMs as encoding *qualitatively different* signals (problem-state vs answer-state). Kudo's iterative-computation result predicts the answer-relevant direction should **rotate continuously** through generation; cos≈0 between prefill and final is then just the endpoint of a long monotonic drift, not a sign of "two different signals." **Mechanism:** under Talk-to-Think, the same low-dim correctness manifold is gradually populated layer-by-layer, token-by-token; orthogonality is geometric distance, not semantic separation. **Falsifiable experiment:** project per-token, per-layer residuals onto the running DoM during MATH-500 CoT. If the angle between the position-`t` DoM and the prefill DoM grows monotonically (with small step variance), the rotation interpretation wins and F-3's "different signals" framing is wrong. If instead the angle jumps abruptly at a single token (e.g., the answer marker), the F-3 framing survives.

3. **Refutes F-8 ("selective-prediction via prefill DoM works at 50% coverage → 71.6% accuracy") at scale**. If CoT is genuinely faithful (Kudo), then on stronger / faithful models the *sharpest* correctness signal should live at **intermediate sub-answer boundaries** (each computed step), not at prefill. F-8's prefill-only gate would then be a quirk of 1.5B's weak/unfaithful CoT and should degrade — or be dominated by step-boundary probes — on 7B. **Mechanism:** prefill encodes only difficulty under faithful CoT; the model truly "decides" piecewise at step boundaries, where information is most distilled. **Falsifiable experiment:** identify step-boundary tokens in MATH-500 K=1 traces (line-break + "Step n:" / "=" / equation tokens), extract L19 activations there, fit DoM, compute selective-prediction at 50% coverage on 1.5B and 7B. If 7B step-boundary AUROC ≫ 7B prefill AUROC, F-8 needs a model-scale-conditioned reframing.

4. **Refutes H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy) directionally**. Kudo's framework predicts that **prefill steering** should be approximately inert (since computation hasn't happened) while **mid-CoT steering** should be the effective lever. Our H-1 currently expects per-position steering to work but is agnostic about which position dominates; Kudo gives a sharp prediction. **Mechanism:** Talk-to-Think implies a temporally local accuracy lever — late positions dominate. **Falsifiable experiment:** when H-1 is run, sweep injection position across {prefill, 25%, 50%, 75% of CoT, post-answer-newline}. If accuracy delta is monotone-increasing with position, H-1's "prefill is special" implicit prior is refuted; if prefill ties or wins, Kudo's claim is wrong on math (vs their arithmetic).

5. **Refutes F-7 ("D-bucket has a distinctive collective prefill-geometry signature") interpretation**. If models do not know answers at prefill (Kudo), D-bucket's distinctive prefill geometry cannot be a "model knows it doesn't know" self-knowledge signal — it must be a **difficulty / out-of-distribution** signal that *correlates* with eventual computational failure during iteration. **Mechanism:** D-bucket geometry = pre-computation difficulty marker, not pre-computed self-doubt. **Falsifiable experiment:** apply Kudo-style intermediate-layer fidelity probes to MATH-500 D-bucket vs A-bucket. D-bucket should show *larger* mid-CoT fidelity drops / more sub-answer revision events than A-bucket; a flat mid-CoT fidelity profile in D-bucket would contradict the "difficulty proxy" reading and resurrect a self-knowledge interpretation.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Logit lens (per-layer next-token decoding via output unembedding)** — read intermediate residuals through `lm_head` to track "what answer is the model leaning toward" at each layer. *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached Stage 2 NPZs for MATH-500 (we already have all-layer residuals for 1.5B and 7B). *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — gives a direct test of when the answer "appears" along the layer×token axis, complementary to DoM probing.
- **Residual-stream activation patching across matched problem pairs** — swap residual activations at a chosen layer/position between two problems with different correctness predictions, observe if the answer flips. *Replication cost*: 4h H100 (forward pass with hooks; need GPU for generation). *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — already queued as `P11-FE3`; this paper is the trigger.
- **Attention pattern patching** — replace attention weights at a target head/layer with those from a matched control problem. *Replication cost*: 8h H100. *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes, secondary to P11-FE3** — sharper localisation of which heads carry the prefill correctness signal (parallel to H-13).
- **Step-boundary probing** — identify CoT tokens that mark sub-answer completion (e.g., "= 42" or end-of-line after a sub-result), extract residuals there, fit per-step probes. *Replication cost*: 1d CPU (regex-extract step boundaries from MATH-500 traces, fit probes on existing NPZs). *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — needed to test refutation #3 on F-8.
- **Layer-wise fidelity / monotonicity metric** — measure how monotonically the model's logit-lens prediction converges to the final answer across layers (cite Heinzerling-Inui 2024 monotonic representations). *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached residuals. *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — would give a clean F-2 vs Kudo discriminator (faithful CoT predicts higher monotonicity in 7B than 1.5B).
- **Multi-hop arithmetic synthetic dataset construction** — generate problems with controlled hop count, operand range, and known sub-answer values for ground-truth fidelity scoring. *Replication cost*: 4h CPU + 4h H100 to generate Qwen traces. *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — gives a clean transfer test for F-2 / F-8 (small, controllable, complementary to MATH-500).

## Approaches & framings

- **"Think-to-Talk vs Talk-to-Think" dichotomy.** Reframes the prefill-correctness story as a *causal-direction* question, not a *predictive-AUROC* question. Direct intersection with F-2; should be added to F-2's "Counter-arguments / open questions" section explicitly.
- **CoT faithfulness = monotonic alignment between intermediate layer representations and emitted reasoning steps.** Sharper than the looser "CoT is post-hoc rationalization" framing; gives a measurable definition. Directly applicable to evaluating whether 7B's CoT is more or less faithful than 1.5B's (and would inform our cross-scale story currently dismantled by truncation issues).
- **Iterative sub-answer computation as an inductive bias on architecture.** Suggests probing should be re-anchored at sub-answer boundaries rather than at prefill or final-token only. Methodological re-orientation for Pathway 11.

## 2412.01113 — Think-to-Talk or Talk-to-Think? LLMs Faithfully and Iteratively Compute Answers During CoT (Kudo et al., 2024)

**Relevance:** Direct mechanistic test of the prefill-vs-during-CoT computation question that underlies our F-2 / F-3 / F-8 prefill-DoM story. Uses logit lens, residual-stream patching, attention patching, and step-boundary fidelity probing on Qwen 2.5 + Mistral-Nemo over multi-hop arithmetic. Their Talk-to-Think (iterative-computation) conclusion is the central crux against which F-2's "prefill predicts because the model knows" interpretation must be tested. Cited in this brief as the trigger for `P11-FE3`, `P11-FE71-73`, `P10-FE71`, `H-72-75`.

**Key claim we tested:** "LLMs do not pre-determine the final answer in the prefill state; they iteratively compute (sub-)answers during CoT generation, and CoT is therefore a faithful reflection of internal computation."

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — we have not yet replicated Kudo's residual-stream patching at L19 prefill, nor their step-boundary fidelity probes. Our existing F-2 (prefill AUROC 0.7731) is consistent with both Talk-to-Think (then prefill = problem-difficulty proxy) and Think-to-Talk (then prefill = pre-computed answer); Kudo's patching protocol is the discriminator. Step-boundary probing on cached NPZs (P11-FE71) gives a partial replication without new generation; full causal test (P11-FE3) needs H100. Note that our prefill/final-token orthogonality finding (F-3, cos 0.046) is *prima facie* easier to reconcile with Kudo's iterative-computation view as continuous rotation than with our current "different signals" framing — H-73 tests this.
