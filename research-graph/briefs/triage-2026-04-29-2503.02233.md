# Triage brief — 2503.02233 — Enhancing LLM Reliability via Explicit Knowledge Boundary Modeling

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02233
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-8 (selective-prediction via prefill DoM at coverage 0.5 = 71.6% on Qwen-2.5-1.5B MATH-500) — output-trained verbalized confidence may dominate the residual probe.** EKBM trains Qwen2.5-7B with 1k SFT + 2k DPO samples and reaches Quality-Acc 92.73 / Final-Acc 95.30 on GSM8K-CoT *without ever reading a hidden state*. The "sure" subset is conceptually equivalent to F-8's "answered" subset under selective prediction. **Mechanism:** SFT with self-consistency-derived sure/unsure tags + DPO with Weighted-F1(α₁=0.25, α₂=0.75) preference data trains the model to *emit* its own coverage decision at the output token, bypassing the need to extract residual activations or fit a logistic probe. **Falsifier:** Replicate EKBM on Qwen2.5-1.5B / MATH-500 with ≤1k SFT + 2k DPO samples; if sure-Accuracy at sure-Coverage ≈ 0.5 ≥ 71.6%, then F-8's pipeline (extract → DoM → 5-fold CV → threshold) is dominated by a no-extraction supervised baseline. F-8 must be downgraded from "the way to do selective prediction" to "an internal-state-only baseline."

2. **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes replace supervised DoM for label-free abstention) — supervision boundary collapses.** EKBM's SFT data construction (§3.2) is *label-free with respect to ground truth*: it uses repeated self-sampling and tags predictions as "sure" only when they are consistent across samples, never accessing the gold answer. This is structurally equivalent to using semantic entropy as a self-supervision signal for *the model itself*, not just an external probe. **Mechanism:** if the model can be aligned to verbalize self-consistency-derived confidence, then the supervised-vs-label-free dichotomy in H-12 is a false binary — supervision is recoverable from sampling, with no oracle labels required. **Falsifier:** train Qwen2.5-1.5B EKBM-style on MATH-500 with sure/unsure tags built from i ∈ {2, 4, 8} self-samples (no ground-truth labels); compare AUROC of the verbalized-confidence flag against semantic-entropy-probe AUROC on a held-out split. If they match within ±0.02 AUROC, H-12's framing of "label-free probes replace supervised DoM" must be re-stated as "self-consistency at training time replaces supervised DoM, regardless of probe vs verbalization."

3. **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM is a strong correctness predictor, AUROC 0.7731 > 0.7186 final-token) — fragility under behavioral alignment.** F-2 treats the L19 prefill correctness signal as a near-stationary property of the trained Qwen2.5-1.5B base. EKBM explicitly *re-trains the model to encode confidence at the output*, which would shift internal residual structure (the SFT step alone re-shapes the very layers the DoM is extracted from). **Mechanism:** if confidence becomes verbalized rather than implicit, gradient flow during SFT/DPO has no incentive to maintain the L19 prefill direction-of-meaning; the structure is incidental to the base model's training, not a robust geometric invariant. **Falsifier:** re-extract L19 prefill activations from an EKBM-aligned Qwen2.5-1.5B on MATH-500 and recompute prefill-DoM AUROC. If it drops below 0.65 (or below final-token AUROC), F-2's "L19 prefill is the natural locus" claim is conditional on un-aligned base models and the prefill-better-than-final ordering may invert.

4. **F-4 (correct trajectories collapse harder than incorrect — asymmetric collapse) — possible behavioral confound.** F-4 attributes the correct/incorrect PR asymmetry to a geometric collapse property of trained transformers. EKBM's "sure" tag is *purely* derived from self-resample-consistency — a pre-geometric, behavioral property. If self-resample-consistency reproduces the F-4 asymmetry as well as the geometric metric, the geometry is a downstream reflection of behavior, not its cause. **Mechanism:** correct trajectories are sampled-consistent because their training distribution is dense; collapse is the geometric shadow of that density, not an independent signal. **Falsifier:** on Qwen2.5-1.5B MATH-500 K=1, sample i=4 additional generations per problem; compute a consistency flag (i/4 agreement) and the F-4 collapse-strength metric; correlate. If their AUROCs differ by <0.01 against ground truth, F-4 is geometrically restating sample consistency.

5. **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy) — refinement-on-unsure may dominate at lower cost.** H-1 plans activation-level steering. EKBM's slow refinement (LLaMA3-8B SFT'd on GPT-4o CoTs of unsure samples) lifts GSM8K-Direct from 19.94 → 79.83 and DST JGA by 15-25pp absolute, all without any activation intervention. **Mechanism:** if a "refine the unsure subset with a stronger reasoner" pass costs ~K=2.5 average and matches H-1's expected steering gain, H-1 is methodologically dominated — there's no reason to attempt activation steering when behavioral refinement is cheaper, more controllable, and architecture-agnostic. **Falsifier:** compute the EKBM-style refinement upper-bound on MATH-500 (route Qwen2.5-1.5B's "unsure" 50% to Qwen2.5-7B for refinement, K=2.5 avg). If accuracy ≥ H-1's projected steering benefit, drop H-1 below MEDIUM priority.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-8 (selective-prediction acc 71.6% at coverage 0.5, K=2.5 avg)**: EKBM's GSM8K-CoT result (Quality-Acc 92.73, Final-Acc 95.30 with K≈coverage of "unsure") is the cleanest external benchmark for this metric. The paper does *not* report a directly comparable "accuracy at sure-coverage = 0.5", but does report "Unsure Proportion" matrices in §4.6.1; with α₁=0.25, α₂=0.75, unsure proportion is in the 5–15% range, far below F-8's 50% — i.e. EKBM is operating at much higher answered-coverage. This is a clean cross-method test bench.

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)**: paper does not extract hidden states. But §5 (Related Work) explicitly enumerates "Internal State Probing" (Li et al., 2024a) as a competing line and notes it "relies on external post-hoc techniques, computationally expensive and lacks integration with the model's endogenous reasoning." That is a direct critique of F-2's modus operandi.

- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM)**: EKBM's "Verbose" prompt-only baseline (model outputs prediction + confidence label) is conceptually a *zero-layer* DoM substitute. The fact that Verbose underperforms full EKBM by 20-30pp Quality-F1 (Table 1) suggests that verbalized confidence is not free — it requires gradient updates to be calibrated. Tangentially relevant: weak prompt-confidence ≪ trained verbalized confidence ≪ trained probes is the expected ordering F-9 implicitly assumes; we should test whether this ordering holds on MATH-500.

- **H-12 (label-free uncertainty probes via semantic entropy)**: EKBM's SFT data construction is essentially a *label-free* method (no ground-truth needed during training, only self-sampling consistency). This blurs the boundary between "label-free probe" (H-12) and "supervised verbalization" (this paper) — see refutation #2.

- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering)**: H-1 is potentially dominated by refinement-on-unsure (refutation #5).

- **H-19 (C_exact verification routing beats C_infer scaling)**: EKBM's two-stage architecture (fast-with-confidence → refine unsure) is structurally identical to H-19's verification routing, but with a *behavioral* gate (verbalized confidence) rather than an *activation* gate (PANL-equivalent DoM). Worth explicitly comparing both gating signals at matched compute.

## Methodologies extracted

- **EKBM training pipeline (SFT + DPO with Weighted-F1 preference)** — fast model with verbalized "sure"/"unsure" labels per slot/answer, slow refinement model triggered by "unsure". *Replication cost*: ~6h H100 for SFT (1k samples) + ~12h H100 for DPO (2k preference pairs); refinement model another ~6h. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly competes with F-8's selective-prediction story on MATH-500 and is essentially a single-week replication.

- **Self-consistency-derived confidence labels (no ground truth)** — sample model i times per training instance; tag "sure" if all i samples agree, "unsure" otherwise; *crucially do not correct erroneous outputs*. *Replication cost*: 30min H100 to generate i=4 samples per MATH-500 problem; then standard SFT. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — orthogonal to all our existing label sources, and makes H-12's "label-free" framing concrete.

- **Weighted-F1 / Weighted-Accuracy as preference scoring function for DPO** — α₁ regulates unsure-error tolerance, α₂ regulates unsure-recall reward; α₁=0.25, α₂=0.75 is best for DST/GSM8K. *Replication cost*: trivial once SFT is in place. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes if EKBM replication is run; needed for the "DPO Post Training" variant which gives best Quality-F1.

- **Refinement model (LLaMA3-8B SFT'd on GPT-4o CoT for unsure outputs)** — fine-grained per-prediction reasoning, applied only to unsure subset. Compares against DeepSeek-R1-Distill-LLaMA-70B and GPT-4o as refiners. *Replication cost*: ~6h H100; OR substitute Qwen2.5-7B / DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-70B (already in-graph as a stub via 2501.12948). *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — H-19's verification-routing FE (P11-FE19) and EKBM's slow stage are nearly identical experimental setups; merging them saves work.

- **Quality-F1 / Quality-Accuracy = Weighted-F1(0.5, 0.5)** as a single-number reliability metric with hierarchical priorities (STP > UTP > UFP > SFP). *Replication cost*: trivial — just a scoring-function update. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — would let us add a Quality-Accuracy column to the F-8 table for direct comparison with this paper.

## Approaches & framings

- **Behavioral encoding of confidence supersedes internal-state extraction.** The paper's framing is that confidence should be *part of the output text*, not inferred from activations or repeated samples post-hoc. This is the diametric opposite of our entire P10/P11 thesis (confidence is geometric and recoverable from L19 residuals). The intersection is uncomfortable: if the EKBM framing wins on MATH-500, our pipeline reduces to "an internal-state-only baseline that no one needs."

- **Hierarchical preference (sure-correct > unsure-correct > unsure-wrong > sure-wrong).** Equation (3) — encodes a four-way preference instead of binary correct/incorrect. Maps onto our A/B/C/D-bucket taxonomy non-trivially: A-bucket ≈ sure-correct, D-bucket ≈ sure-wrong (the worst), B-bucket ≈ unsure-correct (recoverable), C-bucket ≈ unsure-wrong. F-7's D-bucket-is-pathological finding is directly anticipated by this objective.

- **Self-sampling as a fixed-point definition of "knowledge boundary".** The model's knowledge boundary is operationalized as the set of inputs on which i self-samples agree. This is a behavioral analog of "consistent activation pattern across resamples" — collapsing semantic entropy / self-consistency / EKBM tagging into one ladder.

- **Cost as a first-class experimental quantity** — figures 7 and §4.6.2 explicitly trade JGA against Cost (= % unsure tags) on a Pareto frontier. We should adopt this for any selective-prediction or refine-and-spend results — F-8 currently reports a single (coverage, accuracy) point rather than a curve.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Backbones: LLaMA3-8B and Qwen2.5-7B (Qwen family is shared with our P11 setup — this is the operative comparison).
- SFT: 1,000 samples (note: very small).
- DPO: 2,000 preference pairs.
- DPO α₁=0.25, α₂=0.75 is optimal on MultiWOZ-2.4 (Figure 5); these are the values to use unless our scenario differs sharply.
- DPO Post Training (DPO after SFT) > DPO Joint Training for high-capability foundation models (§4.4); for low-capability models, Joint can be competitive.
- Refinement model: LLaMA3-8B SFT'd via GPT-4o-generated CoT reasoning, applied per-prediction at fine grain.
- For GSM8K extension, refinement uses DeepSeek-R1-Distill-LLaMA-70B (in-graph as stub, paper 2501.12948).
- "Direct" baseline is *fully refined* (every output is treated as unsure) since it has no confidence label — establishes the upper-bound cost.
- Code/release status: not stated in the visible content. Authors at SJTU X-LANCE Lab; likely a release on github.com/X-LANCE/ but not verified.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2503.02233 — Enhancing LLM Reliability via Explicit Knowledge Boundary Modeling (Zheng, Xu, Liu, Fan, Fung, Chen, Yu — COLM 2025)

**Relevance:** Direct competitor to F-8 (selective prediction via prefill DoM). Trains LLMs (LLaMA3-8B, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) to verbalize "sure"/"unsure" confidence labels on each output, with a slow refinement model handling the unsure subset. SFT data is built from self-sampling consistency (no ground-truth needed); DPO uses Weighted-F1 as preference score with α₁=0.25, α₂=0.75 optimal. Evaluated on MultiWOZ-2.4, BiTOD, SGD (DST), and GSM8K (math). On Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct GSM8K-CoT, reaches Quality-Accuracy 92.73 / Final-Accuracy 95.30 with only 1k SFT + 2k DPO samples, compared to Direct=86.81 and Self-Consistency=89.41 / 91.48. Same Qwen family as our P11 setup, making the cross-method comparison clean.

**Key claim we tested:** A model can be trained to verbalize self-consistency-derived confidence at the output token, achieving high Quality-Accuracy with no hidden-state extraction — implicitly making prefill-DoM selective prediction (F-8) and internal-state probing (F-2) computationally redundant infrastructure for selective prediction on the Qwen2.5 family.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** Replication on Qwen2.5-1.5B / MATH-500 is not yet run. The paper's GSM8K-CoT regime (model emits final answer + label, with CoT scratchpad) is structurally close to our 1024-tok MATH-500 regime, suggesting the replication is high-likelihood successful at the paper's reported magnitude. If sure-Accuracy at sure-Coverage 0.5 ≥ 71.6% in the replication, F-8's selective-prediction story is methodologically dominated. Cross-method gating comparison (P11-FE73) is the deciding experiment.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE70, P11-FE71, P11-FE72, P11-FE73, H-71, H-72, H-73, P11-FE19 (existing C_exact routing — structural twin of EKBM's two-stage design)

**Status:** TO TEST (HIGH priority — directly threatens F-8's standalone value proposition)
