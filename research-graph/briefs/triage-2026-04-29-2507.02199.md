# Triage brief — 2507.02199 — Latent Chain-of-Thought? Decoding the Depth-Recurrent Transformer

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02199
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutation of F-1 universality.** F-1 claims dimensional breathing is universal across transformer architectures and scales. Lu et al. probe Huginn-3.5B (depth-recurrent, weight-tied across blocks) — an out-of-distribution architecture for our F-1 sweep — and find sharp **discontinuities** in hidden-state semantics across blocks (R4 specifically), not the smooth single-peaked curve Qwen produces. *Mechanism:* tied recurrent weights produce per-block discontinuities that are not consistent with a single-peak breathing curve when re-indexed onto the unrolled depth axis. *Falsifiable:* extract Huginn-3.5B residuals across all 2 + 4·r + 2 unrolled blocks on MATH-500, compute participation ratio per block; if the curve is flat or sawtooth (not single-peak Gaussian-shaped), F-1's "universal" claim must be retracted to "universal across feedforward decoder-only models, NOT tied/recurrent ones."

2. **Refutation of F-2 mechanism (DoM is "the" correctness probe).** Lu et al. show that R4 hidden states are uninterpretable via logit lens (top-5 = `inc, unity, friendships, igne, impulse`) but ~100% numeric via coda lens (top-5 = `6, 5, 1, 7, 2`). Logit lens and coda lens read **the same hidden state** but yield opposite interpretations. F-2 reads Qwen L19 prefill via the model's own LM head (logit-lens-equivalent linear projection). If lens choice can flip a layer from "uninterpretable" to "fully numeric," then F-2's AUROC=0.7731 is conditional on the LM-head readout being the correct probe — a probe-aligned-with-readout could be much higher, or DoM could be missing a different correctness coordinate entirely. *Falsifiable:* train a 1-hidden-layer MLP on L19 prefill targeting correctness; if it materially beats AUROC=0.7731, the linear-DoM framing of F-2 is a partial probe and the "L19 is special" story is more about LM-head alignment than about correctness signal density.

3. **Refutation of H-22 (abstract-CoT shows compressed-but-similar breathing).** H-22 predicts breathing replicates qualitatively under abstract/latent CoT. Huginn is the closest open-weights cousin of abstract-CoT we have. Lu et al. show that even with 256 recurrent steps Huginn-no-CoT only reaches 4.62% GSM8K vs 24.87% with explicit CoT, and rank trajectories show **no temporal phase separation** between intermediate and final result tokens (Fig 4) — i.e., no latent CoT structure emerges. If a depth-recurrent architecture purpose-built for latent reasoning fails to produce structured latent CoT, the "breathing replicates on abstract-CoT" prediction is by extension unlikely to hold: there may simply be no latent reasoning trace to breathe over. *Mechanism:* latent CoT may not exist in current architectures; breathing may be a verbalization artifact tied to discrete token emission. *Falsifiable:* extract per-recurrence-step PR on Huginn-3.5B for MATH-500 problems where it gets the right answer; if PR is flat across recurrence steps while Qwen's per-token PR breathes, breathing is bound to verbalization (refuting H-22's premise even before fine-tuning Qwen with Abstract-CoT).

4. **Refutation of F-3 (prefill/final orthogonality is "two circuits").** Our F-3 reads cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM)=0.046 as evidence of two functionally distinct circuits. Lu et al.'s blockwise discontinuity (R3 numeric via logit, R4 only numeric via coda; ~ orthogonal interpretive content within ~one block) shows that two adjacent slices of the same evolving residual can look orthogonal under linear probes simply because the stream is in a "translational" coordinate at the boundary. *Mechanism:* residual streams may pass through transitional coordinates that look orthogonal to neighboring stages without being functionally distinct circuits. *Falsifiable:* compute pairwise cos(DoM_layer_i_prefill, DoM_layer_j_final) across all (i,j) ∈ {0,…,27}² for Qwen-2.5-1.5B. If the orthogonality is sharply localized to a narrow (i,j) band rather than uniform across the matrix, F-3's "two circuits" reading is upgraded to "one circuit passing through a phase change" — the conclusion changes.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)** — Paper's logit-lens probing is methodologically adjacent to DoM-via-LM-head; their finding that R3 (logit) and R4 (coda) carry opposite interpretive valence directly questions whether L19 in Qwen is privileged because of *correctness signal* or because of *readout alignment*. This is a structural caveat to the AUROC=0.7731 number.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM)=0.046)** — Paper's R3↔R4 sharp discontinuity is a one-architecture analogue of two-stage geometric distinctness. It supplies an alternative reading of F-3 as a phase-change artifact rather than two-circuit evidence.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM)** — Paper finds layer-index dependence is non-monotonic (R1, R2 vs R3 vs R4 each carry different content); if Qwen has a similar non-monotonic profile, CoE's 60-feature ensemble may be capturing something L19 alone misses, and F-9's redundancy claim is fragile to layer-selection details.
- **F-10 (PH on trained residual streams = Gaussian null)** — Paper finds rank trajectories don't show structured stepwise reasoning. Indirect support for F-10's "no reasoning topology" reading: even in an architecture that *could* host latent stepwise reasoning, no stepwise structure emerges in linear probes. PH would also presumably miss it.
- **H-2 (breathing on short CoT reasoning)** — Paper's "no-CoT prompting" regime is exactly what H-2 needs. Their system message ("Always return only the final answer straightway") + 4-shot in-context examples is a directly borrowable harness.
- **H-13 (head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token circuits)** — Paper's R4-as-translator finding ("R4 feeds both R1 next cycle and C1, forcing it to encode a representation that differs markedly from other blocks") is a block-level attribution result. The mechanism (a layer carrying a dual-output role becomes uninterpretable in residual coordinates) is worth borrowing into our head-attribution analysis.
- **H-17 (DoM rotation during generation is RoPE-mechanical)** — Paper observes blockwise discontinuities, not rotations. Doesn't directly speak to RoPE, but rules out a competing "smooth rotation" hypothesis for Huginn — meaning RoPE-mechanical drift is likely Qwen-architecture-specific, not universal.
- **H-22 (abstract-CoT shows compressed breathing)** — Strong indirect refutation pressure (see Refutation 3). H-22's premise (latent reasoning exists to be measured) loses support from this paper's negative latent-CoT result.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Logit Lens (RMSNorm + LM head per layer)** — one-line: project intermediate hidden states through the model's own RMS norm + unembedding to get per-layer top-k tokens. *Replication cost*: ~1 hour CPU, single forward pass per problem. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly applicable to Qwen layers L0-L27 to test Refutation 4 (cos similarity matrix of layer-wise DoMs).
- **Coda Lens (learned mini-decoder as probe)** — one-line: route hidden states through a small learned module before unembedding. *Replication cost*: model-specific; Qwen has no analogous "coda module." Could be approximated by training a tiny MLP head on residuals. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — exactly the MLP probe needed to test Refutation 2 (does AUROC > 0.7731 with a non-linear probe?).
- **Rank trajectory tracking** — one-line: track the rank (in vocab) of a target token across all layers/recurrent steps; expect monotone descent in feedforward Transformers, expect step-pattern under latent CoT. *Replication cost*: 20min CPU on cached MATH-500 prefill activations. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives us a layer-resolved companion to the DoM AUROC story; also produces a naturally interpretable plot for the writeup.
- **Signature-token filtering (intermediate vs final vs random baseline)** — one-line: filter problems where intermediate result and final result are distinct single-digit tokens, plus a control "the" token, then track all three rank trajectories. *Replication cost*: 30min CPU; needs problems where MATH-500 has clearly identifiable intermediate result tokens. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes if we can identify single-token intermediate results in a subset of MATH-500 (likely doable for a 30-50 problem subset).
- **No-CoT prompt harness** — one-line: system message "You are a concise and helpful assistant. Always return only the final answer straightway." + 4 in-context examples that show only final answers. *Replication cost*: trivial (prompt change + re-extract). *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly enables H-2 (breathing on no-CoT runs).
- **Top-5 token enumeration as qualitative probe** — one-line: print top-5 decoded tokens at every layer and inspect whether they are domain-aligned (numeric prefixes for arithmetic problems). *Replication cost*: trivial. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — useful as a qualitative companion when an AUROC number is hard to interpret.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Smooth refinement" vs "discontinuous discrete stages" as a binary lens for reading hidden-state evolution.** Our F-3 "two circuits" framing is implicitly the discontinuous-stages reading. Paper makes this binary explicit and provides Huginn as the canonical "discontinuous" data point. Adopting their language sharpens our F-3 framing — and forces us to test whether the discontinuity is layer-localized or distributed.
- **"Lens applicability is per-layer."** Important caveat applicable to F-2 — our DoM-via-LM-head is itself a logit-lens-equivalent at L19, and we have not validated that the LM head is the right probe for layers other than 19. Suggests a sensitivity analysis: how does AUROC depend on which layer we project into the LM head?
- **"Latent CoT does not emerge even in architectures purpose-built for it"** — a refutational framing for the broader research program. We should adopt this as an explicit caveat in PERSPECTIVES.md when discussing what hidden-state correctness signals could mean.
- **"Translator block" framing (R4 has dual downstream roles).** A specific mechanistic story for why a layer's residual would be uninterpretable in its own architecture's natural coordinates. Could explain Qwen's L19 specificity if L19 is similarly load-bearing for multiple downstream paths.

## Datasets & benchmarks

- **GPT-3 one-digit composite arithmetic** — 2k synthetic questions, format `Question: What is (9 + 8) * 2? Answer: 34`. License: open (GPT-3 paper data). Accessibility: trivial (regenerable). Applicable? yes — useful for the rank-trajectory companion experiment because the intermediate result is a single digit/token, ideal for signature-token analysis.
- **GSM8K (Cobbe et al. 2021)** — 8.5k grade-school math word problems. License: MIT. Accessibility: HF. Applicable? yes — already a candidate cross-benchmark for breathing replication; paper provides Huginn baseline numbers (24.87 explicit, 4.93 no-CoT) we can compare Qwen against.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Huginn-3.5B model (Geiping et al. 2502.05171). Architecture: 2 prelude blocks + 4 recurrent blocks (looped r times) + 2 coda blocks. Default r=16 → 68 unrolled blocks.
- HF checkpoint: tomg-group-umd/huginn-0125 (inferred — paper does not specify exact HF ID; the Geiping paper does).
- 8-shot prompting for GSM8K; 4-shot for arithmetic.
- System message verbatim: `"You are a concise and helpful assistant. Always return only the final answer straightway."`
- R1 receives `(s_2, n)` where `n ~ N(0, σ² I)` — initial state for recurrence is randomly drawn. *Gotcha:* this means Huginn is non-deterministic across runs of the same prompt; rank trajectories are averaged over multiple seeds in the paper.
- Code: https://github.com/wenquanlu/huginn-latent-cot
- Logit lens normalization: `RMSNorm(s_i) @ W_U` (matches Huginn's own pre-unembedding norm).
- Coda lens normalization: `RMSNorm(C(RMSNorm(s_i))) @ W_U` (norm both before and after coda).

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2507.02199 — Latent Chain-of-Thought? Decoding the Depth-Recurrent Transformer (Lu, Yang, Lee, Li, Liu, 2025)

**Relevance:** Probes Huginn-3.5B (depth-recurrent, weight-tied across blocks) with logit lens and coda lens to ask whether latent chain-of-thought emerges in a recurrent architecture. Finds **no** evidence: rank trajectories show smooth descent without phase separation between intermediate and final tokens, and increasing recurrence depth from 4 to 256 yields only 3.11→4.62% on GSM8K vs 24.87% with explicit CoT. Two findings transfer directly to our project: (1) lens choice (logit vs coda) flips the interpretive content of the same hidden state — important caveat for F-2's LM-head-aligned DoM probe; (2) blockwise discontinuities in residual semantics offer an alternative reading of F-3's prefill/final orthogonality as a phase-change-at-a-translator-layer rather than two-circuit evidence.

**Key claim we tested:** Hidden-state probes (linear lens / DoM / rank trajectory) reveal structured latent reasoning in transformer residual streams.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** Their negative result on Huginn motivates two cheap Qwen-side replications: (a) layer-wise DoM cos-similarity matrix to test whether F-3's orthogonality is a phase-change artifact (P11-FE72, H-72); (b) rank trajectory of MATH-500 answer tokens across all 28 Qwen layers to test whether L19 is a peak or just a smooth-descent point (P11-FE73). A third, even cheaper test (P11-FE74, H-73): does a small MLP probe at L19 beat linear DoM? If yes, our F-2 framing of "linear DoM is the correctness direction" is incomplete in the same way Huginn's logit lens turned out to be incomplete relative to coda lens.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE72, P11-FE73, P11-FE74, H-72, H-73, H-74

**Status:** TO TEST
