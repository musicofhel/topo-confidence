# Triage brief — 2603.03163 — Conditioned Activation Transport for T2I Safety Steering

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03163
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy)** — central mechanism.
   The paper's Section 4.3 synthetic experiments show that mass-mean (ActAdd) and Linear-ACT
   *both* fail on (a) variance-mismatched distributions with shared centroids, (b) non-convex
   crescents, and (c) multi-modal XOR layouts where local transport directions oppose. They
   then show this is empirical, not just toy: on Infinity, Linear-ACT achieves apparent
   ASR=0.00% but at CLIP=0.07 — i.e. "safety achieved by linear methods is often a
   byproduct of image corruption rather than precise concept removal" (their words).
   The DoM steering vector v=μ_correct−μ_incorrect we propose in H-1 is exactly the ActAdd
   construction. **Falsifiable test**: in any H-1 run, also sweep an MLP transport map
   (CAT-style, residual `T(z)=z+MLP(z)` with zero-init final layer) trained on prefill
   activations of correct vs incorrect MATH-500 problems. If MLP transport produces ≥+5pp
   accuracy gain at matched fluency degradation vs DoM-α steering, the F-2 direction
   functions as a probe but not as a steering vector — H-1's mechanism is partially refuted.

2. **Refutes F-2 (linear DoM is the right correctness readout at L19)**.
   The variance-mismatch synthetic shows that when source/target distributions share centroids
   but differ in shape, ActAdd-style mean-difference vectors are ~zero while a Mahalanobis-
   weighted classifier is fully discriminative. Mechanism for F-2: if correct and incorrect
   prefill clusters at L19 differ more in covariance than in mean, our logistic probe (linear
   in features) underestimates separability. **Falsifiable test**: on the same cached
   `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/` activations that gave AUROC=0.7731, fit (a) a
   regularized-shrinkage Mahalanobis OOD score (their Eq. 4 + Eq. 6) and (b) a
   Gaussian-Discriminant-Analysis classifier with class-specific Σ̂⁻¹ (their Eq. 5). If
   either raises 1.5B prefill AUROC above 0.7731 by ≥0.02 OOF, the linear-DoM probe is
   leaving covariance-shape signal on the table — a claim F-2 does not currently anticipate.

3. **Refutes F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM)=0.046 means orthogonal correctness circuits)**.
   The variance-mismatch synthetic produces *rotated* source/target distributions whose
   mass-mean difference is approximately zero — i.e. orthogonality of mean-difference vectors
   is what you observe when distributions differ by rotation rather than translation. This
   suggests an alternative reading of F-3: prefill and final-token DoMs may be near-orthogonal
   not because they encode different concepts, but because the prefill→final-token correctness
   flow is rotational/non-linear and ActAdd-style summarization is the wrong reduction.
   **Falsifiable test**: train an MLP transport `T_θ` from prefill_correct → final_correct
   (and a separate one for incorrect) on cached P11 NPZs. Push prefill activations through
   `T_θ` and measure cos with final_DoM. If the transported direction has cos ≥ 0.30 with
   final_DoM (vs 0.046 untransported), F-3's "two distinct circuits" reading is partially
   refuted — they are related by a learned non-linear map even though their first moments
   are orthogonal.

4. **Refutes F-9 (CoE-60 trajectory features are redundant with single-layer L19 DoM)**.
   CAT's argument against CAST (Lee et al. ICLR 2025) is that "unsafe representations emerge
   dynamically at deeper layers, rather than being present in the initial embedding" — so
   the right gating is per-layer geometry-aware, not single-layer. Mechanism for F-9: if
   per-layer Mahalanobis-OOD lights up at exactly the layers CoE-60 weights heavily, the
   "redundancy" finding may be an artifact of comparing CoE-60 (a *non-linear* trajectory
   summary) against the *linear* L19 logistic probe — not against L19's full geometric
   capacity. **Falsifiable test**: compute per-layer Mahalanobis-OOD scores for correct vs
   incorrect prefill on cached P11 per-layer activations; combine across layers via simple
   sum of class-conditional log-likelihoods. If the per-layer Mahalanobis stack outperforms
   single-L19-DoM by ≥0.02 AUROC OOF, F-9's redundancy claim collapses — CoE-60 was tracking
   non-linear per-layer signal that single-layer linear DoM missed.

## Methodologies extracted

- **MLP residual transport map** — `T(z)=z+MLP(z)`; single hidden layer, RMSNorm + GELU,
  zero-init final projection so untrained map is identity.
  *Replication cost*: ~30 min CPU per layer/category on cached prefill NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct H-1 supplement and F-2 ceiling test.

- **Regularized dual-objective loss** —
  `L = ‖z_s − T(z_u)‖² + λ‖z_s − T(z_s)‖²`. The second term penalizes the map for moving
  already-safe inputs, so the MLP learns its own conditioning.
  *Replication cost*: trivial (loss change only).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — λ provides a clean knob for fluency-vs-correctness
  trade-off in any LLM steering experiment.

- **Regularized shrinkage precision matrix (Kubokawa-Srivastava 2008, Eq. 4)** —
  `Σ̂⁻¹ = d·[(N−1)Σ_emp + tr(Σ_emp)·I]⁻¹`; produces stable Σ⁻¹ when d ≫ N.
  *Replication cost*: <1 min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — immediate enabler for any Mahalanobis-style scorer at
  d=1536 / N≈500 (we are in d > N regime).

- **Mahalanobis-OOD conditioning (Eq. 6)** — squared Mahalanobis distance to unsafe
  centroid + quantile threshold η_q (default 0.95) — ellipsoidal decision boundary
  conforming to class covariance.
  *Replication cost*: <1 min CPU on cached features.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct alternative gate for selective prediction
  (F-8) and D-bucket detector (H-7).

- **Gaussian-Discriminant-Analysis classifier (Eq. 5)** — closed-form linear discriminant
  with class-specific shrinkage Σ̂⁻¹: `w_k = Σ̂⁻¹μ_k`, `b_k = ln π_k − ½μ_k^T Σ̂⁻¹ μ_k`.
  *Replication cost*: <1 min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — non-trivial generalization of logistic probe; may
  raise F-2 AUROC if class covariances differ.

- **Affine transport baseline `T_aff(z)=Wz+b` (zero-init)** — explicitly between ActAdd
  (translation only) and full MLP (non-linear); learns rotations.
  *Replication cost*: ~10 min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — disentangles which capability gain comes from rotation
  vs full non-linearity.

- **Synthetic-manifold validation suite** — 4 toy 2D distributions (isotropic Gaussian /
  variance-mismatch / moon / XOR) for stress-testing transport methods.
  *Replication cost*: ~1 hour CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — diagnostic mapping our L19 prefill (correct vs
  incorrect) onto one of these manifold classes via low-dim projection.

- **Layer-wise (vs early-layer) conditioning** — gate fires only when *the current layer's*
  representation is close to the unsafe manifold, not based on the input embedding.
  *Replication cost*: implementation only, no data needed.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — natural fit for the per-layer breathing curve we
  already have from P11 stage markers.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Safety in T2I models is geometrically complex and cannot be resolved by traversing a
  single linear direction."** Direct conceptual challenge to F-2's single-direction reading;
  reframes "DoM" as an artifact of the probe family rather than a fundamental feature.
- **"Apparent safety from linear steering is a side-effect of image corruption — pushing
  activations off-manifold rather than steering them along it."** For our setting:
  a steering vector that lowers MATH accuracy by destroying fluency is not steering
  correctness. Caution applies to interpreting any negative H-1 result.
- **"Conditioning before vs at the layer that produces the concept."** Implies that *where*
  along the network you gate is a non-trivial design choice — for our pipeline, this maps
  to whether the prefill-vs-final-token gate fires on prompt activations (early) or after
  the model has dynamically constructed correctness signal (deeper).
- **"Variance and rotation, not just centroid, encode the concept boundary."** Reframes the
  cos=0.046 result of F-3 as plausibly compatible with non-orthogonal underlying circuits.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- MLP transport: single hidden layer, RMSNorm + GELU activation, **final projection
  zero-initialized** so untrained map = identity (prevents pre-training degradation).
- Regularization weight λ in dual-objective loss (Eq. 3) — explicit knob trading off
  steering aggressiveness vs identity-preservation on already-safe inputs.
- Steering applied only to **second half of model layers** — early-layer steering produced
  "completely degraded images." Consistent with our P11 result that L19 (mid-late, not
  early) carries the correctness signal.
- Pre-steering reduction: **mean-pool over N tokens** before computing gate/transport,
  then broadcast steering δ to all N tokens (Eq. 1 / Algorithm 1). Worth noting we currently
  use the prefill-final-token activation, not a mean-pool.
- Steering strength α swept over {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0}.
- Mahalanobis quantile threshold η_q = 0.95 default.
- Cosine-similarity filter S(z_u, z_s) > 0.7 to retain semantically aligned safe/unsafe
  pairs in dataset construction (Section 3.2) — useful pattern for any contrastive-pair
  curation we do later.
- Code: https://github.com/NASK-AISafety/conditional-activation-transport.

## 2603.03163 — Conditioned Activation Transport for T2I Safety Steering (Chrabąszcz, Szymczyk, Dubiński, Trzciński, Boenisch, Dziedzic, 2026)

**Relevance:** T2I safety paper but its central methodological argument transfers
directly to LLM hidden-state work: linear activation steering (ActAdd, Linear-ACT)
fails on activation manifolds with variance mismatch, non-convex shape, or multi-modal
structure, and apparent "safety" from linear methods is often a side-effect of
pushing activations off-manifold rather than precise concept removal. The proposed
remedy — non-linear MLP residual transport with geometry-aware (Mahalanobis-OOD)
conditioning, and a regularized-shrinkage precision matrix usable in the d ≫ N
regime — is directly applicable to F-2 (linear DoM probe), F-3 (cos=0.046
orthogonality), F-7 / H-7 (D-bucket detection), F-8 (selective prediction), and
H-1 (DoM steering).

**Key claim we tested:** That activation manifolds for high-level concepts often have
covariance/rotation/multimodal structure that a single mean-difference vector cannot
capture, so any ActAdd-style intervention either underperforms or destroys utility
via off-manifold corruption.

**Our result:** **TO TEST.** The paper demonstrates this on T2I architectures
(Z-Image, Infinity) and 4 synthetic 2D manifolds. We have not yet checked whether
our L19 prefill correctness manifold exhibits variance-mismatch or XOR-style
geometry; FE76 / H-76 is the cheap diagnostic that gates the rest. If it does,
F-2's 0.7731 is a linear-projection lower bound and H-1's design needs revision.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE73, P11-FE74, P11-FE75, P11-FE76, P11-FE77;
H-73, H-74, H-75, H-76.
