# Triage brief — 2602.23696 — Optimizer-Induced Low-Dimensional Drift and Transverse Dynamics in Transformer Training

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23696
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

This is a **parameter-space training-dynamics** paper, not an activation/inference paper. The refutations below treat its empirical claims as predictions about what we should observe if the L19 prefill DoM and breathing dynamics are downstream consequences of the optimizer-induced backbone Xu identifies.

1. **Refutes H-8 (DoM stability across training checkpoints) at long horizons.** Xu measures phase-level backbone cosines `|⟨v_E, v_L⟩| ≈ 0.32` between early-phase and late-phase parameter drifts in the same run — a ≈71° rotation. If the L19 prefill DoM is sourced by a parameter-space direction that is itself the (locally smooth, globally rotating) backbone, then H-8's prediction that "prefill DoM is stable across training checkpoints" should fail at long horizons even for a single uninterrupted training run. **Mechanism**: backbone rotates ≈71° between early/late phases; L19 weights re-mix correspondingly; the supervised DoM extracted at L19 must rotate too. **Falsifiable test**: extract L19 prefill DoM from N intermediate Qwen-2.5-1.5B checkpoints (HF release has multiple) and compute cos(DoM_t, DoM_final). If H-8 predicts >0.95, Xu predicts ≤0.5 at any phase boundary (e.g. SFT→DPO).

2. **Refutes the "breathing is content-dependent" framing in F-5.** Xu shows that ≈80% of switching-direction variance is *transverse* to the backbone, that switching events use near-orthogonal directions (pairwise |cos|<0.08), and that this transverse subspace spans ≥10 independent dimensions. He attributes this to optimizer-integrated noise filtering: with β2=0, transverse dynamics become destructive (corr(p_ood, ‖r‖) flips from +0.86 to −0.57). If breathing is the inference-time analog of this transverse cloud, then F-5's claim ("breathing is content-dependent, not AR-mechanics") may be the wrong contrast — breathing might instead be **optimizer-dependent transverse noise** that any AdamW-trained AR model exhibits. **Mechanism**: per-token Δactivations in residual stream span an analogous transverse subspace whose magnitude is set by AdamW's β2 during pretraining, not by problem content. **Falsifiable test**: compare breathing PR profiles on AdamW-pretrained Qwen vs an SGD/momentum-pretrained 51M reference (Xu's released code at github.com/skydancerosel/mini_gpt provides one). If breathing requires AdamW, F-5's content-dependence framing is incomplete.

3. **Refutes H-4 (breathing ↔ Sharpness Dimension / EoS correspondence).** Xu's central finding (Section 4.7, 4.9, 10.2) is that the backbone is **not** the top Fisher eigenvector and **not** aligned with curvature directions — instantaneous gradient projection on the backbone is at the noise floor (~0.01), only optimizer-integrated 200-step updates align (0.15–0.32). Backbone curvature (Rayleigh quotient) increases by ≈3000× during training, but this is a *consequence* of accumulated drift, not its cause. If breathing tracks EoS sharpness (H-4's prediction), and the EoS-relevant Hessian directions are transverse to the backbone, then breathing should **not** correlate with the backbone-direction PR signature predicted by F-1. **Mechanism**: Xu's data show curvature and trajectory geometry decouple. **Falsifiable test**: at Qwen intermediate checkpoints, regress L19 PR magnitude against (a) Hessian top-eigenvalue (EoS prediction), (b) cumulative parameter drift along estimated backbone (Xu's prediction). H-4 expects (a) wins; refutation has (b) win.

4. **Reframes F-3 (prefill–final orthogonality, cos 0.046) as a single-mechanism artifact.** Xu documents a sign reversal in update–backbone alignment at the λ-switch (cos ≈ −0.32 early → +0.11 late), with the late-phase drift living in a direction nearly orthogonal to the early-phase backbone (|⟨v_E, v_L⟩| = 0.32). If Qwen pretraining + post-training (instruct/SFT) is the analog of an objective reweighting, then prefill_DoM and final_DoM may be **the same backbone observed in two different training phases**, not two distinct inference circuits. F-3 currently reads the orthogonality as evidence that prefill and final-token correctness use independent mechanisms. **Mechanism**: same parameter-space direction projects differently into L19 activations under the pre-vs-post-instruct weight regime. **Falsifiable test**: take a base-only Qwen variant (pre-instruct checkpoint, if released) and re-extract prefill_DoM and final_DoM. If F-3 holds, cos stays ≈0.05. If Xu's reframing holds, cos deviates substantially in either direction (likely positive on the base model).

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731, OOF 5-fold)**. Xu's strongest claim is that under AdamW the cumulative parameter trajectory has a single dominant direction ("backbone") that captures 60–80% of long-horizon displacement and is the unique mode above the rolling-window intra-signal spectral gap (k*=1 in 77.8% of windows). If F-2's effective L19 DoM is the residual-stream image of this parameter-space backbone, then the explanation for "why one direction at one layer is so predictive" is: it's the *single* mode above the spectral gap, not a coincidentally-useful linear probe.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046)**. Xu's λ-switch produces a measured sign reversal (early `−0.32` → late `+0.11`) and 71° rotation. The 0.046 prefill–final orthogonality is consistent with two backbone phases of the same mechanism rather than two independent circuits.
- **F-5 (breathing is content-dependent, not AR-mechanics)**. Xu's transverse subspace dynamics are *content/objective-driven* (the λ-switch reorganizes them) but emerge only under AdamW with high β2. F-5's content-dependence and Xu's optimizer-dependence are *both* consistent with breathing.
- **F-6 (7B prefill PR inversion driven by incorrect-group concentration)**. Xu's "stronger rank-1 concentration in late phase" (PC1 ≈ 60% early → 81% late) is a possible parameter-space analog: incorrect-group concentration may correspond to a tighter post-objective-reweight backbone.
- **H-4 (breathing ↔ Sharpness Dimension / EoS)**. Xu directly contradicts the assumption underlying H-4 that high curvature directions are the relevant ones — see Refutation 3.
- **H-8 (prefill DoM stability across training checkpoints)**. Direct test target — see Refutation 1.
- **H-10 (breathing pattern changes after fine-tuning)**. Xu's λ-switch is the cleanest analog of fine-tuning we have access to (synthetic but controlled). His finding that fine-tuning rotates the backbone by 71° and produces a backbone "dead zone" near the transition supports H-10's premise that fine-tuning produces measurable geometric reorganization, but predicts a more specific pattern (rotation through a transient null direction) than H-10 currently formulates.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Uncentered (drift-from-init) trajectory PCA** — SVD of the matrix of parameter drifts θ(t) − θ(0), without mean subtraction. Centering would absorb the monotonic component; uncentered preserves absolute displacement.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly applicable to per-token residual-stream trajectories during MATH-500 generation. Take L19 activations across the prefill→final path per problem; do uncentered SVD on the Δactivation matrix; check if PC1 captures ≥60% of energy and whether PC1 ≈ DoM direction.
- **Rolling-window backbone alignment ρ(t)** — track local PC1 of parameter drift in W=10 windows, measure adjacent-window cosine to identify smooth-vs-rotating regions.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — analog: rolling-window PC1 over per-token residual updates to track DoM rotation across CoT positions.
- **Power-law fits |a(t)| = C·t^γ to backbone and residual norms** — distinguishes acceleration / consolidation / plateau phases.
  *Replication cost*: 15min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — apply to PR(t) and DoM-projected magnitude across token positions to test whether breathing decomposes into the same three phases.
- **Backbone–residual decomposition θ(t) = θ(0) + a(t)·v_b + r(t)** with r ⊥ v_b — separates monotonic drift from oscillatory dynamics.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct analog: decompose L19 activation per token into (DoM-projected scalar) + (orthogonal residual). Test whether residual norm tracks breathing PR.
- **Update-direction vs gradient-direction alignment** — `|cos(u_t, v_b)|` for 200-step cumulative update vs `|cos(g_t, v_b)|` for per-batch gradient.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — analog: cumulative residual change across many tokens vs per-token contribution. The 20–30× ratio Xu reports is a strong testable claim if rephrased for activations.
- **Fisher Rayleigh quotient `q(v) = (1/M)‖Gv‖²` along candidate directions** — measures curvature along the backbone using mini-batch gradients.
  *Replication cost*: 4h H100.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — measure Hessian-vector-product Rayleigh quotient along DoM at L19 across MATH-500 to test whether DoM is or is not a high-curvature direction.
- **β2 ablation sweep {0.99, 0.95, 0.90, 0.80, 0.0}** — graded control on backbone dominance.
  *Replication cost*: not applicable to frozen Qwen.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — we don't retrain Qwen.
- **Reheating protocol** — resume from late checkpoint with fresh AdamW, doubled probe loss, cosine LR schedule.
  *Replication cost*: not directly applicable.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — analog: at a generation step where p_ood-equivalent (correctness signal) has decayed, intervene with a high-LR steering update along DoM and observe whether correctness recovers transiently. Connects to H-1 steering.
- **Stability coefficient α_j = 1 − C·‖ΔG‖_F²/gap_j²** (Davis–Kahan based) — bounds eigenspace rotation per window.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — applied to per-token Δactivation Gram spectrum: α_1 should be high (DoM stable), α_j≥3 should be low (residual-mode rotations free).
- **Intra-signal gap framework (k* = arg max σ_j/σ_{j+1})** — finds the dominant intra-signal spectral gap when D ≫ W and BBP is vacuous.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly applicable to per-token residual update spectra; yields a *parameter-free* DoM identification.

## Approaches & framings

- **Cumulative trajectory geometry as primary object**, replacing instantaneous gradient/loss-landscape geometry. Intersects our framing: we currently treat L19 prefill as an instantaneous activation; Xu's framing suggests treating it as the integrated outcome of token-by-token residual updates and asking whether the *cumulative* signal is stronger than the instantaneous one.
- **Slow–fast manifold decomposition** — backbone is slow (low-dim, smooth), transverse residual is fast (high-dim, oscillatory). Direct analog for our prefill (slow signal) vs final-token (fast oscillatory) split (F-2 vs F-3).
- **Optimizer as geometric shaper, not just convergence accelerator** — AdamW's β2 acts as a frequency filter selecting temporally coherent gradient components. Reframes our F-1/F-5 findings as potential downstream consequences of AdamW pretraining specifically.
- **Spectral gap dynamics as universal mechanism** — Xu claims gap rise/plateau/collapse is universal across grokking and language modeling (companion 2026d work). If true, our breathing/PR temporal curves should map onto this same three-phase structure.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Decoder-only GPT-2-family transformer: 8 layers, d_model=512, 16 heads, d_ff=2048, 51M params total, ~25M trunk params (attn QKV/O + MLP up/down).
- AdamW with β1=0.9, β2=0.95 default, LR 1e-3, cosine decay, 1500-step warmup, weight decay 0.5 (decoupled), effective batch 128 (64×2 grad accum).
- Composite loss `L_LM + λ·L_probe`, λ doubled at step 4000 (the "λ-switch" — central to most of Xu's reorientation findings).
- 51 checkpoints over 10k steps (interval 200), two seeds (42, 271).
- Code: https://github.com/skydancerosel/mini_gpt
- Trunk-only PCA (excludes embeddings, layer norms, positional embeddings, masks).
- Rolling window W=10 checkpoints (~2000 steps). Adjacent-window cosine ρ(t) > 0.7 throughout.
- Phase split: early = steps 1–4000, late = 4000–10000.
- Reheating: zeroed momentum/second-moment buffers, cosine LR over 2000 steps, λ=4.0.
- Companion paper (Xu 2026d, "spectral edge thesis") provides theoretical grounding via Gram spectrum + Davis–Kahan; not yet on arxiv per the bibliography.
