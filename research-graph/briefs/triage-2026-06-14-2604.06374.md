# Triage brief — 2604.06374 — The Illusion of Superposition? A Principled Analysis of Latent Thinking in Language Models

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06374
Triaged: 2026-06-14

Authors: Michael Rizvi-Martel, Guillaume Rabusseau, Marius Mosbach (Mila / Université de Montréal / McGill), 2026 (preprint, under review).

One-paragraph what-it-is: The paper asks whether latent continuous chain-of-thought (Soft Thinking, Coconut) actually exploits *superposition* — maintaining multiple candidate continuations in one hidden state. Across three regimes (training-free Soft Thinking, fine-tuned Coconut, from-scratch Coconut) it uses **Logit Lens** + **entity-level belief-evolution probing** to show that superposition only emerges in small **from-scratch** models. Off-the-shelf and fine-tuned models collapse a superposed input to a single discrete interpretation early in the forward pass (entropy profiles match discrete CoT; KL(soft‖discrete) ≈ 10⁻⁴; cosine(soft, argmax) = 0.998±0.013 on Qwen2-1.5B, 0.996±0.025 on QwQ-32B). Two mechanisms are isolated: (i) **next-token-prediction pretraining forces a last-layer entropy collapse** — shown to be content-independent via synthetic uniform random-token superpositions (k=2…100) that collapse under pretrained weights but stay high-entropy under random-init weights (Fig. 5); (ii) **capacity matters** — even from-scratch, 8L/12L models learn shortcuts while 2L/4L preserve superposition.

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-5 ("Breathing is content-dependent, not AR-mechanics").** The paper's Figure 5 is an existence proof of a *content-independent* representational dynamic: synthetic superpositions built from **uniformly random** token sets (k = 2, 5, 10, 50, 100) all collapse entropy at the final layers under the *pretrained* Qwen2-1.5B weights, but the same inputs stay high-entropy under *random-init* weights. The collapse is driven by the learned unembedding geometry, not by problem content. **Mechanism:** the last-layer logit-lens projection of any near-discrete input commits to its nearest vocabulary atom regardless of semantic content. If our "breathing" (dimensional expansion/collapse, F-1/F-5) is dominated by this same pretraining-induced final-layer commitment, then the "content-dependent" attribution is confounded by an AR-pretraining artifact — the exact thing F-5 claims to have excluded. **Falsifiable:** project cached L19 prefill activations through the Qwen-2.5-1.5B unembedding, compute logit-lens Shannon entropy per problem, and test whether its magnitude is invariant to MATH-500 problem content / correctness (paper predicts near-invariance; F-5 predicts content-dependence). Also run the random-token-mixture control on our extractor.

2. **Refutes / reframes F-4 ("Correct trajectories collapse harder than incorrect at the final token — asymmetric collapse").** The paper shows that the *final layers* of a pretrained model drive entropy to near-zero for **every** input — a saturating commitment regime. **Mechanism:** when a measured quantity (participation ratio / entropy) saturates against a floor at the final token, between-group differences are compressed and a small residual gap can read as "correct collapses harder" while actually being a thresholding artifact of universal saturation, not a correctness-linked geometric signal. **Falsifiable:** measure the correct-vs-incorrect collapse asymmetry at *intermediate* layers (where entropy is still high) and at the final layer; if the asymmetry exists only at/after the saturating final layers and vanishes pre-collapse, F-4 is a final-layer saturation effect, not a deep trajectory-geometry signature. (This is the same direction as the existing H-944 doubt, now with a concrete pretraining-collapse mechanism behind it.)

3. **Refutes the "reasoning-geometry" reading of F-2 / F-8 (single L19 prefill DoM direction → AUROC 0.7731; selective prediction).** The paper's fine-tuned Coconut result — **96.6% accuracy with *no* latent tokens**, with belief-evolution probing showing the answer is extracted from the *question* representation in a single forward pass (a "shortcut", not step-by-step reasoning) — argues that for pretrained models the answer is largely determined by the static question encoding before any reasoning happens. **Mechanism:** if correctness is a single-pass, question-determined property, then a prefill direction read *before generation* (F-2's DoM) is plausibly reading **question difficulty/familiarity** (a static input property), not any reasoning-trajectory geometry. **Falsifiable:** test whether prefill-DoM AUROC (0.7731) is redundant with a pure question-difficulty probe (e.g. 2509.12886 LLM-perceived-difficulty, already REPLICATED) — high redundancy supports the shortcut reading and demotes the "DoM = reasoning geometry" interpretation. This sharpens the existing H-5 and H-950 question.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-5 (content-dependent breathing) ↔ paper §6 / Fig. 5.** Direct mechanistic collision: paper claims a content-independent final-layer entropy collapse under pretrained weights. Our headline geometry numbers (PC1 ≈ DoM, cos(DoM,PC1)=0.9216; cov-spectrum AUROC 0.7928) live in the L19 prefill regime; the paper would predict that any *final-layer* readout is content-flattened.
- **F-4 (asymmetric collapse) ↔ paper "commit to a token in the last layers".** F-4 measures collapse at the final token; the paper's universal last-layer commitment is the natural null for "correct collapses harder."
- **F-2 (L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731) / F-8 (selective pred 71.6% @ cov 0.5) ↔ paper's 96.6%-no-latent shortcut.** If the answer is question-determined and single-pass, the prefill DoM signal is a static-input readout — anchor numbers: prefill DoM 0.7731 vs final-token DoM 0.7186 (the smaller-than-expected final-token number is itself consistent with a question-determined-then-saturated picture).
- **F-3 (prefill/final DoM orthogonality, cos = 0.046) ↔ paper's "latent steps don't change the answer".** A near-orthogonal final-token direction that nonetheless predicts worse (0.7186 < 0.7731) is consistent with the final-token state being a saturated commitment rather than a refined reasoning readout.
- **H-2 (breathing on short CoT), H-5 (prefill = familiarity vs decomposability), H-944 (off-DoM dispersion), H-950 (DoM is a non-concept latent, not difficulty)** all intersect the paper's shortcut / capacity / collapse framing.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Logit Lens entropy profiling across layers** — project intermediate hidden states `h^(ℓ)` through the unembedding `U`, softmax, take Shannon entropy at each layer to trace where the model commits.
  *Replication cost*: ~30min CPU on cached L19 (need only the Qwen-2.5-1.5B LM head `U`, downloadable; one-layer version is trivial). Full 28-layer version needs fresh multi-layer extraction (H100).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — it is the single cheapest cross-check against F-4/F-5 and we already have the L19 prefill cache.
- **Single-token soft→discrete intervention (KL + cosine)** — run two forward passes differing only in one token (soft vs argmax embedding), compare hidden states by KL and cosine across layers.
  *Replication cost*: H100 (needs soft-thinking generation) — not applicable to static cache.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — requires Soft-Thinking decoding we don't run.
- **Entity-level belief-evolution probing** — categorize entities into {correct-next, wrong-neighbor, target, other}, track the normalized logit-lens distribution over these classes across reasoning steps to distinguish step-by-step reasoning from shortcut/echo-chamber.
  *Replication cost*: needs a symbolic-entity task (ProsQA/ProntoQA); not directly MATH-500-shaped. ~1 day to adapt.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — our task has no discrete entity graph; the *belief-evolution-across-CoT-steps* idea is borrowable though (see FE1377).
- **Synthetic uniform-superposition entropy curve (pretrained vs random-init control)** — feed convex combinations of k uniformly-random token embeddings, plot per-layer entropy for trained vs randomly-initialized weights to isolate the pretraining contribution.
  *Replication cost*: ~30min CPU (one extra forward pass on random tokens + random-init control).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — clean control that tells us whether our L19 geometry is pretraining-imposed.
- **Mixing-weight entropy** — Shannon entropy of the convex-combination weights `p_t` used to build a soft token (measures how peaky the "superposition" really is).
  *Replication cost*: trivial but only meaningful for soft-thinking decoding.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no.
- **Capacity/depth ablation (2/4/8/12 layers)** — train from scratch at varying depth, read shortcut-vs-superposition off the belief curve.
  *Replication cost*: needs from-scratch training; not in scope.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Pretraining as a commitment prior."** The reframing that next-token-prediction *itself* installs a last-layer collapse-to-discrete bias is a clean mechanistic lens for our F-4/F-5 collapse phenomena — it predicts a content-independent saturation that our "content-dependent breathing" claim must beat.
- **Forced vs learned superposition (Defs 2–3).** A typed distinction: superposition imposed at the input vs emergent from training. Intersects our framing in that our DoM/PC1 signal is a *learned*-representation property of a pretrained model — by the paper's taxonomy, exactly the regime where superposition collapses, so any "multi-candidate" reading of D-bucket fragility (F-7) is suspect.
- **Shortcut / echo-chamber explanation of latent-CoT gains.** The claim that latent tokens add ~3% via answer-already-in-the-question intersects H-5/H-950: it predicts prefill correctness signal is question-static, not reasoning-dynamic.
- **"Flexibility, not superposition."** The closing reframe (continuous CoT helps by escaping the discretization bottleneck, not by parallel search) is orthogonal to our findings but worth filing for any future latent-reasoning extension.

## Datasets & benchmarks

- **MATH500** (Lightman et al. 2023) — 500 problems, the paper's main-text benchmark. Applicable? **yes** — identical to our canonical MATH-500 1024-tok regime; this is the rare triage paper sharing our exact eval set.
- **GSM8K** (Cobbe et al. 2021, 500-example test subset) — grade-school math. HF, open. Applicable? partially — easier distribution; usable as an OOD difficulty contrast for the shortcut test.
- **AIME2024** — competition math, tiny (30 problems). Open. Applicable? marginal — too small for AUROC but a hard-tail probe.
- **ProsQA / ProntoQA** (synthetic graph-traversal QA, symbolic 40-token tokenizer variant) — used for entity-belief probing. Applicable? **no** — no entity graph in MATH-500; only the methodology transfers.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Models: **QwQ-32B** (reasoning) main text; **Qwen2-1.5B** (base) in appendix — note this is Qwen**2**-1.5B, close kin to our Qwen-2.5-1.5B, so the unembedding-collapse result is near-directly relevant to our model family.
- Logit Lens applied at **5 strategic layers**; entropy sampled every **50 decoding steps**.
- Fig. 5 control: **50 samples per condition**, ±1 std bands; pretrained-vs-random-init weights is the key isolation design.
- From-scratch model: GPT-2-style, 8 heads, 768-dim; 2-layer chosen to match Zhu et al. (2025) theory; trained so step *i* uses *i* latent thoughts to predict node *i* (never trained on the full gold CoT).
- Table 2 anchors: fine-tuned Coconut ProsQA 99.0% (with latents) vs 96.6% (no latents) vs 85.3% discrete CoT; from-scratch 2L: 94.5 (w/) vs 13.8 (w/o) — the with/without gap is the superposition fingerprint.
- No code link surfaced in the body (acknowledges NVIDIA compute; NSERC/CIFAR funding). Logit-lens is standard `softmax(U h^(ℓ))`.

- **Logit-lens entropy on cached L19 prefill**: load Qwen-2.5-1.5B `lm_head` weight `U` (HF), apply `softmax(U @ h)` to the 500×1536 cached L19 prefill activations (`pathway11_h100/...` Stage-2 NPZ), compute per-problem Shannon entropy, split by the correct/incorrect labels in `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/results.json`. Cross-check: does final-(L19)-layer logit-lens entropy differ correct-vs-incorrect (F-4/F-5 prediction) or is it flat (paper prediction)?

- **DoM-vs-logit-lens-entropy redundancy**: regress correctness on DoM score and logit-lens entropy/top-1-prob; report incremental AUROC over the 0.7731 DoM baseline using the existing OOF 5-fold split.
