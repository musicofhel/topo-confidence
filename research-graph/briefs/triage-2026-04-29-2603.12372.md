# Triage brief — 2603.12372 — ReBalance: Efficient Reasoning with Balanced Thinking

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12372
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes a strong reading of F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046)**.
   ReBalance derives a *single* steering direction from hidden-state prototypes
   collected at the first token of each reasoning step (across the entire
   generation, both early and late steps), and applies it everywhere with only
   a scalar (`α_s = λ_s δ_s`) modulated by confidence. They report consistent
   gains across 9 benchmarks and 4 model scales (e.g. +3.4 Pass@1 / −23.1% tokens
   on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B / MATH-500). If the "DoM rotates strongly
   over generation" picture behind F-3 generalised to *any* behavioural
   direction, a single per-step vector could not be both effective and stable
   across step indices. Falsifiable: compute their μ^O − μ^U direction at
   step-positions 1, 5, 10, 20 separately on Qwen-1.5B; if cosines are ≥ 0.5,
   the rotation property is direction-specific (correctness-aligned only) and
   F-3's "directions are orthogonal" headline understates how much rotation is
   driven by the *correctness label* vs intrinsic generation dynamics.

2. **Refutes the "single-layer L19 DoM subsumes trajectory features" claim
   (F-9)**. ReBalance treats *confidence variance over a sliding window* as a
   non-redundant signal — it's their primary discriminator for overthinking vs
   underthinking modes, distinct from the absolute confidence level. F-9 says
   CoE-60 trajectory features add nothing on top of single-layer L19 DoM for
   correctness. Falsifiable: extract per-step max-prob variance over `|W|=2`
   from cached Stage 2 NPZs, fit logistic regression for correctness
   prediction; compare AUROC vs L19 DoM (0.7731) and vs DoM ⊕ variance. If
   variance adds ≥ 0.02 AUROC, F-9's redundancy claim is a sampling artifact
   of the specific CoE-60 parameterisation, not a property of trajectory
   features in general.

3. **Refutes the implicit "correctness label is the only useful steering
   target" reading of F-2 / F-8 / H-1**. ReBalance derives a steering vector
   without ever using a correctness label — it labels steps as overthinking
   (low-c, high-variance) or underthinking (high-c, low-variance) using
   confidence statistics alone, then aggregates hidden states into prototypes.
   This is a label-free analogue of H-1's per-position DoM steering. If their
   training-free direction yields equal or better selective-prediction
   behaviour at matched compute, then F-8's selective-prediction story (which
   requires correctness labels for the DoM fit) is sub-optimal: a label-free
   confidence-variance prototype captures the same direction. Falsifiable:
   compute cos between ReBalance's μ^O − μ^U direction and the F-2 prefill
   correctness DoM on Qwen-1.5B; if |cos| > 0.3, the directions are aligned
   and the supervised DoM is being approximated by an unsupervised method,
   which weakens the "supervised correctness signal is the unique probe" story.

4. **Refutes the "step-boundary token has no special status" assumption
   underlying F-2 (prefill > final-token)**. ReBalance picks the *first token
   of each reasoning step* as its injection point and reports better
   discriminability there than at arbitrary step-internal tokens (Appendix A.5
   linear-probing analysis says deeper layers + step-boundary positions show
   stronger reasoning-mode separation). F-2 frames the "prefill vs final" axis
   as the interesting one; this paper says step-boundaries are also a
   privileged position. Falsifiable: on cached MATH-500 Stage 2 traces, locate
   "Step N:" / "\n\n" boundaries, extract L19 activations, fit DoM for
   correctness; compare AUROC against prefill (0.7731) and final-token
   (0.7186). If step-boundary AUROC is between these, prefill is privileged
   for *unconditional* prediction but step-boundaries carry mid-trace
   correctness updates that the prefill direction can't see — non-trivial
   addition to F-2.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy)** — ReBalance is a
  positive existence proof of the *general* claim with a different recipe.
  Confidence-variance prototypes substitute for per-position DoM banks. Their
  +3.4 Pass@1 on Qwen-1.5B / MATH-500 exceeds the kind of margin H-1 was
  budgeted to detect.
- **H-12 (semantic-entropy / label-free probes replace supervised DoM)** —
  direct match. Their confidence (geometric mean of max-prob) is the
  step-aggregated analogue of token-level entropy; their variance metric over
  `W=2` is the closest possible analogue of "consistency of confidence over a
  short trajectory window".
- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)** — ReBalance's per-step injection
  point is *not* the prefill; their direction lives at step-boundaries.
  Comparing AUROC of (their direction) at the prefill vs at step-boundaries
  is a direct test of whether F-2's privileged position is unique to prefill
  or generalises to any "boundary" token.
- **F-7 (D-bucket: K=1-right, K=8-wrong, distinctive collective signature)** —
  ReBalance's overthinking definition (low-confidence + high-variance) is
  near-mechanically what D-bucket trajectories should look like.
  Specifically, D-bucket members get the right answer the first time but lose
  it on resampling — which the ReBalance framework would call "underthinking"
  in their notation (high-confidence, low-variance, then exploration too
  shallow). Cross-mapping their O / U buckets onto our A/B/C/D buckets is a
  one-shot experiment.
- **F-8 (selective-prediction at coverage 0.5 → 71.6% on answered)** — their
  framework gives accuracy lifts under fixed compute (same K=1) by changing
  the trajectory; F-8 gives selective-prediction by routing to abstain. These
  are orthogonal mitigations for the same correctness gap.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 ↔ L19 DoM redundancy)** — the variance-over-window feature is
  a different trajectory summary than CoE; F-9's null result for CoE-60 is
  not evidence that *all* trajectory features are redundant with L19 DoM.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Step-wise confidence (geometric mean of max token probs per step)** —
  `c_s = exp((1/|T_s|) Σ ln p^max_t)`. *Replication cost*: trivial,
  ~1 line over cached log-probs. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives a
  scalar to align against L19 DoM.

- **Confidence variance over sliding window `|W|=2`** — short-term fluctuation
  of step-wise confidence. *Replication cost*: trivial, post-processing of
  cached confidences. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — feeds FE73 below.

- **Quantile-thresholded over-/under-thinking labels** — `O = {s : c_s ≤ τ_c^L
  ∧ Var(c_s) ≥ τ_v^H}`, `U = {s : c_s ≥ τ_c^H ∧ Var(c_s) ≤ τ_v^L}`.
  *Replication cost*: trivial. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives label-free
  bucket assignment to compare against our A/B/C/D buckets.

- **Hidden-state prototype averaging at step-boundary token** —
  `μ^O = mean(h_{t_s^(1)} : s ∈ O)`, same for U; first-token-of-step extraction
  from a deep, auto-selected layer. *Replication cost*: 30min H100 if we
  re-extract; <10min CPU on cached Stage 2 NPZs once we tag step-boundary
  tokens. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes.

- **Linear-probing-driven layer auto-selection** — pick the deep layer with
  highest R² for predicting confidence-mode label from the hidden state.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU on cached NPZs, all 28 layers × O/U labels.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — same idea as our DoM AUROC sweep but with
  a different target label.

- **Sign-modulated tanh dynamic strength function** —
  `g(c, v) = sign(c − τ_c^H) · B(c, v) · tanh(|c − τ_c^H|)`.
  *Replication cost*: ~10 lines. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — drop-in for
  H-1 steering at inference.

- **Variance-aware amplitude `B(c_s, v_s)`** — piecewise function over
  (moderate, overthinking, underthinking) regions with boundaries `B_m, B_o,
  B_u ∈ [0, 0.2]`. *Replication cost*: trivial. *We'd plausibly run this*:
  optional — only after the simpler tanh modulation is shown to give traction.

- **First-token-of-step injection (Eq 8)** — apply steering at the residual
  stream before final projection at step boundary only, not every token.
  *Replication cost*: 30min H100 to integrate into our generation harness.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — much cheaper than per-token steering and
  could rescue the per-position DoM steering plan in H-1.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Overthinking vs underthinking" as a continuous behavioural axis distinct
  from correctness** — frames the steerable property as a generation-dynamics
  mode (variance of confidence), not as a correctness label. Intersects H-1
  (steering target need not be the correctness DoM) and H-5 (problem
  familiarity vs decomposability — their "underthinking" is plausibly the
  unfamiliar-but-decomposable case our prefill direction conflates).

- **Steering at step-boundaries rather than every token** — a sparse-injection
  approach. Intersects F-3 (DoM rotation) — sparse injection at a privileged
  token-class might side-step the rotation problem H-1 has been wrestling with.

- **Confidence variance as a "reasoning-dynamics" signal** — reframes what
  we'd been treating as just a noise quantity. Intersects F-9 by giving a
  different operational definition of "trajectory feature" than the CoE
  parameterisation that was killed.

- **Training-free framework via offline prototype + online modulation** —
  matches the stance behind H-1 (all our experiments are inference-time, no
  fine-tuning) and provides a concrete control surface.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Models tested: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-{1.5B, 7B}, Qwen3-14B, QwQ-32B,
  OpenPangu-Embedded-7B-V1.1. Note that DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B and
  Qwen-2.5-1.5B-Instruct (our model) share the base — non-trivial transfer
  expectation but distillation-specific behaviour likely differs.
- Steering injection layer: auto-selected via per-layer linear-probing R²;
  authors report mid-late layers (Appendix A.5 figure). Our headline layer is
  L19 of 28, which is roughly the same regime — supports cross-paper
  applicability.
- Calibration set: 500 random MATH problems (one-pass, offline).
- Window size for variance: `|W| = 2` (default; sensitivity in Table 11).
- Quantile thresholds: `q_L, q_H ∈ (0, 1)` empirical, model-specific.
- Mode boundary `B_u ∈ [0, 0.2]` (Table 11).
- No fine-tuning; no RL; no extra inference cost beyond +1 vector add per
  step boundary.

## Replicable intermediates

- **Step-wise confidence + variance on cached MATH-500 Stage 2 traces** —
  cache stores per-token logits → trivial post-processing. Can compute
  `c_s, Var(c_s)` for all 500 problems × all reasoning steps without any
  re-generation. ~30 min CPU.
- **Step-boundary token tagging** — regex on token text for "Step N:" or
  "\n\n" patterns over cached generated traces. ~10 min CPU.
- **Their O / U bucket vs our A/B/C/D bucket cross-table** — once the above
  two are computed, just a join. ~5 min.
- **Cosine sim of (μ^O − μ^U) against prefill DoM, final-token DoM, CoE-60
  direction** — once prototypes are extracted from cached L19 NPZs, three
  cosines. ~10 min CPU.

None of the above need fresh generation; everything sits on the existing
P11 H100 1024-tok caches.

vs underthinking" behavioural axis from confidence statistics, derives a

function. Reports +3.4 Pass@1 / −23.1% tokens on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B

direction yields *both* accuracy lifts and length reductions on MATH-style

correctness DoM.
