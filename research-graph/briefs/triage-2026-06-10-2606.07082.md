# Triage brief — 2606.07082 — On the Geometry of On-Policy Distillation

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07082
Triaged: 2026-06-10

> **Scope caveat up front.** This is a *weight/parameter-space* paper. Every
> diagnostic operates on ΔW = W₊ − W₀ between training checkpoints (SFT vs OPD
> vs RLVR on Qwen3-8B/4B/14B). Our entire program is *activation/residual-stream*
> geometry on a single frozen Qwen-2.5-1.5B — we hold **no training
> checkpoints**. So none of the paper's headline claims transplant literally.
> What transplants is the *mathematics*: stable rank, principal-angle subspace
> rotation, normalized spectral shift, and Hill-tail estimation are
> matrix-spectral tools that apply equally to a cached 500×1536 activation
> matrix as to a ΔW. The genuine experimental contact point is the H-8 / H-10
> family (is the L19 DoM direction a training-regime artifact?), which this
> paper makes newly testable and newly threatening.

## Refutations

1. **F-1 (dimensional breathing is *universal* across architectures and scales)
   — refuted-as-confounded if breathing is a post-training-regime fingerprint.**
   The paper shows that the *effective dimension* of the weight update is
   sharply regime-dependent: SFT *expands* its update subspace, RLVR *contracts*
   it, and OPD *locks* into a narrow rank-band early (Fig 4, §4.1, stable rank
   Eq. 8). F-1's claim is that activation-covariance participation ratio (the
   prefill ~20-dim / mid-gen ~60-115 / final-collapse ~2-18 curve) is universal.
   **Mechanism:** if the rank structure of the *activation* covariance is
   inherited from the rank structure of the *weight update* that produced the
   model, then the PR breathing curve is not a property of "transformers doing
   reasoning" but of "models that happen to have been RLVR-tuned vs OPD-tuned vs
   SFT-tuned." F-1's four-architecture replication (Qwen, Phi, Llama) does not
   control for post-training recipe — those four were trained by *different
   labs with different RL/distillation pipelines*, so a shared breathing curve
   could even be coincidental rather than universal. **Falsifiable experiment:**
   take one base model and its SFT-only, OPD, and RLVR derivatives (the paper's
   own Qwen3-8B triple), extract the prefill→mid→final PR curve on MATH for each.
   If the three PR curves differ in amplitude/peak-position beyond noise, F-1's
   "universal" qualifier is regime-confounded and must be downgraded.

2. **F-2 / H-8 (a single stable L19 prefill DoM direction, AUROC 0.7731;
   "the prefill direction is stable across training checkpoints") — directly
   threatened by off-principal + subspace-rotation findings.** The paper
   measures principal-angle rotation of the top-k singular subspaces of weight
   matrices (Eq. 2) and finds SFT rotates them >10°, OPD ~1°, RLVR <0.5° (§3.2).
   **Mechanism:** the L19 DoM is read out of W_o / W_down at L19; if that
   layer's principal subspace rotates by >10° under SFT, then a DoM direction
   fit on one checkpoint will *not* be the maximally-correctness-aligned
   direction on a differently-trained checkpoint of the same base — the
   correctness axis moves with the weights. H-8 asserts cross-checkpoint
   stability; this paper supplies the exact instrument (principal-angle
   rotation) and a prediction (SFT rotates a lot, RLVR barely) under which H-8
   would *fail for SFT-derived checkpoints but hold for RLVR-derived ones*.
   **Falsifiable experiment:** fit DoM on checkpoint A, transport to checkpoint
   B (same base, different recipe), measure cos(DoM_A, DoM_B) and ΔAUROC.
   Predict cos high for RLVR pairs, low for SFT pairs.

3. **F-10 / FE881 (residual-stream covariance spectrum vs a *Gaussian* null;
   PC1-residualized top-20 log-eigvals AUROC 0.7928) — the null model may be
   the wrong family.** The paper's Hill tail estimate (Appendix E) treats the
   singular-value spectrum as *heavy-tailed / power-law* and tracks its tail
   exponent as a diagnostic. F-10 benchmarks residual-stream topology/spectrum
   against a *matched-covariance Gaussian* null. **Mechanism:** if the L19
   activation covariance eigenvalue spectrum is heavy-tailed (finite Hill α,
   power-law bulk) rather than Marchenko-Pastur/Gaussian, then F-10's Gaussian
   null is a strawman — the "signal" FE881 extracts from top-20 log-eigvals
   could be partly the generic heavy-tail shape that *any* heavy-tailed null
   would also produce, and the PH-vs-Gaussian comparison is uninformative about
   whether the spectrum is special. **Falsifiable experiment:** compute the Hill
   tail exponent of the cached L19 prefill covariance spectrum; if it is finite
   and stable (heavy-tailed), re-run the FE881 probe against a *heavy-tailed*
   surrogate null and check whether AUROC-over-null survives.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **H-8 (prefill direction stable across training checkpoints)** — this is the
  single most direct contact. The paper hands us the *exact* checkpoint-pair
  diagnostic (principal-angle rotation, NSS, update-mask overlap) and a
  regime-stratified prediction. Anchor: F-2 AUROC **0.7731** is the number that
  would move if the DoM direction proves checkpoint-unstable.
- **H-10 (breathing pattern changes after fine-tuning)** — the paper's
  SFT-expands / RLVR-contracts / OPD-locks stable-rank trajectory is a
  *parameter-space* version of exactly this hypothesis. Predicts the sign and
  magnitude of how breathing should change per recipe.
- **F-7 (D-bucket has a collective, not per-problem, geometric signature)** —
  the paper's stable-rank-of-update concept (Eq. 8) is a one-number effective
  dimension; applied to the *activation* covariance of the D-bucket vs A-bucket
  it is a cheaper sibling of the participation-ratio collapse story (peak PR
  contrast). Anchor: cov-spectrum probe **0.7928**.
- **F-3 (prefill ⊥ final-token DoM, cos 0.046)** — the paper reframes
  "two directions are near-orthogonal" as principal-angle subspace rotation;
  its observation that RLVR keeps subspace rotation <0.5° suggests near-zero
  cosine between two *activation* readouts may be *generic* rather than
  meaningful. Anchor: cos **0.046**.
- **F-1 (breathing PR curve)** — stable rank is a second effective-dimension
  estimator alongside PR; cross-checking the two on cached activations is a
  free robustness control for the headline breathing curve.

## Methodologies extracted

- **bf16-aware update sparsity** (Eq. 1) — fraction of weights unchanged at bf16
  rounding (η=1e-3 relative threshold). *Replication cost*: N/A — needs two
  checkpoints; we have one frozen model. *We'd plausibly run this*: no — no
  checkpoint pairs in scope.
- **Principal-angle subspace rotation** (Eq. 2) — cosines of principal angles
  between top-k left/right singular subspaces of two matrices. *Replication
  cost*: ~5min CPU on cached activation covariances (correct-group vs
  incorrect-group, or prefill vs final). *We'd plausibly run this*: yes —
  directly reframes F-3's cos=0.046 as a richer subspace measure and tests F-7.
- **Normalized spectral shift / NSS** (Eq. 3) — L2 distance of sorted singular
  values, normalized. *Replication cost*: ~2min CPU on cached cov spectra.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — cheap distance between correct/incorrect
  spectra, complements FE881's log-eigval probe.
- **Stable rank** srank = ‖·‖_F² / ‖·‖_op² (Eq. 8) — effective dimension from
  the spectrum. *Replication cost*: ~2min CPU on cached L19 cov. *We'd plausibly
  run this*: yes — second opinion on F-1 PR and F-7 collapse, one scalar.
- **Hill tail estimate** (Appendix E, Hill 1975) — power-law tail exponent of
  the singular-value distribution. *Replication cost*: ~10min CPU on cached cov
  eigenvalues. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — tests whether F-10's Gaussian
  null is the right family (heavy-tail check).
- **Update-mask overlap** (principal mask vs low-magnitude mask, §3.1) —
  where visible updates land relative to high-curvature SVD-reconstruction
  entries. *Replication cost*: needs ΔW. *We'd plausibly run this*: no —
  weight-update specific.
- **Top-K subspace similarity to final** Sim_K (Eq. 9) — Frobenius alignment of
  top-K right singular subspace across a *trajectory*. *Replication cost*: needs
  a training/checkpoint trajectory. *We'd plausibly run this*: no — no
  trajectory of weights; (could be repurposed for a *generation-position*
  activation trajectory, but speculative).
- **Rank-K projected training** g ← gV_K V_Kᵀ (Eq. 10) — constrain gradients to
  an early subspace to test functional sufficiency. *Replication cost*: H100,
  requires fine-tuning. *We'd plausibly run this*: no — training-time, off our
  local-only compute budget.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Relaxed off-principal regime" / Three-Gate account** (§3.3): updates are
  filtered by (1) a distributional anchor bounding update norm, (2) pretrained
  geometry routing updates off the dominant spectral directions, (3) bf16
  precision deciding which coordinates become visible. *Intersection:* if our
  L19 DoM correctness direction is *off-principal* in the weight sense, that
  predicts it is fragile to SFT (which rewrites principals) but robust to RLVR
  — a sharp, testable framing for H-8/H-10.
- **Subspace locking** (§4): the functional update lives in a narrow,
  early-emerging, persistent low-dim channel. *Intersection:* an
  activation-space analog would be "the correctness signal occupies a fixed
  low-dim channel from prefill onward" — conceptually adjacent to F-2's
  single-direction reading and F-9's CoE-60↔L19 redundancy.
- **OPD as its own geometry, not an SFT↔RLVR interpolation** (§6): reframes a
  method as a distinct regime rather than a blend. *Intersection:* mild — a
  reminder that "between two known things" is an empirical claim to test, not
  assume; relevant to how we narrate cross-recipe DoM stability.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Diagnostics are computed **offline on saved checkpoints**, per weight matrix,
  then averaged across matrices — a clean, embarrassingly-parallel pattern that
  matches our per-cached-array workflow.
- bf16-aware comparison: cast both W₀ and W₊ to bf16 then back to fp32 before
  diffing; "unchanged" = relative diff ≤ η·max(|w₀|,|w₊|), η=1e-3.
- Endpoint selection by eval-plateau, *not* by tuning to optimize the
  diagnostics (SFT≈5k, OPD≈300, RLVR≈1k steps) — guards against
  diagnostic-overfitting; a discipline worth mirroring when we pick which
  activation snapshot to call "the" L19 state.
- Stable rank used as the primary trajectory diagnostic; Frobenius-norm and
  Hill-tail used specifically to *rule out the small-update confound* (OPD moves
  more than RLVR yet ends at comparable stable rank). The "norm rules out the
  trivial explanation" move is a reusable rigor pattern for our PR claims.
- Rank-16 chosen as a *stringent* bottleneck (slightly below observed effective
  dim) for the functional-sufficiency test.
- No code link in the paper text; AI-usage note says plotting/analysis scripts
  drafted with ChatGPT, author-verified.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2606.07082 — On the Geometry of On-Policy Distillation (Shen, Li, Yin, Leong, Wang, Chen, Han, Lee, Fung; HKUST et al., 2026)

**Relevance:** A parameter-space (weight-update) characterization of on-policy
distillation, placing OPD in a "relaxed off-principal regime" between SFT
(dense, principal-distorting) and RLVR (sparse, geometry-preserving), and
showing OPD undergoes early "subspace locking" into a narrow rank-band. It does
not touch residual-stream activations, hidden-state probing, or correctness
prediction, so it makes no claim we can directly replicate. Its value is
twofold: (1) it supplies a clean toolbox — stable rank, principal-angle subspace
rotation, normalized spectral shift, Hill-tail estimation — that transplants
onto our cached activation covariances as cheap robustness checks on F-1, F-3,
F-7, and F-10; and (2) it makes the H-8/H-10 question (is the L19 DoM direction
a training-regime artifact?) newly sharp by predicting SFT rotates principal
subspaces >10° while RLVR keeps them <0.5°.

**Key claim we tested:** In our terms — the geometric signature a model carries
(here, the rank/principal structure of its weight update) is strongly determined
by *which* post-training recipe produced it, not just by the task; "between two
known regimes" is a distinct regime, not an interpolation.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** No experiment run yet. Three Tier-1 CPU checks
(stable rank of L19 cov, Hill tail of the FE881 spectrum, prefill/final
principal angles) and one Tier-2 H100 checkpoint-stability test of the DoM
direction are queued (P11-FE1082–1085). The paper's strongest implication for us
is adversarial: if the L19 DoM is an off-principal weight read, F-2's
single-direction result and F-1's universality claim may be partly artifacts of
Qwen-2.5's specific SFT/RL pipeline.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE1082, P11-FE1083, P11-FE1084, P11-FE1085;
H-2082, H-2083, H-2084; H-8, H-10; F-1, F-2, F-3, F-7, F-10; EXP-57 (FE881).
