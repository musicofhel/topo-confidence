# Triage brief — 2603.25052 — Closing the Confidence-Faithfulness Gap in Large Language Models

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25052
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-3 (prefill ⊥ final-token DoM, cos 0.046) may be a special case of task-elicited
   orthogonality, not a position/sequence phenomenon.**
   *Cite:* F-3.
   *Mechanism:* Miao & Ungar show that the linear direction recovered from a "pure
   correctness" prompt and the direction from a "pure confidence" prompt are nearly
   orthogonal across **all** layers (cos < 0.04 in Qwen-2.5-7B). Our prefill activations
   are extracted at the last prompt token of a *joint* CoT-then-answer prompt; final-token
   activations are extracted post-generation, after the model has committed to an answer.
   At those two positions the model is doing different things — at prefill it is closer
   to the "pure correctness / problem-comprehension" mode, at final-token it is closer to
   "verbalized confidence / answer commitment." The 0.046 cos may therefore be a
   re-discovery of *their* dissociation under a different label.
   *Falsifying experiment:* Re-extract prefill L19 activations on MATH-500 under a
   no-CoT, answer-only prompt (their "pure correctness" analog) and a confidence-only
   prompt. If pure-correctness-prefill DoM aligns with our existing prefill DoM
   (cos > 0.5) **and** confidence-prefill DoM is orthogonal to it (cos < 0.1), then F-3 is
   downstream of their finding rather than a position-specific architectural fact.

2. **F-2 (prefill DoM AUROC 0.7731) may be partially a "reasoning contamination"
   artifact rather than clean accuracy probing.**
   *Cite:* F-2.
   *Mechanism:* The paper documents a "Reasoning Contamination Effect": the cosine
   between contrastive confidence and contrastive accuracy directions falls from +0.26
   (pure confidence prompt) to **−0.63** (joint solve-and-rate prompt) at L15 of
   Qwen-2.5-7B. Our prefill DoM is fit on labels collected under joint CoT generation.
   Even though prefill activations precede CoT tokens, the *labels* that supervise the
   probe come from a contaminated distribution. If the same effect operates in 1.5B,
   our 0.7731 may under-estimate the AUROC achievable from a pure-correctness probe and
   may simultaneously be inflated by the model's own difficulty-as-effort signal
   (which Miao & Ungar argue gets inverted into confidence under joint prompting).
   *Falsifying experiment:* Fit prefill L19 DoM under a no-CoT pure-correctness prompt on
   the same MATH-500 questions, generate K=8 to obtain labels with no joint-prompt
   contamination. If pure-correctness AUROC > joint-prompt AUROC by ≥0.03, F-2's
   headline number is a contaminated estimate that should be supplemented or replaced.

3. **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy upward) is at risk of
   actively eroding accuracy.**
   *Cite:* H-1.
   *Mechanism:* §3.4 shows that CAA steering on the verbalized-confidence direction
   under joint solve-and-rate prompts erodes accuracy as |α| grows — the more the model's
   confidence channel is moved, the worse the underlying problem solving becomes. Their
   two-stage pipeline (Stage 1 probe / Stage 2 steer) is **explicitly designed** to avoid
   touching the residual stream during the solve pass for this reason. H-1 proposes to
   inject a per-position DoM bank during generation; if our prefill DoM lives in the same
   joint-contaminated subspace as theirs, steering with it during answer generation will
   exhibit the same accuracy-erosion side effect. The +11 net gain claimed by P4 Track A
   was at 256-tok labels (now graveyarded); we have no clean 1024-tok evidence that
   steering helps.
   *Falsifying experiment:* Apply ±α prefill-DoM CAA at L19 during MATH-500 generation
   on Qwen-2.5-1.5B at 1024-tok. If MATH-500 K=1 accuracy degrades monotonically with
   |α| (e.g., drops below the 48.6% baseline for α = 0.5, 0.75), H-1 must be re-framed:
   prefill DoM is a read-only signal, not a steerable handle on accuracy.

4. **F-8 (selective prediction acc 71.6% at coverage 0.5) may be dominated by a
   probe-and-calibrate baseline that converts the same probe to ECE rather than
   abstention.**
   *Cite:* F-8.
   *Mechanism:* Miao & Ungar's two-stage pipeline takes a probe identical in spirit to
   our prefill DoM (linear, residual stream, predicts empirical accuracy) and uses it as
   a *target* for steered verbal output, achieving ECE = 3.3–10.7 across three 7B
   models on MATH. Our F-8 stops at the abstention level (binarize the probe, abstain
   below threshold). If the same probe used for selective abstention also drives a
   calibration metric that beats SteerConf and unsteered verbalized confidence, then
   abstention-only F-8 is leaving most of the headline value on the table — and the
   "right" framing of P11 is calibration improvement, not selective accuracy.
   *Falsifying experiment:* Take the existing prefill L19 DoM probe outputs on MATH-500,
   apply isotonic regression on a held-out fold, compute ECE / Brier / MAE against
   per-question empirical accuracy from the existing K=8 generations. If ECE < 5.0 (i.e.,
   probe-only beats their unsteered-verbal numbers without any steering at all), F-8's
   selective-accuracy framing is undersold and should be rewritten as a calibration
   result.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 / F-3 numerical twin.** Paper reports cos < 0.04 between pure-correctness and
  pure-confidence probe directions across all 28 layers of Qwen-2.5-7B-Base. We report
  cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046 in `scratch/pathway10_temporal_and_verifier_results.json`.
  Two different cuts (theirs: prompt-task-elicited; ours: position-elicited) land at the
  same numeric range. Either coincidence, or the two orthogonalities reflect the same
  underlying factorization of the residual stream.
- **F-2 effect-size match.** Their gold-calibration probe achieves Cohen's d = 1.88 and
  r = 0.80 with empirical accuracy on Qwen-2.5-7B at L21. Our prefill L19 DoM AUROC =
  0.7731 on Qwen-2.5-1.5B (Pearson r ≈ 0.5–0.6 typical conversion). The 7B is stronger
  than 1.5B, consistent with scale, and their layer (L21 of 28) sits at a similar
  fractional depth as our L19 (of 28 in the 1.5B). This is a clean **CORROBORATED**
  signal for F-2's mechanism (a single linear direction in mid-late layers tracks
  correctness).
- **F-8 calibration analog.** Their adaptive pipeline reports MATH ECE 3.3 (Mistral),
  3.7 (Llama), 10.7 (Qwen) — the same probe-driven correction we use for selective
  abstention at 71.6% acc / coverage 0.5.
- **H-1 / H-12 method analog.** Their probe→steer pipeline is exactly what H-12
  (label-free refusal probes) wants to operationalize, except they keep label
  supervision from empirical accuracy rather than going semantic-entropy. Sets a strong
  baseline for any H-12 implementation.
- **H-19 (C_exact verification routing).** Their pipeline is a two-pass routing: pass 1
  solves, pass 2 calibrates. Conceptually adjacent to H-19's "verification routing beats
  inference scaling" framing.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Gold calibration linear probing** — ridge regression on residual-stream activations
  from a "pure correctness" prompt (no confidence wording) against empirical accuracy
  computed as fraction-correct over 50 samples per question.
  *Replication cost:* ~2h CPU on cached prefill NPZs + 6h H100 if we need to re-extract
  under a no-confidence prompt template.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* yes — directly tests F-2 prompt-template robustness.
- **Pure-confidence prompt + linear probe** — extract activations under a confidence-only
  prompt (no answer), probe verbalized confidence.
  *Replication cost:* 4h H100 to re-run on Qwen-2.5-1.5B + 1h CPU for probe.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* yes — establishes the verbalized-confidence direction we
  could compare to prefill DoM.
- **Joint-prompt orthogonality contrast (cos = −0.63)** — extract contrastive
  confidence direction (mean H − mean L over K=11 framings, within-question) and
  contrastive accuracy direction; measure cosine.
  *Replication cost:* 6h H100 (need K=11 framings × MATH-500) + 1h CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* yes — directly probes whether prefill DoM is contaminated.
- **Within-question CAA contrast (Eq. 1)** — per-question contrast across K confidence
  framings, then average across Q. Controls for question-level confounds (difficulty,
  topic, format) that a global mass-mean ignores.
  *Replication cost:* 1h CPU once K=11-framing activations cached.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* yes — better controlled than our DoM (mean-correct − mean-incorrect).
- **PCHIP-inverted α* transfer function** — sweep α ∈ [−2, +2] at 0.1 steps on
  validation; PCHIP-invert α → mean-verbal-confidence to obtain α*(c*) per question.
  *Replication cost:* 30min CPU once α-sweep generations exist.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* maybe — only relevant if we adopt their two-stage
  calibration framing.
- **Two-stage adaptive steering pipeline** — Stage 1 probe target c*; Stage 2
  question-specific α* steering to match.
  *Replication cost:* 8h H100 end-to-end on Qwen-2.5-1.5B at MATH-500 (need K=11
  framings + α-sweep + adaptive generations).
  *We'd plausibly run this:* yes — high ROI for converting F-8 to a calibration story.
- **Isotonic regression calibration of probe outputs** — held-out validation split.
  *Replication cost:* 5min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* yes — trivial add-on to F-8.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Readout failure, not knowledge deficit" framing.** Recasts miscalibration as a
  generation-side problem: the signal is in the residual stream, the model just fails
  to surface it. Aligns with our F-2 ("model knows from prefill") but adds a normative
  conclusion (correct the readout, not the model).
- **Reasoning Contamination Effect.** Joint solve-and-rate prompts force the
  effort-encoding direction to be re-interpreted as confidence, inverting the
  confidence/accuracy alignment from +0.26 to −0.63. New conceptual handle for our F-3
  orthogonality story — possibly the *mechanism* behind it.
- **Within-question contrast vector design.** Computing CAA contrasts within a single
  question (across K framings) before averaging across questions removes question-level
  confounds. Cleaner alternative to our mean-correct − mean-incorrect DoM.
- **Prompt-completion vs answer-completion extraction equivalence.** They report both
  yield similar steering vectors and use prompt-completion for practicality. We use
  prefill-completion as the headline; this paper provides supporting evidence that
  prompt-position activations are sufficient for confidence-relevant probing.
- **Probe-as-target reframing.** Rather than thresholding the probe for abstention
  (F-8), use it as the regression target the model should *match*. Reframes selective
  prediction as adaptive calibration.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- **Activation extraction site:** residual stream after MLP sublayer; both
  prompt-completion and answer-completion positions yield similar vectors. They use
  prompt-completion throughout. Matches our prefill protocol.
- **Steering-layer choices found by validation sweep:** Qwen2.5-7B → L21,
  Llama-3.1-8B → L24, Mistral-7B → L27. All sit at fractional-depth ~0.75–0.96 of model.
  Our L19 in 28-layer Qwen-2.5-1.5B is at fractional-depth 0.68 — slightly earlier than
  their best layers but in the same regime.
- **Steering injection at answer-token outperforms last-prompt-only or both-jointly.**
  Slight effect, but consistent.
- **K=11 confidence framings, τ_hi = 0.75, τ_lo = 0.25 thresholds** for high/low-confidence
  partition (within-question).
- **All generations T = 1.0** — matches the temperature used during activation
  collection.
- **Steering vectors are unit-normalized then rescaled by mean activation norm at the
  layer** (Eq. 3). Gives α a model/layer-independent interpretation.
- **Validation split selects steering layer + strength** before any test eval.
- **Code/repo:** none linked in the abstract; would need to email authors or
  reverse-engineer from §2.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2603.25052 — Closing the Confidence-Faithfulness Gap in Large Language Models (Miao & Ungar, 2026)

**Relevance:** Direct geometric counterpart of our F-2/F-3/F-8 cluster. Across three
7B base+instruct families (Llama-3.1, Qwen2.5, Mistral) and four datasets (MATH, MMLU,
TriviaQA, TruthfulQA), they find that gold-calibration (linear probe of empirical
accuracy from a "pure correctness" prompt) and verbalized confidence (probe from a "pure
confidence" prompt) are encoded in nearly orthogonal directions (cos < 0.04), and that
joint solve-and-rate prompts invert the relationship from +0.26 to −0.63 ("Reasoning
Contamination Effect"). They use this to build a two-stage adaptive steering pipeline
that drops MATH ECE 4–7× over unsteered verbalized confidence and over SteerConf
prompt-based baseline.

**Key claim we tested:** A single linear direction in the residual stream tracks
empirical accuracy and is recoverable via a linear probe from prompt-completion
activations.

**Our result:** **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED.** Our F-2 reports prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731
on Qwen-2.5-1.5B — directly analogous to their gold-calibration probe at r=0.80 on
Qwen-2.5-7B at L21. Their cos < 0.04 between pure-correctness and pure-confidence
directions is numerically twin to our cos = 0.046 between prefill and final-token DoM
(F-3) — but the two cuts are conceptually different (theirs: task-elicited; ours:
position-elicited). H-75 tests whether they are the same phenomenon under different
labels. Their joint-prompt contamination effect (−0.63) raises the possibility that our
joint-prompt-trained DoM is itself contaminated; H-77 / FE-77 directly tests this by
re-extracting under pure-correctness prompts.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE75, P11-FE76, P11-FE77, P11-FE78, P11-FE79; H-75, H-76,
H-77, H-78.

**Status:** PARTIALLY CONFIRMED (F-2 mechanism corroborated at 7B); TO TEST (whether
F-3 is a position-elicited or task-elicited orthogonality, and whether their two-stage
pipeline transfers to 1.5B).
