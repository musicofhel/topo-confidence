# Triage brief — 2605.02167 — Manifold-Aligned Guided Integrated Gradients for Reliable Feature Attribution

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02167
Triaged: 2026-05-06

## Refutations

**Refutation quality caveat:** This paper is about gradient-based feature attribution for image classifiers (VGG16, ResNet18, InceptionV1 on ImageNet/Oxford datasets). It does not study LLMs, residual streams, correctness prediction, or transformer internals. All three refutations below are conceptual transplants, not direct challenges grounded in the paper's experiments.

1. **F-2 (L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731) — DoM direction may be off-manifold.**
   The paper's Proposition 3.1 proves that straight-line directions in ambient space cause first-order drift from the data manifold when the manifold is curved. The DoM is a difference-of-means vector in ambient 1536-d space. If the L19 activation manifold is curved, the DoM direction may cut through off-manifold regions rather than following the manifold's intrinsic correct→incorrect geometry. A manifold-aligned "difference direction" (geodesic between cluster centroids) could differ substantially from the Euclidean DoM, meaning the 0.7731 AUROC is capturing a mixture of on-manifold signal and off-manifold noise.
   *Mechanism:* Ambient-space DoM ≠ manifold-intrinsic separation direction; the AUROC is partly probing off-manifold structure.
   *Counter:* cos(DoM, PC1) = 0.9216 strongly suggests the manifold is approximately flat in the direction that matters — the DoM is nearly the leading principal component, which IS the dominant tangent direction.
   *Falsifiable test:* Compute ISOMAP geodesic distances on cached L19 activations, derive a geodesic-weighted difference-of-means direction, and compare cos(geodesic_DoM, euclidean_DoM). If cosine > 0.95, manifold curvature is irrelevant and the refutation fails.

2. **F-10 (PH-at-null) — Euclidean distances may systematically inflate, blurring topological structure.**
   The paper argues that straight-line (Euclidean) interpolation between data points traverses off-manifold regions where model behavior is undefined. VR-PH computes pairwise Euclidean distances to build the filtration. If L19 activations lie on a curved manifold, Euclidean distances overestimate geodesic (on-manifold) distances between points on different branches, collapsing genuine topological features into noise. F-10's Gaussian null could be partly a metric artifact — using Euclidean distance on manifold data.
   *Mechanism:* Ambient-space Euclidean metric ≠ manifold-intrinsic metric; VR-PH misestimates true topology.
   *Counter:* F-10 compared against a Gaussian null at matched mean+covariance, which already accounts for the dominant linear structure. Curvature effects would need to be strong to matter beyond this null.
   *Falsifiable test:* Recompute VR-PH using ISOMAP k-NN geodesic distances on cached L19 activations and compare barcodes to Gaussian null. If still null, curvature-based metric distortion is not the explanation.

3. **F-3 (prefill/final DoM orthogonality, cos = 0.046) — ambient-space angle may not reflect manifold-intrinsic angle.**
   The paper's core argument is that ambient-space geometry is misleading when data lies on a manifold. Two directions that are orthogonal in 1536-d ambient space might be non-orthogonal (or even aligned) when projected onto the activation manifold's tangent space. The cos = 0.046 measurement is computed in ambient coordinates; on a curved manifold the "true" angle between the prefill and final-token DoM directions could be different.
   *Mechanism:* Ambient-space cosine ≠ manifold-intrinsic cosine; F-3's structural orthogonality claim depends on which metric is used.
   *Counter:* Both DoM directions are near-PC1 of their respective activation clouds, meaning the dominant tangent directions at each token position. Tangent-space projection would likely preserve orthogonality unless the manifold is pathologically curved between the two positions.
   *Falsifiable test:* Estimate tangent spaces at prefill and final-token activation centroids (via local PCA), project both DoMs into a shared tangent frame, and recompute the angle. If cos remains < 0.1, the refutation fails.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (AUROC 0.7731):** The paper's manifold-alignment framework raises the question of whether the DoM direction is on-manifold or off-manifold relative to the L19 activation geometry. Since cos(DoM, PC1) = 0.9216, the DoM is nearly the leading tangent direction, suggesting it IS on-manifold. The paper's concerns about off-manifold paths are unlikely to apply here, but a formal check (geodesic DoM vs Euclidean DoM) would confirm this.

- **F-10 (PH-at-null):** The paper's critique of Euclidean distance in curved manifold settings is conceptually related to the long-standing question of whether F-10's null is metric-dependent. This connects to H-50 (Heat-distance vectorization) and H-47 (Zigzag PH) as alternative approaches that might respect manifold geometry better. The paper doesn't directly address PH but its manifold-awareness argument provides a theoretical framing for why Euclidean-VR-PH might miss structure.

- **H-67 (DoM as task-recognition vs per-problem correctness probe):** If the DoM is on-manifold (as suggested by the PC1 alignment), then the manifold's intrinsic structure encodes the correct/incorrect separation at a geometric level. This would be consistent with H-67's "task-recognition" interpretation — the manifold itself is organized by task difficulty/familiarity.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Manifold-aligned interpolation via VAE latent space** — Encode endpoints to VAE latent space, construct attribution path by iteratively updating low-gradient latent dimensions, decode intermediates to data space. All intermediates stay on the learned data manifold.
  *Replication cost*: Days to weeks — requires training a VAE on L19 activations (500 samples × 1536 dims is severely underdetermined for a VAE).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: No — sample count is far too low for a meaningful VAE, and we don't need gradient-based attribution for linear probes.

- **Jacobian-based tangent space estimation** — Use decoder Jacobian J_D(z) to map latent perturbations to on-manifold perturbations in data space. The column space of J_D defines the tangent space at each decoded point.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU (local PCA serves as a non-parametric equivalent for tangent space estimation on cached activations).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: Yes, but only as local PCA, not the VAE-Jacobian variant — we already have global PCA and could do k-NN local PCA cheaply.

- **Low-gradient-dimension selection for path construction** — At each step, identify feature dimensions where the classifier gradient magnitude is below a quantile threshold τ, and only update those dimensions. This prevents the path from taking large steps in gradient-sensitive directions.
  *Replication cost*: 15min CPU if adapted to our logistic regression probe on cached activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: No — for a linear probe, gradients are constant (the weight vector), so dimension selection is trivial and doesn't add value over examining probe weights directly.

- **DiffID metric** — Measures relative divergence between insertion/deletion curves rather than absolute values, neutralizing shared distributional bias.
  *Replication cost*: 10min CPU to implement.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: No — we don't use insertion/deletion evaluation for our probes.

## Approaches & framings

- **Off-manifold drift as a systematic error source.** The paper formalizes (Proposition 3.1) how linear interpolation in ambient space causes first-order drift from the data manifold. This framing could be transplanted to argue that any analysis of L19 activations using Euclidean geometry (distances, means, PCA) has potential manifold-bias — but since our PCA already captures >90% of variance in few components, the manifold appears approximately flat and this concern is muted.

- **"Perfect Autoencoder" assumption as a benchmark for manifold methods.** Assumption 3.2 defines conditions (exact reconstruction, surjectivity, smooth immersion) under which latent-space operations faithfully represent manifold-level operations. This provides a theoretical checklist for evaluating any future VAE/autoencoder-based analysis of activations.

If residual is near-zero at small k, the DoM is on-manifold. We already know cos(DoM, PC1) = 0.9216, which strongly suggests this, but a formal k-sweep (k=1..20) would quantify it. *Script:* Could be done with `pathway11_h100/pca_covariance/` cached PCA components + DoM direction from `prefill_gated_compute/results.json`.

- **Geodesic vs Euclidean distance comparison.** Compute ISOMAP (k=10) geodesic distance matrix on the 500 × 1536 cached L19 activations and compare to Euclidean distance matrix (Pearson/Spearman correlation). If r > 0.95, manifold curvature is negligible and the paper's concerns about off-manifold analysis don't apply.
