# Triage brief — 2308.10248 — Steering Language Models With Activation Engineering

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.10248
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-3 (prefill/final orthogonality, cos = 0.046) is refuted if ActAdd transfers across positions.**
   Mechanism: ActAdd takes the contrast-pair activation difference `h₊^l − h₋^l` at a single layer `l` and a single position `a=1`, then *scales by c<15 and adds it once* at the user prompt's first position. The intervention then propagates autoregressively for tens of generation tokens and reliably shifts behavior (e.g. IMDb Neg→Pos = 0.669 success on LLaMA-3, RealToxicityPrompts toxicity = 0.108). If a single residual-stream direction injected at one prefill position durably changes *final-token* sampling, the prefill and final geometries cannot be as orthogonal as F-3 claims — at minimum the direction must have a non-zero, behaviorally-load-bearing projection on the final-token subspace. Our cos = 0.046 was measured on supervised DoMs, but ActAdd's success implies *some* direction must cross the boundary cleanly.
   Falsifiable experiment: On cached `pathway11_h100/` Qwen-1.5B prefill states, build an ActAdd vector at L19 from "answer math correctly" / "answer math incorrectly" contrast prompts; inject at `c ∈ {3,5,8,12}` at the user-prompt last token; rerun MATH-500 K=1 generation. If accuracy moves >+3pp, F-3 needs reframing — orthogonality may be a property of the *DoM directions we've measured*, not of the underlying steerable subspace.

2. **F-7 ("D-bucket has a distinctive collective, not per-problem, geometric signature") is refuted if a 1-pair ActAdd vector recovers D-bucket membership.**
   Mechanism: F-7's "collective" framing implies the D-bucket signal is not just a contrastive-prompt-style feature — it requires aggregating across problems. But ActAdd's whole thesis is that *one well-chosen prompt pair* yields a behavior-relevant direction. If a contrast pair like "this problem is genuinely hard" / "this problem is easy" computed at L19 produces a direction whose projection on cached prefill states classifies D-bucket at AUROC > F-7's reported separation, the "collective signature" claim collapses to "supervised DoM rediscovered the contrast-pair direction the long way around." This would also explain why DoM is a strong correctness predictor (F-2): correctness aligns with a difficulty/familiarity contrast that any sensible prompt pair would expose.
   Falsifiable experiment: 30 min CPU on cached Stage 2 NPZs. Build 3–5 D-bucket-targeted contrast pairs, compute L19 differences, project all 500 problems' prefill states, compare per-pair cluster purity against F-7's published numbers.

3. **H-1 (per-position DoM steering bank moves MATH-500 accuracy) is over-engineered if single-vector ActAdd at fixed a=1 already moves it.**
   Mechanism: H-1 proposes a *per-position* activation bank derived from supervised DoM, motivated by F-3's orthogonality. ActAdd reports SOTA cross-domain effects with a *single* vector at `a=1` and `c<15`, with no per-position machinery. If the simpler recipe works for math correctness (or measurably moves accuracy in either direction), H-1's per-position complexity is unmotivated. Even a *negative* steering result for ActAdd at fixed a=1 would change H-1's framing: it would be evidence that math correctness is the rare domain where per-position is *necessary* (because correctness is genuinely RoPE-frequency-coupled, à la H-17), and we should run H-1 against ActAdd-fixed as the baseline rather than against no-steering.
   Falsifiable experiment: P11-FE71 below — single-vector ActAdd MATH-500 sweep, 30 min H100. Outcome decides whether H-1 stays as currently framed or pivots into "ActAdd works elsewhere but fails on math, and here's why."

4. **(Bonus) F-2 ("Prefill L19 DoM is a strong correctness predictor, AUROC 0.7731") is *strengthened* by ActAdd's mid-layer optimum, not refuted.**
   GPT-2-XL's optimal layer for wedding steering was layer 6 of 48 (~12.5%); LLaMA-3's grid search ranges 6–24. Qwen-1.5B's L19 of 28 (~68%) is mid-to-late, not mid. The ActAdd "middle layers are most effective" prior weakly predicts that Qwen's optimum should be earlier than L19. Worth a layer sweep (cheap on cached data) to check whether L19 is genuinely correctness-optimal or a local maximum upstream from the *steering-optimal* layer. Not a refutation, but a precise probe of F-2's layer choice.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM, AUROC 0.7731)** — ActAdd is the supervised DoM's unsupervised twin: contrast-pair difference instead of class-mean difference. ActAdd's "single direction is causally sufficient at one layer" claim is the *causal* version of F-2's *predictive* claim. Direct comparison is cheap.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046)** — ActAdd assumes one fixed direction works across the full generation. F-3 says the geometry rotates. Mutual constraint.
- **F-7 (D-bucket collective signature)** — see refutation 2.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer DoM)** — ActAdd's mid-layer optimum is consistent with F-9's "one layer is enough" message. Weak corroboration.
- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering on MATH-500)** — ActAdd is the canonical baseline H-1 must beat, not just compare against. See refutation 3.
- **H-13 (head-level attribution of prefill vs final circuits)** — ActAdd doesn't decompose by head, but its successful single-direction injection bounds what fraction of variance heads can independently contribute.
- **H-17 (DoM rotation is RoPE-mechanical)** — ActAdd's fixed-position injection succeeds for sentiment but is untested on long-form math. If ActAdd fails on math at fixed `a` while succeeding on sentiment, that's evidence rotation is a property of *long structured reasoning*, not of generation per se.

## Methodologies extracted

- **ActAdd / contrastive activation addition** — `h^l ← h^l + c·(h₊^l − h₋^l)` at fixed layer `l`, fixed position `a=1`, scalar `c<15`. *Replication cost*: 30 min on cached Qwen-1.5B prefill extractor + a generation pass. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — it's the canonical baseline for any steering experiment in this repo and we don't have it implemented.
- **Sequence-alignment padding for contrast pairs** — pad `p₊` and `p₋` to equal length so position-aligned subtraction is well-defined. *Replication cost*: trivial. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — adopting this convention before P10/P11 steering experiments avoids an avoidable confound.
- **Coefficient sweep `c ∈ [3,20]`** — small grid search per task. *Replication cost*: linear in #generations. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes, embedded in P11-FE71.
- **Layer sweep at coarse stride** — Turner et al. tested `l ∈ [6,24]` on a 48-layer model, found mid optimal. *Replication cost*: cheap on cached states, expensive on generation. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — relevant to F-2's layer choice.
- **Off-target capability evaluation via ConceptNet (LAMA, n=29,774)** — measure P@K for unrelated factual completion to detect collateral damage. *Replication cost*: 1 hr H100 per intervention. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — H-1 needs an off-target check before any accuracy claim is taken seriously.
- **Perplexity ratio on OpenWebText, partitioned by topical relevance** — separate "wedding-related" and "wedding-unrelated" subsets to show selective effect. *Replication cost*: 1 hr CPU per intervention. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — adapt to "math-related" / "math-unrelated" splits as steering side-effect probe.
- **KL-divergence drift between steered and baseline distributions** — token-level distribution comparison. *Replication cost*: trivial alongside generation. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — cheap supplementary metric for any H-1 result.

## Approaches & framings

- **Linear representation hypothesis as a *causal* claim, not just predictive** — Turner et al. argue that activation steering only works if features are encoded as linear directions *and* the residual stream supports compositional add-ons. Our F-2/F-3 work treats DoM as a probe (predictive); ActAdd reframes the same direction as a causal lever. This is the bridge to H-1's steering claim.
- **"Forward passes compose despite no training for it"** — the paper's mechanistic explanation. Implication for us: if our F-3 orthogonality is real, then either (a) ActAdd-style composition would *fail* on Qwen-1.5B math (interesting!) or (b) composition succeeds but not through the DoM directions we measured (also interesting).
- **Gradient-free, single-pair, training-free** — establishes a price-floor for any "calibration via probing" claim: if a 1-pair ActAdd matches a supervised probe, the supervision was wasted compute.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Pad contrast prompts to equal length before subtraction; misaligned positions destroy the signal.
- Inject at *one* position (default a=1, the BOS-adjacent slot) — not every position.
- Coefficient typically `c < 15`; degeneration ("steering off the manifold") above that.
- Mid-layer is consistently optimal across GPT-2-XL, OPT, LLaMA-3 — not last layer.
- Algorithm 1 in the paper is the canonical pseudocode; one-page implementation.
- Reference implementation: https://github.com/montemac/activation_additions (verify before relying).

If cos > 0.5, F-2's "supervised DoM" is essentially recovering an ActAdd-style direction; if cos ≈ 0, the two directions are doing different things and both are interesting.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2308.10248 — Steering Language Models With Activation Engineering (Turner, Thiergart, Leech, Udell, Vazquez, Mini, MacDiarmid, 2023)

**Relevance:** ActAdd is the foundational contrastive activation-addition method and the direct unsupervised cousin of our F-2 supervised DoM. Single layer, single position (a=1), single contrast pair, scalar coefficient c<15 — and SOTA on sentiment + detoxification. Critically constrains how we frame F-2 (predictive direction) and F-3 (prefill/final orthogonality), and is the unavoidable baseline H-1 must beat. Their mid-layer-optimal finding across three model families is also a precise probe of whether our L19 layer choice is correctness-prediction-optimal vs steering-optimal.

**Key claim we tested:** A single contrast-pair direction at one mid-layer, injected at one position, durably steers downstream generation across many tokens.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — pending FE71 (single-vector ActAdd at L19 on MATH-500), FE72 (cosine/AUROC head-to-head with F-2 supervised DoM), and FE73 (layer sweep). Refutation 1 above is the central question: can a fixed-position direction transfer through F-3's orthogonality? Refutation 2 asks whether F-7's "collective signature" survives an ActAdd-style 1-pair recovery test.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE71, P11-FE72, P11-FE73, P10-FE53, H-1, H-71, H-72, H-73
