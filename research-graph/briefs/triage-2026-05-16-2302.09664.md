# Triage brief — 2302.09664 — Semantic Uncertainty: Linguistic Invariances for Uncertainty Estimation in Natural Language Generation

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09664
Triaged: 2026-05-16

## Refutations

1. **Refutes utility premise of F-2 (prefill DoM AUROC 0.7731) and F-8 (selective prediction at 50% coverage).**
   Semantic entropy achieves AUROC 0.828 on TriviaQA (OPT-30B) and 0.77 on CoQA for predicting model correctness — using *only output samples*, zero hidden-state access. If semantic entropy applied to MATH-500 with Qwen-2.5-1.5B achieves comparable or higher AUROC than 0.7731, the internal-geometry approach becomes dominated by an output-only method. The refutation mechanism: if output diversity (clustered by meaning) is a strictly better predictor of correctness than single-pass prefill geometry, then there is no practical reason to probe internal representations for this task.
   *Falsifiable by*: Generate M=10 CoT completions per MATH-500 problem at temperature 0.5, cluster by exact-answer equivalence, compute semantic entropy AUROC. If SE-AUROC > 0.7731, F-2's utility claim is weakened; if SE-AUROC > 0.7928 (our cov-spectrum ceiling), the entire internal-geometry line is dominated.

2. **Refutes F-3 (prefill/final orthogonality as a structurally meaningful distinction).**
   The paper demonstrates that sampling temperature creates a tradeoff between diversity and accuracy (Fig 3b). If the "orthogonal" prefill and final-token directions merely reflect the two endpoints of this tradeoff — prefill capturing "knowledge certainty" (answer accuracy) while final-token captures "expression diversity" (sampling variability) — then their orthogonality is not a deep geometric fact but simply the independence of two output-level statistics (accuracy vs diversity) projected back into activation space.
   *Falsifiable by*: Compute the rank correlation between prefill DoM scores and semantic entropy (or its proxy, number-of-distinct-final-answers) across MATH-500 problems. If ρ > 0.8, prefill DoM is just a noisy single-pass estimate of output-based SE, and the orthogonality with final-token direction becomes a trivial consequence of prefill ≈ knowledge and final ≈ expression.

3. **Refutes implied necessity of hidden-state probing in F-9 (CoE-60 redundancy) and the broader research program.**
   The paper shows that even the *count* of semantically distinct clusters achieves AUROC 0.79 on TriviaQA — a trivial statistic requiring no representation analysis whatsoever. If MATH-500's per-problem "number of distinct answers in K samples" achieves AUROC ≥ 0.77, then the entire chain from CoE-60 trajectory features → single-layer DoM → cov-spectrum top-20 is engineering a complicated proxy for something directly measurable from outputs. This would not invalidate the science (the geometric facts are still true) but would refute the *practical* motivation for internal probing.
   *Falsifiable by*: From existing K≥2 MATH-500 completions, compute per-problem answer-cluster-count, measure AUROC against correctness labels. If cluster-count AUROC ≥ 0.75, the practical value of internal probes is questionable for this task.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)**: Direct comparator. Semantic entropy achieves 0.828 on TriviaQA but requires 10 generation samples. Our approach requires zero generations — a single prefill pass. The key question is whether the ~0.05 AUROC gap (if it transfers to MATH-500) justifies 10× compute cost.

- **F-8 (selective prediction 71.6% at 50% coverage)**: Semantic entropy's higher AUROC would likely yield a tighter coverage-accuracy Pareto curve. But our method operates at *zero marginal generation cost* after a single pass, making it uniquely suited to pre-generation abstention (deciding whether to attempt a problem *before* spending compute on CoT).

- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046)**: The paper's finding that generation diversity and accuracy are competing forces at different temperatures may explain the orthogonality: prefill encodes the "how much does the model know" signal while final-token encodes "how diversely would it express answers."

- **F-10 (PH null)**: Tangential. The paper is purely output-based with no topology.

- **H-5 (prefill encodes familiarity vs decomposability)**: If semantic entropy captures the same signal as DoM, the "familiarity" interpretation gets a concrete operational definition: familiarity = low output diversity under sampling. If DoM and SE diverge, it suggests prefill captures something *beyond* output consistency.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Bi-directional entailment clustering** — Cluster generated outputs into semantic equivalence classes using a DeBERTa-large NLI model. Two outputs are "same meaning" iff they entail each other in both directions.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU (DeBERTa-large inference on M² pairs per problem, M=10).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — as a baseline/oracle for benchmarking DoM against output-based methods.

- **Semantic entropy computation** — Sum sequence probabilities within each meaning-cluster, compute entropy over cluster probabilities. Uses Monte Carlo with M samples.
  *Replication cost*: Requires M=10 generations per problem on GPU (10× base inference cost for MATH-500 CoT).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes, but only as a one-shot baseline comparison — prohibitively expensive for production abstention.

- **Temperature-optimized sampling** — Finding optimal temperature (0.5) that balances diversity vs accuracy for uncertainty estimation. Neither too greedy (no diversity) nor too hot (garbage outputs).
  *Replication cost*: 2h GPU for temperature sweep.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — our method is single-pass, temperature is irrelevant.

- **Simplified cluster-count uncertainty** — Simply count the number of semantically distinct answers as a cheap uncertainty proxy (AUROC 0.79 on TriviaQA).
  *Replication cost*: 5min CPU given existing generations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — trivial baseline if we have K≥2 completions cached.

## Approaches & framings

- **Meaning-space vs token-space distinction**: Uncertainty should be measured over *meanings* not *token sequences*. This maps onto our project as: DoM captures something in "meaning space" (the model's internal representation of semantic content) while token-probability entropy operates in token space. Both are approximations to meaning-uncertainty, but DoM does it in one pass.

- **Semantic invariance as a fundamental NLG challenge**: The paper formalizes that identical meanings have many surface forms. For our MATH-500 setting, this is less severe (mathematical answers have canonical forms) but still relevant for chain-of-thought diversity.

- **Unsupervised vs supervised uncertainty**: The paper positions SE as unsupervised (no task-specific training), analogous to how our DoM is extracted without correctness labels initially (CAST PC1 ≈ supervised DoM per cos=0.9216). The supervised/unsupervised boundary is the same across both approaches.

## Datasets & benchmarks

- **TriviaQA** — ~97k QA pairs, open-license, HF available. Applicable? no — closed-book factual QA, not mathematical reasoning. Different signal structure from MATH-500.
- **CoQA** — ~8k conversational QA pairs (dev split), open-license, HF available. Applicable? no — open-book conversational QA, no reasoning chains.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Uses OPT models (2.7B–30B) via HuggingFace transformers
- DeBERTa-large fine-tuned on MNLI for NLI classification
- Multinomial sampling with temperature 0.5, M=10 samples per question
- Beam search (num_beams=5, do_sample=True) for the "best answer" used in correctness evaluation
- Code: https://github.com/lorenzkuhn/semantic_uncertainty (MIT license)
- 80GB A100 for experiments
- RougeL > 0.3 as correctness criterion (robust to alternative thresholds per ablation)
- Clustering is O(M²) worst case but efficient in practice (M<20, DeBERTa much smaller than generator)

  - Correlate cluster-count ranking with DoM ranking to test signal overlap

- Cached L19 prefill activations (500×1536) can be correlated with any output-based uncertainty score to measure mutual information between internal geometry and output diversity.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2302.09664 — Semantic Uncertainty: Linguistic Invariances for Uncertainty Estimation in Natural Language Generation (Kuhn, Gal, Farquhar; ICLR 2023)

**Relevance:** Foundational output-based uncertainty method that achieves AUROC 0.828 (TriviaQA, OPT-30B) for predicting model correctness — directly comparable to our DoM AUROC 0.7731 (MATH-500, Qwen-2.5-1.5B). The key methodological contrast: they require M=10 generated samples + NLI clustering; we require a single prefill pass. Establishes the output-based ceiling against which internal-geometry approaches must be justified.

**Key claim we tested:** Semantic entropy (entropy over meaning-clusters rather than token sequences) is more predictive of correctness than token-level entropy or self-evaluation (p(True)).

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — We have not yet computed semantic entropy on MATH-500 with our model. Preliminary comparison possible using cached K≥2 completions (answer-cluster-count as SE proxy). The critical question is whether DoM captures a signal orthogonal to output diversity (justifying the internal approach) or is merely a cheaper approximation of it.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE960, P11-FE961, P11-FE962, P11-FE963, H-830, H-831

**Status:** TO TEST
