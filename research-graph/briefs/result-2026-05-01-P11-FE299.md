## EXPERIMENT_LOG entry

## EXP-46: P11-FE299 — Within-topic prefill DoM AUROC
**Date:** 2026-05-01
**Status:** COMPLETE
**Motivated by:** Refutation 2 in the original F-2 brief: if within-topic
AUROC collapses toward 0.5 while overall stays at 0.77, the prefill DoM
signal is dominated by topic-familiarity base-rates rather than per-
problem decomposability — refuting H-5's framing. This is the
complement of FE719: same data, opposite split.
**Hypothesis:** Within-topic AUROC > 0.55 ⇒ F-2 is per-problem (NOT a
pure topic-detector). Within-topic AUROC ≤ 0.55 ⇒ F-2 is topic-base-
rate.
**What we actually tested:** For each of 7 MATH-500 subjects, took the
n_subject problems' L19 prefill activations (from `m15b_prefill.npz`)
and ran k-fold stratified OOF DoM (k = min(5, min-class-count),
seed=9999) within that subject. Computed AUROC over the within-subject
OOF scores.
**Key result:** Mean within-topic AUROC **0.7143**. Per-subject:
Algebra 0.6903 (n=124), Counting & Probability 0.6500 (n=38), Geometry
0.8128 (n=41), Intermediate Algebra 0.6683 (n=97), Number Theory
**0.6000** (n=62), Prealgebra **0.8305** (n=82), Precalculus 0.7481
(n=56). 7/7 subjects scored.
**Verdict:** F-2 NOT a pure topic-detector. The 0.7143 within-topic
mean is well above the 0.55 refutation threshold. The ~6pp gap between
within-topic mean (0.7143) and overall (0.7731) quantifies a small
topic-base-rate component — the bulk of the signal is per-problem.
**Changed our understanding of:** Number Theory is the consistent weak
spot. Both FE719 (LOCO 0.6032) and FE299 (within 0.6000) bottom out at
Number Theory — the prefill DoM direction is informative across most
subjects but ~⅔-strength on Number Theory specifically. Possible
follow-up: per-subject DoM cosine matrix to see whether NT problems
genuinely use a different correctness direction.
**Phase 1 decision branch status:** Counter unchanged: 1 of 5 below
0.70 (FE448 residualized only). FE299 0.7143 is above 0.70. F-2 stays
MODERATE; the length confound remains the dominant counterargument.
**Files:** `pathway11_h100/within_topic/recompute_fe299.py` (regen),
`pathway11_h100/within_topic/results.json` (output).
**Caveat:** k=2-5 within-subject is small; the Counting & Probability
result (0.6500, n=38, k=2) is noisy. Geometry 0.8128 (n=41, k=2) is
also small-n.
**Depends on:** EXP-037, EXP-43.
**Enables:** Per-subject DoM cosine matrix (a possible H-N about
correctness-direction stability across subjects).

**Claim.** On Qwen-2.5-1.5B-Instruct, a 5-fold OOF logistic probe fit on
mean-pooled L19 activations at position 0 (last prompt token, pre-generation)
achieves **AUROC 0.7731**. Final-token DoM AUROC is **0.7186**. Per-layer
sweep (EXP-45): L19 sits on a 3-layer peak plateau (L19=0.7705,
L20=0.7706, L21=0.7718). Leave-one-subject-out CV (EXP-43): mean
0.7427, worst 0.6032 (Number Theory). Within-topic CV (EXP-46): mean
0.7143, worst 0.6000 (Number Theory), best 0.8305 (Prealgebra). After
partialing out predicted-length from prefill (EXP-44), in-sample DoM
AUROC drops from 0.7834 to **0.6647** — gray zone between
length-artifact (≤0.55) and survives-partialing (>0.65). The 7B shows
the same prefill-pattern with a larger gap to final-token.

**Strength:** MODERATE (signal real, survives topic and within-topic
controls; ~⅔ of AUROC is length-explainable).

**Evidence:** EXP-037
(`pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/results.json` fields
`prefill_DoM_auroc_oof` = 0.7731, `final_token_DoM_auroc_oof` = 0.7186).
EXP-43 (`pathway11_h100/loco_subject/results.json`: LOCO mean 0.7427,
worst 0.6032). EXP-44
(`pathway11_h100/length_partial/results.json`: length R² 1.0,
auroc_length_alone 0.7986, auroc_residualized 0.6647). EXP-45
(`pathway11_h100/per_layer_sweep/results.json`: peak_layer 21,
peak_auroc 0.7718, auroc_l19 0.7705). EXP-46
(`pathway11_h100/within_topic/results.json`: within_topic_mean
0.7143, worst 0.6000, best 0.8305).

**Controls passed:**
- 5-fold stratified OOF (seed 9999) — not a test/train leak.
- Leave-One-Subject-Out CV (EXP-43) — mean AUROC 0.7427, worst 0.6032.
- Per-layer sweep (EXP-45) — L19 within ±0.005 of L21 peak.
- **Within-topic CV (EXP-46, FE299)** — mean AUROC 0.7143, well above
  the 0.55 topic-detector refutation threshold. Per-problem
  decomposability dominates topic base-rate.

**Controls partially failed:**
- Length partial-correlation (EXP-44, FE448) — DoM AUROC drops 0.7834 →
  0.6647 after partialing predicted-length. Not refuted (0.6647 > 0.55)
  but does not survive partialing (0.6647 ≤ 0.65). Length is a major
  confound.

**Strongest counterargument:** Sequence length alone (AUROC 0.7986)
predicts correctness *better* than raw DoM at L19. The DoM direction may
be largely re-discovering "this prompt will get a short / long
generation" — which itself proxies for problem familiarity. Number
Theory remains a consistent weak fold across all topic controls (0.60
in both FE719 and FE299), suggesting NT problems use prefill geometry
slightly differently from the rest of MATH-500.
