# Triage brief — 2502.05242 — Beyond External Monitors: Enhancing Transparency of Large Language Models for Easier Monitoring

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05242
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM, AUROC 0.7731 OOF on Qwen2.5-1.5B) is treated as a near-ceiling on what supervised residual-stream probes can extract.** TELLME shows that lightweight LoRA-based contrastive disentanglement at a single target layer (80% of depth, layer-norm-equipped, last-token only) raises inter-class `l_2` distance by 273.5%, decreases eRank by 57.3%, and pushes the trivial Self-Sim (cosine-to-class-mean) monitor on Llama-3.1-8B from 68.3% → 83.2% on binary safety classification — a 14.9-pt jump for free, with general-capability degradation < 1 pt on GSM8K/MMLU/AGIEval (Tables 1, 2, 5). Mechanism for refutation: if we apply TELLME to Qwen2.5-1.5B with positive pairs drawn from K=1-correct prefills and negatives from K=1-incorrect prefills, the post-edit prefill DoM AUROC could plausibly exceed 0.90 — and the K=2.5-budget selective-prediction figure (F-8, 71.6% at 50% coverage) could shift well above 80%. That would refute the implicit framing that 0.7731 is what *the model can tell us*; it is what *this base model with no monitor-friendly training tells us*. Falsifiable concretely: clone `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/` extraction, run TELLME LoRA at L19 on the train fold, re-extract on holdout fold, recompute OOF AUROC.

2. **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046, "the two directions are orthogonal") is treated as an architectural fact about transformer residual streams.** TELLME demonstrates that the geometry at a chosen layer is highly malleable to contrastive objectives — Hausdorff distance between behavior sets increases 324.5% across four model families (Table 1) — and Appendix B.7 reports the effect propagates so that monitoring accuracy is uniformly high across layers 40-90% of depth. Mechanism: the prefill→final orthogonality may simply reflect that the base model never had a training signal aligning these two events on the same axis. If we run TELLME with positive pairs constructed as `(prefill_t, final_token_t)` from same-correctness problems and negatives across correctness, the post-edit cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) could shift toward 1.0, refuting the claim that orthogonality is "a property the program found" rather than "a property nothing has trained against." Falsifiable: TELLME with positional-mixed positive pairs on cached Qwen2.5-1.5B activations, then re-fit DoM probes from edited prefill / edited final embeddings.

3. **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes / label-free probes substitute for supervised DoM for refusal) frames the choice as "which probe at the base model".** TELLME's Self-Sim numbers (Table 5: 53.3-69.7% before, 78.3-83.2% after across Llama / Qwen / Mistral) show that the right move may be neither supervised DoM nor label-free SEP at the base model — but rather modify the model so the trivial mean-cosine probe matches the supervised probe. Mechanism: if Self-Sim post-TELLME catches up with Linear Probe post-TELLME (the gap closes from ~13 pt to ~1 pt in their Table 5 binary-risk row), the Linear-Probe-vs-SEP comparison that H-12 wants to run is meaningless on TELLME-edited models — both probes saturate. H-12 should be reformulated as "after TELLME-style disentanglement, do supervised and label-free probes converge?" Falsifiable: run TELLME with safety/correctness pairs on Qwen2.5-1.5B, compare Self-Sim, supervised DoM, and SEP-style entropy probes on the same holdout.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)** — TELLME's Linear Probe baseline on Llama-3.1-8B reaches 81.5% on a binary safety classification task at the base model (Table 5). Different signal (safety vs correctness), different task setup (LoRA-edited monitors trained per-model vs OOF 5-fold), but the magnitude is comparable to our 0.7731 AUROC. Suggests that residual-stream linear separability of meaningful behavior axes is a generic property at the order of 0.75-0.85, not unique to correctness.

- **F-8 (selective-prediction 71.6% at 50% coverage on K=2.5 avg budget)** — TELLME's detoxification result (Table 3) shows safety rate from 83.1% → 95.5% on Llama-3.1-8B after disentanglement *without any reward signal telling the model which behavior is preferred* (page 9). The mechanism — separation of behaviors in representation space alone is enough to shift output behavior — is a precedent for the F-8 selective-prediction story being more lever-able than passive thresholding currently treats it.

- **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes for refusal)** — TELLME's Self-Sim baseline is structurally label-free at the probe stage (the labels are spent on the LoRA training, not the probe). Reframes the question.

- **H-13 (head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token circuits)** — TELLME's layer-wise ablation in Appendix B.7 (cited as Table 11) shows σ-stability across 10%-90% layer location with std-dev < 1% on MMLU. Indicates the disentangled axis lives at *a* layer, not *the* layer — relevant to whether prefill-vs-final has a head-level locus or a smeared depth-distributed one.

## Methodologies extracted

- **TELLME contrastive disentanglement loss (`L_d`)** — InfoNCE over residual-stream representations: `z_i = h^l_t / ||h^l_t||`, with positive pairs from same behavior and negative pairs across behaviors, temperature `σ` (Eq. 3).
  *Replication cost*: 1d H100 for LoRA on Qwen2.5-1.5B + dataset construction. Code public: https://github.com/AI45Lab/TELLME
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — the pivotal experiment that disambiguates whether F-2's 0.7731 is a model-property or a probe-only ceiling.

- **Retention loss (`L_r`)** — `l_2`-norm constraint on representations of held-out general-capability data + KL penalty on output distributions for the disentangled-behavior data (Eq. 4). Coefficient `λ` balances against `L_d` (Eq. 5).
  *Replication cost*: trivial once `L_d` is set up.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — without `L_r` the LoRA destroys MMLU; we'd need this to not nuke the 1.5B's MATH-500 baseline.

- **Self-Sim baseline (mean-of-class cosine probe)** — for each behavior class, compute mean of representations; classify a new point by argmax cosine to means.
  *Replication cost*: 20 min CPU on cached `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/` NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives a label-free correctness AUROC floor on cached data without any new training; checks whether the F-2 supervised DoM is wringing signal that any trivial probe finds.

- **Latent Guard (cross-attention disentangler)** — separate module attends across the residual stream to extract behavior label (Liu et al., cited as ref [48]).
  *Replication cost*: implementation work, ~3d engineering.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — orthogonal direction, not in our roadmap.

- **Five disentanglement metrics on residual streams** — Coding Rate (rate distortion of subspace, intra-class compression), eRank (effective rank, dimensionality of the inter-class span), `l_2` distance between class means, average angle between class means, Hausdorff distance between class point clouds.
  *Replication cost*: 30 min CPU on cached prefill activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — these directly characterize how much disentanglement headroom the base 1.5B has on correct/incorrect MATH-500 prefills.

- **80%-depth target-layer heuristic** — TELLME selects the LoRA target layer at 80% of model depth (e.g., layer 22 for a 28-layer Qwen). Note: this is *deeper* than F-2's L19 (≈68% of 28). Layer-wise ablation in Appendix B.7 reports near-flat MMLU degradation across 10%-90% range (std < 1%).
  *Replication cost*: minimal — a hyperparameter choice.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — the 80% vs L19 (68%) gap is the kind of non-trivial layer-locus question we already track in F-2 (e.g., is L19 special, or is the optimum drifting deeper?).

## Approaches & framings

- **"Make the model monitor-friendly, not the monitor smarter."** Inverts the standard probe-engineering paradigm: instead of choosing between supervised DoM (F-2) and label-free SEP (H-12), modify the model so trivial monitors work. Intersects every supervised-probe finding (F-2, F-7, F-8) by suggesting the probe ceiling is partly a model-architecture artefact.

- **Optimal-transport / Wasserstein generalization-bound theory.** Theorem 1 (proven in their ref [14]) bounds the zero-one expected loss by a sum over `Lip(g, j) · τ · Var_n_j(φ#p_j) + R̂_τ,n`. Reduces to: smaller intra-class `k`-variance + better margin → tighter bound. Connects to our F-1 breathing-as-population-geometry framing — `k`-variance ≈ within-bucket dispersion ≈ bucket-bound prefill DoM ratio.

- **Disentanglement-as-transparency.** Equates "the model is interpretable" with "behaviors are separable in the residual stream", measured by 5 metrics. Intersects F-7 (D-bucket distinctive geometric signature) by giving us a 5-metric vocabulary for *how distinctive* a bucket is.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- LoRA on a single decoder layer at 80% × `num_layers` (so for Qwen2.5-1.5B with 28 layers → L22; F-2 currently uses L19, ~68%).
- Edit applied to **last token of input** only (i.e., the prefill end-position) — directly aligns with our "prefill" definition.
- `σ` (InfoNCE temperature) and `λ` (loss balance) are the two key hyperparameters; specific values in Appendix B.2 (which we'd need to read for replication).
- KL penalty in `L_r` uses the *original* model's output distribution as reference — standard preference-tuning trick.
- Code: https://github.com/AI45Lab/TELLME (CC-BY-4.0 paper; code license unspecified in abstract, must check).
- Reported batch sizes for "B kinds of behaviors" not in main text — Appendix B.2.
- Layer-wise stability: std-dev of MMLU < 1% across 10%-90% target-layer choices (Appendix B.7, Table 11).

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2502.05242 — Beyond External Monitors: Enhancing Transparency of Large Language Models for Easier Monitoring (Chen et al., 2025)

**Relevance:** Inverts the standard probe-engineering paradigm relevant to the F-2 / F-8 line. Where this program treats the prefill L19 DoM as a discovery about the base model, TELLME shows that lightweight LoRA-based contrastive disentanglement at one layer can raise even a trivial mean-cosine monitor on Llama-3.1-8B from 68.3% to 83.2% binary-safety accuracy without degrading general capability — implying that "AUROC ceilings on residual-stream probes" are at least partly a base-model artefact rather than an architectural property. Their five disentanglement metrics (Coding Rate, eRank, `l_2`, Angle, Hausdorff) and optimal-transport-based generalization-bound theory provide a quantitative vocabulary that this program currently lacks for F-7 (D-bucket distinctiveness). Most importantly: TELLME's evidence that the orthogonality of disentanglement axes is *malleable* under a contrastive objective applies directly to F-3's cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046 finding — that orthogonality may be the absence of a training signal, not the presence of an architectural constraint.

**Key claim we tested:** Residual-stream representations at a chosen layer are sufficiently malleable that contrastive disentanglement can substantially raise *any* monitor's accuracy on that layer without harming general capability. Predicted consequence in our terms: applying TELLME to Qwen2.5-1.5B with correct/incorrect MATH-500 prefills as pairs would raise prefill DoM AUROC from 0.7731 to ≥ 0.85.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — we have not yet run TELLME on our pipeline. Two cheap precursor experiments (Self-Sim baseline and 5-metric disentanglement audit on cached NPZs) are queued (P11-FE74, P11-FE75) and will calibrate the expected lift before committing 1d H100 to FE73.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE73 (TELLME on 1.5B correctness), P11-FE74 (Self-Sim baseline), P11-FE75 (5-metric audit), P10-FE56 (TELLME for F-3 orthogonality test), H-91, H-92.
