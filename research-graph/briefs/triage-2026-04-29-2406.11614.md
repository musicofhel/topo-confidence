# Triage brief — 2406.11614 — Intrinsic Test of Unlearning Using Parametric Knowledge Traces

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11614
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-2 mechanism (parametric vs activation locus).** Hong et al. show that
   concrete-concept knowledge in LLaMA2-7B / OLMo-7B sits in a *small set of
   single columns* of the second MLP weight matrix (`W_V`) in middle-upper
   layers (12–27 in LLaMA, 8–28 in OLMo), and that adding `N(0, 0.1)` Gaussian
   noise to a *single* column kills the corresponding concept while leaving 5
   unrelated concepts intact. Our F-2 frames the L19 prefill DoM as a dense
   residual-stream direction. **Mechanism**: if math-correctness is encoded the
   way concepts are, the AUROC 0.7731 signal should reduce to a sparse set of
   `W_V` columns at L19 whose vocabulary projection contains
   correctness-flavored tokens (e.g. `\boxed`, digits, `=`, `\frac`); the dense
   DoM would then be a *readout artifact*, not a primary mechanism. **Falsifier**:
   compute Hong et al.'s logit-mean score over the 11008 columns of `W_V`
   at L19 of Qwen2.5-1.5B, project the top-200 tokens, then add `N(0, 0.1)`
   noise to the top-K columns and measure the change in the prefill-DoM
   AUROC on the 500 MATH problems. If a single-digit number of columns
   accounts for >50% of the AUROC drop, F-2's residual-stream framing is
   incomplete.

2. **F-7 D-bucket signature could be parametric, not collective.** F-7
   asserts D-bucket has a "distinctive collective (not per-problem) geometric
   signature." Hong et al.'s Causal Validation step shows ablation of *one*
   MLP column flips QA behavior for a concept while leaving 4–5 unrelated
   concepts untouched. **Mechanism**: D-bucket problems (K=1 right, K=8 wrong)
   may share a *single* parametric vulnerability — one or two MLP columns whose
   vocabulary projections are "fragile-domain" tokens. If true, the "collective"
   framing of F-7 is a residual-stream shadow of a far more localized parametric
   trace. **Falsifier**: pull L19 activations on the 36 D-bucket problems vs
   207 A-bucket problems (cached in `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/`),
   identify the top-10 MLP columns most differentially activated for D vs A,
   project them to vocabulary, and run the same Gaussian-noise ablation. If
   one column's ablation moves D→A bucket transitions but not A→A, F-7's
   "collective" claim drops to "parametrically localized."

3. **H-13 head-level attribution is the wrong unit.** H-13 plans
   head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token DoM circuits. Hong et al.
   work entirely *below* the head granularity — at single MLP-output column
   vectors (one of 11008 per layer). **Mechanism**: if the relevant unit for
   the prefill correctness signal is `W_V` columns, head-level patches will
   blur over many concept vectors and either (a) over-attribute to whole heads
   when only one column matters, or (b) under-detect because the column lives
   in the MLP path, not the attention path. **Falsifier**: run H-13's planned
   attention-head patching pass *and* a parallel MLP-column patching pass at
   L19. If MLP-column patching has higher single-unit causal effect on
   correctness than the most-causal head, H-13's framing of the question
   ("which heads") needs replacement with "which MLP columns."

4. **F-3 prefill/final orthogonality could be position-conditioned vocab readout.**
   F-3 reports `cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046`. Hong et al. demonstrate
   that the *same* MLP column gets activated more strongly under jailbreak (Table
   2: vanilla 2.50 → GCG 4.02), i.e. activation `m_j^ℓ` is highly
   position/prompt-conditioned even though the vector `v_j^ℓ` is fixed.
   **Mechanism**: prefill-DoM and final-DoM may be readouts of two
   *non-overlapping* position-conditioned activation patterns over the *same*
   underlying set of `W_V` columns. The 0.046 cosine in residual space then
   reflects orthogonal activation at two positions, not two distinct
   computational mechanisms. **Falsifier**: compute the activation vector
   `m^L19 ∈ R^{11008}` at prefill vs final-token positions over MATH-500;
   project both to identify their top-correlated `W_V` columns. If the same
   columns dominate both (just at different magnitudes), F-3's "two circuits"
   reading collapses to "one set of columns, two activation regimes."

5. **F-8 selective-prediction may overestimate parametric encoding.** Hong et
   al.'s headline finding is that *behavioral* unlearning evaluation (BLEU/Rouge
   on QA) overstates internal change: gradient-based unlearning gets target-QA
   BLEU < 0.25 while leaving Jaccard similarity > 0.98 in the concept vector's
   top-200 tokens. **Mechanism**: F-8's selective-prediction at 50% coverage
   (71.6% accuracy) is a *behavioral* metric on the residual-stream probe; if
   the underlying parametric correctness encoding is largely unchanged across
   problems and the probe is reading off an inference-time *suppression* signal
   (e.g. routing, refusal-style) rather than a stable knowledge trace, the
   AUROC 0.7731 may not transfer across distribution shifts where the
   suppression mechanism breaks down. **Falsifier**: train the prefill DoM
   probe on MATH-500, then evaluate on a jailbreak-style perturbation of the
   prompt (Lynch et al.'s "Crafted1/Crafted2" prompts adapted to math). If
   AUROC degrades to chance under perturbations that don't change the
   underlying problem, F-8 is reading suppression, not parametric correctness.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2** (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731): Hong et al.'s `W_V` column-vector

  vocabulary-projecting the columns of L19 `W_V` in Qwen2.5-1.5B and asking
  whether any top-token sets correspond to "correctness/answer-formatting"
  concepts.
- **F-3** (`cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046`): paper's activation-vs-vector
  distinction (`m_j^ℓ` activation vs `v_j^ℓ` vector) provides a
  position-conditioned reframing of orthogonality. Read F-3 body.
- **F-7** (D-bucket collective signature, peak PR 67/88): single-column Gaussian
  ablation methodology gives a precise refutation tool — see Refutation 2.
- **F-8** (selective-prediction 71.6% acc at 50% coverage): paper's behavioral
  vs intrinsic split is the analog of F-8's behavioral AUROC — see Refutation 5.
- **H-1** (per-position DoM steering): Hong et al.'s "Needle" (oracle direct
  ablation of a single MLP column) is methodologically the *opposite* of
  H-1's additive steering — same target (concept-coded MLP space), opposite
  intervention (subtract the vector vs add a steering vector). Strong
  comparator for any H-1 implementation.
- **H-13** (head-level attribution): unit-of-attribution refutation — see
  Refutation 3.
- **H-12** (semantic-entropy probes for refusal): Hong et al. show
  Jaccard top-token similarity of `W_V` columns as a *parametric* analog of
  vocab-distribution-based abstention signals. H-12 gains a parametric-side
  comparator.
- **H-17** (DoM rotation = RoPE-mechanical): if the relevant correctness unit
  is an MLP column whose vector is *fixed*, the rotation is purely in the
  activation field `m_j^ℓ`, supporting H-17's "not computed" reading.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Vocabulary projection of MLP value-matrix columns** — for each column
  `v_j^ℓ` of the second MLP weight `W_V` at layer ℓ, compute `E v_j^ℓ ∈ R^|V|`
  and read its top-k tokens to identify the concept it promotes.
  *Replication cost*: ~1h CPU per layer for Qwen2.5-1.5B (28 layers, d_i ≈ 8960).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct probe of whether L19 has a
  correctness-coded `W_V` column.

- **Logit-mean column ranking** — score `v_j^ℓ` by
  `Σ_i (e_i · v_j^ℓ)/|V|` to rank columns by how strongly they promote a
  specific (any) concept; Hong et al. use this to drop 30% of candidates per
  layer.
  *Replication cost*: minutes CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — cheap pre-screen for L19 column candidates.

- **GPT-4-scored concept coherence** — top-k tokens fed to GPT-4 with a
  rubric prompt that scores 0–1 for "clarity of concept." Used as second-stage
  filter.
  *Replication cost*: ~$5 per layer (200 columns × short prompts).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes for high-priority layers (L19, L23 cross-check).

- **Causal validation by Gaussian noise** — `v_j^ℓ ← v_j^ℓ + ε`,
  `ε ~ N(0, 0.1)` to a single column; behavioral effect measured by BLEU/Rouge
  drop on concept-related vs unrelated questions.
  *Replication cost*: ~30 min H100 per noised column × N columns. Cheap because
  only one weight changes per run.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — drop-in as a causal sanity check on any
  L19 column we identify as correctness-coded.

- **Activation-vs-vector decomposition** — separate the static parameter
  `v_j^ℓ` from the input-conditioned activation scalar `m_j^ℓ`, reporting
  changes to either independently across jailbreak/unlearning conditions
  (Table 2 reports activations under 6 jailbreak variants).
  *Replication cost*: trivial — already the natural hidden-state pipeline.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives F-3 a cleaner mechanistic frame than
  raw residual-stream cosine.

- **Needle (oracle ablation baseline)** — directly impair a single concept
  column; cited as the upper bound of unlearning effectiveness.
  *Replication cost*: trivial once column identified.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — as causal upper-bound comparator for H-1
  steering experiments.

- **Jaccard@200 over top-token sets** — set-overlap of the top-200 vocab
  tokens of a column before vs after intervention, as a behavior-independent
  measure of parametric change.
  *Replication cost*: trivial.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives a parametric stability metric for
  H-8 (DoM stability across checkpoints).

## Approaches & framings

- **Parametric vs behavioral evaluation gap.** Hong et al. center the gap
  between "model can't answer" and "knowledge is gone." Reframes F-8
  selective-prediction as a behavioral metric whose parametric counterpart we
  haven't measured. Suggests a *parametric* stability check on the L19 DoM
  probe across model variants.

- **Concept vectors as lower-bound necessary conditions.** Hong et al. argue
  that ablating their identified vectors is *necessary* but *not sufficient*
  for knowledge erasure. By analogy, identifying L19 `W_V` columns
  parametrically encoding correctness signals gives a necessary set the DoM
  probe must intersect for any mechanistic claim.

- **Adversarial activation as a mechanistic probe.** Jailbreak attacks
  *increase* concept-vector activation `m_j^ℓ` rather than reaching new
  parameters (Table 2). This activation-side reading is directly portable
  to F-3: prefill vs final-token DoM may be activation-side, not vector-side.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- LLaMA2-7B-chat MLP intermediate dim `d_i = 11008`, 32 layers → 352,256
  candidate columns total. They keep top 70% per layer after logit-mean
  ranking, then GPT-4 score, then manual review.
- Concept vectors live in **middle-upper layers** (12–27 for LLaMA, 8–28 for
  OLMo); early-layer columns are syntactic/general (matches Geva 2021).
  L19 of Qwen2.5-1.5B sits squarely in this regime.
- Top-k vocab projection uses **k = 200** tokens.
- Causal noise: `N(0, 0.1)` standard deviation; single-column edit.
- Behavioral evaluation thresholds: 10 QA pairs / 10 completion queries per
  concept; 5 unrelated concepts for specificity.
- Code & benchmark: https://github.com/yihuaihong/ConceptVectors

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2406.11614 — Intrinsic Test of Unlearning Using Parametric Knowledge Traces (Hong, Yu, Yang, Ravfogel, Geva, 2024 / EMNLP 2025)

**Relevance:** Methodologically central, not a competitor. Hong et al. give a
turnkey vocab-projection + causal-validation toolkit for identifying
single-MLP-column "concept vectors" in middle-upper layers of LLMs, plus a
single-column Gaussian-noise oracle ("Needle"). Although the paper's stated
target is *unlearning evaluation*, every step transports directly to our
question: is the L19 prefill DoM (F-2) a dense residual-stream direction or a
readout of a small number of parametric concept vectors in `W_V`? The paper
also gives a clean activation (`m_j^ℓ`) vs vector (`v_j^ℓ`) decomposition that
reframes F-3's prefill/final orthogonality as potentially position-conditioned
activation rather than two distinct circuits.

**Key claim we tested:** "Concrete-concept knowledge in LLMs localizes to a
small set of `W_V` columns in middle-upper MLP layers; vocab projection of
those columns reveals the concept; single-column Gaussian-noise ablation
causally erases it."

**Our result:** **TO TEST.** Two FE blocks (P11-FE85 vocab projection of
Qwen2.5-1.5B L19 `W_V`; P11-FE86 single-column ablation) and one orthogonality
decomposition (P11-FE87) operationalize a direct test on F-2, F-3, F-7. No
existing JSON addresses parametric localization of the correctness signal.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE85, P11-FE86, P11-FE87, H-114, H-115; bears on
F-2, F-3, F-7, F-8, H-1, H-12, H-13, H-17.
