# Triage brief — 2025.35346 — SMITIN: Self-Monitored Inference-Time INtervention for Generative Music Transformers

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2025.35346

DOI `10.1109/ojsp.2025.3534686`. The actual arxiv id is **2404.02252**
(Koo, Wichern, Germain, Khurana, Le Roux — IEEE OJSP 2025; preprint Apr 2024).

Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes the implied "single-layer L19 is enough" reading of F-2 / F-9.**
   SMITIN trains independent logistic-regression probes on **every** attention-head
   output (1,536 heads in MusicGen-large = 48 × 32) and shows that probe accuracy
   varies smoothly with head index, with a long upper tail (best 96.2% / mean
   77.5% on real-vs-synthetic). Mechanism: head-level probing exploits a
   distributed correctness code that single-layer DoM at L19 collapses into one
   direction. **Falsifiable test**: train per-head logistic-regression probes on
   the cached Qwen-2.5-1.5B prefill activations across all 28 layers × 16 heads
   = 448 heads. If a top-K (or soft-weighted) ensemble of head probes yields
   AUROC > 0.7731 + 0.02 (≥ 0.79) on the same OOF split, F-2's "L19 DoM is the
   correctness signal" claim becomes "L19 DoM is one *projection* of the correctness
   signal", and F-9's "CoE-60 is redundant with single-layer DoM" must be
   re-examined — head-level features may carry orthogonal information that CoE-60
   captures and L19 DoM does not.

2. **Refutes naive constant-α framing of H-1 (per-position DoM steering).**
   SMITIN explicitly documents that **constant-strength** ITI-style intervention
   produces "temporally incoherent" output and motivates their entire
   self-monitoring mechanism (Eq. 4 + threshold `τ = median(𝒜) − std(𝒜)` and
   the three-rule decay/reset/zero schedule). Mechanism: the steered direction
   stops being correctness-relevant once the model's hidden state moves into a
   regime where the probe sigmoid saturates, so further pushes degrade quality
   without improving the target signal. **Falsifiable test**: run H-1 in two
   variants on Qwen-1.5B MATH-500: (a) constant α prefill DoM addition at every
   generation step; (b) SMITIN-style sparse (every s=5 tokens) intervention with
   median-probe-confidence threshold. If (a) catastrophically degrades MATH-500
   accuracy below the 48.6% baseline while (b) preserves or improves it, the
   "static direction, static strength" reading of H-1 is dead — H-1 must be
   rewritten as a controller hypothesis, not a vector hypothesis.

3. **Refutes the "single fixed direction" reading underlying F-3 (prefill / final
   orthogonality, cos = 0.046).**
   SMITIN's soft-weighting `w_{l,h} = ((acc − acc_min)/(acc_max − acc_min))^c`
   with c = 3 places almost all the steering mass on a tail of heads, not on a
   single layer-level direction. Mechanism: the cosine between two L19
   layer-mean DoMs is a marginal statistic; the "real" direction may live in a
   sparse head-level subspace where prefill and final-token probes share many of
   the same high-accuracy heads (high cosine in head-coordinates) even though
   the L19 layer-mean DoMs are orthogonal. **Falsifiable test**: train head-level
   prefill probes and head-level final-token probes on the same Qwen-1.5B data,
   compute cosine in **head-weight-space** (concatenated `w_{l,h}·θ_{l,h}`
   vectors). If that cosine is materially > 0.046 (say > 0.3), then F-3's
   orthogonality is an artifact of layer-mean pooling and must be re-stated as
   "the *layer-mean* prefill and final-token DoMs are orthogonal, while the
   underlying head-level circuits overlap substantially".

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2** (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731, OOF 5-fold). SMITIN's per-head logistic
  probes are the head-level analogue of our layer-level DoM. The `θ_{l,h}` is
  literally a logistic-regression weight on `z_{l,h}(t)` — same probe family,
  different granularity. Direct ceiling test for F-2: does head ensembling at
  L19 alone (16 heads) match the layer-level 0.7731, and does cross-layer
  pooling (448 heads) push it materially higher?
- **F-3** (cos 0.046 between prefill and final DoMs). SMITIN's distributed-head
  framing is a candidate alternative explanation: orthogonality may be a
  pooling artifact rather than a circuit-level fact (see Refutation 3).
- **F-7** (D-bucket has collective, not per-problem, geometric signature).
  SMITIN's `acc_{l,h}` distribution (drum probes ~95% best, ~85% mean; piano
  probes ~60% mean) suggests trait-specific concentration in head subsets. If
  D-bucket has a narrow head signature (say, 5–10 heads with much higher
  D-vs-rest probe accuracy than the rest), F-7 sharpens from "collective
  geometric signature" to "narrow head circuit".
- **F-8** (selective-prediction acc 71.6% at coverage 0.5, K=2.5 avg). SMITIN's
  median-probe-confidence `𝒞̄(t)` and threshold `τ` are exactly a runtime
  correctness gate analogous to F-8's coverage decision. The decay/reset/zero
  rules are a more sophisticated abstention-and-resume schedule than our
  current binary coverage cut.
- **H-1** (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy). SMITIN is the
  closest published precedent for H-1 in spirit, *and* a methodological
  warning: see Refutation 2.
- **H-13** (head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token circuits). SMITIN
  is essentially the music-domain version of H-13. The methodology (per-head
  probes, accuracy-ranked, soft-weighted) ports almost directly. H-13 should
  inherit SMITIN's soft-weighting + self-monitoring as the implementation
  template, not pure top-K ITI from Li et al.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Per-head logistic-regression probes** — train one binary logistic
  regression on each `z_{l,h}(t) ∈ ℝᴰ` head output, with D = head dim (64 in
  MusicGen). Replicates head-by-head; gives an `acc_{l,h}` matrix of shape
  (#layers × #heads).
  *Replication cost*: 20–40 min CPU on cached Stage-2 NPZs (Qwen-1.5B has
  28 × 16 = 448 heads; sklearn LogisticRegression on ~500-sample splits per
  head is trivial).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct ceiling test for F-2 and direct test
  of H-13.

- **Soft-weighting by relative probe accuracy** — `w_{l,h} =
  ((acc − acc_min)/(acc_max − acc_min))^c`, c = 3, normalized to [0,1].
  Avoids top-K cutoff tuning; concentrates mass on the upper tail.
  *Replication cost*: trivial once head probes exist (one-line transformation).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — replaces our current "L19 only" hard cut
  with a smoother weighting that may capture cross-layer signal.

- **Sparse intervention with self-monitoring** — apply intervention every s=5
  tokens; at each intervention step, compute `𝒞̄(t) = median(sigmoid(⟨θ, z⟩))`
  over the top-K heads; if `𝒞̄ < τ = median(𝒜) − std(𝒜)` decay weights, if
  ≥ τ zero them out, with reset rule when stuck at zero.
  *Replication cost*: H100 day for full MATH-500 generation pass with
  intervention; the controller logic itself is ~50 lines of Python wrapped
  around the existing forward hooks.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — this is the realistic version of H-1; naive
  constant-α steering is the wrong baseline.

- **Probe direction normalization** `σ_{l,h}` — std of probe projections on
  the calibration set, used to scale intervention so heads with bigger
  natural-scale projections aren't over-steered.
  *Replication cost*: trivial (one std per head).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — it's a free correction.

- **Calibration-set ablation showing 10 paired examples ≈ stabilized at 500** —
  empirical demonstration that probe direction is sample-efficient.
  *Replication cost*: 1 h CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly informs H-8 (DoM stability across
  training checkpoints) by giving a baseline for "how few examples is enough"
  to fit a stable direction.

## Approaches & framings

- **Steering as a controller, not a fixed-direction add** — the paper's central
  conceptual move is that the intervention strength must be modulated in
  response to the probe's own output, not held constant. This intersects H-1
  directly: H-1 as currently written is a vector hypothesis ("DoM steering
  works"); SMITIN forces it to become a controller hypothesis ("DoM steering
  works *with* a feedback rule").
- **Distributed head-level coding of a high-level trait** — SMITIN treats a
  musical trait as a property of a *set* of heads, not a single direction.
  This is the head-level reframing of F-2/F-3 (see Refutations 1 and 3) and
  the natural framework for H-13.
- **Probe AUROC distribution as a circuit-discovery tool** — the long-tailed
  `acc_{l,h}` distribution is itself the deliverable, before any intervention
  is run. This reframes head-level attribution from "find the circuit" to
  "rank heads, look at the tail". Useful for F-7 (where in the head map does
  D-bucket separate from A?).

## Datasets & benchmarks

- **MusicCaps + MUSDB18 + Slakh + internal MERL set for instrument probing**
  — total ~35 hours across drums/bass/guitar/piano, plus a real-vs-synthetic
  set. Audio domain only. Not applicable to MATH-500 / hidden-state pipeline,
  but the **probe-calibration sample sizes** (8.3 h / 8.0 h / 13.1 h / 5.7 h)
  give a rough budget signal: probe directions stabilized with as few as
  ~10 paired examples, full convergence by ~500.
  *Applicable*: no for direct use; yes as a sample-budget reference for H-8 /
  H-13 calibration.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- **MusicGen-large**: 48 layers, 32 heads/layer, head dim 64, 3.3B params.
  Smaller variants (300M, 1.5B) also tested. Probes scale to 1,536 heads in
  the 3.3B model — head-level methods are tractable up to that size.
- **Default config**: α = 5.0, sparse step s = 5, top-K = 16 for monitoring,
  power factor c = 3, threshold τ = median(𝒜) − std(𝒜).
- **Probes trained with sklearn-style logistic regression**, no regularization
  notes given but standard L2 implied.
- **Probe direction θ_{l,h}** is the unit-normalized logistic weight vector;
  intervention is applied as `+ α · w_{l,h} · σ_{l,h} · θ_{l,h}` *added to
  the post-attention head output before the output projection W^O*.
- **Sparse-intervention placement** matters: intervention is at attention-head
  output, not residual stream — meaningful detail for porting (we currently
  read residual stream).
- **Code**: https://github.com/merlresearch/smitin — MIT-licensed,
  PyTorch + AudioCraft.

## Proposed HYPOTHESES.md additions

### H-92: Head-level probe ensemble beats single-layer L19 DoM on prefill correctness
**Priority:** HIGH
**Motivated by:** 2025.35346 + F-2
**Test:** Train 448 logistic-regression probes (one per (layer, head)) on the
cached Qwen-1.5B Stage-2 prefill activations, predicting K=1 MATH-500
correctness. Compare two ensemble AUROCs against F-2's 0.7731 layer-mean L19
baseline on the same OOF 5-fold split: (a) top-K=16 vote, (b) SMITIN
soft-weighted (c=3) sum of probe sigmoids. Est: 30 min CPU.
**Requires:** CPU; cached Stage-2 NPZs (already exist).
**Would change:** If best ensemble AUROC ≥ 0.79, F-2 is restated as "L19 DoM
captures one *projection* of the correctness signal", and F-9's "CoE-60 is
redundant with single-layer DoM" must be retested against the head ensemble
(CoE-60 may genuinely add over single-layer but be subsumed by head-level).
If ensemble ≤ 0.78, F-2 strengthens — single-layer DoM is the correctness
sufficient statistic at the prefill stage.
**Blocks:** H-13 (head-level attribution gets actual numbers from this
experiment), H-93 (the controller hypothesis below).

### H-93: DoM steering on math reasoning requires SMITIN-style self-monitoring, not constant α
**Priority:** CRITICAL
**Motivated by:** 2025.35346 + H-1
**Test:** Two-arm comparison on Qwen-1.5B MATH-500 K=1: Arm A applies prefill
L19 DoM as a constant-α residual add at every generation token; Arm B applies
the same direction sparsely (every 5 tokens) with the SMITIN three-rule
controller (decay if probe confidence below τ = median(acc) − std(acc), reset
when stuck at zero, zero out when above τ). Sweep α ∈ {2, 5, 10}. Baseline
48.6%. Est: H100 day.
**Requires:** H100 pod; SMITIN controller code ported (~50 lines around
existing forward hooks).
**Would change:** If Arm A accuracy collapses below 30% at α = 5 while Arm B
preserves or improves on the 48.6% baseline, H-1 must be rewritten as a
controller hypothesis — the *direction* is necessary but a *static add* is
the wrong intervention. If both arms preserve baseline accuracy, the
self-monitoring machinery is unnecessary and H-1 in its current static-vector
form is admitted to the queue. If both collapse, the prefill direction is not
causally usable for steering and H-1 dies.
**Blocks:** H-1 in its current form (this experiment decides which form
H-1 should take), and any future per-position-steering FE.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2025.35346 — SMITIN: Self-Monitored Inference-Time INtervention for Generative Music Transformers (Koo, Wichern, Germain, Khurana, Le Roux, IEEE OJSP 2025; arxiv 2404.02252)

**Relevance:** SMITIN is the closest published methodological precedent for
the head-level/per-position interventions our project proposes (H-1, H-13).
It extends Li et al. ITI (2306.03341) with two innovations directly portable
to Qwen-1.5B math reasoning: a soft-weighting of attention heads by probe
accuracy `w = ((acc − acc_min)/(acc_max − acc_min))^c` with c=3, and a
median-probe-confidence self-monitoring controller with threshold
τ = median(𝒜) − std(𝒜) that decays / resets / zeros intervention strength
during generation. The paper's empirical content (audio domain) does not
transfer; the *machinery* does. Most consequentially, SMITIN documents that
constant-α intervention degrades output coherence — a warning signal for any
naive form of H-1.

**Key claim we tested:** That ITI-family head-level probing carries
correctness signal beyond what a single-layer mean direction (our DoM)
captures, and that any per-position intervention requires a feedback
controller rather than a static residual add.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** (H-92 and H-93 are the two falsification arms;
P11-FE71 / P11-FE72 / P11-FE73 are the FE blocks). Until those run, SMITIN
sits as the strongest single source of methodological pressure on F-2
(single-layer-DoM ceiling), F-3 (layer-mean orthogonality vs head-space
overlap), and the entire framing of H-1 (vector vs controller).

**Related experiments:** P11-FE71, P11-FE72, P11-FE73, H-92, H-93.
Cross-references: H-1 (per-position steering), H-13 (head-level attribution),
F-2 (prefill DoM AUROC), F-3 (orthogonality), F-9 (CoE-60 vs single-layer).

**Status:** TO TEST
