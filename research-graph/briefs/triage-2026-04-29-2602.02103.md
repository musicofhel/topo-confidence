# Triage brief — 2602.02103 — No Global Plan in Chain-of-Thought: Uncover the Latent Planning Horizon of LLMs

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02103
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-2 (prefill L19 DoM as correctness predictor) — myopic-horizon mechanism.**
   Tele-Lens shows that final-answer planning *only emerges 1–2 reasoning steps before
   completion* (Parity: 49–52% answer-prediction accuracy at positions −4..−2 vs 97–99%
   at the last position). On their reading, the prefill / early-CoT hidden state contains
   *no precise answer information*. Yet F-2 reports 0.7731 OOF AUROC for prefill L19
   DoM on Qwen-2.5-1.5B / MATH-500 K=1. Either (a) Tele-Lens's "myopic" finding does
   not generalize to MATH-500 + 1.5B (the paper's strongest myopic results are on
   synthetic compositional tasks; their MATH/MMLU/GPQA Top-5 next-token accuracy is
   *much lower*), or (b) the 0.7731 prefill signal is **not planning** — it's
   problem-difficulty / familiarity / answerability, which would mean we have been
   misnaming what F-2 measures. Falsifiable: train a Tele-Lens-style low-rank (r=256)
   final-answer probe at L19 prefill on our cached
   `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute` activations (Qwen-2.5-1.5B, MATH-500). If
   prefill Tele-Lens AUROC is at chance, the 0.7731 F-2 number is measuring something
   *not in the answer-encoding subspace* and the framing in FINDINGS.md needs to change
   from "prefill predicts correctness" to "prefill predicts answerability."

2. **Refutes F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM) — pivot-token mechanism.**
   Tele-Lens's Top-5 pivot strategy (positions selected by lowest final-answer entropy)
   beats whole-trajectory aggregation by **+9 pp AUROC** (0.69 vs 0.57 on the perplexity
   baseline; +0.05–+0.06 pp over self-certainty / entropy on Qwen3-32B). This is a
   nontrivial *intra-trajectory selection* effect that single-layer DoM and CoE-60
   uniform-aggregation cannot capture. F-9 was established by comparing CoE-60 against
   uniform L19 DoM aggregation — never against a learned pivot subset. Falsifiable:
   on cached Qwen-2.5-1.5B MATH-500 trajectories, compute final-answer entropy per
   token, take the top-5 minimum-entropy positions per trajectory, average L19 DoM at
   those positions, OOF-CV the resulting score against correctness. If AUROC > 0.7731,
   F-9 collapses — pivot selection beats single-layer DoM and CoE-60 was looking in
   roughly the right place but with the wrong aggregator.

3. **Refutes F-3 (prefill–final orthogonality, cos=0.046) — incremental-transition mechanism.**
   The paper's central claim is that LLMs do *incremental* hidden-state transitions
   without precise long-horizon planning. Under that reading, the natural prediction is
   that the answer-encoding subspace should *grow continuously through the trajectory*,
   so prefill and final hidden states should share substantial answer-relevant geometry
   *but only on tasks where answer information is present at prefill at all*. Our
   measured cos=0.046 between prefill_DoM and final_DoM is consistent with incremental
   transition *if* prefill carries non-answer signal (problem encoding) and final
   carries answer signal — but it is *inconsistent* with the framing in FINDINGS.md
   that "the model uses two functionally distinct correctness signals." Tele-Lens
   suggests there is just one signal (answer-relevant hidden state), which is absent
   at prefill and emerges late. Falsifiable: at every CoT position (every 50 tokens,
   say) extract the L19 DoM-style mean-difference direction (correct minus incorrect),
   and measure cos similarity between consecutive directions. If similarity is high
   and increasing toward the end, F-3's "two functionally distinct directions" framing
   is wrong; we'd reframe as a single direction that emerges late.

4. **Refutes H-5 (prefill encodes "problem familiarity vs decomposability") — null
   on knowledge tasks.** Tele-Lens explicitly tests prefill-time answer prediction on
   knowledge benchmarks (CSQA, MMLU, GPQA, QuALITY) and reports much weaker effects
   than on compositional tasks. If prefill DoM at L19 truly encoded problem
   familiarity, it should work *better* on knowledge benchmarks (where retrieval
   dominates) than on compositional ones (where decomposition matters). H-5 makes the
   opposite prediction. Falsifiable: extend the prefill-DoM probe to MMLU /
   CommonsenseQA on Qwen-2.5-1.5B; if AUROC drops below ~0.65 on knowledge tasks
   while staying ~0.77 on MATH-500, H-5's familiarity-vs-decomposability framing is
   wrong and we need a new mechanism story.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC = 0.7731, vs 0.7186 final-token).** Tele-Lens's myopic
  finding directly predicts F-2 should not exist in the form we report — see
  Refutation 1.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046).** The orthogonality is the empirical
  fact; Tele-Lens supplies a competing mechanistic story (incremental transition →
  one direction emerging late, not two functionally distinct directions) — see
  Refutation 3.
- **F-8 (selective-prediction 71.6% acc at coverage 0.5, K=2.5 avg).** Tele-Lens's
  CoT-bypass result (2.8% bypass rate, −0.03% accuracy on Qwen3-32B) is the
  upstream-of-trajectory analog of F-8. Their mechanism (intra-CoT pivot uncertainty)
  is orthogonal to ours (pre-CoT prefill DoM), so the two are stackable. Stacking
  should raise the 71.6%@50% coverage number.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 ≈ single-layer L19 DoM).** Tele-Lens's Top-K pivot strategy is exactly
  the missing aggregator that CoE-60 uniform pooling and single-position DoM both
  lack — see Refutation 2.
- **H-5 (prefill encodes familiarity vs decomposability).** Tele-Lens's domain
  comparison (compositional vs knowledge) is a direct test bed — see Refutation 4.
- **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes, label-free).** Tele-Lens's pivot-selection mechanism
  uses *internal probe entropy* (final-answer head entropy) as the selection
  criterion — same family as semantic-entropy probes, possibly stronger because the
  entropy is over a low-dim head rather than over full vocab.
- **H-19 (C_exact verification routing).** Tele-Lens's CoT bypass with no accuracy
  drop is a *cheaper* compute-routing primitive than verification routing — early
  termination at high-confidence pivots could be folded into the same routing budget
  framework.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Tele-Lens probe (low-rank bottleneck adapter)** — at each layer k, transform
  hidden state via H̃ = GeLU((H + Emb(δ))·A)·B with A∈ℝ^{d×r}, B∈ℝ^{r×d}, rank r=256;
  Emb(δ) injects an offset embedding for "predict δ steps ahead." Trained ~5K steps
  with early stopping on dev. *Replication cost*: 30min H100 per layer probe on our
  cached Stage 2 NPZs (rank 256 is tiny). *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — it is the
  obvious upgrade over the linear DoM probe in F-2 and is the headline tool of the
  paper.

- **Three-headed probing protocol (subsequent-token / reasoning-length / final-answer).**
  Three independent heads off the same low-rank bottleneck: predict next-m tokens,
  regress total reasoning length, classify final answer from a 20-token vocab.
  *Replication cost*: 1h H100 per model. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes for the
  final-answer head (refutation tests above); maybe for the length-regression head
  (interesting orthogonal signal we haven't probed).

- **Top-K pivot selection by final-answer entropy.** Instead of aggregating the whole
  CoT, pick the K positions whose Tele-Lens final-answer head has lowest entropy
  (highest confidence) and aggregate just those. K=5 is the sweet spot; their
  paragraph 8 figure shows pivots concentrate near CoT completion. *Replication cost*:
  20min CPU on cached final-answer logprobs. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct
  refutation test for F-9.

- **CoT bypass thresholding.** Stop generating once pivot uncertainty drops below
  threshold τ=0.1; emit the model's then-current best-answer. Achieves 2.8% bypass
  with -0.03% accuracy on Qwen3-32B. *Replication cost*: 2h H100 (needs new
  generation runs). *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — would slot under H-19 routing.

- **GRPO-shortened in-domain CoT.** They train Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct via GRPO so that
  CoTs are ~1K characters instead of ~10K, to create a controlled in-domain
  comparison. *Replication cost*: 2 days H100. *We'd plausibly run this*: probably
  not — too far from our pipeline, but it's a useful trick if we ever want length-
  controlled CoT comparisons for H-9 (breathing amplitude vs difficulty).

- **Compositional task taxonomy (Parity / Cycle / Subsum).** Synthetic tasks where
  reasoning length is mechanically controllable; lets them disentangle "model knows
  the answer early" from "model knows reasoning will be long." *Replication cost*:
  1 day generation + extraction. *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — would help H-2
  (breathing on short CoT).

## Approaches & framings

- **Myopic-horizon framing.** Reframes the whole "models plan ahead" literature as
  "models plan only the next 1–2 tokens precisely." This is a direct challenge to
  F-2's "prefill predicts correctness" headline — if the model isn't planning, what
  is the prefill signal? Forces us to either rename F-2 ("prefill predicts
  answerability") or to explain why MATH-500 / 1.5B is special.

- **Pivot-token reframing of selective prediction.** Selective prediction is usually
  framed as "find a per-trajectory score" (CoE, INSIDE, our DoM). Tele-Lens reframes
  it as "find a *per-position* score and use it to pick where to look." Stronger
  intersection with H-12 (semantic-entropy probes).

- **Latent planning ≠ verbal planning.** They explicitly separate latent-state
  planning from CoT verbalization — verbal CoT can be long while internal planning
  is myopic. Conceptually adjacent to H-22 (Abstract-CoT) which separates reasoning
  computation from verbalization.

## Replicable intermediates

- **Tele-Lens-at-prefill probe.** Train a rank-256 low-rank bottleneck on cached L19
  prefill activations from `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute` to predict the
  20-class answer letter (we'd need to extract the gold-letter labels from MATH-500
  outputs). If AUROC ≪ 0.7731, refutation 1 lands and F-2 needs renaming.
- **Top-K pivot DoM.** On cached MATH-500 trajectories, compute per-token final-answer
  entropy from saved logprobs (or reconstruct from the model), pick the 5 lowest-entropy
  positions per trajectory, aggregate L19 DoM at those positions, score correctness.
  If AUROC > 0.7731, refutation 2 lands and F-9 collapses.
- **Per-position DoM cos-similarity sweep.** From the cached temporal hidden states
  in `scratch/pathway10_temporal_and_verifier_results.json`, recompute mean-difference
  directions every ~50 tokens and the cos-similarity matrix. If similarity rises
  monotonically toward the end, refutation 3 lands and F-3 reframes.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2602.02103 — No Global Plan in Chain-of-Thought: Uncover the Latent Planning Horizon of LLMs (Xu, Yu, Meng, Zhou, 2026)

**Relevance:** Direct competitor / collaborator for F-2 (prefill correctness probe),
F-3 (prefill–final geometry), F-8 (selective prediction), and F-9 (CoE-style
trajectory aggregation). Introduces "Tele-Lens" — a rank-256 low-rank bottleneck
adapter with three probing heads (next-token, reasoning-length, final-answer) — and
uses it to argue that LLMs are *myopic*: precise answer information emerges only
1–2 reasoning steps before the answer token. The pivot-token uncertainty estimator
(top-5 lowest-entropy positions) gives +9 pp AUROC over per-trajectory perplexity
baselines on Qwen3-32B. Their CoT-bypass result (2.8% bypass with -0.03% accuracy)
is an upstream-of-trajectory analog to our F-8 selective prediction.

**Key claim we tested:** LLMs do not plan precisely far ahead in latent state;
instead, the answer-encoding hidden subspace emerges late, and uncertainty
estimation should pick a *small subset of high-confidence pivot positions* rather
than aggregate the whole CoT.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** (HIGH priority). The myopic claim directly challenges
F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC = 0.7731 is implausible if the model is genuinely
myopic — implication: F-2 measures answerability not planning). The pivot-token
claim challenges F-9 (CoE-60 ≈ single-layer DoM was tested without pivot
aggregation). FE73, FE74, FE75, FE76 enumerate the refutation tests.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE73, P11-FE74, P11-FE75, P11-FE76, H-73, H-74.

**Status:** TO TEST
