# Triage brief — 2604.19018 — Local Linearity of LLMs Enables Activation Steering via Model-Based Linear Optimal Control

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19018
Triaged: 2026-04-29

Authors: Julian Skifstad, Xinyue Annie Yang, Glen Chou (Georgia Tech). Preprint April 22, 2026.
Code: https://github.com/trustworthyrobotics/lqr-activation-steering

## Refutations

1. **F-2 / H-1 — causal vs correlational refutation of prefill DoM as a correctness signal.**
   The paper's A-LQR + LFS is a *closed-loop* feedback steerer that can drive `v_k^T z_k` to any
   target `β_k* = λ μ_k` with theoretically bounded tracking error (Cor. 4.3) and SOTA empirical
   tracking. F-2 currently rests on a single static AUROC (0.7731 OOF on Qwen-2.5-1.5B): a
   *predictive* claim. **Refutation mechanism**: instantiate A-LQR with `v_19 = prefill DoM`
   from `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/results.json` and λ chosen to push the
   incorrectly-predicted half across the DoM hyperplane. The paper guarantees the activation can
   be moved to that setpoint while preserving fluency (TruthfulQA T·I went from 48.6→67.8 on
   Gemma-2-2B without informativeness loss). **Falsifiable test**: run A-LQR on the 257
   incorrect MATH-500 problems for Qwen-2.5-1.5B with the prefill L19 DoM as feature direction.
   If K=1 accuracy on those problems stays at ~0% (or the corrected fraction is no better than
   λ=0 jitter), the prefill DoM is correlationally tied to a confound (e.g. problem difficulty
   features in the prompt) rather than causally exploitable. That would substantially weaken
   F-2's interpretation as a "correctness direction."

2. **F-3 / H-17 — Jacobian-coupling refutation of "two functionally distinct directions".**
   Sec. 5.1 / Fig. 5 shows that within a fixed layer, transformer-block Jacobians at *different
   reachable activations* have top-m subspace similarity ~0.5–0.8 (Gemma-2-2B; replicates on
   Qwen-2.5-3B and Llama-3-8B per Fig. 6 and App. C). I.e. layer-wise dynamics are
   approximately content-independent. F-3 reports `cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046` and
   reads it as evidence for two functionally distinct directions. **Refutation mechanism**: if
   layer-Jacobians are content-stable, the orthogonality of prefill vs final-token DoMs is
   forced by the *position* (different `k`, different `Φ̂_{k,j}` cascade) and by *initial
   conditions* (prefill activation vs final-token activation), not by computed-by-the-model
   distinct features. **Falsifiable test**: compute Jacobians ∂φ_k/∂z at L19 across 50
   B-bucket and 50 D-bucket Qwen-2.5-1.5B prefill activations (Eq. 24, paper's nuclear-norm
   metric). If sim_m ≥ 0.6 with no bucket-level structure (B/D/A blocks not visible in the
   pairwise grid), then F-3's "orthogonal directions = orthogonal computations" framing fails;
   F-3 reduces to "different positions in a near-LTI cascade". H-17's "RoPE-mechanical" framing
   would also need rewriting — the effect is just *layer-wise Jacobian composition*, not RoPE
   per se.

3. **F-5 — content-dependence of breathing collapses to amplitude scaling.**
   F-5 ("Breathing is content-dependent, not AR-mechanics") is the project's strongest claim
   that PR curves carry semantic content. But the paper's local-linearity result directly
   implies: dimensional structure at layer k is governed by the (near-shared) Jacobian
   `∂φ_k/∂z`, applied to a content-set initial condition. **Refutation mechanism**: if all PR
   variation across prompts comes from `‖z_0‖` and the projection of `z_0` onto the dominant
   singular subspace (Fig. 4 shows ~5–10 dominant modes per layer), then "content-dependent
   breathing" is just *amplitude* differences in a fixed near-LTI mode structure. **Falsifiable
   test**: extract Jacobians on cached Stage 2 activations for 50 high-PR-peak and 50
   low-PR-peak prompts at the L13–L19 peak region. If the linearized PR (`PR(A_k @ ... @ A_1 @
   z_0)` with a *shared* Jacobian set across prompts) reproduces the observed PR curve to
   within ε≈0.05, F-5 is refuted: breathing is dynamics-dictated, not content-dictated. This
   joins F-10's PH=null result in collapsing the "breathing is computational signature" line.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)**: A-LQR provides the *causal-control* test absent from
  the F-2 evidence base. Connecting via H-1's per-position DoM steering experiment makes
  `pathway10_h100` the natural home (steering pathway).
- **F-3 (cos = 0.046)**: paper's content-stable layer Jacobians reframe what "orthogonal DoM
  directions" can mean mechanistically.
- **F-1, F-5 (breathing universality + content-dependence)**: layer-wise Jacobian alignment at
  ~0.5–0.8 across different prompts is the same observation as our breathing universality, but
  read in linear-systems terms. Either F-5 reduces to amplitude scaling (refuted), or the
  Jacobian alignment is weaker at L13–L19 in Qwen-2.5-1.5B (corroborates F-5 mechanistically).
- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy)**: A-LQR is the strongest available
  baseline for H-1; replaces the original fixed-vector ActAdd plan.
- **H-17 (DoM rotation is RoPE-mechanical)**: paper supplies a non-RoPE alternative —
  composition of layer Jacobians — that would explain the same phenomenon without invoking
  RoPE. Refines the hypothesis.
- **F-7 / F-8 (D-bucket signature, selective-prediction at coverage 0.5)**: paper's setup is
  agnostic to MATH; no direct connection to the bucket geometry. CITED ONLY.
- **F-10 (PH = Gaussian null)**: paper is consistent with F-10 — if local dynamics are
  near-linear at each layer, persistent homology of the residual stream should track Gaussian
  null behavior (no surviving topological cycle structure beyond what an LTV system would
  yield).
- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM)**: A-LQR's multi-layer LQR (joint

  multi-layer K_k cascade does not beat a single-layer-only K_19 ablation for correctness
  steering on MATH-500, F-9 gains independent corroboration.

## Methodologies extracted

- **A-LQR (Activation-LQR closed-loop steering)** — solve LTV-LQR Riccati recursion `S_k =
  A_k^T (S_{k+1} - S_{k+1} B_k Γ_k B_k^T S_{k+1}) A_k + Q_k`; apply `u_k* = K_k (β_k* - v_k^T
  z_k) v_k` online. Q, R are hyperparameters (App. E).
  *Replication cost*: ~1 day H100 to run on Qwen-2.5-1.5B with a contrastive set; offline
  Jacobian computation is VRAM-heavy.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — closed-loop steerer with prefill DoM as v is the right test
  for H-1 / F-2 causality.

- **Linear Feature Setpoint (LFS, β_k* = λ μ_k)** — adapts setpoint to per-layer activation
  norm μ_k = ‖e_k‖ where e_k = z_{k,+} - z_{k,-}.
  *Replication cost*: 20 min CPU once Jacobians and contrastive activations are cached.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — drop-in replacement for fixed-α ActAdd in any steering trial.

- **Top-m subspace similarity score (Eq. 24)** — `sim_m(W,X) = ‖A‖_* / (‖Σ(W_m)‖_F · ‖Σ(X_m)‖_F)`,
  with `A = (U_W^(m) Σ_W^(m))^T U_X^(m) Σ_X^(m)` and ‖·‖_* = nuclear norm. Generalises
  Aubry et al. 2025's matrix coupling to weight by singular values.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU on cached Jacobian samples per layer.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct test for refutations R2, R3.

- **Closed-loop tracking error bound (Thm 4.2 / Cor 4.3)** — bounds `|ε_k| ≤ Δ_1^v + Σ ‖v_k^T
  Φ̂_{k,i+1}‖ (‖ε_i‖ + L_i/2 ‖δz_i‖^2)`, with empirical `L̂_i = 2‖r_i‖/‖δz_i‖^2`.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU once L_i is empirically estimated from rollouts.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — useful only if we adopt A-LQR as a tool, then this gives
  formal guarantees on prefill-DoM tracking under our F-2 setup.

- **A-LQR+ (per-token-position variant)** — same K_k applied at every token position, with
  per-position online error signals; more invasive but lifts jailbreaking ASR (e.g.
  Gemma-2-9B 0.144 → 0.769).
  *Replication cost*: 1 day H100.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — jailbreaking is out of scope; flagging because intervention
  spread across positions is the most relevant variant if H-1 fails on last-token-only.

- **S-PID baseline** — drop-in PID controller tracking the LFS setpoint. Cheap, useful as
  intermediate step before full LQR.
  *Replication cost*: 4h CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — sanity-check before A-LQR.

## Approaches & framings

- **LLM inference as a linear time-varying (LTV) dynamical system** with `z_{k+1} = A_k z_k +
  B_k u_k + w_k`. Recasts hidden-state geometry as a control problem; complements our
  geometry-as-prediction framing. Direct intersection with H-17 (rotation as a mechanical
  cascade rather than RoPE).
- **Layer-Jacobian content-coupling** — within a fixed layer, Jacobians at different reachable
  activations are highly correlated (~0.5–0.8 nuclear-weighted top-m subspace similarity).
  Complements Aubry et al. 2025's *across-layer* coupling. Direct intersection with F-1, F-3,
  F-5, F-10.
- **Closed-loop vs open-loop steering** — feedback control on online error vs precomputed
  vector addition. Reframes ActAdd / ITI / Linear-AcT as the open-loop limit, A-LQR as the
  closed-loop limit. Direct intersection with H-1.
- **Adaptive layer-norm-scaled setpoint (LFS)** — encodes the prior fact that per-layer DoM
  magnitudes vary substantially (Vu & Nguyen 2025). Direct intersection with the
  per-position DoM bank concept underneath H-1.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Code release: https://github.com/trustworthyrobotics/lqr-activation-steering
- Models tested: Llama-3.2-1B/3B, Gemma-2-2B/9B, Qwen-2.5-3B/14B/32B, Llama-3-8B,
  Llama-3.1-8B/70B. **Qwen-2.5-3B is in their evaluation grid** — closest sibling to our
  Qwen-2.5-1.5B and 7B, so transfer to our setup is plausible.
- Intervention: additive perturbation to last-token residual stream at all 28 layers
  (Karnik & Bansal 2025 convention; Hedstrom et al. 2025 confirms across-all-layers is best).
- Contrastive sets: D+, D− with mean-difference vector `e_k = z_{k,+} - z_{k,−}`, unit
  direction `v_k = e_k / μ_k`.
- A_k Jacobian = `∂φ_k/∂z|_(z̄_k=z_{k,+}, ū_k=0)`. B_k = I.
- Riccati gains computed *offline* once per (model, contrastive set, Q, R, λ) tuple;
  reused online without recomputation. K_k storage is the bottleneck (paper notes VRAM
  overhead in App. F.3).
- Compute complexity: O(ℓ d^3) on CPU, O(log ℓ · log^2 d) on GPU via Fang & Chou 2026.
- LQR Q, R, λ tuned empirically to maximise toxicity reduction subject to PPL/MMLU floor;
  per-model tuning needed (App. E.2).
- Limitation explicitly flagged in §9: Q, R, λ sensitivity; Jacobian VRAM cost; conformal
  prediction (Srinivasan et al. 2026) listed as future direction for low-rank K compression.

## Replicable intermediates

- **L19 single-layer Jacobian alignment test (refutation R2/R3)**: cache 50 prefill
  activations from `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/results.json` (we have B/D/A bucket
  labels per problem). Compute `∂φ_19/∂z` at each (PyTorch `torch.autograd.functional.jacobian`
  on a frozen Qwen-2.5-1.5B). Compute pairwise top-m=64 subspace similarity (Eq. 24). Stratify
  by bucket. **Cost**: ~4h H100 for Jacobian extraction + 1h CPU for subspace similarity.
- **Linearized PR test (refutation R3)**: from cached Jacobians at L1…L28, apply LTI cascade
  `A_28 ∘ … ∘ A_1` to an initial-condition `z_0 = z_{embedding}` for 50 high-peak-PR and 50
  low-peak-PR Stage 2 prompts. Compute PR of linearized trajectory; compare to actual PR. If
  curves overlap to ε<0.05, F-5 is refuted. **Cost**: 2h CPU once Jacobians are cached.
- **Closed-loop A-LQR steering test for H-1**: install
  https://github.com/trustworthyrobotics/lqr-activation-steering, instantiate with
  Qwen-2.5-1.5B and v_k = our prefill L19 DoM (already cached, cosine-normalized). Run on the
  243 incorrect MATH-500 problems with λ ∈ {0.5, 1, 2, 5}. Measure (a) downstream K=1 accuracy
  delta, (b) PPL drift, (c) MMLU drift. **Cost**: ~1 day H100.
- **S-PID precursor**: reuse the same v_k as A-LQR; PID is dramatically cheaper. If S-PID
  fails to flip any incorrect→correct, A-LQR is unlikely to either. **Cost**: 4h CPU.

## 2604.19018 — Local Linearity of LLMs Enables Activation Steering via Model-Based Linear Optimal Control (Skifstad, Yang, Chou, 2026)

**Relevance:** Closed-loop activation steering via LQR with formal tracking guarantees,
plus a novel empirical claim that within-layer Jacobians are highly correlated across
reachable activations (subspace similarity ~0.5–0.8 on Gemma-2-2B, Qwen-2.5-3B,
Llama-3-8B). Directly relevant to (a) H-1 as the strongest available steering baseline

### Methodologies
- A-LQR closed-loop steerer (Riccati-precomputed K_k, online u_k* = K_k (β_k* - v_k^T z_k) v_k).
- Linear feature setpoint (LFS): β_k* = λ μ_k (per-layer-norm-scaled adaptive target).
- Top-m subspace similarity score (Eq. 24): nuclear-norm-weighted alignment metric for Jacobians.
- Closed-loop tracking error bound (Thm 4.2 / Cor 4.3): formal guarantees with empirical L_i.
- A-LQR+ all-token-position variant.
- S-PID baseline (PID over LFS) as light-weight alternative.

### Approaches
- LLM as LTV dynamical system; layer Jacobians as local descriptors.
- Within-layer Jacobian content-coupling complements Aubry et al. 2025's across-layer coupling.
- Closed-loop feedback steering vs open-loop activation addition.
