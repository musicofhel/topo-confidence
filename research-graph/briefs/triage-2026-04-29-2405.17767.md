# Triage brief — 2405.17767 — Linguistic Collapse: Neural Collapse in (Large) Language Models

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17767
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-4 (asymmetric collapse) may be a re-label of NC1 (within-class variability collapse).**
   Wu & Papyan show NC1 (CDNV, eqn 4) develops with scale and correlates with
   generalization at R²=0.19, p=0.0485 *independent of architecture* (Table 1).
   F-4 frames "correct trajectories collapse harder than incorrect at the final
   token" as a novel geometric correctness signature — but if a "correct" final
   embedding is by definition the one that lands near its class mean, then F-4 is
   exactly NC1 measured per-correctness-class instead of per-token-class.
   *Mechanism:* CDNV scales with class-mean separation in the denominator
   (eqn 4) — incorrect predictions land far from the *intended* class mean,
   inflating their CDNV; correct predictions land close, deflating theirs. This
   produces "asymmetric collapse" without any reasoning-specific geometry.
   *Falsifiable:* compute CDNV (with k=2 power on mean-distance, per their
   eqn 4 specialization for imbalanced classes) on cached pathway11_h100
   final-token activations, partitioned by ground-truth correctness. If the
   correct/incorrect CDNV ratio is what F-4 has been calling "asymmetric
   collapse," F-4 is a special case of NC1 — not a finding about reasoning.

2. **F-3 (prefill ⊥ final-token, cos=0.046) may rebrand the NC3 violation Wu & Papyan document.**
   Their Fig. 3 / Table 1 shows that *self-duality* (NC3 — class means
   aligning with classifier weight rows) does **not** consistently develop in
   CLMs at scale (R²=0.31). Final-token L19 activations are immediately
   projected through W_unembed, so the final-token DoM direction is structurally
   constrained to live close to a classifier-row subspace. Prefill activations
   never get that projection, so the prefill DoM is closer to a *class-mean*
   subspace. Their NC3-violation result is exactly the prediction "classifier
   space and class-mean space don't align" — which is just F-3 with different
   labels.
   *Mechanism:* if cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM)=0.046 is approximately
   cos(classifier-row, class-mean) in the same vocabulary, F-3 is geometric
   bookkeeping not a reasoning insight.
   *Falsifiable:* compute cos similarity between prefill DoM and the row of
   W_unembed for the most-frequent next-token; compute same for final-token DoM.
   If final_DoM aligns with W_unembed rows (cos > 0.3) and prefill_DoM doesn't,
   F-3's orthogonality is the structural consequence of where each direction
   lives — refutable as a non-trivial finding about reasoning circuits.

3. **F-1 (universal dimensional breathing) may be width-driven, not universal.**
   Wu & Papyan find NC properties depend strongly on *width* (more than depth):
   "Scaling dramatically reduces normalized variance, more so by width than
   depth" (§4.1). They also find weight-decay regime (β=0.1 vs β=0.0005)
   substantially modulates NC. F-1 claims universality across "architectures and
   scales" but our 1.5B/7B comparison is two points on a width axis at one
   weight-decay regime — *that's not universality, that's a one-dimensional
   slice*.
   *Mechanism:* if breathing amplitude is width-controlled (matching their NC1
   width sensitivity), then "breathing universality" merely says "deeper/wider
   models breathe more," which is weakly informative.
   *Falsifiable:* if a Qwen-0.5B (smaller width) is added and breathing
   amplitude scales monotonically with d, F-1's "universal" framing collapses
   to "width-correlated."

4. **F-10 (PH at Gaussian null) may be a basis-choice artifact masked by NC2 violation.**
   Wu & Papyan show that average pairwise interference plateaus at ~10⁻³ but
   the *variation* of interference rises with scale (§4.2) — interpretable as
   "structure is concentrated in a few class-pair directions, while most
   directions are isotropic." Standard persistent homology applied to raw
   residual streams will be dominated by the isotropic bulk — exactly the
   Gaussian-null signature F-10 reports. The structure is there, but in a
   subspace.
   *Falsifiable:* project residual streams onto the top-K class-mean basis
   *first* (using their CDNV-based selection of confusable pairs), then run
   Vietoris-Rips PH. If meaningful H_1 structure emerges in the projected
   subspace while the orthogonal complement is Gaussian, F-10's "PH = null"
   conclusion was a sampling-basis artifact.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-1 (universal breathing) ↔ NC1+NC4 scaling.** Their Fig. 1 shows NC
  properties strengthen across 230 CLMs spanning 3.4M–205M params; this
  directly corroborates the *direction* of F-1 (collapse increases with scale)
  while sharpening the mechanism (CDNV-driven, width > depth). Existing graph
  edge `F-1 -[CORROBORATED_BY]-> 2405.17767` (per `prior_admission_note`) is
  consistent.

- **F-4 (asymmetric collapse) ↔ NC1 (CDNV).** Their CDNV permutation-test
  R²=0.192 (p=0.0485) is the closest published quantitative analog of F-4's
  qualitative "collapse depends on correctness." Possible
  `F-4 -[METHOD_DIFFERS]-> 2405.17767` if F-4 survives refutation #1, since
  we partition by correctness rather than by token class.

- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM) ↔ NC4 single-metric
  generalization correlation.** Their NC4 (NCC classifier agreement) hits
  R²=0.49 (p=0.0001), the *strongest* permutation-test correlation in Table 1.
  This corroborates the F-9 claim that a single-layer geometric metric carries
  most of the trajectory-feature signal — the trajectory adds little once a
  good single-layer metric is captured.

- **F-2 (prefill DoM AUROC 0.7731) ↔ NCC classifier framing.** Their NC4
  metric *is* nearest-class-centre classification accuracy. If we re-cast
  prefill DoM as an NCC classifier between "correct" and "incorrect"
  representation classes, AUROC 0.7731 becomes a quantitative anchor that
  the "correctness class means" are well-separated.

- **H-9 (breathing amplitude predicts difficulty) ↔ permutation-test design.**
  Their §3.8 design (fix architecture, vary seed, permutation test 10⁴ trials)
  is the cleanest published template for testing whether a geometric quantity
  predicts performance independent of capacity. Could be borrowed wholesale
  for H-9.

## Methodologies extracted

- **CDNV (class-distance normalized variance) with k=2 power augmentation** —
  ratio of within-class variance to mean-distance² between class-mean pairs,
  with extra `‖μ_c - μ_c'‖²` divisor to downweight well-separated pairs in
  imbalanced settings (eqn 4).
  *Replication cost*: 20min CPU on cached pathway11_h100 NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct test of refutation #1 on F-4.

- **Hyperspherical uniformity via logarithmic inverse distance kernel** —
  -log‖a-b‖₂⁻¹ kernel on pairwise class-mean distances; quantifies GNC2 when
  C > d+1 prevents simplex ETF (eqn 7).
  *Replication cost*: ~1h CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct probe of whether residual streams
  have hidden hyperspherical structure invisible to raw PH (refutation #4).

- **Coefficient-of-variation (CoV) of pairwise interference** — eqn 6, ratio
  of std to mean for pairwise cosine similarities; their "equiangularity"
  measure.
  *Replication cost*: trivial on cached NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives a direct number for "how
  ETF-like is our hidden state" without needing exact ETF.

- **NCC classifier agreement metric** — proportion of test samples where
  argmax linear classifier matches argmin distance-to-class-mean classifier
  (eqn 10).
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU once class means are computed.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives a baseline for whether
  prefill DoM is "just an NCC classifier."

- **Permutation test (10⁴ trials) on fixed architecture, varied seeds** —
  §3.8: train ≥20 instances of the same architecture with different shuffling
  seeds, compute correlations between geometry metrics and validation loss,
  permute pairings to get null distribution.
  *Replication cost*: H100-week (would need ≥20 Qwen-1.5B trains).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — too expensive at our scale; but the
  *design pattern* is reusable for any future small-model verification.

- **Augmented `neural-collapse` PyPI/GitHub package** — generic NC quantities
  (Wu's footnote 3, https://github.com/rhubarbwu/neural-collapse). Drop-in
  measurement tool for CDNV / interference / equinormness on any tensor of
  embeddings + class labels.
  *Replication cost*: 2h to wire into our cached NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — fastest path to running all four
  NC metrics on pathway11_h100 data with consistent definitions.

## Approaches & framings

- **C ≫ d+1 framing.** When classes vastly outnumber embedding dimensions
  (their setting: C=29,233, d ≤ 1024; our Qwen-2.5-1.5B: C=151,936, d=1536),
  the simplex ETF is unreachable but *generalized* hyperspherical uniformity
  GNC2 is. This gives us a principled lens for analyzing per-token activations
  in the residual stream that is consistent with our breathing/PH framings.

- **Self-duality (NC3) vs Uniform-duality (UNC3) dichotomy.** §5.2 — within
  fixed scale, NC3 develops; across scales, it doesn't. Suggests that
  self-duality is a *fine-grained generalization indicator* while uniform
  duality is a *capacity proxy*. Sharp lens for our F-3 prefill⊥final-token
  finding: "orthogonality" might be self-duality at one resolution and
  uniform-duality at another.

- **Generalization-without-scale via permutation tests.** Their §3.8 method
  (fix everything but seed, run permutation test) is a usable design pattern
  for separating *intrinsic* geometric signal from *capacity-correlated*
  geometric signal — a recurring confounder in our pathway 5–11 work.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- 30 architectures × widths {64, 128, 256, 512, 768, 1024} × depths {1, 2, 4,
  8, 12} × epochs {1, 3, 10} × weight-decay β ∈ {0.0005, 0.1}; total ~230 CLMs
  trained.
- GPT Neo backbone (Black et al. 2021), tokenizer subset of 50K, only 29,233
  tokens actually appear in TinyStories.
- Their permutation test: 21 instances of `(d=768, L=2, β=0.0005, 1 epoch)`
  with different seeds; 10⁴ permutations.
- Code: https://github.com/rhubarbwu/linguistic-collapse (paper code) and
  https://github.com/rhubarbwu/neural-collapse (generic NC measurement
  package) — the latter is the import-and-go tool.
- Aggressive weight decay β=0.1 produces stronger NC than β=0.0005 (§5.3) —
  worth checking which regime Qwen-2.5-1.5B was trained under before
  comparing.

## Replicable intermediates

- Run `neural-collapse` package (their footnote-3 GitHub) on
  `pathway11_h100/.../prefill_activations.npz` and `final_activations.npz`,
  partitioned by correctness label (F-2 / F-4 setup). Outputs CDNV,
  interference, equinormness, classifier agreement.
- Compute hyperspherical uniformity (eqn 7) on the same data, two
  partitions: per-correctness-class and per-token-class. Compares whether
  geometric structure is correctness-organized or token-organized.
- Compute cos similarity between final-token L19 DoM and rows of W_unembed
  for the top-K most-frequent answer tokens — single-line cosine on
  cached weights + the existing DoM JSON. Tests refutation #2 directly.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2405.17767 — Linguistic Collapse: Neural Collapse in (Large) Language Models (Wu & Papyan, NeurIPS 2024)

**Relevance:** Trains 230 GPT-Neo CLMs (3.4M–205M params) on TinyStories,
measures all four neural-collapse (NC) properties — within-class variability
collapse (NC1 / CDNV), generalized hyperspherical uniformity (GNC2),
classifier–class-mean alignment (NC3 / UNC3), and NCC classifier agreement
(NC4) — and reports their development with scale and weight regularization.
Their permutation test on 21 fixed-architecture seeds shows NC1, NC2
equiangularity, GNC2, NC3, and NC4 *all* correlate with generalization
*independent of scale* (p < 0.05). Directly relevant to F-1 (universal
collapse), F-4 (asymmetric collapse), F-3 (prefill⊥final orthogonality), and
F-10 (PH at Gaussian null) — provides published, canonical-implementation
metrics that may rebrand each as a special case of generic NC phenomena.
Their `neural-collapse` PyPI package is a drop-in measurement tool for our
cached pathway11_h100 NPZs.

**Key claim we tested:** Neural-collapse properties (NC1, GNC2, NC3, NC4)
develop with model scale in causal LMs and correlate with generalization
*independent of capacity* (permutation-test R² up to 0.49, p ≤ 0.0001).

**Our result:** **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED** for the *direction* of the
scale-correlated collapse (matches F-1 direction). The deeper claims — that
F-4, F-3, F-10 are independent reasoning-specific findings — are **not yet
tested** against their canonical NC metrics; H-7767, H-7768, H-7769 are
explicitly designed to perform that test. After those run, this entry is
upgraded to **REPLICATED + EXTENDED** or downgraded to **CONTRADICTED** for
F-4/F-3 framing, depending on outcome.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE7767, P11-FE7768, P11-FE7769, P11-FE7770;
H-7767, H-7768, H-7769, H-7770
