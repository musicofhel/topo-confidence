# Triage brief — 2509.10625 — No Answer Needed: Predicting LLM Answer Accuracy from Question-Only Linear Probes

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.10625
Triaged: 2026-04-29

Authors: Cencerrado, Padrés Masdemont, Hawthorne, Africa, Pacchiardi (ICLR 2026, submitted Sept 2025).

## Refutations

1. **F-2 may be dataset-internal, not a universal "correctness direction" — refutation by cross-domain DoM transfer.**
   The paper's headline negative result is that a TriviaQA-trained DoM probe drops to AUROC 0.499–0.601 on GSM8K (six 7–70 B models, near-random) while staying high on factual OOD (Cities 0.732–0.880, Notable People 0.680–0.825, Math Operations 0.782–0.858). F-2 (prefill L19 DoM = 0.7731 on MATH-500) is trained AND tested on MATH-500 itself, so an in-distribution-only signal would still produce that AUROC even if no transferable correctness direction exists. **Mechanism:** our DoM may be capturing problem-difficulty surface features in the MATH-500 distribution, not a model-internal correctness anticipation. **Falsifiable test:** train DoM on Qwen-2.5-1.5B prefill activations from TriviaQA (HF, 60 K) at L19, evaluate on cached MATH-500 prefill activations from `pathway11_h100/`. If AUROC < 0.6, F-2 is downgraded from "deeper signal" to "in-distribution probe" and the universality framing in the abstract collapses.

2. **F-3 (prefill ⊥ final-token, cos 0.046) may be Qwen-1.5B / MATH-specific — refutation via paper's saturation finding on larger models.**
   The paper reports the question-only direction's predictive power **saturates** in intermediate layers across Llama-3.1-8B / 70B, Mistral-7B, Ministral-8B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B, Qwen-2.5-7B — i.e. one stable direction usable for the whole rest of the forward pass. F-3's strict orthogonality (cos = 0.046) implies a *rotation* during generation, not a saturation. **Mechanism:** if the same "question-only" direction is both prefill-discriminative and aligned with the final-token correctness direction in 7-B-class models, F-3 is a 1.5-B-specific or RoPE-artifact phenomenon, not a fundamental temporal-decoupling claim. **Falsifiable:** on Qwen-2.5-7B prefill + final-token cached activations from `pathway11_h100/exp1_cross_model/`, compute cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) at the saturating layer. If cos > 0.5, F-3's universality claim breaks.

3. **H-5 (familiarity vs decomposability) is *partially refuted by their split between single-op math and multi-step math*.**
   H-5 frames the prefill signal as "problem familiarity vs decomposability." The paper directly bisects the math case: Math Operations (single-step arithmetic) transfers cleanly at 0.782–0.858, GSM8K (multi-step word problems) collapses at 0.499–0.601. This is the **opposite** of H-5's framing — the predictive boundary is **single-step computation vs multi-step reasoning chains**, not familiarity-of-domain. **Mechanism:** if MATH-500 is mostly multi-step (it is — most problems require ≥3 algebraic steps), then per H-5 we'd expect prefill DoM to fail on MATH-500 — but F-2 says it works at 0.7731. So either (a) their single/multi-step boundary is wrong, (b) MATH-500 prefill DoM is leaking surface features, or (c) Qwen-2.5-1.5B is computing single-step lookups even on multi-step problems. All three are testable. **Falsifiable:** stratify MATH-500 by GPT-4-judged step-count, recompute prefill DoM AUROC per stratum on cached `pathway11_h100/exp1_cross_model/qwen2.5-1.5b/prefill_dom_oof.json`. If AUROC monotonically drops with step count toward 0.5, H-5 is dead and the paper's reframing wins.

4. **F-6 (7B prefill-PR inversion) is inconsistent with the paper's clean monotone scale trend.**
   The paper shows DoM AUROC on TriviaQA scales smoothly: Llama-3.1-8B 0.804 → Llama-3.3-70B 0.826, Qwen-2.5-7B 0.758, Mistral-7B 0.796. No inversion, no "incorrect-group concentration" pathology. F-6 attributes the 7B prefill PR inversion to incorrect-group concentration. **Mechanism:** if PR inversion is real geometry (not DoM-readable), DoM should still scale cleanly with model size — they do see this. But F-6 implicitly predicts that 7B prefill is *worse* at correctness signaling than 1.5B because of the PR inversion. The paper says no — bigger is better. **Falsifiable:** compute prefill DoM AUROC on Qwen-2.5-7B from cached `pathway11_h100/` and compare to Qwen-2.5-1.5B (0.7731). If 7B > 1.5B by ≥0.03, F-6's "PR inversion implies degraded correctness signaling" reading is wrong even though the PR finding is mathematically correct.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731 on Qwen-2.5-1.5B):** The paper's six-model results (Llama-3.1-8B 0.804, Llama-3.3-70B 0.826, Qwen-2.5-7B 0.758, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B 0.735, Mistral-7B 0.796, Ministral-8B 0.734) place our 0.7731 squarely in the cross-model band. Qwen-2.5-7B at 0.758 is the closest comp and is suspiciously close to our 1.5B 0.7731 — suggests scaling within Qwen family is flat. **Direct independent replication of F-2 on TriviaQA scale**, but their paper carries the additional negative result on GSM8K that F-2 does not address.
- **F-8 (selective-prediction acc 71.6% at coverage 0.5, K=2.5 avg):** Their IDK-correlation finding (Section 4.5) — "questions where the model says IDK are at the negative extreme of the correctness direction" — is the *abstention version* of our selective-prediction routing. They give qualitative evidence for the same mechanism F-8 quantifies.
- **F-3 (cos prefill ⊥ final = 0.046):** Paper does not measure this; the saturation result is layer-axis, not token-axis. Their framing assumes one stable direction, which is the null hypothesis for our orthogonality claim.
- **F-7 (D-bucket distinctive collective signature):** Paper does not test per-problem pathology; their probe operates at problem-level binary classification. D-bucket would presumably appear as "high-score-but-incorrect" outliers — not analyzed.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 ≈ single-layer DoM):** Their layer-saturation finding ("predictive power saturates in intermediate layers, deeper layers add little") directly corroborates the spirit of F-9 — late-layer features are redundant with intermediate-layer DoM.
- **H-5 (familiarity vs decomposability):** Refuted in the prior section; replace with a single-step / multi-step decomposition.
- **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes replace supervised DoM for refusal):** Their assessor baseline (LR / XGBoost on text-embedding-3-large, 3 072-d) is a label-free analogue. Underperforms DoM on OOD — suggests H-12 will probably underperform F-2.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Difference-of-means probe with explicit centering** — `w = μ_true − μ_false`; score `s(h) = (h − μ)ᵀw / ‖w‖` where `μ = ½(μ_true + μ_false)`.
  *Replication cost*: <10 min CPU on cached NPZs (we already compute DoM, just need μ-centering check).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — verify our F-2 implementation is using the centered formula; if not, AUROC may shift slightly.
- **Cross-distribution OOD transfer** — train DoM on TriviaQA, test on Cities / Notable People / Math Ops / Medals / GSM8K.
  *Replication cost*: 30 min H100 for new TriviaQA prefill extraction on Qwen-2.5-1.5B, then CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly tests F-2 universality (Refutation #1).
- **3-fold CV layer selection on a held-out set** — they pick the saturating layer per-model rather than fixing L19.
  *Replication cost*: ~20 min CPU using cached per-layer activations from `pathway11_h100/`.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — currently we hard-code L19 from earlier exploration; CV-selecting may shift the layer.
- **Verbalised-confidence baseline** — prompt model to output 0–100% confidence after the answer, calibrate against correctness.
  *Replication cost*: 1 h H100 (re-run K=1 with confidence prompt, parse).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives us a second non-DoM baseline to anchor F-2's "0.7731 vs 0.5" claim.
- **LLM-independent assessor** — train logistic regression / XGBoost on OpenAI text-embedding-3-large (3 072-d) of the question alone.
  *Replication cost*: 30 min CPU + ~$0.10 OpenAI embeddings for 500 MATH-500 questions.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — baseline for "is the prefill activation telling us something the question-text doesn't?"
- **IDK-correlation at probe extremes** — locate IDK responses on the score distribution; check density at negative tail.
  *Replication cost*: <10 min CPU; we'd need to define IDK on MATH-500 (low-confidence tokens / "I cannot solve").
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — MATH-500 doesn't elicit explicit IDK; would have to define a proxy.

## Approaches & framings

- **"In-advance correctness direction"** — recasts the prefill-DoM as a *direction* (single vector, single layer), not a *probe* (parameterised classifier). Aligns with our framing in F-2 but emphasises layer-axis universality (their saturation) over our token-axis decoupling (F-3).
- **Single-step vs multi-step boundary** as the predictive limit. Replaces our "familiarity vs decomposability" intuition (H-5) with a sharp, mechanism-specific boundary that maps cleanly to MATH-500 step-count stratification.
- **Probe direction = confidence direction** (IDK correlates with score extreme). Suggests the prefill DoM and the model's *epistemic state* are the same vector — strong claim, would predict F-8's selective-prediction reflects model self-knowledge, not external statistical regularity.
- **Generic trivia as the canonical training set** for "knowledge-correctness directions." Sets a benchmark our pipeline doesn't currently use; suggests adding TriviaQA as a sanity-check dataset for any new direction we discover.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Activation hook is at the **last token of the question prompt**, before any generation token is sampled. Ours (P11) extracts at the post-question / pre-generation boundary as well — confirm the exact token alignment matches.
- Score formula uses **midpoint centering** `μ = ½(μ_true + μ_false)` before projection. Our DoM may or may not be centered — verify in `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/`.
- They use **AUROC across all thresholds**, no sigmoid, no calibration. Consistent with our F-2 reporting.
- Training set sizes for the probe are **not large** (subsampled subsets per dataset); the paper's small-N stability is a useful precedent for our 500-problem MATH-500.
- Layer selection is **3-fold CV on the training set**, not held-out OOD. Different from our fixed-L19 pick.
- Six models tested: `meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B`, `meta-llama/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct`, `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct`, `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B`, `mistralai/Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3`, `mistralai/Ministral-8B-Instruct-2410`. Code/repo not located in the abstract / HTML pull — to find via the published version.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2509.10625 — No Answer Needed: Predicting LLM Answer Accuracy from Question-Only Linear Probes (Cencerrado, Padrés Masdemont, Hawthorne, Africa, Pacchiardi, 2025)

**Relevance:** Direct independent replication of our prefill-knows-best result (F-2) on six 7–70B models with TriviaQA-trained DoM at AUROC 0.734–0.826, but extends with critical OOD-transfer evidence: the direction transfers across factual domains (Cities, Notable People, single-step Math Ops) but **fails entirely on multi-step math reasoning (GSM8K AUROC 0.499–0.601)**. Reports intermediate-layer saturation across all six models, an IDK/probe-extreme correlation that mirrors our F-8 selective-prediction mechanism, and outperforms verbalised-confidence and OpenAI-embedding-based assessor baselines. The paper threatens F-2's universality claim because F-2 is trained-and-tested in-distribution on MATH-500; the GSM8K-failure pattern would predict that a TriviaQA→MATH-500 cross-domain probe should fail.

**Key claim we tested:** A question-only DoM direction at intermediate layers predicts answer correctness across model families and factual OOD, but does not transfer to multi-step math reasoning. The same direction also captures IDK / abstention behaviour.

**Our result:** **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED + UNTESTED REFUTATION GATE.** F-2's in-distribution AUROC (0.7731 on Qwen-2.5-1.5B / MATH-500) sits within their 0.734–0.826 cross-model band — direct numerical corroboration. F-8's selective-prediction mechanism aligns with their IDK-correlation finding. However, the cross-domain transfer test (TriviaQA → MATH-500) predicted by their GSM8K-failure result is **not yet run**: H-95 / P11-FE110625A is the explicit refutation gate. F-3's strict orthogonality (cos 0.046) is at face-tension with their intermediate-layer saturation framing; H-98 / P11-FE110625D tests whether F-3 is 1.5B-specific.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE110625A (TriviaQA→MATH transfer), P11-FE110625B (step-count stratification), P11-FE110625C (layer saturation), P11-FE110625D (7B/8B prefill-final cosine), H-95, H-96, H-97, H-98.
