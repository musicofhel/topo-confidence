# Triage brief — 2603.24787 — ReLope: KL-Regularized LoRA Probes for Multimodal LLM Routing

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24787
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-2 (prefill L19 DoM is the best simple correctness probe).** ReLope's *baseline* probe — a 5-layer MLP over single-layer hidden states with no LoRA, no attention aggregation — already reaches AUC 83.86 averaged across five multimodal benchmarks on Qwen2.5-VL-7B (95.36 on text-only ScienceQA per their abstract). Our F-2 reports 0.7731 AUROC for prefill L19 DoM (a *linear* logistic probe on mean-pooled L19) on Qwen-2.5-1.5B / MATH-500. **Mechanism:** restricting to a linear projection on a single token at a single layer leaves substantial signal on the table; an MLP over the same prefill activations would refute "DoM is the right probe family." **Falsifiable test:** fit a 5-layer MLP on the cached prefill L19 activations from `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/`; if OOF AUROC > 0.82, F-2 is downgraded from "L19 DoM is the signal" to "L19 contains the signal but DoM under-extracts it."

2. **Refutes F-3 (prefill and final-token DoM are orthogonal, cos = 0.046).** ReLope's central observation is that correctness signal is *distributed across preceding tokens and layers*, not localized — the Attention Probe `ẑ = Σᵢ βᵢ zᵢ⁽ˡ⁻¹⁾` lifts AUC by ~1pt over single-token baseline. **Mechanism:** if the "two orthogonal directions" framing is an artifact of single-position single-layer probing, then aggregating across positions would yield a *single* near-aligned correctness direction whose projection onto either prefill-only or final-only DoM is < 0.5 cos. **Falsifiable test:** train an attention probe over L18–L20 across all prefill positions and across all generation positions on the cached MATH-500 NPZs; compute the cos between the *aggregated* probe weight vectors. If cos > 0.4, F-3's "two orthogonal signals" claim collapses into "one signal that single-token probes alias differently."

3. **Refutes H-12 (semantic-entropy / label-free probes can replace supervised DoM).** ReLope's headline lift over the baseline probe is +4.35 AUC absolute (88.21 vs 83.86 avg) and is *fully supervised* — BCE + KL regularization. The KL term gives a 5.30→1.44 ΔAUC robustness boost under input perturbations. **Mechanism:** if supervised + variational bottleneck gives a distribution-shift advantage label-free methods cannot match, then H-12's framing ("semantic entropy is enough") under-fits. **Falsifiable test:** on the same prefill states, compare OOF AUROC of (a) supervised DoM, (b) supervised ReLope-style LoRA probe, (c) Semantic Entropy Probe (Kossen et al. 2406.15927). If (b) − (c) > 0.05 *and* survives a problem-difficulty distribution shift, H-12 should be parked.

4. **Refutes F-8's coverage curve as the ceiling for selective prediction.** F-8 reports 71.6% accuracy on answered at coverage 0.5 with prefill DoM gating. ReLope's KL-regularized variational bottleneck is exactly the kind of architectural change that drops perturbation-induced AUC degradation by ~3.7× in their setting. **Mechanism:** if the same gain transfers to text correctness signals (i.e., the variational compression preferentially keeps causal signal vs spurious-length / spurious-difficulty correlations), F-8's selective-prediction ceiling rises and the headline 71.6% is suboptimal probe design, not a fundamental limit. **Falsifiable test:** swap the linear probe in `prefill_gated_compute/` for a ReLope-style LoRA + KL probe at L19, re-run the refuse-and-spend curve; if coverage-0.5 acc > 75%, the F-8 curve is updated and the gating story shifts from "L19 DoM is the ceiling" to "L19 *layer* is the ceiling, but extraction matters."

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731):** Direct head-to-head — ReLope is a stronger probe family on the same primitive (hidden-state-conditional correctness prediction). Their text-only baseline of 95.36 on ScienceQA is on a much easier benchmark than MATH-500, but the *family of probe* (5-layer MLP, attention-aggregated, LoRA-regularized) is directly applicable.
- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046):** Their attention-aggregation finding — that signal is distributed across preceding tokens/layers — directly questions whether single-position DoMs are the right unit of analysis.
- **F-8 (selective-prediction 71.6% on answered at coverage 0.5, K=2.5 avg):** ReLope's robustness gains under perturbation (ΔAUC 1.44 vs 5.30) suggest the same KL-regularized variational bottleneck would tighten the F-8 coverage curve.
- **F-10 (PH at Gaussian null on trained residuals):** Tangential — ReLope assumes *separability* of correctness in hidden states (their core complaint is that visual inputs *weaken* this); F-10 says the PH-detectable structure is null. Compatible: ReLope finds linear-decodable signal even when the global topology looks Gaussian.
- **H-12 (label-free probes for refusal):** ReLope's supervised ceiling is the bar H-12 must clear.
- **H-13 (head-level attribution of prefill vs final circuits):** ReLope's attention-probe `βᵢ` weights provide a *token-level* attribution map at almost zero compute cost; this is methodologically adjacent to head-level attribution and could be a cheap pre-screen.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Attention Probe (token-level hidden-state aggregation)** — learnable query vector `q` produces softmax weights `βᵢ` over preceding-layer hidden states; weighted sum becomes probe input.
  *Replication cost:* 30min CPU on cached L18–L20 NPZs (MATH-500 × 500 × 3 layers × 1.5B activations).
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — directly tests F-3's "orthogonal directions" claim and is the cheapest possible probe-architecture upgrade. No retraining needed.

- **ReLope (KL-regularized LoRA probe with variational bottleneck)** — LoRA rank 128, α=256, inserted into a single transformer layer; output mapped to Gaussian posterior `N(μ, σ²)`; trained with BCE + β·KL(N(μ,σ²) ‖ N(0,I)), β∈[0.5, 1.0] optimal.
  *Replication cost:* 4h H100 to train per layer + per model (no full-model retraining; LoRA only).
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — direct probe-family upgrade for F-2/F-8, and the KL term is the right architectural answer to the perturbation-robustness story.

- **5-layer MLP probe head** — used as the classifier on top of aggregated/LoRA features.
  *Replication cost:* trivial, 10min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — minimum-effort upgrade from logistic to nonlinear probe; settles whether F-2's 0.7731 ceiling is linear-probe-bound.

- **Cross-dataset OOD probe evaluation (train on N−1, test on held-out)** — they report ReLope's strongest cross-dataset transfer at 76.66 (MMMU), 83.68 (AOKVQA), etc.
  *Replication cost:* 1h CPU once probes are trained.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **partially** — we have only one MATH-500 split, but we could split by problem difficulty (level 1–5) and run a leave-one-difficulty-out variant of F-2 / F-8, which would also tighten F-7 (D-bucket).

- **Variational bottleneck for distribution-shift robustness** — gauges ΔAUC under controlled perturbations.
  *Replication cost:* depends — for text we don't have an obvious analogue of image noise, but paraphrase / prompt-format jitter is the equivalent.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes, downstream** — useful as the next-after-ReLope-port experiment if H-12 is parked.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Signal is distributed across positions and layers, not localized"** — directly opposes the single-position L19 DoM framing of F-2/F-3. Forces us to revisit whether prefill/final orthogonality is real or aliasing.
- **"Variational bottleneck = robust correctness representation"** — a principled architectural answer to the implicit assumption in F-8 that linear probing is sufficient. Frames probe design as a regularization problem, not a representation-extraction one.
- **"Routing as the downstream task, probe quality as the upstream bottleneck"** — their problem statement (multimodal probes degrade) is structurally identical to ours (Qwen-1.5B prefill DoM saturates at 0.7731). The F-8 selective-prediction setup is a one-model special case of their cascade-routing setup.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- **Probe layer placement:** L24 of 32 for Qwen2.5-VL-7B / Phi-4-MM (≈ layer 0.75 of depth); L40 of 48 for Gemma3-12B (≈ 0.83). Our F-2 uses L19 of 28 (Qwen-2.5-1.5B) ≈ 0.68 — we are slightly shallower than their optimum. Worth checking L21–L23 in cached NPZs.
- **LoRA rank ablation:** 32, 64, 128, 256 — plateau at 128.
- **KL coefficient β ablation:** 0.0, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0 — optimal 0.5–1.0.
- **Optimizer:** AdamW, lr 1e-4, A100 80GB.
- **Probe head:** 5-layer MLP (architecture details not given in fetched excerpt).
- **GitHub code released** per abstract — not yet located; recommend a follow-up fetch before serious replication.
- **Robustness eval:** image noise / JPEG / Gaussian blur (multimodal-only, not directly portable).
