# Triage brief — 2510.01105 — Geometric Properties of Neural Multivariate Regression

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01105
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-4 (asymmetric collapse) under their regression-collapse framework.**
   The paper's central theorem (Sard's argument): if last-layer feature ID_H is
   below target manifold ID_Y, the smooth map `H → Y` cannot be surjective,
   so over-collapsed models *necessarily* underperform. Our F-4 says correct
   MATH-500 trajectories collapse *harder* at the final token (lower PR) and we
   treat this as a correctness signal. If their framework generalizes from MLP
   regression to LLM next-token prediction (whose pre-unembedding step is a
   linear regression onto the vocab logit space), the prediction inverts:
   over-collapsed final-token residuals should *fail*, not succeed.
   *Mechanism*: NRC1 (their PCA-residual collapse metric) computed per-trajectory
   on L19 final-token residuals should be *higher* (less collapse) for correct
   trajectories if their theory governs LLM correctness, opposite to F-4.
   *Falsifiable experiment*: compute NRC1 on cached pathway11_h100 final-token
   L19 NPZs, split by correct/incorrect on Qwen-2.5-1.5B MATH-500, and check
   whether NRC1 anti-correlates or correlates with correctness.

2. **Refutes F-1 (breathing is universal across architectures and scales) as
   a content phenomenon, by reattributing it to weight-decay-style regularization.**
   The paper's Figure 3 + weight-decay sweep (λ_WD ∈ {0, 1e-5 … 3e-3}) shows
   NRC1 and ID_H are knobbed almost entirely by regularization strength: lower
   λ_WD → higher ID_H → less collapse. Our F-1 + F-5 claim breathing is content-
   dependent (not AR-mechanics, not regularization). If their causal chain
   transfers to pretraining-time regularization, breathing magnitude should
   correlate primarily with the model's effective weight-decay history, not
   prompt content. *Mechanism*: an LLM trained with stronger weight decay
   should breathe with smaller amplitude regardless of input difficulty.
   *Falsifiable experiment*: contrast peak-PR amplitude across models with
   documented divergent weight-decay schedules (Qwen-2.5-1.5B λ=0.1 vs Llama-3.2-1B
   λ=0.1 vs Pythia-1.4B λ=0.01) at matched scale and matched prompt set; if
   amplitude tracks λ rather than scale/family, F-1's "universal across
   architectures" framing weakens.

3. **Refutes H-9 (breathing amplitude predicts problem difficulty) — wrong
   covariate.** The paper's "two regimes" carve-up shows that test-MSE vs
   ID_H is U-shaped under *low-data/noisy* regimes and monotonic under
   *high-data/low-noise* — i.e. optimal ID is set by per-task conditional
   noise, not by anything resembling task difficulty per se. Translated to
   LLMs: per-problem breathing amplitude should track per-problem answer
   entropy (a noise proxy: K=8 majority dispersion) rather than annotated
   MATH-500 difficulty levels. *Mechanism*: H-9 implicitly assumes the
   noise-difficulty-amplitude axis is monotonic; their framework predicts
   non-monotonic structure controlled by an orthogonal axis.
   *Falsifiable experiment*: regress per-problem breathing amplitude on
   (a) MATH-500 difficulty 1–5, (b) K=8 majority entropy, (c) generation
   length. If (b) dominates and (a) partials out, H-9's framing is wrong.

4. **Refutes F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM).** The paper's
   layer-wise ID-tracking result (Figure 5) distinguishes collapsed from
   non-collapsed models by the *shape* of the per-layer ID curve (monotonic
   decay vs non-monotonic). If this distinction transfers, then the per-layer
   ID *trajectory* across all 28 Qwen-2.5-1.5B layers should carry signal
   beyond what any single-layer DoM at L19 captures. F-9 explicitly claims
   CoE-60 (per-layer trajectory features) is redundant with L19 alone.
   *Mechanism*: per-layer TwoNN-ID is a different functional of the
   trajectory than CoE's mean-cosine summary, so F-9's redundancy claim
   does not automatically extend.
   *Falsifiable experiment*: compute the 28-dim per-layer TwoNN-ID vector
   per problem, fit a logistic on it for correctness, and compare OOF AUROC
   to single-layer L19 DoM (0.7731). If the layer-ID-vector feature
   contributes >0.02 AUROC over L19 DoM alone, F-9 is too strong.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-4 (asymmetric collapse, AUROC contribution from final-token PR direction):**
  The paper provides a clean alternative collapse metric (NRC1) and a
  theoretical reason (Sard) why over-collapse should *hurt*, not help. F-4's
  observation that correct trajectories collapse *more* runs against the
  paper's prediction; the headline cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046 means
  the final-token signal is structurally distinct from prefill, so a NRC1-style
  test bears specifically on F-4, not on F-2 (prefill DoM AUROC 0.7731).
- **F-1 (breathing universal across architectures and scales):** The paper's
  weight-decay knobbing of ID directly competes with the universality claim.
  Selective-prediction acc 71.6% at coverage 0.5 sits on top of an assumed
  content-driven breathing mechanism — if breathing is regularization-driven
  rather than content-driven, the *interpretation* of F-8 changes even though
  the predictive signal remains.
- **F-7 (D-bucket has distinctive collective geometry):** Their "ID_H < ID_Y
  → over-compression" framework offers a *mechanism* for D-bucket: D-bucket
  problems may be those where the model's L19 features collapse below the
  effective answer-space dimensionality. Could *extend* F-7 rather than refute.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM):** See Refutation 4.
  Their layer-wise ID-trajectory framing offers a different per-layer functional
  than CoE.
- **F-10 (PH on trained residuals at Gaussian null):** Mostly orthogonal —
  TwoNN measures local manifold dimension, PH measures topological invariants;
  finding ID structure that PH missed would *not* refute F-10 (which is
  specifically about PH features being null).
- **H-16 (Marchenko-Pastur correction preserves breathing):** This paper offers
  TwoNN as a fundamentally different ID estimator (nearest-neighbor based, not
  eigenvalue based). H-16 tests robustness to bias correction within
  PR/eigenvalue family; this paper opens an across-family robustness check.
- **H-9 (breathing amplitude predicts difficulty):** See Refutation 3.

## Methodologies extracted

- **TwoNN (Two-Nearest-Neighbor) intrinsic dimension estimator.** For each
  point, take r₁, r₂ to first and second NN; ratio μ = r₂/r₁ follows Pareto
  CDF F(μ) = 1 − μ^(−d) under local uniformity; estimate d by linear
  regression of log-transformed pairs through the origin. Stable for ID < ~20
  with modest n; scale-aware via subsampling.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached pathway11_h100 NPZs — small
  Python call, no extra dependencies.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — cleanest cross-check on participation-ratio
  breathing using a non-eigenvalue-based estimator; complements H-16
  (Marchenko-Pastur) along the orthogonal "estimator family" axis.

- **NRC1 metric (Neural Regression Collapse residual).** With normalized
  feature vectors h̃_i and the d×n matrix H_PCA of top-n principal components
  (n = target dim), NRC1 = (1/M) Σ_i ‖h̃_i − proj(h̃_i | H_PCA)‖². Small NRC1
  ⇒ features collapse to an n-dim subspace.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU on cached MATH-500 L19 NPZs (need to pick what
  plays the role of "n = target dim" — vocab embedding rank? answer-token
  basis?).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — provides a per-trajectory collapse measure
  to test the F-4 NRC1-vs-correctness direction (Refutation 1).

- **Layer-wise ID profile as a model-state diagnostic** (their Figure 5).
  Track ID across all hidden layers; monotonic decay = healthy/non-collapsed,
  non-monotonic = collapsed. Their use is qualitative; we'd use it as a
  feature vector.
  *Replication cost*: 2h CPU — already have per-layer L0–L27 activations
  cached for the MATH-500 pathway11 sweep.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly tests F-9 redundancy (Refutation 4).

- **Weight-decay-as-ID-knob protocol.** Sweep λ_WD ∈ {0, 1e-5, 1e-4, 3e-4,
  5e-4, 7e-4, 1e-3, 3e-3} during training; track NRC1 + ID_H evolution;
  identifies regime boundary.
  *Replication cost*: requires retraining — H100 days for any meaningful LLM
  scale; not feasible at the 1.5B+ scale we operate.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — far outside our compute envelope; flag as
  literature-only.

- **Sard-theorem framing for over-compression bound.** Theorem 1: smooth map
  from m-manifold to n-manifold with m < n cannot be surjective. Imports
  cleanly to LLM next-token prediction if one frames the unembedding linear
  layer as a regression onto vocab logits.
  *Replication cost*: derivation only.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: this is a framing tool, not an experiment —
  worth citing in PROJECT_RECORD §1f if Refutation 1 fires.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Two regimes" framing — over-compressed (ID_H < ID_Y) vs under-compressed
  (ID_H > ID_Y).** Reframes feature dimensionality as a *bidirectional*
  knob rather than a "more-is-better" or "compression-is-better" axis. For
  our LLM work, this re-frames asymmetric collapse (F-4) into a question
  about which side of an effective regression-collapse threshold the
  per-trajectory L19 representation sits on. Sharper than the current
  PR-amplitude-as-confidence story.

- **Content-vs-regularization causal axis.** The paper *operationalizes*
  the regularization-driven account of dimensional structure (weight decay
  → ID_H → collapse). Our F-5 (content-dependent) implicitly rejects this;
  the paper provides a concrete experimental knob to challenge that rejection.

- **Manifold-topological lower bound on regression error.** Sard-based
  unavoidable-error argument is a different shape of theoretical claim
  than the AUROC/PR-based empirical claims dominating our findings, and
  its import is to predict *unsolvable* over-compressed regimes — relevant
  for D-bucket interpretation under F-7.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- MLP grid: depth ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4, 5}, width ∈ {64, 128, 256, 512, 1024}, ReLU.
- Optimizer: Adam, lr 1e-3 to 5e-3.
- Weight-decay sweep (the load-bearing one): λ_WD ∈ {0, 1e-5, 1e-4, 3e-4,
  5e-4, 7e-4, 1e-3, 3e-3, …}.
- TwoNN linear regression is fitted *through the origin* on log-transformed
  (log μ, −log(1 − F_emp(μ))) pairs — easy to mis-implement.
- NRC1 uses *normalized* h̃_i (unit norm) — without normalization, scale
  contaminates the residual.
- Layer-wise ID is computed at every hidden layer with the same TwoNN
  protocol (their Figure 5 panel).

## Proposed HYPOTHESES.md additions

### H-71: NRC1 inverts the F-4 asymmetric-collapse direction
**Priority:** HIGH
**Motivated by:** 2510.01105 + F-4
**Test:** Compute NRC1 (PCA-residual collapse metric, normalized features) on
cached L19 final-token residuals from pathway11_h100 MATH-500 sweep
(Qwen-2.5-1.5B). Define target subspace as top-n PCA of correct-only
answer-token activations. Per-problem NRC1, then 2-sample test on
NRC1(correct) vs NRC1(incorrect). Wall-clock ~1h CPU.
**Requires:** existing pathway11_h100 final-token L19 NPZs (no fresh data).
**Would change:** If NRC1 is *higher* (less collapse) for correct trajectories,
F-4's "collapse-as-correctness" framing aligns with 2510.01105's regression-
collapse-is-bad theory and F-4 needs re-interpretation as a regression
phenomenon not a confidence-collapse phenomenon. If NRC1 anti-correlates with
correctness, F-4 survives the import and the paper's framework does not
generalize cleanly to next-token prediction.
**Blocks:** nothing — independent test.

### H-72: Per-layer TwoNN-ID profile carries predictive signal beyond L19 DoM
**Priority:** MEDIUM
**Motivated by:** 2510.01105 + F-9
**Test:** For each MATH-500 problem on Qwen-2.5-1.5B, compute TwoNN-ID at
L0..L27 → 28-dim feature vector. Fit OOF 5-fold logistic on correctness,

**Requires:** cached per-layer activations from pathway11_h100 (already on disk).
**Would change:** If the layer-ID profile lifts AUROC by >0.02 over L19 DoM

no lift, F-9 strengthens against this orthogonal feature family.
**Blocks:** nothing.

### H-73: Breathing amplitude tracks per-problem K=8 majority entropy more than annotated difficulty
**Priority:** LOW
**Motivated by:** 2510.01105 + H-9

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2510.01105 — Geometric Properties of Neural Multivariate Regression (Andriopoulos, Dong, Adhikari, Ross, 2025)

**Relevance:** Provides a manifold-geometric framework (intrinsic dimension
ID_H of features vs ID_Y of targets) that *predicts the opposite direction*
of our F-4 asymmetric-collapse finding when imported to LLM next-token
prediction (Sard-theorem-based unavoidable-error argument). Also supplies
TwoNN, an across-family alternative to participation-ratio that complements
H-16's within-family Marchenko-Pastur correction, and NRC1, a per-trajectory
PCA-residual collapse metric that gives us a clean diagnostic for the F-4
direction question. No language/transformer experiments in the paper itself
— the methodologies (TwoNN, NRC1, layer-wise ID profile) are what transfer.

**Key claim we tested:** "Collapsed models exhibit ID_H < ID_Y, leading to
over-compression and poor generalization." Imported to our setting: more-
collapsed (lower-ID) final-token residuals should produce *worse* outputs,
not better — the opposite of F-4.

**Our result:** **TO TEST.** Both F-4 inversion test (H-71 / FE-72) and per-
layer ID profile test (H-72 / FE-73) are READY on cached NPZs and pending.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE71 (TwoNN breathing reproduction), P11-FE72
(NRC1 vs F-4 direction), P11-FE73 (per-layer ID profile vs F-9), H-71, H-72,
H-73.

**Status:** TO TEST
