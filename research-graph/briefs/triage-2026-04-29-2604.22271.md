# Triage brief — 2604.22271 — How LLMs Detect and Correct Their Own Errors: Internal Confidence Signals

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22271
Triaged: 2026-04-29

Authors: Kumaran, Patraucean, Osindero, Velickovic, Daw (2026).
Models: Gemma 3 27B (62L, d=5376), Qwen 2.5 7B (28L, d=3584).
Datasets: TriviaQA (n=7227 Gemma, 3500 Qwen), MNLI neutral (n=3395), foil set (n=1929), causal subset (n=1000).
Headline AUROCs:
- Verification (whether model will say its own answer is correct): logprob 0.668, verbal-conf 0.832, behavioral-combined 0.908, **PANL L30 = 0.986**.
- A2-correctness on incorrect-changed trials (the "knows it can fix it" claim): behavioral 0.475 (below chance), verbal-conf 0.524, verification-Δlogp 0.531, **PANL = 0.614** (Gemma); **PANL = 0.774** (Qwen).
- MNLI neutral correctability: PANL 0.862, baseline ≈ chance.
- Foil correctability: PANL 0.813–0.858.
- Accuracy: A1 75.5% → A2 79.2% on TriviaQA Gemma (+3.7 pp, McNemar χ²=202.3).
Cosines / correlations: PANL probe r=0.91 with verification logprob, r=0.21 with answer logprob (full), r=0.02 within incorrect, **cos(verification probe, P(IK) probe) = +0.007 at L30**.
Causal: clean d′=1.17, corrupted d′=0.09; PANL alone at L30 restores d′=0.89 (~74% recovery); LAT necessary early-to-mid; PANL not strictly necessary (redundancy with LAT).

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-2 framing ("prefill L19 DoM is the strongest correctness signal, better than final-token")** — the paper finds a *third* token position (PANL = post-answer-newline) that beats both prefill-style and final-token signals on its own task (verification AUROC 0.986 vs logprob 0.668), and beats behavioral baselines on correctability within incorrect (0.614 vs 0.475). Mechanism: PANL is a *cached evaluative slot* the model writes after committing the answer, which is structurally absent from our prefill (pre-generation) and our "final-token" (last CoT token) probes. Falsifiable experiment: on cached pathway11_h100 1.5B 1024-tok residuals, locate the post-answer-newline token (the `\n` after the boxed answer / numeric answer in MATH-500 generations), train an L₂-logistic probe on its L19 (and full layer sweep) residuals against MATH-500 K=1 correctness, compare to the canonical prefill L19 DoM AUROC = 0.7731. If PANL-equivalent ≥ 0.85, F-2's "prefill is the headline correctness signal" is superseded.

2. **Refutes F-3 interpretation ("prefill DoM and final-token DoM are orthogonal because they encode two distinct confidence factors", cos=0.046)** — the paper reframes orthogonality as *generative vs evaluative* (PANL r=0.02 with answer logprob within incorrect). Under that frame, our prefill direction is *neither* — it is pre-generation, so it cannot be the "generative" signal, but it also cannot be the post-answer "evaluative" signal because no answer has been committed. The cos=0.046 may therefore reflect prefill ⊥ generation rather than two-evaluators. Mechanism: if we run a verify-then-correct prompt on the same 500 MATH problems and extract a verification-PANL DoM, an *evaluative* prefill direction should correlate strongly (|r| > 0.5) with verification-PANL DoM; a *problem-difficulty* prefill direction will not. Falsifiable experiment: replicate on Qwen 1.5B with verify-then-correct prompt, train PANL probe, compute cosine and Pearson correlation between prefill-DoM scores and PANL-DoM scores per problem. If |corr| < 0.2, F-3's "two confidence directions" reading is wrong and prefill DoM is a *difficulty/familiarity* axis (consistent with H-5).

3. **Refutes F-8 (selective prediction at 50% coverage = 71.6% on answered as the headline pipeline)** — the paper offers a strictly dominant alternative: instead of refusing 50% of problems, route all problems through verify-then-correct (TriviaQA: A1 75.5% → A2 79.2%, +3.7 pp at full coverage). If a parallel verify-then-correct on Qwen 1.5B MATH-500 1024-tok also yields a positive net-accuracy delta at matched compute, then F-8's framing as a *selective* (refusal-based) pipeline becomes the suboptimal choice — the same probe can be redeployed as a *correction-routing* gate covering 100% of problems. Mechanism: PANL-style routing converts the wrong-and-fixable subset (B-bucket / D-bucket recovery) into accuracy gains rather than abstentions. Falsifiable experiment: implement the three-phase paradigm on cached P11 Stage 2 outputs (already have CoT generations for the 500 MATH problems), score net accuracy at matched K-budget against (a) F-8 selective prediction, (b) pure C_infer K=2.5. If verify-then-correct ≥ selective-pred at matched compute, F-8 is downgraded.

4. **Refutes F-7's "uniqueness-to-self-generation" framing** — the paper's foil experiment shows PANL correctability AUROC 0.813–0.858 on *non-self-generated* answers (synthetic hard/easy/unrelated foils). This implies the "knows the answer is wrong AND can fix it" signal — which is precisely D-bucket geometry (K=1 right, K=8 wrong: pathological recovery dynamics, F-7) — is a property of question-answer-fit evaluation in general, not of self-generation dynamics. Mechanism: if we paste *another model's* incorrect answer into Qwen 1.5B and extract PANL, we should still see D-bucket-like geometric collective behavior. Falsifiable experiment: pick 100 problems where 7B answers wrongly and 1.5B answers wrongly differently, paste 7B's wrong answer into 1.5B's verification prompt, extract residual streams; if the 1.5B "foil-PANL" geometry collectively matches D-bucket signature, F-7's "collective signature unique to generation dynamics" framing must broaden to "collective signature of question-answer-fit evaluation."

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (prefill L19 AUROC 0.7731)** — the paper's PANL is a *post-answer* analog. PANL on TriviaQA Gemma achieves 0.986 verification-AUROC; on Qwen 7B it achieves 0.774 A2-correctness within incorrect — both well above our 0.7731 prefill number, but on different tasks. Direct connection: both findings establish that *single-layer linear residual-stream probes* are sufficient for strong correctness prediction without architectural changes; they disagree on which token position carries the maximum signal.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Single-layer L₂-logistic probe on z-scored residual stream activations** at specific token positions (PANL, LAT, prompt-last). C=0.001, 5-fold stratified CV, returns OOF predicted probability per trial.
  *Replication cost*: 20 min CPU on cached NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — drop-in on pathway11_h100 cached residuals; this is essentially what we already do for prefill DoM, just at a different token position.

- **Verify-then-correct three-phase paradigm** (Phase 0 generate + verbal confidence; Phase 1 binary Y/N verification with explicit "single letter only"; Phase 2 self-correction conditional on Phase 1).
  *Replication cost*: 1 H100 day (need fresh generations on Qwen 1.5B/7B with the Phase 1 + 2 prompts).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct test of H-19 / P11-FE19, and the Phase-1 prompt gives us the PANL token trivially.

- **Activation patching with mean-ablation corruption + single position-layer restoration**, scored via signal-detection-theory d′ rather than accuracy.
  *Replication cost*: 1 H100 day.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes for D-bucket validation (P11-FE3 is already similar), high-information experiment because it cleanly separates causal sufficiency vs necessity.

- **Mean-ablation necessity test** (replace activation with calibration-set mean rather than zero; uses 200-trial balanced calibration over SDT cells).
  *Replication cost*: 0.5 H100 day if patching infra exists.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — discriminates "this representation matters here" from "this representation is redundant with another."

- **Surface-feature TF-IDF baseline** (100 each for question/answer + token lengths) as control for shallow-statistics confound.
  *Replication cost*: 30 min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — cheap and addresses an obvious refutation channel for any probe AUROC we report.

- **Foil verification experiment** — verify non-self-generated answers (hard, easy, unrelated foils per question).
  *Replication cost*: 1 H100 day for generation, fast probe training.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly tests whether D-bucket signature is self-generation-specific or general (Refutation #4 above).

- **GPT-4o judge for free-form answer scoring** with reported agreement rates against exact-match (93.2% A1, 83.8% A2).
  *Replication cost*: ~$ for API.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: only if/when we leave MATH-500 (which has unambiguous boxed-numeric answers; we don't need a judge).

- **Signal-detection-theory partition** (hits / misses / correct rejections / false alarms) and d′ as the headline correctness-detection metric.
  *Replication cost*: trivial.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — better headline metric for verify-then-correct than raw accuracy because it factors out base rate.

## Approaches & framings

- **Second-order vs first-order confidence (decision neuroscience).** Reframes our prefill / final-token / PANL story as: generation signal (logprobs) is structurally first-order and saturates at the chosen response, so error detection requires a *partially independent* evaluative signal. Intersects F-2 / F-3: prefill DoM and final-token DoM may not both be "confidence" — one of them is plausibly first-order (generative) and the other second-order (evaluative).

- **"Cached evaluative slot" model of PANL.** The model writes a representation immediately after committing the answer that is read back during downstream verification. Framing intersects H-13 (head-level circuits): predicts a specific cache-write circuit at LAT-to-PANL handoff.

- **Causal sufficiency ≠ causal necessity, decomposed via patching + ablation.** Their finding that PANL is sufficient at L30 but *not* necessary (LAT carries redundant info) is a methodological template for any future causal claim we make about prefill or D-bucket.

- **"Knows it can fix it" partition.** Operational distinction between (a) detect error and fix, (b) detect error and fail to fix, (c) miss error. Intersects F-7 / D-bucket: this is exactly the partition that defines fixable-incorrect, and they have a 0.614–0.774 AUROC probe for it.

- **Foil-as-control.** Synthetic non-self-generated answers as a control for whether evaluative signals are generation-coupled — directly applicable as a refutation lever for F-7 uniqueness claims.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- HuggingFace Transformers, greedy decoding (temperature=0).
- L₂-logistic probe: C=0.001, 5-fold stratified CV, z-scored residual stream activations.
- Mean-ablation calibration: 100 trials for corrupt-and-restore, 200 trials balanced (50/SDT-cell) for ablation.
- Patching is applied *after* the MLP block at each layer.
- Surface-feature control: 100 question + 100 answer TF-IDF dims + token lengths → 0.564 verification-AUROC ceiling for shallow baseline.
- Phase-1 prompt structure (verbatim):
  ```
  Question: {question}
  Your answer: {model_answer}
  ⟨NL⟩      ← PANL position, residual stream extracted here
  Verify your answer. Correct? (Output ONLY a single letter, Y/N):
  ```
  PANL is the activation at the trailing newline *before* the verification instruction — not the verification token itself.
- d′ used as causal metric (clean=1.17, corrupted=0.09); avoid raw accuracy for low-base-rate analyses.

## 2604.22271 — How LLMs Detect and Correct Their Own Errors: Internal Confidence Signals (Kumaran, Patraucean, Osindero, Velickovic, Daw, 2026)

**Relevance:** Identifies a *post-answer-newline* (PANL) residual-stream signal that is causally sufficient for error detection on Gemma 3 27B (verification AUROC 0.986 vs answer-logprob 0.668; A2-correctness within incorrect 0.614 vs behavioral baseline 0.475 below chance) and replicates on Qwen 2.5 7B (PANL A2-correctness 0.774). Critical for our pipeline because PANL is a *post-answer evaluative* analog of our *pre-answer* prefill DoM — and the paper's framing (second-order confidence: evaluative signal partially independent of generative signal) directly challenges our F-2 ("prefill is the headline") and F-3 ("two confidence directions") interpretations. The cos = +0.007 between their verification probe and Kadavath's P(IK) probe is structurally identical to our cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046 — both suggest *generative ⊥ evaluative*, but our prefill is neither.

**Key claim we tested:** A single residual-stream probe at one specific token position predicts correctness, predicts error correctability, and is causally sufficient when answer information is corrupted. Verify-then-correct (3-phase) yields +3.7 pp accuracy over single-pass (TriviaQA Gemma 75.5% → 79.2%, McNemar χ²=202.3).
