# Triage brief — 2605.14685 — Spontaneous symmetry breaking and Goldstone modes for deep information propagation

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14685
Triaged: 2026-05-17

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-1 (Dimensional breathing is universal across transformer architectures and scales).**
   The paper demonstrates that coherent signal propagation through depth is controlled by *symmetry-breaking phase structure* (order parameter c^l), not by any universal geometric property of activations. If breathing is merely an artifact of being near the edge-of-chaos critical point (which their framework subsumes as the σ_W = 1 boundary), then F-1's "universality" claim reduces to a trivial consequence of initialization-regime commonality rather than a deep architectural invariant. **Falsifiable by:** Measure whether the L19 breathing signature (PR expansion/collapse) correlates with the order parameter c^l = (1/N)E[|ϕ(z)|²] across layers. If breathing tracks phase-transition proximity rather than a distinct geometric signal, F-1 is weakened.

2. **Refutes F-3 (Prefill and final-token DoM directions are orthogonal).**
   The Goldstone mode framework predicts that the *phase* φ^l of inter-input correlations is exactly conserved across depth (Eq. 11: φ^(l+1) = φ^l), while *magnitude* Δ^l decays exponentially. If DoM is essentially a "protected Jacobian component" (their Eq. 7), the prefill-vs-final orthogonality (cos = 0.046) could be explained by the decaying Δ^l rather than fundamentally different directions — the direction remains approximately the same but its observable magnitude vanishes at final-token depth, leaving only noise. **Falsifiable by:** Compute the protected Jacobian component d^L (their Eq. 7 analogue) for L19 vs final-token in Qwen-2.5-1.5B. If the "protected" component is nearly parallel across layers while the total Jacobian is not, F-3's orthogonality finding is reframed as magnitude decay rather than directional rotation.

3. **Refutes F-10 (PH adds no signal beyond Gaussian covariance null).**
   The paper identifies topological defects (vortices, domain walls) in the SSB phase that persist stably across depth (Section 4, Appendix D.3). These are *non-Gaussian* structures that PH would detect — specifically, the paper shows 2D convolutional RNNs with U(1) symmetry develop long-lived vortices. If analogous topological defects exist in transformer residual streams (which have approximate permutation equivariance), then F-10's null result may stem from using V-R persistent homology on the wrong topological invariant (H_0/H_1 point clouds rather than field-theoretic defect detection on the phase structure). **Falsifiable by:** Decompose L19 prefill activations into radial (magnitude) and angular (phase) components under a suitable group action. Run PH on the phase field specifically. If non-trivial persistence appears in the phase field but not in the raw point cloud, F-10 is refuted as an instrument-specificity artifact rather than a genuine null.

4. **Refutes H-4 (Breathing ↔ Sharpness Dimension / EoS correspond formally).**
   The paper explicitly separates the SSB phase from the edge-of-chaos (EoC): "These modes are outside the conventional 'edge of chaos' paradigm for deep neural networks, which assume no extra structure." If breathing is a Goldstone-mode phenomenon rather than an EoC phenomenon, then the hypothesized formal correspondence between breathing and sharpness dimension (H-4) is wrong — they would be orthogonal mechanisms. **Falsifiable by:** Check whether the Jacobian's protected component (SSB signature) and the sharpness dimension measure correlate or are independent across our cached L19 activations.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-1 (Dimensional breathing):** The paper's order parameter c^l (Eq. 6) — the mean squared activation magnitude per layer — is precisely a layer-wise "breathing" metric. Their Fig. 2 shows c^l transitioning from 0 (collapsed) to finite O(1) values across the phase boundary. This is structurally analogous to our participation-ratio breathing across layers, though defined on magnitude rather than effective dimensionality. Anchor: our AUROC 0.7731 DoM probe operates on what could be the "protected Jacobian direction" in their framework.

- **F-2 (Single L19 prefill direction predicts correctness at AUROC 0.7731):** Their Eq. 7 proves that in the SSB phase, a specific component of the input-output Jacobian remains O(1) regardless of depth — it is "protected" by symmetry. The DoM direction (≈PC1, cos = 0.9216) could be such a protected direction, where the correctness signal lives in the Goldstone degree of freedom that carries information coherently. The question is whether L19 is the "symmetry-breaking layer" in the Qwen architecture.

- **F-4 (Asymmetric collapse — correct harder than incorrect):** In the SSB framework, collapse to the origin (c^l → 0) means symmetry is *unbroken* and information is lost. Correct problems collapsing "harder" (lower PR at final token) could mean correct trajectories enter the SSB phase more deeply — they commit to a specific vacuum on the ring of minima, which destroys information diversity but preserves the phase signal.

- **H-783 (L19 as phase-transition layer):** The admission note flags this directly. The paper provides the theoretical scaffold: L19 could be where σ_W crosses 1 (the critical point) in Qwen's architecture, creating the SSB phase that enables coherent signal propagation to later layers.

  *Replication cost*: 2-4h H100 (requires computing Jacobians through the model).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no for now — requires fresh forward passes with Jacobian computation, expensive.

  *Replication cost*: 15min CPU (compute pairwise complex correlations on cached 500×1536 activations, treating them as complex vectors).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly applicable to our 500 problems' cached activations at L19.

  *Replication cost*: N/A (architectural, not analytical).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — this is a training-time architectural choice, not applicable to pretrained Qwen.

  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU (compute effective rank of activation matrices at each of 28 layers from cached multi-layer extractions, if available).

## Approaches & framings

- **SSB phase diagram as alternative to edge-of-chaos:** Instead of asking "is the network at the edge of chaos?", ask "is the symmetry spontaneously broken?" This reframes breathing as a phase-transition phenomenon rather than a criticality phenomenon. Intersects H-4's hypothesized breathing ↔ sharpness correspondence by suggesting they may be independent phenomena.

- **Goldstone mode = protected information channel:** Information propagates stably because symmetry protects certain degrees of freedom from decay. This reframes our "DoM direction carries correctness signal" (F-2) as "the DoM direction is a Goldstone mode that the architecture protects from gradient collapse."

- **Magnitude decay with phase conservation (Δ decays, φ exact):** A specific prediction about what decays vs. what is preserved across layers. Directly testable against our layer-wise data: does the *direction* of the correctness signal rotate (as F-3 suggests) or does it stay constant while its *amplitude* decays?
