# Triage brief — 2605.13612 — Deep Learning as Neural Low-Degree Filtering: A Spectral Theory of Hierarchical Feature Learning

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13612
Triaged: 2026-05-18

## Refutations

**R1 — Refutes F-2 (single L19 direction predicts correctness) and the cov-spectrum ceiling (0.7928).**
Neural LoFi's core operator is the *label-weighted moment matrix* C^(ℓ) = (1/n) Σ yᵢ zᵢ zᵢᵀ, not the unsupervised covariance. Our DoM direction is the first-order component of this operator (label-weighted mean û = (1/n) Σ yᵢ zᵢ), and our cov-spectrum probe uses eigenvectors of the *unsupervised* covariance. The paper proves (Theorem 1, parts i–ii) that the second-order eigenvectors of C^(ℓ) capture additional signal orthogonal to the first-order mean — features invisible to both DoM and unsupervised PCA. If the label-weighted moment eigenvectors at L19 yield AUROC > 0.7928, F-2's "single direction" framing is incomplete and the cov-spectrum probe was approximating the wrong operator.
*Mechanism*: Compute C^(ℓ) on cached L19 prefill activations (500 × 1536, binary correctness labels), extract top-k eigenvectors, fit logistic regression on projections. Compare AUROC to 0.7731 (DoM) and 0.7928 (cov-spectrum).
*Falsifiable*: Label-weighted moment top-k probe AUROC significantly exceeds 0.7928.

**R2 — Refutes F-9 (CoE-60 trajectory features are redundant with single-layer L19 DoM).**
Neural LoFi's low-degree compositionality principle (Section 3.2) predicts that each layer extracts compositionally new features invisible to previous layers. CoE-60 aggregates 60 layers via trajectory statistics (norms, participation ratios) — this is NOT the spectral decomposition the paper prescribes. If layer-wise label-weighted moment leading eigenvectors across layers 1–28 are compositionally distinct, the 28-dimensional ensemble should beat L19 DoM alone. F-9's redundancy conclusion would then be an artifact of CoE's aggregation method masking per-layer compositional structure, not evidence that multi-layer information is truly redundant.
*Mechanism*: Compute C^(ℓ) at each layer (requires per-layer cached activations — if only L19 is cached, this is Tier 2), extract leading eigenvector per layer, concatenate, fit classifier.
*Falsifiable*: Per-layer label-weighted moment ensemble AUROC > 0.7731.

**R3 — Refutes the interpretation behind F-10 (PH null = topology is uninformative).**
The emergence criterion (Equations 17–18) predicts that features become detectable only when the spectral gap of C^(ℓ) exceeds a sample-complexity threshold. F-10 tested PH on *raw* L19 activations and found them indistinguishable from a Gaussian null. But Neural LoFi says the signal lives in the *label-weighted* spectral directions, not in the raw point-cloud geometry. Projecting activations onto the top-k eigenvectors of C^(ℓ) concentrates the data into the "signal subspace" — PH on this projected cloud could reveal non-trivial topology that F-10 missed. This wouldn't mean PH is generally useful (the projection is supervised), but it would refute F-10's blanket conclusion that "topology summary statistics on residual streams are not distinguishable from a Gaussian null" — they could be distinguishable once projected onto the right subspace.
*Mechanism*: Project L19 prefill activations onto top-k eigenvectors of C^(ℓ), compute Vietoris-Rips PH, two-sample test vs Gaussian null at matched mean + covariance.
*Falsifiable*: Projected PH features reject the Gaussian null (p < 0.01) where raw PH did not.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (AUROC 0.7731)**: The paper's label-weighted moment operator directly subsumes our DoM computation. DoM is the first-order component û^ℓ; the paper proves the second-order eigenvectors carry additional signal (Theorem 1 part ii). The gap between DoM (0.7731) and the full 1536-d ceiling (0.7847) could be precisely the signal living in these second-order directions.

- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundancy)**: Neural LoFi's compositionality principle predicts per-layer features should be distinct if the target function has hierarchical structure. The CoE-60/DoM redundancy might indicate MATH-500 correctness is a *shallow* (low-degree) function of activations rather than a hierarchical one — which would mean depth doesn't help for this specific task, consistent with L19 being sufficient.

- **F-10 (PH null)**: The paper's framework is entirely spectral/algebraic with no role for topological invariants. This provides independent theoretical support for the PH null: the "right" tool for analyzing learned representations is spectral decomposition of label-conditioned moments, not persistent homology of point clouds. The effective-dimension criterion (Definition 1) could explain WHY the null holds — if the signal subspace has low effective dimension, the point cloud is effectively low-rank and appears Gaussian to PH.

- **cos(DoM, PC1) = 0.9216**: Under Neural LoFi, the first-order component (label-weighted mean) and the first second-order component (leading eigenvector of C^(ℓ)) are generically different. The near-alignment DoM ≈ PC1 would mean the label signal has leaked into the dominant variance direction of the unsupervised representation — expected if the network has been trained to optimize on these labels (or highly correlated ones). The paper's effective-dimension framework predicts this should happen when the task has low effective dimension relative to the representation.

- **Cov-spectrum AUROC 0.7928**: The top-20 log-eigenvalues of the unsupervised covariance are a noisy proxy for the spectral structure the paper's operator directly captures. The paper predicts a cleaner, potentially higher-performing probe from the label-weighted operator.

- **H-4 (Breathing ↔ Sharpness Dimension)**: Neural LoFi reframes "sharpness" as the spectral gap of C^(ℓ), not Hessian-based flatness. The breathing phenomenon (expansion then collapse of participation ratio) could correspond to the spectral gap of C^(ℓ) growing across layers as compositional features emerge — a testable prediction.

- **H-796 (cov-spectrum ceiling is basis limitation)**: Neural LoFi directly predicts the basis limitation: the unsupervised eigenvectors are the wrong basis. The label-weighted eigenvectors should lift the ceiling.

- **H-802 (DoM-orthogonal residual carries independent signal)**: The paper's second-order features are precisely this — signal in the DoM-orthogonal subspace, captured by eigenvectors of C^(ℓ) with the first-order component removed.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Label-weighted moment operator** — Form C^(ℓ) = (1/n) Σ yᵢ zᵢ zᵢᵀ at a target layer, extract top-k eigenvectors as supervised spectral features. The key insight vs. standard PCA is that eigendecomposition is weighted by labels, selecting directions with maximal second-order correlation to the target.
  *Replication cost*: 5min CPU on cached 500×1536 activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — trivial to compute on cached NPZs, directly addresses the DoM → cov-spectrum gap.

- **Effective dimension criterion** — D^eff(r) measures the complexity of the feature class at scale r within the RKHS induced by the current representation. Feature emergence requires ρ ≫ r√(D^eff(r)/n). Provides a principled sample-complexity threshold for when a spectral feature becomes detectable.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU (need to estimate RKHS norms from data).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — could answer "do we have enough samples (n=500) to reliably detect the DoM direction at L19?"

- **Low-degree compositionality test** — Check whether depth adds compositionally new features by computing the label-weighted moment operator at each layer and measuring spectral gap. The information exponent at each layer determines the degree of label-feature correlation that's accessible.
  *Replication cost*: 2h CPU if per-layer activations cached; needs H100 forward pass otherwise.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes for L19 (cached), partially for multi-layer (needs fresh extraction for non-L19 layers).

- **Task-adaptive kernel recursion** — Construct K_0 → K_1 → ... → K_L where each transition is supervised. Compare performance of the LoFi kernel at L19 to the NTK (lazy regime) kernel.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU for kernel computation on 500 samples.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — could test whether the LoFi kernel at L19 matches or exceeds our linear probe ceiling (0.7847).

- **Feature emergence threshold prediction** — Use Equations 17–18 to predict the minimum n for which each eigenvector of C^(ℓ) becomes detectable. Compare predicted threshold to our n=500 and the observed spectral gap.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — would quantify whether our 500 MATH-500 samples are near or far from the detection threshold.

## Approaches & framings

- **Feature learning as iterative spectral selection**: Reframes what deep networks do as layer-wise eigendecomposition of label-conditioned moments. Intersects our F-2/F-9 findings — if L19 is the layer where the "right" eigenvectors emerge, this explains why single-layer L19 dominates multi-layer aggregation (the compositional chain has converged by layer 19).

- **Feature emergence as phase transition**: Features become detectable above a sample-complexity threshold (not gradually). Intersects H-839 (DoM stability under bootstrap): if 500 samples is near the phase-transition threshold, the DoM direction might be on the edge of identifiability — explaining why bootstrap resampling could show instability.

- **Depth helps iff low-degree compositionality holds**: Depth is useful only when each layer exposes new low-degree signal. Intersects F-9 directly: CoE-60 redundancy with L19 DoM could mean the correctness signal is NOT hierarchically compositional — it's already fully exposed at layer 19, and deeper layers don't add compositionally new correctness-relevant features.

- **Task-adaptive kernel sequence**: The recursion K_0 → K_L provides a principled way to measure how much the representation adapts to the task at each layer. This could provide a formal framework for the "breathing" phenomenon (F-1) — breathing might correspond to the kernel norm changing across layers as the representation adapts.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Label-weighted moment operator is just `C = (1/n) * (y[:, None] * Z).T @ Z` where `y` is ±1 labels and `Z` is the activation matrix. Leading eigenvectors via standard `np.linalg.eigh`.
- The first-order term (label-weighted mean) is extracted separately: `u = Z.T @ y / n`. This IS our DoM direction (up to normalization).
- Eigenvectors of C^(ℓ) beyond the leading one are the second-order features. The centered operator `C - u u^T` isolates the pure second-order part.
- Code at https://github.com/IdePHICS/Neural-LoFi-Theory — Python, likely JAX/NumPy.
- The effective dimension estimation requires computing the RKHS norm, which for random features is tractable via the Gram matrix.
