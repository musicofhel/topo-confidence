# Triage brief — 2603.20895 — LLM Router: Rethinking Routing with Prefill Activations

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20895
Triaged: 2026-06-12

Authors: Tanay Varshney, Annie Surla, Michelle Xu, Gomathy Venkata Krishnan, Maximilian Jeblick, David Austin, Neal Vaidya, Davide Onofrio (NVIDIA, 2026).

One-line: prefill activations from one ("Encoder") model predict the correctness of a *different* ("Target") model; a joint multi-output MLP (SharedTrunkNet) over concatenated PCA-reduced prefill features routes a model pool, closing 45.58% of the oracle gap at 74.31% cost savings. Layer choice driven by **Fisher Separability J**; **effective dimensionality d_eff** and **anisotropy α** are label-free diagnostics.

## Refutations

1. **Refutes `geometry-portable-signal` (REFUTED premise) — would force resurrection. Cites F-2 / F-9 / `geometry-portable-signal`.**
   Our premise was refuted by P11-FE-EDGEGEN: activation-geometry features add *no portable correctness signal over free baselines (length, logprob) out-of-family*. This paper's entire result set is the opposite claim — prefill geometry (Fisher-J layer + PCA-100, last-token) beats a strong semantic-router suite that *includes* GPT-2 mean-logprob / perplexity / token-entropy features (their §4.2 "free-ish" baseline) AND transfers across model families and across MMLU-Pro/HLE/LiveCodeBench (their Table 2–7, per-target AUC 0.73–0.91; SharedTrunkNet mean AUC 0.856 frontier vs semantic best 0.804).
   *Mechanism:* if a Fisher-J-selected prefill layer carries correctness signal that survives both a logprob baseline and a model-family change, the EDGEGEN refutation was regime-specific (MATH-500 1.5B/7B), not general.
   *Falsifiable:* on our cached L19 1.5B prefill, run their exact protocol — Fisher-J layer pick → PCA-100 → L2-LR — and pit it against the free length+logprob readout *out-of-family* (train on 1.5B, score 7B labels). If geometry wins out-of-family, `geometry-portable-signal` must be un-refuted.

2. **Refutes the "single, model-idiosyncratic direction" reading of F-2. Cites F-2.**
   F-2 establishes a *single* L19 prefill direction (DoM ≈ PC1, cos 0.9216) that predicts a model's *own* correctness, and the cross-architecture control reads it as "each model predicts itself at ~⅔ depth." This paper's headline empirical finding is that a **foreign** encoder (Qwen3.5-122B) consistently *outperforms a target model's own hidden states* at predicting that target's correctness (their §7, Tables 2/6/7 — Qwen3.5-122B is bold-best on nearly every target including closed-source GPT-5/Claude). 
   *Mechanism:* if a different model's prefill geometry predicts your correctness better than your own, the predictive signal is not a model-specific direction but a *shared, transferable* difficulty manifold — F-2's single-direction framing is then a within-model shadow of a cross-model object.
   *Falsifiable:* extract Qwen-1.5B L19 prefill features → train probe → score Qwen-7B MATH-500 correctness labels (and the reverse). If cross-model AUROC ≥ the within-model 0.7731/0.7186, F-2's model-idiosyncrasy assumption is wrong.

3. **Refutes `escalation-beats-introspection` (CONFIRMED premise) if their router survives a matched-cost escalation arm. Cites F-8 / `escalation-beats-introspection`.**
   We confirmed (P11-FE-SHIPGATE): at matched token-FLOP cost, *no* confidence probe beats spending the same tokens on escalation to a stronger model. This paper claims a prefill *confidence-routing* probe closes 45.58% of the oracle gap at 74.31% cost savings (their Table 4) — i.e. introspective routing IS the win. Their evaluation never includes a "route-everything-to-the-strongest-model at matched cost" arm; their baseline is static single-model Pareto (P-AUCCC(models)).
   *Mechanism:* their P-AUCCC gain could be an artifact of a missing escalation comparator; the same cost budget poured into escalation might dominate the router's Pareto.
   *Falsifiable:* reproduce their P-AUCCC sweep on our 1.5B/7B pool, then add the escalation arm (spend the router's "saved" budget escalating low-confidence queries to 7B). If escalation dominates the router curve, `escalation-beats-introspection` stands and the paper's framing is incomplete; if the prefill router dominates at matched cost, the premise flips.

(Secondary, not counted: their §7 finding that d_eff/anisotropy peaks do **not** coincide with separability peaks, and that label-free geometry summaries underperform the label-aware Fisher J, *corroborates* F-10's PH-null — label-free geometric summary statistics are weak. Listed under connections, not refutations.)

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (AUROC 0.7731 prefill / 0.7186 final).** Their per-target self-prediction AUCs land in exactly our band — e.g. Qwen3-8B-on-itself ≈ 0.79, DS-R1D-Qwen-7B ≈ 0.78–0.80 (Table 6). Our 0.7731 single-direction number is a within-model instance of their cross-model sweep; their best foreign-encoder AUCs (0.85–0.91) are the ceiling F-2 has not reached.
- **F-8 (selective prediction 71.6% @ coverage 0.5).** Routing is the multi-model generalization of F-8's single-model refuse-and-spend: instead of refuse/answer, route to the cheapest model predicted correct. Their λ-sweep (Eq. 3) is our coverage axis with a model-choice dimension added.
- **F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM).** Their §7: "Concatenating layers selected independently by [d_eff/α/J] yielded no material AUC improvement." Independent confirmation that single-layer selection captures the signal — multi-layer trajectory features are redundant.
- **F-10 (PH summaries indistinguishable from Gaussian null).** Their finding that *label-free* geometry (d_eff, anisotropy) peaks fail to track separability, while only the *label-aware* Fisher J works, is the same lesson as F-10: unsupervised geometric summary statistics carry weak correctness signal; supervision is doing the work.
- **Cov-spectrum probe FE881 (0.7928) / 2-feat PC1,PC9 (0.7856) / FE882 ceiling (0.7847).** Their d_eff = (Σσ)²/Σσ² (participation ratio, Eq. 8) and anisotropy α (Eq. 9) are computed from the *same* covariance spectrum our FE881/FE291 already cache — direct methodological overlap; we can compute J/d_eff/α from existing NPZs in minutes.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Fisher Separability J** — `J = ||μ₁−μ₀||² / (tr(Σ₀)+tr(Σ₁))`, multivariate Fisher criterion on PCA-reduced features, classes = correct/incorrect. Label-aware layer-selection heuristic that they find tracks empirical probe sweeps better than d_eff or α.
  *Replication cost*: 10min CPU on cached L19 features.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives a principled, cheap layer/feature-selection criterion we've never computed.
- **Effective dimensionality d_eff** — participation ratio of covariance eigenvalues, `(Σσ)²/Σσ²`. Label-free "is the signal spread out" diagnostic.
  *Replication cost*: 5min CPU — eigenvalues already cached in FE881 NPZ.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — trivial, and lets us check whether L19 is a high-d_eff layer.
- **Representational anisotropy α** — mean pairwise cosine similarity of hidden states; low α = isotropic = avoids "narrow-cone" collapse of class differences.
  *Replication cost*: 5min CPU (or O(n²) but n=500 is tiny).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — one more cheap geometric descriptor of the cached prefill cloud.
- **Encoder-Target Decoupling** — extract prefill features from model A, predict correctness labels of model B (open encoder → closed target).
  *Replication cost*: ~1hr CPU — we have BOTH 1.5B and 7B prefill cached (`prefill_gated_compute`), so cross-model probing needs no new forward passes.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — this is the single highest-value cheap test (refutations 1 & 2).
- **SharedTrunkNet** — joint multi-output MLP over concatenated per-target PCA features (BCEWithLogitsLoss, 10 seeds, top-5 by val BCE averaged), one forward pass → P(correct) for all K targets, giving cross-model context + shared calibration.
  *Replication cost*: needs ≥3 target-model label sets; we have 2 (1.5B, 7B). Mini-version only.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: partial — a 2-target shared trunk on cached features is cheap; a real pool needs fresh extraction (Tier 2).
- **last-token vs mean-pooling sweep** — they compare both pooling modes for the prefill feature.
  *Replication cost*: 20min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — our DoM uses one pooling convention; worth checking the other doesn't beat it.
- **Routing-metric suite (P-AUCCC, MDP-AUCCC, Oracle Distance)** — normalized inverse-cost-vs-accuracy area metrics for cost-aware selection.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU once a 2-model cost model is set.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives F-8's selective-prediction story a cost-aware, publishable metric.

## Approaches & framings

- **Encoder ≠ Target decoupling.** Reframes "a model knows its own correctness" into "any sufficiently capable model can read another's correctness from prefill geometry." Intersects F-2/F-3 directly: if true, the prefill correctness direction is a property of the *task manifold*, not the *model*, which reframes F-2's single direction as a projection of a shared object and pressures the refuted `geometry-portable-signal`.
- **Label-aware > label-free layer selection.** Fisher J (supervised) beats d_eff/α (unsupervised) for picking the informative layer. Reinforces our F-10 stance that unsupervised geometry summaries are weak, and suggests our layer choices should be J-driven, not eyeballed.
- **Routing as the deployment surface for confidence probes.** Casts the whole correctness-probe program as a multi-model cost-aware routing problem rather than single-model selective prediction — a strictly larger framing of F-8 that adds the `escalation-beats-introspection` tension.
- **Consensus-regime partition (all-correct / all-fail / disagreement).** Only the disagreement regime carries routing value; evaluating on the full set inflates apparent gains. A useful evaluation hygiene lens for any cross-model claim we make.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Hidden states taken from **upper half of encoder layers (L/2 … L)** — our L19/28 ≈ 0.68 depth sits inside this band, consistent with their and our ⅔-depth sweet spot.
- **PCA d ∈ {50, …, 300}**, **last-token mode chosen** for all headline tables (Tables 2/6/7 footnotes: "PCA=100, last-token mode").
- Layer/feature selection via **5-fold stratified CV with L2-regularized logistic regression** — same OOF discipline as our 5-fold 0.7731.
- SharedTrunkNet: **BCEWithLogitsLoss, Adam, 85/15 split, 10 seeds, retain top-5 by val BCE, average at inference**, early stopping. Calibration suite: Platt, isotonic, percentile-map, z-score.
- Cost model: `C = n_in·r_in/1e6 + ñ_out·r_out/1e6`, output length approximated by **median training output tokens** (verbosity proxy); observed OSL deliberately excluded from features to avoid ground-truth leakage — a leakage gotcha worth copying.
- Over 1,300 semantic-router configs swept; IRT/MIRT-2PL, GNN routers, LLM-as-judge soft labels, disagreement-weighted training, two-stage tiered routing all "yielded no improvements distinguishable from noise" — saves us from re-exploring those.
