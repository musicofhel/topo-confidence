# Triage brief — 2402.03744 — INSIDE: LLMs' Internal States Retain the Power of Hallucination Detection

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03744
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutation of F-9 (CoE-60 redundant with single-layer L19 DoM).** INSIDE's
   EigenScore computes the LogDet of a K×K covariance matrix over K=10 *sampled*
   sentence embeddings (last token, middle layer L=int(L/2)). The signal is
   *cross-sample variance*, not single-pass trajectory shape. CoE-60 is
   single-pass, K=1. If EigenScore on Qwen-2.5-1.5B MATH-500 with K=10 at L14
   exceeds the F-9 ceiling (CoE-60 ≈ single-layer L19 DoM AUROC ≈ 0.77), then a
   third information source — across-sample geometric spread — exists that
   neither CoE-60 nor single-layer DoM captures. F-9's "redundant" claim
   downgrades to "redundant within the K=1 family". **Falsifiable test**:
   resample K=10 generations per MATH-500 problem at temperature 0.5, compute
   EigenScore at L14, compare AUROC against 0.7731 (prefill L19 DoM, OOF) and
   the CoE-60 number. If EigenScore > 0.80, F-9 needs revision.

2. **Refutation of F-2 (prefill L19 is the special layer).** INSIDE's layer
   ablation (Fig 3b) explicitly reports that *shallow and final* layers are
   significantly inferior, with middle-layer (L=17 of 32 in LLaMA-7B; "int(L/2)"
   rule) winning. For Qwen-2.5-1.5B with 28 layers, the int(L/2) rule predicts
   L14 — not L19 — as the optimum. If we run a per-layer EigenScore sweep on
   Qwen MATH-500 K=10 and find L13–L15 outranks L19, then F-2's "L19 is
   special" claim collapses to an artifact of (a) supervised-DoM extraction
   protocol and (b) single-sample regime. Mechanism: under multi-sample
   covariance, semantic divergence concentrates earlier than the
   answer-formatting-dominated L19 region. **Falsifiable test**: sweep layers
   {5, 10, 14, 19, 23, 27} for both EigenScore and supervised DoM AUROC on the
   same K=10 cache; if EigenScore-best ≠ DoM-best, the "L19 effect" is
   protocol-bound.

3. **Refutation of F-8 (selective prediction at 50% coverage = 71.6% accuracy
   via prefill DoM).** INSIDE achieves AUROC 80.4 (CoQA, LLaMA-7B) and 81.5
   (SQuAD) using **no labels** — purely unsupervised K=10 covariance. F-8's
   71.6% answered-accuracy at 50% coverage was driven by supervised prefill
   DoM. If unsupervised EigenScore on MATH-500 K=10 yields a better
   coverage/accuracy tradeoff than prefill DoM (e.g. ≥73% answered-accuracy at
   50% coverage), then the supervised-probe pipeline is dominated by an
   unsupervised alternative — F-8's "best selective predictor" status is
   refuted. Mechanism: K=10 sample variance is a stronger uncertainty signal
   than a single-vector projection because it integrates over the model's
   actual output distribution rather than approximating it via one direction.
   **Falsifiable test**: build the same risk-coverage curve using EigenScore
   ranking instead of prefill-DoM-margin ranking; if EigenScore curve
   stochastically dominates, F-8 is refuted.

4. **Soft refutation of F-10 (PH on residual streams = Gaussian null).** F-10
   established that persistent homology on K=1 residual streams contains no
   above-null signal. INSIDE shows that *spectral* (eigenvalue) features of
   the K-sample covariance carry strong correctness signal. This *narrows*
   F-10's scope: PH is at null because PH is a topological summary that
   discards spectral structure. If EigenScore works on MATH-500 K=10 final-token
   embeddings, it doesn't refute F-10 directly, but refutes the broader read of
   F-10 as "all geometric structure of the residual cloud is null". Mechanism:
   the cloud's eigenvalue spread carries information; only its 0/1-dim
   topology doesn't. **Falsifiable test**: on the cached Stage 2 K=1 NPZs,
   compute PH and EigenScore-equivalent (single-pass, K=1 — degenerate); on
   the new K=10 cache, compute PH and EigenScore — if EigenScore signal exists
   and PH is still null, F-10's "geometric null" claim is too broad as
   currently worded.

## Methodologies extracted

- **EigenScore** — for K sampled responses, take the last-token hidden state
  at the middle layer (L=int(L/2)) as sentence embedding **z** ∈ R^d; form
  Z ∈ R^{d×K}; compute Σ = Z^T J_d Z where J_d = I_d − (1/d) 1_d 1_d^T is the
  centering matrix (yielding Σ ∈ R^{K×K}); add α·I (α=0.001) for full rank;
  EigenScore = (1/K) Σ_i log λ_i(Σ + αI). Higher = more semantic divergence
  = more likely hallucination.
  *Replication cost*: 6h H100 (resample K=10 at T=0.5 for 500 MATH-500
  problems on 1.5B+7B) + 30min CPU (eigendecomposition + AUROC).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — directly tests F-9 and F-2; uses
  existing infrastructure; small cost.

- **Test-time feature clipping (FC)** — at the *penultimate* layer, maintain a
  rolling memory bank of N=3000 token activations; per-neuron, set
  h_min/h_max to the bottom/top p=0.2 percentiles; clip activations outside
  [h_min, h_max] **during generation**. Reduces overconfident-and-wrong
  generations.
  *Replication cost*: ~30min CPU implementation + 4h H100 to regenerate
  K=10 samples with FC active.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **maybe** — interesting because it modifies
  generation, not just probing; would test whether 7B's overconfident-incorrect
  bucket (B-bucket / D-bucket, see F-7) shrinks under FC. Lower priority
  than EigenScore baseline.

- **Sentence embedding via last-token vs averaged-token, middle vs final
  layer** — paper's ablation shows last-token-in-middle-layer > avg-token-in-
  final-layer. Mirrors but inverts our P10/P11 finding that L19 (late-middle,
  not final) prefill DoM > L19 final-token DoM (F-2 vs F-3 orthogonality).
  *Replication cost*: included in the EigenScore sweep above.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — falls out of the layer ablation.

- **K-sweep at K∈{5,10,15,20,30,40}** — paper finds plateau at K≈15–20.
  Useful as a sanity calibrator before committing to K=10.
  *Replication cost*: 1.5× the EigenScore baseline (extra K=20 sample set).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** if EigenScore K=10 results are
  ambiguous.

- **Energy score** baseline (Liu et al. 2020) — token-level energy averaged
  to sentence-level; used here as an OOD-detection proxy for hallucination.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached logits.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **no** — they show it's the *worst* baseline
  (often <50 AUROC); not worth the engineering.

## Approaches & framings

- **Multi-sample covariance as differential entropy.** Paper's Remark 1 shows
  EigenScore equals differential entropy of a Gaussian fit to the sample
  cloud. This re-frames *sample diversity* as *information-theoretic
  uncertainty*, complementing our project's *single-pass* uncertainty framings
  (DoM probe, CoE-60). Connects to F-10: PH-null + EigenScore-positive ⇒ the
  cloud's "shape" is null but its "spread spectrum" is informative. New axis
  for the H-12 family.

- **Self-consistency without language-space comparison.** Paper sidesteps
  Rouge-L / NLI similarity (the SelfCheckGPT / Lin-2023 approach) by
  measuring consistency in dense-embedding space. Relevant for MATH-500
  where Rouge-style overlap is degenerate (numeric answers either match
  exactly or not). Reframes our K=8 majority-voting (which is language-space
  consensus) as a special case of an embedding-space agreement metric.

- **Penultimate-layer feature clipping as overconfidence rectification.**
  Frames overconfident-incorrect generations as driven by extreme penultimate
  activations. Bridges to F-7's D-bucket (collective signature of pathological
  problems) — possibly the same population.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Code: https://github.com/alibaba/eigenscore (Apache-licensed, Alibaba).
- PyTorch + transformers; no fine-tuning.
- Sampling: temperature 0.5, top-p 0.99, top-k 5, **K=10** generations per
  question.
- Sentence embedding: last-token hidden state at layer **int(L/2)**
  (LLaMA-7B → L17, OPT-6.7B → L16, Qwen-1.5B → L14).
- Regularization: α = 0.001 added to covariance diagonal pre-eigendecomp.
- Memory bank for FC: N = 3000; per-neuron percentiles p=0.2.
- FC threshold scales with three-sigma rule.
- AUROC computed against ROUGE-L > 0.5 (or sentence cosine > 0.9) as
  ground-truth correctness.
- Ablation note: K plateaus at 15–20 → if budget-constrained, K=15 is
  the knee point.
- Key limitation (paper-internal): sensitive to temperature; temperature > 1
  collapses both EigenScore and Lexical Similarity. Our T=0.5 default in
  Pathway 11 K-sweeps is fine; T=1.0 cross-checks would not be.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2402.03744 — INSIDE: LLMs' Internal States Retain the Power of Hallucination Detection (Chen et al., ICLR 2024)

**Relevance:** Methodologically the closest external cousin to our F-9
(CoE-60) and F-2 (prefill DoM) findings. EigenScore is the cross-sample
analogue (K=10 covariance LogDet) of CoE-60's single-pass trajectory
features. The middle-layer/last-token recipe directly tests whether F-2's
"L19 specialness" is a supervised-extraction artifact or a representation
property. The penultimate-layer feature-clipping mechanism is a candidate
generation-time intervention for F-7's D-bucket. Datasets (CoQA, SQuAD, NQ,
TriviaQA) are not directly applicable to MATH-500, but methodology transfers
cleanly.

**Key claim we tested:** Cross-sample covariance LogDet over K=10 sampled
sentence embeddings (last token, middle layer) is a better correctness signal
than single-pass uncertainty (perplexity, length-normalized entropy) or
language-space consistency (Lexical Similarity). On QA, EigenScore reaches
80.4–83.9 AUROC vs supervised DoM's QA-domain ceiling. They also frame
EigenScore as differential entropy of a Gaussian fit to the sample cloud.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** Direct head-to-head on Qwen-2.5-1.5B MATH-500 has
not been run. Status flips to PARTIALLY CONFIRMED if H-66 finds EigenScore ∈
[0.70, 0.80], or REPLICATED if EigenScore ≥ 0.80. Status flips to
CONTRADICTED if EigenScore < 0.65 on MATH-500 (math-reasoning regime resists
multi-sample covariance the way TriviaQA's short-answer regime made
perplexity dominant).

**Related experiments:** P11-FE74 (EigenScore on MATH-500), P11-FE75 (per-
layer ablation), P11-FE76 (feature clipping on D-bucket), H-66, H-67, H-68.

**Status:** TO TEST (HIGH priority for H-66; MEDIUM for H-67, H-68).
