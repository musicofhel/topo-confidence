# Triage brief — 2601.01552 — HalluZig: Hallucination Detection using Zigzag Persistence

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01552
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

**1. F-10 (PH on trained residual streams is at Gaussian null) — DIRECT MECHANISTIC CHALLENGE.**
F-10 is anchored on EXP-026, which tested **static** Vietoris–Rips PH on residual-stream point clouds and got real-PH AUROC 0.690 vs null-PH 0.693 (gap zero). HalluZig's central ablation (§5.1, Fig. 7) is *precisely the same axis we never explored*: their **Static TDA** baseline (per-layer standalone PH) underperforms full zigzag-PH-on-attention-graph-evolution by **+12.78% F1**, **+12.46% TPR@5%FPR**, accuracy 61.95% → 73.91%. Mechanism of refutation: F-10 collapses the static/dynamic distinction and the residual/attention distinction — both moves HalluZig demonstrates matter. Our parking of "Zigzag PH across layers" in HYPOTHESES.md historical/abandoned ("killed by EXP-026") was premature: EXP-026 was static-PH-on-residuals, not zigzag-PH-on-attention-graphs.
*Falsifiable:* on cached P11 H100 1024-tok attention dumps, replicate HalluZig's pipeline (top-k attention edges → mean-pool heads → zigzag filtration G_l ↪ G_l ∪ G_{l+1} ↩ G_{l+1} → 1-D persistence → PersImg) and run the **same Gaussian-null control as EXP-026** on the zigzag features. If real-zigzag-PH AUROC > null-zigzag-PH AUROC by ≥0.05 on MATH-500 correctness, F-10 generalizes too far and must be narrowed to "static-PH-on-residuals is at null".

**2. F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM is the privileged correctness signal at AUROC 0.7731) — SUBSTRATE REFUTATION.**
HalluZig achieves AUROC 0.823 (Vicuna-7b PersImg, FAVA), 0.826 (Llama-3-8b PersImg, FAVA), 0.821 (Llama-2-7b PersImg, FAVA) on hallucination detection using *only* attention-matrix topology — no residual-stream hidden-state probe. F-2's framing implicitly asserts the residual stream is the privileged geometric locus. If a HalluZig-style attention-graph zigzag-PH probe on Qwen-2.5-1.5B reaches AUROC ≥ 0.7731 on MATH-500 correctness, the residual-stream framing is unnecessary — attention dynamics suffice. Mechanism: HalluZig's signal lives in the structure of *which tokens attend to which across layers*, which is causally upstream of the residual stream and should therefore be at-least-as-informative.
*Falsifiable:* on cached P11 1024-tok generations for Qwen-2.5-1.5B (243 correct, 257 incorrect), extract per-layer attention matrices, build zigzag PH features (PersImg, top-k=1% edges, 1-D homology), 5-fold CV with RandomForest, compare AUROC against 0.7731. ≥0.7731 directly refutes the residual-stream-privileged framing.

**3. F-7 (D-bucket has distinctive collective geometric signature) — LOCALIZATION REFUTATION.**
F-7 places the D-bucket signature in residual-stream geometry. HalluZig demonstrates that distinctive topological signatures live in the **attention-graph evolution** across layers — and the visualization (Fig. 6) shows persistence-diagram multiplicity differs strikingly between hallucinated and non-hallucinated. If D-bucket problems (K=1 right, K=8 wrong — pathological per F-7) show distinctive zigzag-PH signatures in their attention-graph evolution, but A-bucket problems do not, F-7 localizes to the wrong substrate. Stronger refutation if zigzag-PH attention features distinguish A vs D vs B vs E buckets at higher AUROC than residual-stream PR / DoM features.
*Falsifiable:* compute zigzag-PH attention features for the four-bucket partition (A/B/D/E) on the 500-problem P11 set; report 4-class macro-F1 against the residual-stream collective-geometry baseline used in F-7.

**4. F-9 (CoE-60 trajectory features are redundant with single-layer L19 DoM) — TRAJECTORY-VS-SINGLE-LAYER REFUTATION.**
F-9 says trajectories add nothing over a single layer's DoM. HalluZig's Fig. 9 (depth ablation) shows F1-score climbs monotonically with depth from ~77 (30%) to ~82 (100%) on Vicuna-7b — i.e., trajectory across layers strictly dominates single-layer information for hallucination. If the analogue holds for MATH-500 correctness (zigzag PH across L0–L27 > single-layer L19 DoM), F-9 collapses.
*Falsifiable:* compute zigzag-PH attention features at progressive depth cutoffs (10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, 100% of layers) and report MATH-500 correctness AUROC at each cutoff. If 100%-cutoff > L19-only by ≥0.03 AUROC, F-9 reverses.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Zigzag persistent homology of attention-graph evolution** — for each layer l, build graph G_l = (V=tokens, E_l=top-k% attention edges from mean-pooled heads). Apply zigzag filtration G_l ↪ G_l ∪ G_{l+1} ↩ G_{l+1} across all L layers. Compute H_0 (components) and H_1 (loops) zigzag persistence. Vectorize via PersImg / PersEntropy / Betti curves. Train RandomForest on resulting feature vector.
  *Replication cost:* H100 day to extract attentions for 500 MATH-500 problems × 28 layers × Qwen-2.5-1.5B (we already have P11 H100 hidden states; need a fresh hook to also dump attentions). Plus ~4–8 CPU-hours for FastZigzag persistence on 500 × 27 zigzag steps. Plus 5-fold CV. Total ~1 day end-to-end.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — direct test of F-10 narrowness and F-2 substrate claim. Code released at https://github.com/TDA-Jyamiti/halluzig (Apache-style; need to verify license).

- **Static-TDA baseline (per-layer standalone PH)** — Vietoris–Rips on attention graph one layer at a time, no zigzag connection between layers, average features across layers OR concatenate.
  *Replication cost:* sub-half of the above (~4 CPU-hours).
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — necessary as the inner control to attribute lift to *dynamics* vs *attention-graph-as-substrate* (which itself differs from residual-stream PH).

- **Mean-pool attention heads → single graph per layer** — averaging A^{(l,h)} across heads h to get A^{(l)} ∈ ℝ^{T×T}, top-k% threshold for edges.
  *Replication cost:* trivial.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — but with the explicit caveat that paper's Limitations acknowledges this dilutes head-specific signals (H-13 alignment).

- **Top-k attention edge thresholding** — keep edges whose weight is in the top k% of all attention weights in A^{(l)}.
  *Replication cost:* trivial.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — but ablate k (paper does not justify their choice; sensitivity check needed).

- **Persistence Image (Adams 2017) vectorization** — Gaussian-kernel 2D heatmap of (birth, death) points on the persistence diagram.
  *Replication cost:* trivial via Gudhi.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — paper's strongest vectorizer, worth using as default.

- **Persistence Entropy (Atienza 2020)** — Shannon entropy of the (death-birth) lifetime distribution. Single scalar.
  *Replication cost:* trivial.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — cheap scalar feature, worth including in ABC-44 next-gen.

- **Betti curve vectorization (Umeda 2017)** — number of active bars at each filtration value, sampled discretely.
  *Replication cost:* trivial.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — third option; redundant with PersImg in most settings but worth seeing if attention-graph case differs.

- **LLM-as-a-judge labeling (GPT-4o-mini)** — for QA datasets without explicit hallucination labels (TruthfulQA, NQ-Open), use GPT-4o-mini to annotate response as hallucinated or not.
  *Replication cost:* API costs; ~$5 for 500 problems.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **no** — we already have ground-truth correctness labels for MATH-500.

- **Zero-shot cross-model classifier transfer** — train RandomForest on Llama-3-8b zigzag-PH features, test on Vicuna-7b features without retraining (and vice versa). F1 ~80.
  *Replication cost:* trivial once features exist.
  *We'd plausibly run this:* **yes** — directly tests universality of the topological substrate (H-22 / cross-model claims). If feature dimensions match (depends on layer count), test Qwen-2.5-1.5B → Qwen-2.5-7B transfer.

## Approaches & framings

- **Dynamic-topology framing**: hallucinations are encoded in *how* attention graphs transform across layers, not in any single layer's snapshot. Reframes our F-10 null result narrowly: PH at null applies to static-residual-stream PH, not necessarily to zigzag-PH on dynamic-attention-graph evolution. Tightens F-10's scope.
- **"Cycles = consolidated concepts; many short cycles = spurious thought loops" interpretation**: long-lifetime 1-D zigzag bars represent stable attention loops (factual consolidation); short-lived multiplicitous bars represent unstable circular reasoning (hallucination). Maps onto our prefill-vs-final asymmetry: F-3's near-orthogonal cos = 0.046 may be partially explainable as a switch from "stable cycles forming" to "stable cycles being read out", which would be detectable as zigzag bar lifetime distribution shifts.
- **Attention as the substrate, residual stream as the projection**: HalluZig implicitly argues attention dynamics are upstream of residual-stream content. Our pathway-1-through-11 program has anchored on residual streams. Reframes whether F-2 / F-7 / F-9 should be re-tested in attention space.
- **70%-depth shortcut**: hallucination signature is fully formed by 70% of layers. For Qwen-2.5-1.5B (28 layers), 70% ≈ L19 — *exactly the layer at which DoM is strongest in F-2*. This convergence between two very different probes (DoM and zigzag-PH) on the same depth bucket is striking and may reflect a common mechanistic origin around the upper-middle layers.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Code: https://github.com/TDA-Jyamiti/halluzig — verify license before bulk reuse.
- Persistence library: **FastZigzag** (Dey & Hou 2022) for zigzag persistence (custom, not Gudhi for the zigzag part).
- Vectorization library: **Gudhi** (Carrière et al. 2025 scikit-learn-like persistence representations).
- Classifier: RandomForest, scikit-learn defaults; "best run across multiple random seeds" reported in main results, mean ± std in Appendix B (we should hold them to this — verify the appendix actually reports stds and that the gap to baseline survives 1-σ).
- Models tested up to 13B (Llama-2-13b). Authors flag scaling beyond 13B as future work.
- Threshold k% (top-k attention edges) — the paper does not explicitly justify their choice. We should ablate k ∈ {0.5%, 1%, 2%, 5%, 10%} to ensure their numbers are not threshold-cherrypicked.
- Mean-pooling across heads — flagged in their Limitations as a known dilution. Consistent with H-13's framing.
- Causal mask makes attention matrices lower-triangular (decoder-only); they handle this cleanly via top-k thresholding.
- 70%-of-layers performance achieves 98%+ of full-depth performance.
- Attention-Score baseline numbers (LLM-Check) come from Sriramanan et al. 2024, not re-run by HalluZig — be cautious of methodological mismatch.
