# Triage brief — 2102.11107 — Towards Causal Representation Learning

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11107
Triaged: 2026-04-29

Authors: Schölkopf, Locatello, Bauer, Ke, Kalchbrenner, Goyal, Bengio. Published Proceedings of the IEEE, 2021. Review/position paper — no new empirical results; introduces ICM Principle + Sparse Mechanism Shift (SMS) Hypothesis as inductive biases for representation learning, frames causal-vs-anticausal direction analysis for SSL/adversarial robustness/OOD, and proposes causal representation learning (recovering high-level causal variables from low-level perceptual input) as a research program.

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-2's implicit causal interpretation if H-1 (per-position DoM steering) fails.**
   F-2 reports prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731 — a strong *predictive* relationship.
   Schölkopf et al.'s ICM Principle (§IV) draws a hard line between *statistical
   association* and *causal mechanism*: a real mechanism must be intervenable.
   The "storks predict birth rates" example is the explicit anti-pattern. If H-1
   (steering along the prefill DoM direction at H100 inference time, P10-FE1 +
   P11-FE3 already queued) shows that pushing residuals along DoM moves AUROC
   but does not shift K=1 accuracy (i.e., the direction is downstream of
   correctness rather than causally upstream), Schölkopf's framework labels the
   prefill DoM as an anticausal correlate. **Mechanism**: prefill DoM is a
   *readout* of an upstream commitment that is itself the cause of correctness;
   intervening on the readout doesn't change the cause. **Falsifiable test**:
   P11-FE3 + P10-FE1 — if both interventions fail to cross the F-2 baseline
   accuracy on MATH-500 holdout despite shifting AUROC, F-2 is recategorized
   from "correctness predictor" to "correctness epiphenomenon."

2. **Refutes F-3 (prefill/final-token orthogonality, cos = 0.046) as evidence of
   independent mechanisms.** The 0.046 cosine is currently read as "two
   distinct geometric processes." Schölkopf's ICM (§IV) gives a sharper
   prediction than orthogonality: independent mechanisms must satisfy SMS —
   sparse interventions on one shouldn't propagate to the other. **Mechanism**:
   if prefill-DoM and final-token-DoM are genuinely independent ICM modules,
   then perturbing prefill content (e.g., paraphrasing the problem statement,
   adding distractor sentences, swapping numerals) should change the prefill
   activation projection without proportionally moving the final-token
   projection. If we observe that prompt-only perturbations move both
   projections jointly (especially in the same sign), the orthogonality is
   *geometric coincidence*, not mechanism independence — F-3's "two pathways"
   reading collapses. **Falsifiable test**: small prompt-edit study with
   paraphrasing + numeric-swap interventions on a 100-problem subset, measure
   ∂(prefill-DoM)/∂(prompt) vs ∂(final-DoM)/∂(prompt). SMS predicts the cross-
   sensitivity to be near-zero.

3. **Refutes F-7 (D-bucket distinctive collective signature) under SMS.**
   F-7 claims D-bucket (K=1-right, K=8-wrong, 36 problems) has a distinctive
   collective geometric signature. Schölkopf §VII-C frames OOD as a min-max
   over a set of interventional distributions. **Mechanism**: if D-bucket is
   a *real coarse-grained causal variable* (an SCM node, in Schölkopf's
   §VI sense), small interventions (sampling-temperature shift, decoding-seed
   change) should move at most a sparse subset of D-bucket members across the
   bucket boundary — SMS predicts the bucket membership is interventionally
   stable. If a temperature 0.1→0.3 sweep reshuffles >30% of D-bucket members
   between A/B/C/D, the bucket is a *fitting artifact* of one decoding regime,
   not a causal variable, and F-7's "distinctive signature" is the signature
   of overfitting to a particular sampling distribution. **Falsifiable test**:
   re-run the K=1 + K=8 protocol at temperature 0.3 on the same 500-problem
   set; quantify Jaccard overlap of D-bucket membership across temperatures.

4. **Refutes the universality framing of F-1 (dimensional breathing).**
   F-1 claims breathing is universal across transformer architectures and
   scales. Schölkopf §IV.b's modularity-via-ICM and §VII.D's analysis of
   pretraining inductive biases warn that apparent universality across a
   *single architectural family* (Qwen 1.5B, Qwen 7B, plus 28-layer probes —
   all decoder-only LLMs trained on overlapping corpora) is exactly the
   kind of "shared spurious mechanism" SMS would not protect against.
   **Mechanism**: breathing may be an artifact of a shared training objective
   (next-token CE) on a shared causal direction (Sparse-Causal vs anticausal
   token prediction, §VII.A) — not a universal property of "reasoning."
   **Falsifiable test**: H-3 (breathing on code generation) and a stronger
   variant — replicate F-1 on a non-Qwen family (Llama-3 8B, Mistral 7B, GPT-2
   XL) with no architectural overlap with Qwen-2.5. If breathing collapses or
   inverts on those families, F-1's universality claim is downgraded to
   "common in Qwen-tradition decoder-only models," and the ICM-style invariance
   reading (which would justify treating breathing as a real causal mechanism)
   is refuted.

5. **Refutes F-8 (selective-prediction at 71.6% / coverage 0.5) under
   anticausal-direction analysis.** Schölkopf §VII.A makes a sharp distinction:
   in the *causal* direction (X→Y), SSL should be futile; in the *anticausal*
   direction (Y→X), SSL works. **Mechanism**: if the prefill-DoM probe is
   classifying in the anticausal direction (i.e., prefill state is *caused by*
   the latent commitment-to-correctness, which is also the cause of the final
   answer), then F-8's selective-prediction performance should *degrade more
   than expected* under distribution shift — a property the paper predicts.
   If we test F-8 on a shifted MATH-500 variant (e.g., GSM-Hard, or numeric
   perturbations of MATH-500 problems) and selective-prediction-at-coverage-0.5
   collapses below the 50%-coverage random baseline (~50%), F-8 is revealed as
   a within-distribution probe, not a transferable confidence signal. The
   anticausal interpretation also predicts: SSL using unlabeled MATH-500
   prefill activations should improve the DoM probe — testable with held-out
   unlabeled data.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731)** — Schölkopf's ICM Principle gives a
  formal language for the question "is the prefill DoM a *causal* feature or
  a *correlate*?" The paper does not contradict the predictive AUROC, but
  motivates the FE-stack (P11-FE3 activation patching, P10-FE1 steering) that
  would discriminate between the two readings. Direct alignment.

- **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046)** — orthogonality is consistent
  with but not sufficient for ICM mechanism-independence; SMS gives a stronger
  test (refutation #2 above). The paper sharpens the F-3 reading from "two
  geometric directions" to "two candidate ICM modules, pending intervention
  test."

- **F-1 (universal breathing)** — the paper's caution about cross-architecture
  invariance (§VI Problem 2 — "transferable mechanisms") sets a higher bar
  than the within-Qwen-family tests so far. Connects to H-3 + a
  cross-architecture replication.

- **F-7 (D-bucket signature)** — paper's §VI framing of "coarse-grained causal
  variables" provides a principled criterion for whether D-bucket is a real
  variable or a fitting artifact (SMS-stability under decoding-temperature
  intervention).

- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering moves accuracy)** — direct test of whether
  the prefill DoM is causal in Schölkopf's sense. Outcome of H-1 is the
  highest-leverage update on F-2's interpretation.

- **H-5 (prefill encodes familiarity vs decomposability)** — the paper's
  "learning disentangled representations" framing (§VI Problem 1) is the
  natural reframing of H-5: are familiarity and decomposability two latent
  causal variables that get linearly mixed in the prefill DoM? If yes,
  per-axis interventions should be possible; if no, H-5 is misframed.

- **H-8 (prefill direction stable across training checkpoints)** — directly
  parallels Schölkopf §VII-G's discussion of multi-task learning: a stable
  causal mechanism should persist across training stages, while a shortcut
  feature should drift. H-8 outcome is interpretable in causal-vs-correlate
  terms.

- **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes replace supervised DoM)** — paper's §VII.D
  on self-supervision; semantic-entropy probes are exactly the
  "label-free pretext task" Schölkopf flags as a candidate for ICM-aligned
  representation learning.

## Methodologies extracted

- **ICM Principle as inductive bias for probe design** — one-line description:
  treat candidate features (prefill DoM, final-token DoM, breathing PR) as
  candidate ICM modules and test mechanism-independence via SMS rather than
  statistical independence.
  *Replication cost*: ~4h CPU on cached NPZs (perturb-and-measure pipeline).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — clean refutation test for F-3 and the
  "two pathways" interpretation; ride on existing P10/P11 NPZs.

- **Sparse Mechanism Shift (SMS) test via prompt perturbation** — apply
  small interventions (paraphrase, numeric swap, distractor injection) to
  prompts and measure how few features in a candidate causal factorization
  move; quantify "sparseness" by ℓ₀ / ℓ₁ ratio of the change vector.
  *Replication cost*: 1 day H100 for re-extraction of perturbed-prompt
  activations on 100 MATH-500 problems × 4 perturbation types.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — discriminates F-3's "independent
  mechanisms" reading from "geometric coincidence."

- **Anticausal-direction SSL diagnostic** — train a label-free SSL probe on
  unlabeled prefill activations (e.g., contrastive on cached representations);
  if the SSL probe improves DoM AUROC, we are in the anticausal direction
  (per §VII.A). If not, we are causal — sharper diagnostic than orthogonality.
  *Replication cost*: 1 day CPU on cached Stage 2 NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — converts a long-standing question (is
  the probe causal or correlational?) into a one-shot test.

- **Algorithmic-information ICM test (Kolmogorov-style)** — compress prefill
  DoM trajectory and final-token DoM trajectory separately vs jointly;
  ratio ≈ 1 ⇒ independent mechanisms in algorithmic-information sense.
  Practical implementation: gzip on quantized trajectories.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU on cached temporal NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes (low-cost) but expect noisy signal —
  good to bookmark, low priority.

- **Min-max OOD generalization formulation** — instead of single-distribution
  AUROC, evaluate F-2 / F-8 / F-9 over a worst-case set of interventional
  distributions (different MATH subsets, numeric perturbations, prompt
  paraphrases).
  *Replication cost*: 1 day H100 for re-extraction.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — strongly recommended for any selective-
  prediction claim before publication.

- **Disentanglement via SMS as label-free supervision** — use sparse-shift
  signal across naturally-occurring problem variations as supervision for
  factorizing prefill DoM into latent causal axes.
  *Replication cost*: 1 week H100; needs new training pipeline.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no for this project — well outside scope; flag
  as a long-tail item if the project ever pivots to representation learning.

- **Causal-vs-anticausal classification of probing problems** — diagnostic
  procedure to classify any probe (prefill DoM, final-token DoM, CoE-60)
  as causal-direction or anticausal-direction prediction, using SSL
  improvability + intervention-response signature.
  *Replication cost*: 4h CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — meta-level lens on F-2 / F-9.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Statistical model vs causal model" taxonomy (§II + Table I)** — sharp
  distinction between i.i.d.-prediction-only models and intervention-capable
  models. Reframes our probe stack: F-2, F-8, F-9 are currently i.i.d.
  predictors; the project's value-add is upgrading them to intervention-
  capable mechanisms (which is exactly the H-1/P11-FE3 program).

- **ICM Principle as a prior for representation learning** — independent
  mechanisms shouldn't inform/influence each other. Maps onto the F-3
  orthogonality reading: orthogonality is a consequence of ICM but not a
  proof of it. SMS gives the proof-test.

- **Sparse Mechanism Shift hypothesis** — distribution shifts are sparse in
  the causal factorization. Connects to H-9 (breathing amplitude predicts
  difficulty): if breathing is a *causal mechanism* of difficulty, small
  difficulty shifts should move breathing sparsely, not globally.

- **Causal-vs-anticausal directionality of probing problems** — when X→Y is
  the causal direction, SSL is futile; when Y→X (anticausal), SSL helps.
  Recasts the entire DoM-probing program: are we predicting in the causal
  direction (residual → correctness) or anticausal (correctness → residual)?
  Different evidence applies.

- **Coarse-grained causal variables (§VI)** — high-level variables S₁,…,Sₙ
  obtained from low-level X via X = G(S). Reframes B/D-bucket structure
  (F-7) and length-band stratification (H-15) as candidate coarse-grainings;
  validity criterion is whether interventions on S act predictably on X.

- **Modular architecture as ICM prior (§VI Problem 2 + §VII)** — modularity
  in representation as a means to robust transfer. Cross-cuts H-11 (big-to-
  small distillation) and H-13 (head-level attribution): the head-level
  attribution work is essentially asking whether the prefill DoM lives in
  a single head/circuit module (ICM-compatible) or is distributed (non-modular
  shortcut feature).

## Implementation details worth capturing

- **§VII-C OOD risk formulation**:
  R^OOD_{P_G}(g) = max_{P_y ∈ P_G} E_{P_y}[loss(Y, g(X))], where P_G is a set
  of interventional distributions over the causal graph. Useful as a target
  formulation for a "robust DoM probe" objective if the project ever trains
  a probe end-to-end.
- **Anticausal SSL test recipe**: train a label-free contrastive objective
  on unlabeled prefill activations; if held-out probe AUROC improves, we
  are in the anticausal regime.
- **Algorithmic-ICM compression test**: K(prefill_traj) + K(final_traj) ≈
  K(joint_traj) ⇔ independent mechanisms (Kolmogorov complexity, approximated
  via gzip on quantized trajectories).
