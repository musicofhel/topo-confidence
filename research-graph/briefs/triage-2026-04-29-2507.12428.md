# Triage brief — 2507.12428 — Can We Predict Alignment Before Models Finish Thinking? Towards Monitoring Misaligned Reasoning Models

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12428
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes F-3 (prefill/final-token orthogonality, cos = 0.046).**
   Section 5.2 "Present-trained" probes are fit on the *final-token, last-layer*
   residual at the *end of full CoTs* (Section 3.1) and then applied OOD to
   final-token activations from *partial* CoTs of the same model. They not only
   transfer — they *outperform* probes trained directly at the partial-CoT
   position (Figure 3a vs 3b). This requires the *direction* fit at the late
   position to align well with the predictive direction at much earlier
   positions. Translated to our setup: cos(probe_final-token_pos_500,
   probe_final-token_pos_5) on Qwen-2.5-1.5B L19 should be high (≳0.5), not
   0.046. **Mechanism**: if a single linear direction can monitor alignment
   across positions in their setup, the same should hold across positions in
   ours unless math correctness is mechanistically distinct from refusal.
   **Falsifiable test**: refit DoM at 5–10 distinct token positions during
   MATH-500 generation on the cached Stage 2 NPZs, compute the full pairwise
   cosine matrix. If most cosines stay in [0.00, 0.20] (as F-3 claims for
   pos 0–69), F-3 stands and the safety-vs-correctness asymmetry needs
   explanation. If cosines climb to ≳0.5 once we leave the prefill window, F-3
   is a prefill-only artifact and the orthogonality story collapses.

2. **Refutes F-2 (prefill L19 DoM beats final-token by Δ-AUROC 0.054).**
   Their entire pipeline probes *only* the final-token, last-layer activation
   (Section 3.1). Their best F1/PR-AUC numbers (78–88 PR-AUC, Table 1) come
   from this single position, with no prefill-position probe ever evaluated.
   Their "present-trained" curve hits F1 ≈ 75 at 50% observed CoT and ≈ 80 at
   100% — i.e. final-token alone saturates predictive power. **Mechanism**:
   if final-token activations at the *current* generation step carry the same
   alignment signal as final-token at *future* steps, then "prefill is a
   privileged probe site" is wrong — the signal lives at the moving final
   token, not at a special anchor. **Falsifiable test**: refit a final-token-
   trained DoM on `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/` cache and apply it
   OOD to the prefill activations. If it matches our 0.7731 AUROC within 0.02,
   F-2 is wrong (final-token direction = prefill direction, just measured
   later); if it stays at ~0.7186 AUROC, F-2 holds and the prefill anchor is
   genuinely privileged.

3. **Refutes H-12 (label-free semantic-entropy probes can replace supervised
   DoM for refusal).** Their text-based monitors (GPT-4.1-nano, ModernBERT,
   CoTpara_max/majority) all extract *semantic* signal from CoT content — the
   same kind of signal SEPs (Kossen et al., 2406.15927) try to recover without
   labels. Across 3 RLMs × 3 safety benchmarks, supervised activation probes
   beat the strongest text classifier by 13–18 F1 points (Table 1). The gap
   stems from "performative CoTs" (~11% of data) where CoT *semantics*
   contradict the actual response. **Mechanism**: if the response alignment is
   determined by latent computation that the surface CoT does not faithfully
   reflect, no label-free semantic method can recover it. **Falsifiable test**:
   train a true SEP (label-free, semantic-clustering of responses) on
   StrongReject + s1.1-7B activations, compare to their linear probe. If
   SEP-F1 stays within 2 points of the supervised probe, H-12 holds; if SEP
   collapses on performative CoTs, H-12 is refuted for the refusal setting.

4. **Partial refutation of F-8 (selective-prediction sweet spot at 50%
   coverage = 71.6% accuracy).** Figure 4 shows F1 increases *near-linearly*
   in the proportion of CoT observed — there is no inflection point or sweet
   spot, just a smooth tradeoff. If prefill-DoM selective prediction on math
   has the same shape, then "50% coverage" is not special — it's an arbitrary
   cut. **Mechanism**: alignment-relevant signal accumulates monotonically
   with observed sentences in their setup; if math-correctness signal does
   the same in DoM space, F-8 needs reframing as "any coverage you pick has a
   matched accuracy on this curve". **Falsifiable test**: build the full
   coverage-vs-accuracy curve on `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute/`
   instead of the single 50% point, look for a knee. If linear, F-8 is
   weakened to an arbitrary operating point; if knee at 50%, F-8 stands.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Logistic-regression linear probe on residual stream activations (final
  token of last layer) + PCA-50 dimensionality reduction.**
  Standard LR classifier, 5-fold reseeding, mean ± SE reported.
  *Replication cost*: 20 min CPU on cached NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — this is essentially F-2's pipeline with
  PCA-50 added. PCA preprocessing is the only new ingredient and worth
  testing as a regularizer for the prefill DoM probe.

- **"Future-trained vs present-trained" probe paradigm.** Train at one
  observed/foresight combo, test at another. Decouples "is the direction
  stable?" from "is the signal present at this position?".
  *Replication cost*: 1 h CPU on cached Stage 2 NPZs (need per-token
  activations across many positions).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — this is exactly the experiment that would
  refute F-3 cleanly.

- **Per-sentence CoT segmentation + re-generation with truncated trace.**
  Force the model to terminate after sentence i, generate response A_i, label
  with judge J. Yields {(T_{1:i}, A_i, label_i)} tuples per prompt.
  *Replication cost*: H100 day per model (re-generation is expensive).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — overkill for math correctness where the
  answer is a single token, but useful for H-19 verify-then-correct setup.

- **"Performative CoT" detection filter.** Stability threshold p% (label
  unchanged for >p% of post-midpoint sentences) AND unfaithfulness threshold
  q% (>q% of CoT-text predictions diverge from ground truth). They use
  p=80, q=50 → ~11% of data flagged.
  *Replication cost*: 30 min CPU once we have per-sentence labels.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — useful as a "performative-math-CoT"
  detector to find problems where intermediate CoT predictions diverge from
  K=1 outcome (overlap with B/D-bucket?).

- **Three text-baseline variants for long-context CoT classifiers**
  (CoTfull, CoTpara_max, CoTpara_majority) to handle context-window limits.
  *Replication cost*: trivial.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — orthogonal to our hidden-state pipeline.

- **Data-efficiency curve (50 → 2000 examples).** Compares LR linear probe
  vs Zhang et al. MLP probe; LR effective at 50 samples, MLP fails to F1=0
  at low n.
  *Replication cost*: 1 h CPU on cached NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct test of whether our 91/91 invariant
  numbers would hold under aggressive sample-size reduction (relevant for
  small benchmarks like ABC-44).

## Approaches & framings

- **CoT unfaithfulness as an empirical wedge.** Frames the gap between
  text-based and activation-based monitoring as evidence that "the surface
  CoT does not reflect the latent decision". Intersects our F-3 (prefill and
  final-token live in different subspaces — i.e. *latent* computation may be
  orthogonal to *surface* trajectory).
- **"Alignment determined early."** The eventual response class is encoded
  in the residual stream early; later CoT sentences may be performative
  rather than computational. Direct conceptual sibling of F-2 (prefill ≥
  final-token for prediction): both say "the answer is set early".
- **Forward simulation as the framing for monitor evaluation.** Adopts
  Jacovi & Goldberg (2020) forward-simulation protocol — predict downstream
  behavior from intermediate representations. Useful framing for our F-8
  selective prediction story (currently framed as "decide whether to trust",
  could be reframed as "forward-simulate K=1 correctness").
- **Performative artifacts.** Coins a name for the "the CoT is internally
  consistent but disconnected from the answer" failure mode. Useful for
  reframing our D-bucket (F-7): D-bucket may be the performative-math
  analogue (problem solved at prefill, K=8 sampling diverges anyway).

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Code: https://github.com/BatsResearch/cot-monitor
- Activation extraction: residual stream at *final token position of the
  last layer* — single position, single layer.
- Dimensionality reduction: PCA fit on training split only, reduce d → 50,
  apply to train/val/test (no leakage).
- Probe: scikit-style logistic regression (called "linear probe" throughout).
- Reported with 5 random seeds, mean ± standard error.
- Thinking-budget control: 500 / 2,000 / 4,000 tokens (s1-style budget
  prompting from Muennighoff et al., 2025).
- Judge thresholding: continuous 0–1 judge scores binarized at 0.5.
- Class-imbalance handling: F1 of the *rare class* (typically safe/refusal,
  since RLMs comply ~91% of the time on jailbreaks). Baseline = always-rare
  classifier matched to training-set proportion.
- "Observed/foresight" notation: observed = past CoT sentences available;
  foresight = sentences remaining until final response. Lets them quantify
  "how far ahead can we predict?".

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2507.12428 — Can We Predict Alignment Before Models Finish Thinking? Towards Monitoring Misaligned Reasoning Models (Chan, Yong, Bach, 2025)

**Relevance:** This is the safety-refusal sibling of our F-2 / F-8 program.
They run a logistic-regression linear probe on the residual stream
(final-token, last layer) + PCA-50 of open-weight RLMs (s1.1-7B/14B/32B,
R1-Qwen-7B, R1-Llama-8B), trained to predict whether the *future final
response* will be safe or unsafe given a partial CoT. Across 3 safety
benchmarks × 3 models, the linear probe beats GPT-4.1-nano CoT monitoring
by 13–18 F1 points and reaches 78–88 PR-AUC. Two findings have direct
bearing on us: (1) the probe direction transfers OOD across observed/
foresight combinations — implying a single probe direction works at many
positions in the trajectory, which puts pressure on F-3 orthogonality;
(2) the probe trained at the end-of-CoT *outperforms* probes fit at each
specific position when applied OOD, which puts pressure on F-2's claim
that the prefill anchor is privileged.

**Key claim we tested:** A single linear direction in residual-stream
activations (last-layer, final-token, PCA-50) suffices to monitor a
behavioral property of the final response, and that direction is
*position-stable* across the CoT trajectory.

**Our result:** **TO TEST** Their position-stability claim is testable
on our cached MATH-500 NPZs without new generation (P11-FE73, FE74). If
their claim transfers, F-2 and F-3 both get reframed; if it doesn't, the
safety-vs-correctness geometric asymmetry is itself a finding worth
naming.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE73 (cosine-stability matrix), P11-FE74
(final-token-trained probe applied to prefill), P11-FE75 (PCA-50
ablation), P11-FE76 (coverage curve), P11-FE77 (performative-math-CoT),
H-73, H-74, H-75, H-76.

**Status:** TO TEST
