# Triage brief — 2405.20974 — SaySelf: Teaching LLMs to Express Confidence with Self-Reflective Rationales

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20974
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-8 (selective-prediction via prefill DoM at 50% coverage = 71.6% on answered, K=2.5 avg) could be matched-or-beaten by SaySelf-style RL-trained verbalized confidence with no probe at all.**
   *Mechanism*: SaySelf produces a fine-grained 1–10 confidence integer at decode time, calibrated by PPO with quadratic reward `R = 1 − 2·(I(correct) − conf)²`. On HotpotQA, this drives ECE from baseline DP=0.6667 down to 0.3558 with AUROC competitive with self-consistency — without any hidden-state extraction. If the same recipe applied to Qwen-2.5-1.5B on MATH-500 yields a selective-prediction accuracy at 50% coverage that meets-or-exceeds 71.6%, then F-8's framing — "prefill DoM is the gating signal" — collapses into "RL-tuned verbalized scalar is sufficient; the geometric story was incidental".
   *Falsifiable test*: Apply SaySelf's two-stage SFT + PPO pipeline (or release-grade verbalized-confidence baseline) to Qwen-2.5-1.5B on MATH-500's training split, evaluate selective accuracy at 50% coverage on the held-out 500. If ≥71.6% with K=1, F-8's mechanism claim is dominated.

2. **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731, *better than final-token's 0.7186*) presumes the model's *output-space* confidence is the weak baseline. SaySelf's RL'd verbalized confidence may invert this ordering.**
   *Mechanism*: SaySelf demonstrates that final-state, post-hoc, output-token-level confidence — when properly RL-calibrated against task supervision — can produce AUROC competitive with or exceeding self-consistency on HotpotQA. F-2 silently assumes the probe is needed *because* output-space confidence is poorly calibrated. If SaySelf-trained Qwen-2.5-1.5B yields output-space AUROC ≥ 0.7731 on MATH-500 K=1, then "prefill DoM beats final-token" is a fact about *un-RL'd* models, not a structural property of residual-stream geometry.
   *Falsifiable test*: Reproduce SaySelf training on Qwen-2.5-1.5B, run on MATH-500 with the prefill+final L19 cache, compare verbalized-confidence AUROC against probe AUROC head-to-head.

3. **F-3 (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046, "directions are orthogonal") interpreted as evidence of two independent computations is undercut if a single output-space training signal couples them.**
   *Mechanism*: SaySelf's PPO reward gradient flows through the *generation policy* which spans every token from prefill through final answer. After RL, both prefill and final residual streams should reorganize toward whatever subspace minimizes the calibration loss. F-3's orthogonality is currently measured *pre-RL* on stock Qwen. If post-SaySelf the cosine rises (e.g., to ≥ 0.3), then "prefill-final orthogonality" is a property of the un-RL'd training distribution, not of the architecture or task.
   *Falsifiable test*: Train SaySelf on Qwen-2.5-1.5B (HotpotQA SFT + PPO is ~2 GPU-hours per the paper), then re-extract prefill_DoM and final_DoM on MATH-500 and recompute the cosine. Predict ↑.

4. **H-12 (semantic-entropy probes are the label-free path to refusal) is dominated if SaySelf's pure-self-consistency-cluster-size labels work as well or better.**
   *Mechanism*: SaySelf's confidence target is `c = round(S_c / N · 10)` where `S_c` is the cluster size of the correct response's semantic cluster — *no probes, no entropy estimator, no hidden-state access*. If this yields better calibration than SEPs on a head-to-head OOD evaluation (TruthfulQA, FEVER), the SEP framing in H-12 — "an internal-state estimator gives us label-free uncertainty" — loses its niche; cluster-frequency from output sampling is simpler and competitive.
   *Falsifiable test*: On Qwen-2.5-1.5B, compute SaySelf's cluster-size confidence vs an SEP probe on MATH-500 K=8 samples; compare ECE and AUROC. SaySelf paper reports SC AUROC of 0.3288 on HotpotQA (binary correctness); SEP papers report ~0.7+ on similar tasks; the gap-or-overlap is informative.

5. **F-9 (CoE-60 trajectory features are redundant with single-layer L19 DoM) is *over-stated* as the universal "redundant with DoM" claim if the verbalized scalar that SaySelf emits is also redundant with — or carries information beyond — prefill DoM.**
   *Mechanism*: F-9 claims trajectory features collapse to L19 DoM. SaySelf's confidence is a final-token verbal output that is *not* a trajectory feature but is also not a hidden-state probe. If SaySelf-trained verbalized confidence on Qwen-1.5B *adds* to prefill-DoM predictivity (residual AUROC > 0 after partialling), then "a single L19 direction explains all correctness signal" is wrong — there's at least one independent channel.
   *Falsifiable test*: Logistic regression of correctness on {prefill_L19_DoM, SaySelf_verbal_confidence} vs each alone. Likelihood-ratio test for the verbal feature.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2** (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731): SaySelf reports HotpotQA AUROC 0.3288 for raw self-consistency, ~0.36–0.37 across baselines on HotpotQA — *much* lower than 0.7731 on MATH-500. Either MATH is far easier to discriminate than knowledge-QA, or the prefill DoM probe genuinely beats output-space methods on reasoning tasks. Useful method-differs comparator.
- **F-8** (Selective prediction at 50% coverage = 71.6% on answered, K=2.5 avg): SaySelf's unanswerable-vs-answerable confidence gap on SQUADRUN (∆ = 0.2444) is the closest analogue — it shows verbalized confidence *does* differentiate answerable from unanswerable, but the absolute confidence levels (0.4962 unanswerable, 0.7406 answerable) are coarse. Our F-8 framing as a *coverage-accuracy curve* is more rigorous; SaySelf's contribution is the training recipe, not the evaluation rigor.
- **H-12** (Semantic-entropy probes / label-free uncertainty): SaySelf is a *training-time* alternative — uses cluster-size as label without needing a probe. Method-differs comparator: same goal (label-free calibrated uncertainty), opposite axis (train-into-the-output vs probe-the-state).
- **H-5** (Prefill encodes problem familiarity vs decomposability): SaySelf's self-reflective rationales explicitly verbalize "knowledge gaps" — this is a behavioral readout of what the prefill might be encoding. If we cluster SaySelf rationales on MATH-500 problems, the cluster structure could *operationalize* H-5's "familiarity vs decomposability" axis as a labeled categorization.
- **H-1** (per-position DoM steering): No direct connection — SaySelf is purely output-space; doesn't touch steering.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Cluster-size-as-confidence-label** — sample N=100 reasoning chains, cluster by Instructor embedding similarity (T=0.9), confidence c = round(S_c / N · 10) where S_c is cluster size of correct response.
  *Replication cost*: ~30 min H100 per problem at N=100 + 10 min CPU clustering per 500 problems. For MATH-500: ~4h sample generation, 1h clustering. Already have K=8 per-problem MATH-500 samples in `pathway11_h100/` — could approximate at N=8.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — tag MATH-500 problems by cluster size as a *secondary* label and check whether prefill DoM correlates with cluster-size-derived confidence (proxy for "internal uncertainty signal").

- **GPT-4-summarized self-reflective rationales** — feed clusters of representative responses to GPT-4 with a "summarize why model M is uncertain" prompt; output is the SFT target.
  *Replication cost*: ~$0.10/problem in GPT-4 API calls × 500 = $50, ~3h walltime.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — would let us test whether D-bucket problems (F-7 pathological set) generate qualitatively different rationales than A-bucket problems. Possible cluster-of-rationales analysis to operationalize H-5.

- **Quadratic confidence reward** — `R = 1 − 2·(I(correct) − conf_normalized)²`. Strongly penalizes both under-confident-correct and over-confident-incorrect; ablation shows it beats a linear `R = I(correct)·conf − I(incorrect)·conf` reward (the "Naive RF" row, ECE 0.6129 vs 0.3558 on HotpotQA).
  *Replication cost*: PPO loop already requires reward-fn implementation; this is a 5-line change.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes if we ever PPO-tune Qwen for selective-prediction calibration. Reusable recipe.

- **Two-stage SFT-then-PPO with task-supervision-only reward** — SFT on (q, s, r, c′) tuples, then PPO on (correctness, confidence) reward. Each stage ~1h on 4×A6000 for Mistral-7B / Llama-3-8B. Ablations: w/o RL ECE 0.3704, w/ RL ECE 0.3558 — RL contributes ~5% of the gain; most comes from SFT on rationales+confidence.
  *Replication cost*: ~2h × 4 A6000 ≈ 8h H100-equivalent for Qwen-2.5-1.5B. Tractable.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes for a single FE — gives us a well-calibrated baseline to compare prefill-DoM-based selective prediction against.

- **Inconsistency-driven label generation** — generic recipe: anywhere you have an output that should *quantify* something but doesn't (confidence, difficulty, ambiguity), use cluster-size or cluster-disagreement as the supervision signal.
  *Replication cost*: depends on domain.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: possibly — e.g., generate "difficulty estimates" for MATH-500 problems via cluster-disagreement, compare against P11 D-bucket assignments.

## Approaches & framings

- **Verbalized fine-grained confidence as a first-class output token** — recasts uncertainty from a probe-time analysis into an *in-distribution generation target*. Intersects F-8 by suggesting selective-prediction can be done without any post-hoc probe; intersects F-2/F-3 by hypothesizing that the right architecture-level fix is RL-into-output-space, not direction-mining.
- **Self-reflective rationale as label-free introspection** — model verbalizes *what specifically it's uncertain about*. Intersects H-5 by giving us a behavioral way to label "familiarity vs decomposability" without hand-coding categories.
- **Cluster-size as ground truth for soft-label confidence** — implicit framing: there is no "true" confidence target; consensus among samples *is* the target. Intersects F-9 (single-layer redundancy) by suggesting that consensus-based labels could replace correctness labels for DoM probe training (a low-cost variant of H-12's label-free goal).

## Implementation details worth capturing

- N=100 samples per problem at T=1.2 for SFT data generation. Clustering via Instructor embeddings (instruction-finetuned text embedding model), threshold T=0.9 cosine similarity. Greedy clustering: walk responses, group with first cluster within threshold.
- Correctness check via "annotated answer must appear as substring in response" — heuristic, high precision on knowledge-QA, **would not work for MATH-500** (numerical answers can appear coincidentally; need proper answer extraction).
- SFT learning rate 7e-5, batch size 8. PPO learning rate 1e-5, batch size 8. ~1h each on 4×A6000.
- Reward function quadratic penalty (Eq. 2) crucial — ablation w/ Naive RF (linear) gives ECE 0.6129, much worse than 0.3558 with quadratic.
- Inference is single-shot (no resampling at test time), unlike SC which needs N samples.
- Code: https://github.com/xu1868/SaySelf

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2405.20974 — SaySelf: Teaching LLMs to Express Confidence with Self-Reflective Rationales (Xu et al., EMNLP 2024)

**Relevance:** Method-differs comparator to F-2 / F-8 / H-12. SaySelf is the canonical *output-space* training approach to calibrated fine-grained confidence: use cluster-size from N=100 self-consistency samples as a soft confidence label, SFT on (question, response, GPT-4-rationale, confidence) tuples, then PPO with a quadratic reward `R = 1 − 2·(I(correct) − conf)²` to differentiate confidence levels. No hidden-state probing anywhere; the paper is a clean opposite-axis to our DoM probe story. Reported on Mistral-7B and Llama-3-8B across HotpotQA (in-dist) + 5 OOD QA datasets — drives HotpotQA ECE from 0.6667 (direct prompting) to 0.3558.

**Key claim we tested:** Calibrated fine-grained confidence can be obtained without any hidden-state access — output-space verbalized scalar suffices, given the right training signal (cluster-size labels) and reward (quadratic anti-overconfidence penalty).

**Our result:** **TO TEST** — claim is intriguing as a refutation channel for F-2 / F-3 / F-8 / H-12 but has not been replicated on our regime (Qwen-2.5, MATH-500, 1024-tok labels). H-95 / H-96 / H-97 propose the cheap-to-heavy escalation: cluster-size proxy on cached samples (~30 min CPU), then verbalized-confidence elicitation on stock Qwen (~2h H100), then full SFT+PPO replication (~10h H100 + $50 API). The faithfulness rationale-evaluation protocol (cluster the responses, ask GPT-4 if rationale matches the inconsistencies) is independently reusable and should be cited if we ever publish on D-bucket-A-bucket distinctions (F-7).

**Related experiments:** P11-FE74 (cluster-size proxy), P11-FE75 (verbalized confidence), P11-FE76 (full SaySelf replication), H-95, H-96, H-97.

**Status:** TO TEST.
