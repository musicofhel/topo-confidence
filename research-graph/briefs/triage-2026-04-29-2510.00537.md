# Triage brief — 2510.00537 — Spectral Scaling Laws in Language Models: How Effectively Do Feed-Forward Networks Use Their Latent Space?

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00537
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-1 (dimensional breathing) may be a width-allocation artifact, not a reasoning signature.**
   *Mechanism.* Jha & Reagen show the **post-activation spectrum of FFNs grows tail-first**: soft rank scales ~D¹ while hard rank (= participation ratio) scales only ~D^0.5 with high noise. Since residual streams are the running sum of FFN outputs, our prefill-PR ≈ 20 → mid-CoT PR ≈ 60–115 → final-token PR ≈ 2–18 trajectory could simply be tracking how many low-energy tail directions a SwiGLU FFN happens to populate at each position. If breathing largely reflects FFN tail occupancy varying with token type rather than a property of "reasoning," F-1's strength claim weakens.
   *Falsifiable test.* On cached Qwen-2.5-1.5B activations, separately compute PR on (i) full residual stream, (ii) attention output only, (iii) FFN output only, at L19 across positions. If the breathing curve appears in (iii) but is flat or muted in (ii), F-1 is a tail-allocation phenomenon, not a reasoning phenomenon. If breathing is preserved in (ii) (attention-only), F-1 survives.

2. **F-7 (D-bucket distinctive collective signature) may collapse if we re-measure with eDim instead of bare PR.**
   *Mechanism.* The paper argues that PR alone is *misleading* — it ignores meaningful tail growth and conflates spectra of very different shapes. They advocate the harmonic-mean composite **SUI / eDim = 1 + (D−1)·SUI** which only rises when *both* dominant and tail capacities improve. F-7's "distinctive geometry" is currently defined via PR/CoE-60 features that are dominant-mode sensitive. If recomputing the D-bucket signature with eDim or with (PR̃, eR̃, SC) triples shows the D-bucket lies *inside* the same spectral region as the A-bucket, the "collective signature" claim is undercut.
   *Falsifiable test.* On the cached pathway11_h100 NPZs, compute (Hard Rank, Soft Rank, Spectral Concentration, SUI, eDim) per problem at L19 prefill, and run a 3-way classifier across A/B/C/D buckets. If the D-bucket separates only in raw PR but not in SUI/eDim, the signature is dominant-mode-only and likely a tail-occupancy fluctuation.

3. **H-4 (breathing ↔ Sharpness Dimension formal correspondence) gets a competing — and earlier — explanation: spectral bias / tail-first growth.**
   *Mechanism.* The paper explicitly frames the asymmetric scaling as a consequence of spectral bias (Rahaman et al. 2019): "capacity is allocated unevenly across modes." If breathing dynamics during CoT are governed by the same tail-first-growth dynamic that dictates training-time spectral utilization, then the EoS / Sharpness Dimension correspondence H-4 hypothesizes is *one of several formally equivalent framings*, not a uniquely good one. We may have overweighted EoS as the source theory.
   *Falsifiable test.* Fit the residual-stream eigenvalue spectrum at L19 across positions to a truncated power law λ_k ∝ k^{−α} (their §4.3). If α stays in the 1.1–1.3 "intermediate" band that the paper reports for trained LLaMA, breathing is spectral-bias-driven and H-4's Sharpness/EoS framing is redundant. If α swings sharply with position (e.g., α ≈ 2.0 at prefill, α ≈ 0.8 at mid-CoT), we have evidence that breathing is *not* a static spectral-bias signature and H-4 retains a unique testable prediction.

4. **H-16 (Marchenko-Pastur bias correction) may be the wrong null for residual-stream PR.**
   *Mechanism.* The paper computes covariance with **all N tokens, no subsampling, no MP correction** ("we compute the covariance using all N tokens without any sub-sampling or statistical approximations to capture the true behavior of the model"). Their power-law fits succeed without MP correction. If their results — including the Asymmetric Scaling Law — replicate cleanly without MP, the relevant noise floor for residual-stream PR may be *spectral-bias-shaped*, not Wishart-shaped, and H-16's MP-corrected breathing magnitude could over- or under-correct.
   *Falsifiable test.* On the cached prefill NPZ, compute breathing PR three ways: (a) raw, (b) MP-corrected, (c) re-fit to a truncated power law λ_k ∝ k^{−α} and use eDim. If (b) and (c) disagree on which positions count as "saturated dominant subspace," H-16 needs to broaden into a comparison of nulls rather than committing to MP.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-1 (dimensional breathing universal):** Direct methodological overlap. Both works use Participation Ratio (Gao et al. 2017) as the headline dimensionality metric. Their finding that PR-based hard rank is "noisy" (R² ≈ 0.24–0.68 across LLaMA scales) and that **eDim** (their composite) is more stable should make us nervous about reporting headline PR ≈ 60–115 for breathing without confidence intervals derived from a stable composite. *Numerical anchor:* the headline final-token PR collapse to 2–18 (F-1 evidence) lands inside the high-α "spectral collapse" regime they report (α > 1.5, top-10% of PCs hold > 90% variance) — consistent, suggesting final-token state is *spectrally collapsed* in their formal sense.
- **F-7 (D-bucket collective signature):** The paper's notion that "tail capacity" and "dominant-mode capacity" are decoupled axes is exactly the framing H-7 / F-7 should be tested under. If D-bucket differs from A-bucket on tail (soft rank) but not dominant (hard rank), that is a much sharper claim than "distinctive geometry."
- **H-4 (breathing ↔ Sharpness Dimension):** Tail-first growth provides an alternative, training-time mechanism that may already explain inference-time PR dynamics without invoking EoS. This makes H-4 *less unique* but also gives it a clean falsification target.
- **H-16 (Marchenko-Pastur correction):** Direct competitor null model. Their truncated-power-law fitting (§4.3) is a methodological alternative to MP correction that may be more appropriate for trained transformers (echoes Martin & Mahoney 2021 heavy-tailed self-regularization, which they cite).
- **H-22 (Abstract-CoT shows compressed breathing):** The eDim metric and the SUI-vs-α decomposition would be a more powerful test than raw PR for distinguishing "compressed" from "qualitatively different" breathing curves.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Participation Ratio (Hard Rank, PR = (Σλᵢ)² / Σλᵢ²)** — already in use (F-1). One-line: dominant-mode counter, spike-sensitive.
  *Replication cost*: trivial — already implemented in P11.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — already do; would benefit from re-running in their normalized form PR̃ = (PR−1)/(D−1).

- **Soft Spectral Rank (Shannon Rank, eR = exp(−Σpᵢ log pᵢ) where pᵢ = λᵢ/Σλⱼ)** — entropy of the eigenvalue distribution as probability.
  *Replication cost*: 20min CPU on existing cached covariance matrices.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives a complementary tail-sensitive view of breathing not captured by PR.

- **Spectral Concentration (SC, area between cumulative-eigen curve and uniform baseline)** — front-loadedness of variance.
  *Replication cost*: 20min CPU on existing covariance matrices.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly tests whether final-token "PR collapse" is also "front-loadedness collapse" (i.e. variance concentrating into 1–2 directions, which is testable independently of PR).

- **Spectral Utilization Index (SUI = 2·PR̃·eR̃ / (PR̃ + eR̃))** — harmonic mean of normalized hard and soft ranks.
  *Replication cost*: 5min CPU once HR/SR computed.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — single robust composite for cross-model breathing comparisons (1.5B vs 7B vs Phi vs Llama, F-1 evidence).

- **Effective Dimension (eDim = 1 + (D−1)·SUI)** — interpretable count of active principal components.
  *Replication cost*: free once SUI computed.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — replaces raw PR in narrative claims when comparing across layer widths or token positions.

- **Truncated-power-law fit λₖ ∝ k^{−α} on the eigenspectrum** — characterizes the *shape* of the spectrum, distinguishing dilution (α < 1) from collapse (α > 1.5).
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU on existing covariance matrices.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — gives a one-number summary of "where breathing sits on the dilution↔collapse axis" at each position.

- **Differentiable spectral diagnostics during training** — they advertise these as differentiable so they can be used as regularizers/loss terms.
  *Replication cost*: high — requires retraining or fine-tuning a model.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — we don't pretrain. Worth flagging if H-22 (Abstract-CoT fine-tune) ever runs.

## Approaches & framings

- **Reframe dimensionality measurement as a *trade-off between dilution and collapse*, not a single number.** This intersects F-1 directly: our breathing curve goes from collapsed (prefill, PR ≈ 20) to dilute-ish (mid-CoT, PR ≈ 60+) to maximally collapsed (final, PR ≈ 2–18). Their two-failure-mode framing (dilution vs. collapse, both bad in different ways) gives a sharper vocabulary than "high PR / low PR."
- **Tail-first growth as a unifying principle linking spectral bias (function space) to activation eigenspectra (representation space).** Implies that breathing dynamics may be a *projection* of training-time spectral bias onto inference-time positional dynamics. Reframes H-4 as a special case of a broader spectral-bias story.
- **LayerNorm placement as a spectral lever.** They show Pre-LN vs Post-LN vs Mix-LN substantially shift the asymmetry. Qwen-2.5 uses Pre-LN with RMSNorm; if F-1 breathing depends on LayerNorm regime, we should expect *different* breathing patterns in Post-LN families (BERT, original GPT-2). EXP-040 already tested Phi-3 and Llama-3.2 (both Pre-LN/RMSNorm); a Post-LN replication would be a sharp falsification target.
- **eDim as a layer-wise efficiency monitor.** During inference, plotting eDim across the 28 Qwen layers at each position gives a "saturation map" that may reveal which layers are doing the breathing and which are passive — a finer-grained successor to F-1's "mid-generation peak."

## Implementation details worth capturing

- **Width sweep convention:** D = αd with α ∈ {1, 2, 2.67, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8} — useful framing if we ever need to think about why Qwen has α ≈ 5.4 for FFN width.
- **Hyperspherical normalization (HNorm) and weight normalization (WNorm) as collapse remedies in Post-LN training.** Not directly applicable but flags that **nGPT** (Loshchilov et al. 2025) is a hyperspherically-constrained model whose residual-stream geometry should be qualitatively different from standard transformers — a potential cross-architecture replication target for F-1.
- **SUI / eDim closed-form** is *parameter-free* and runs in O(D) once eigenvalues are known. Trivial to add to existing P11 pipeline.
- The paper does *not* release code (no GitHub link in the body). Implementation must be re-derived from definitions in Table 1.

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2510.00537 — Spectral Scaling Laws in Language Models: How Effectively Do Feed-Forward Networks Use Their Latent Space? (Jha and Reagen, 2025)

**Relevance:** Methodologically adjacent to F-1 (dimensional breathing) and H-4 (Sharpness/EoS correspondence). The paper formalizes a four-metric spectral-utilization suite (Hard Rank = Participation Ratio, Soft Rank = Shannon entropy of eigenvalues, Spectral Concentration, SUI / eDim) and establishes an "Asymmetric Spectral Scaling Law" for FFN width (soft rank ~D¹, hard rank ~D^0.5) on LLaMA, GPT-2, and nGPT trained from scratch on C4 at 70M–250M parameters. The dilution-vs-collapse failure-mode framing is directly applicable to our prefill→mid-CoT→final breathing trajectory; the eDim composite is a more robust narrative substrate than raw PR; and the tail-first-growth principle offers a competing null for F-1 ("breathing is FFN tail occupancy modulating with token type") and a competitor framing for H-4 (spectral bias as a more general source theory than EoS).

**Key claim we tested:** *(Their primary claim is about training-time width scaling on FFNs, which we cannot test directly — we don't pretrain. Their secondary methodological claim — that PR alone is misleading and eDim/SUI is the right composite — we can test on cached residual-stream covariance matrices.)* Translated into our terms: "the breathing trajectory should be re-narrated using eDim/SUI rather than raw PR, and an FFN-vs-attention decomposition should determine whether breathing is reasoning-specific or FFN-tail-driven."

**Our result:** **TO TEST.** Cached pathway11_h100 covariance matrices support the eDim recomputation (P11-FE73, ~1h CPU) and the truncated-power-law α-fit (P11-FE75) immediately. The FFN-vs-attention decomposition (P11-FE74) requires an H100 re-extraction since current NPZs hold only the summed residual.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE73, P11-FE74, P11-FE75, P11-FE76, H-87, H-88.
