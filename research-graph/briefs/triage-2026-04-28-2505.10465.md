# Triage brief — 2505.10465 — Superposition Yields Robust Neural Scaling

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10465
Triaged: 2026-04-28

Authors: Yizhou Liu, Ziming Liu, Jeff Gore (2025; NeurIPS 2025 Best Paper Runner-up).

Headline: weight-decay-controlled superposition in Anthropic's toy model produces a generic L ∝ 1/m scaling under "strong superposition", and they argue open-source LLMs (OPT/GPT-2/Pythia/Qwen, ~100M–70B) sit in that regime with measured α_m = 0.91 ± 0.04, broadly consistent with Chinchilla's α_N reinterpreted as 2.52·α_N = 0.88 ± 0.06.

## Refutations

1. **F-3 (prefill ⊥ final-token DoM, cos=0.046) may be a superposition-geometry artefact, not a "two-circuit" claim.**
   Mechanism: under strong superposition, Liu et al. predict any two task-derived unit vectors in residual space behave like ETF members with squared overlap ⟨cos²⟩ ≈ 1/m and Beta(1/2, (m-1)/2)-distributed cos². For Qwen2.5-1.5B (d_model=1536) the geometric floor is 1/√1536 ≈ 0.0255 and ⟨cos²⟩ ≈ 6.5e-4. Our observed cos=0.046 → cos²=0.0021 ≈ 3.2/m — only ~3× the random-ETF floor. So F-3's "orthogonal directions imply distinct computational circuits" framing is potentially uninformative: orthogonality is what you'd see by default in a strong-superposition regime even if the directions encoded the same logical feature with different "labels."
   Falsification: build a null distribution of cos between two *random* logistic probes trained on the same prefill activations with random label-shuffles; if the empirical 0.046 lies inside the 95% interval of that null, F-3's interpretation collapses.

2. **F-1 / F-5 (content-dependent dimensional breathing) is in tension with strong-superposition's prediction of a near-flat participation-ratio floor.**
   Mechanism: Liu et al. argue trained-LLM representations are "ETF-like with minimal variance" and the squared-overlap distribution concentrates at 1/m — which means the participation ratio of *any* batch of residual-stream activations should be pinned near m (the geometric ceiling) regardless of which features are active. F-1 reports ~5–10× swings in PR over generation, and F-5 shows random-token PR is flat. If Liu et al. are right, the breathing curve is not "feature-load fluctuation" but the residual deviation between the empirical Gram and the ETF target — i.e. the breathing magnitude reduces to a *bias-correction* term, not a computational state variable.
   Falsification: re-derive PR as PR_observed − PR_ETF where PR_ETF is the participation ratio of the closed-form ETF Gram for n_features estimated from token vocabulary size. If the corrected curve is flat or noise, F-1 and F-5 lose their "breathing tracks computation" reading.

3. **F-10 (PH at the Gaussian null on trained residual streams) is *predicted by* strong superposition and therefore is not a null result.**
   Mechanism: ETF-like geometry with ⟨cos²⟩ ≈ 1/m gives pairwise distance distributions that are asymptotically indistinguishable from Gaussian in moderate dimension — so any topology measure that compares against an iid Gaussian null *will* see no excess persistence by construction. F-10 is currently filed as "no signal beyond Gaussian"; under Liu et al.'s lens, F-10 is "trained representations achieved the geometric optimum predicted by strong superposition," which is a positive finding and predicts that *fine-tuned* or *intermediate-checkpoint* models should show non-null PH.
   Falsification: rerun PH on Pythia checkpoints at 10M / 100M / 1B steps from the public scaling suite; if persistence diagrams converge to the Gaussian null monotonically with training compute, F-10 reframes from "null" to "ETF-convergence test."

4. **H-9 (breathing amplitude predicts problem difficulty) directly contradicts Liu et al.'s feature-frequency-only loss decomposition.**
   Mechanism: Liu et al. derive L = ⟨v²⟩ Σ_{i>ϕ_{1/2}n} p_i (weak regime) and L ∝ 1/m via ETF interference (strong regime). In *both* regimes loss is set by *aggregate* feature frequency, not per-problem activation-vector dynamics — so a per-problem geometric variable (breathing amplitude) shouldn't carry difficulty information beyond what's captured by the feature-frequency prior. If H-9 holds (correlation between PR-curve amplitude and difficulty), Liu et al.'s decomposition is incomplete and per-problem geometry encodes computation that the superposition theory does not predict.
   Falsification: H-9 is already proposed; running it answers this directly. Strong negative correlation refutes Liu et al. on this point; null correlation refutes H-9 and confirms Liu et al.

5. **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731 > final-token 0.7186) sits in tension with Liu et al.'s prediction that all geometric directions in a strong-superposition regime carry comparable interference noise.**
   Mechanism: under ETF-like geometry, the projection of correctness labels onto any unit direction should give comparable signal up to the 1/√m noise floor. F-2's 0.05-AUROC gap between prefill and final-token L19 DoM is *not* explained by superposition theory — superposition would predict no preferred temporal index. Either F-2's gap is real (Liu et al. underspecify), or the gap collapses under proper bias correction.
   Falsification: bootstrap CI on the AUROC gap with cos²-noise correction; if the gap survives, F-2 stands and Liu et al.'s "anywhere works equally well" prediction fails for correctness probing.

## Methodologies extracted

- **Decoupled weight-decay control of superposition strength** — train a 2-layer ReLU autoencoder (Anthropic toy-model setup) with γ ≥ 0 (standard decay) or γ < 0 (unit-norm constraint, W_{i,t+1} = W_{i,t} − η_t γ W_{i,t}(1/‖W_{i,t}‖₂ − 1)). Lets you sweep "weak" → "strong" superposition without changing model size.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU for a small toy model; 4h H100 to fine-tune Qwen2.5-1.5B with modified weight decay on a held-out set.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — useful as a generator of training-time-controlled hidden-state geometries, for testing whether PR and DoM AUROC track γ.

- **ϕ_{1/2} feature-fraction observable** — fraction of representation columns with ‖W_i‖₂ > 1/2; ϕ_{1/2} ≈ m/n marks the weak↔strong boundary.
  *Replication cost*: 10min CPU on cached W matrices.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — direct diagnostic for whether Qwen's residual-to-vocab unembedding sits in strong superposition.

- **Squared-overlap histogram vs Beta(1/2, (m-1)/2) reference** — compute ⟨cos²(W_i, W_j)⟩ across all unembedding columns or DoM directions, compare to closed-form Beta distribution.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU on a Qwen unembedding matrix.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — provides the exact null we need for refutation 1 (F-3 cos=0.046 vs ETF floor).

- **Power-law feature-frequency fit α from token-frequency** — fit p_i ∝ 1/i^α to corpus token frequencies; relate to scaling exponent α_m via α_m ≈ 2(α−1) (strong) or α−1 (weak).
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU with The Pile or RedPajama token counts.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: maybe — only if we want to predict α_m for Qwen and test against measured loss curves; not directly relevant to MATH-500 correctness probing.

- **Chinchilla cross-check via α_m = 2.52·α_N reinterpretation** — translates Chinchilla's compute-optimal exponent α_N into a width exponent α_m = 0.88 ± 0.06 by assuming L ∝ 1/m.
  *Replication cost*: paper math only.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — outside our scope (we don't claim scaling laws).

## Approaches & framings

- **Loss as additive feature-frequency residual** (L = ⟨v²⟩ Σ_{i>ϕ_{1/2}n} p_i) — reframes "what the model does well" as "which features have ‖W_i‖ > threshold." Intersects F-7 (D-bucket signature): a D-bucket problem might be one whose latent feature index i lies just above ϕ_{1/2}n — barely-represented and therefore brittle to sampling.
- **Strong-superposition regime as "default geometric prior"** — the implication is that any geometric measure (cosine, PR, persistence, kernel alignment) on a trained residual stream needs an ETF-baseline subtraction before it's interpreted. This is the methodological move that, if right, retroactively reframes F-1, F-3, F-10 — and validates H-16 — as bias-correction problems rather than discoveries.
- **"Vocabulary-size-bound" scaling-breakdown prediction** — Liu et al. predict L → 0 as m approaches true linguistic-feature count. Doesn't intersect our problem domain directly but suggests a bound on how informative dimensional breathing can be at very large m.
- **Skewed vs even feature-frequency dichotomy** — gives two regimes (α_m = α−1 weak vs 2(α−1) strong). If breathing is feature-load fluctuation, peak PR should track effective active-feature count, which depends on regime — testable by comparing PR curves on naturally-frequent token spans (high p_i) vs rare-token spans.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- **Toy-model architecture**: 2-layer ReLU autoencoder, n input features, m hidden, n inputs sparse (Bernoulli·Uniform), squared-error loss. Code referenced as based on Anthropic's published toy-models repo.
- **Decoupled weight decay** with both signs of γ, including unit-norm projection branch — non-standard, requires patching optimizer step.
- **ϕ_{1/2} diagnostic** is the simplest "are we in strong superposition?" check — one number per layer.
- **Beta(1/2, (m-1)/2) reference** for cos² is the closed-form null we need.
- **Predicted exponent**: α_m ≈ 1 in strong-regime, even-frequency limit; α_m = 0.91 ± 0.04 measured in LLMs is the headline empirical anchor.
- **Code link**: not extracted from the HTML/PDF on this pass — would need to check the paper's GitHub link in the references section if we replicate.

## 2505.10465 — Superposition Yields Robust Neural Scaling (Liu, Liu, Gore, 2025)

**Relevance:** Reframes residual-stream geometry as ETF-like by construction in the strong-superposition regime, with squared overlap ⟨cos²⟩ ≈ 1/m and trained representations near the geometric optimum. **Key claim we tested:** Trained LLMs sit in a strong-superposition regime where any pair of unit directions in residual space has cos² distributed as Beta(1/2, (m-1)/2) with mean 1/m, and the participation ratio of the residual stream is pinned near the ETF ceiling.

Outcome will determine whether 2505.10465 is REPLICATED-in-our-setting (forcing reinterpretation of three findings) or CITED ONLY (Qwen is in weak superposition, prediction doesn't transfer).
