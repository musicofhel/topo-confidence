# Triage brief — 2604.24662 — Information bottleneck for learning the phase space of dynamics from high-dimensional experimental data (DySIB)

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24662
Triaged: 2026-06-14

Authors: K. Michael Martini, Eslam Abdelaleem, Paarth Gulati, Ilya Nemenman (2026).

One-line: DySIB (Dynamical Symmetric Information Bottleneck) learns low-dimensional, interpretable dynamical state variables from high-dimensional time-series (pendulum video) **entirely in latent space**, by maximizing predictive mutual information between compressed past and future windows (InfoNCE) while a KL penalty + bottleneck dimension regularize — no reconstruction. A residual "δ-predictor" (`z_y = z_x + μ_δ(z_x)`) encodes differential dynamics. The MI-saturation-under-DPI criterion *self-consistently* reads off the intrinsic latent dimension `k_z` (=2 for the pendulum) and the temporal order `n_F` (=2, the frames needed to resolve velocity).

## Refutations

This is a physics/representation-learning paper, not an LLM paper, so refutations come from *method transfer*: applying DySIB's machinery to our cached residual-stream trajectories would, if it fires, overturn specific claims.

1. **Refutes F-3 (prefill/final-token DoM are *structurally* orthogonal, cos = 0.046 / −0.0617 raw / 0.0008 residualized).** *Mechanism*: F-3 measures the angle between two **static** linear directions extracted at two positions. DySIB's δ-predictor models the future latent as a small residual increment of the past latent (`z_y = z_x + μ_δ(z_x)`, Eq. 9). If the prefill→final-token residual stream is one smooth low-dimensional **dynamical manifold**, then "prefill DoM" and "final DoM" are not orthogonal *axes* but two *states along one trajectory*, and the near-zero cosine is a coordinate artifact of comparing tangent directions at distant points of a curved flow rather than evidence of "geometrically unrelated subspaces." *Falsifiable*: train a DySIB δ-predictor mapping cached prefill latent → final-token latent (X = prefill cloud, Y = final-token cloud) on the 1.5B L19 cache; if InfoNCE predictive MI is high and `μ_δ` is small/smooth, F-3's "two unrelated circuits" framing is wrong — they are one dynamical process. (Parallels the existing H-926 flow recasting but supplies a concrete latent-dynamics estimator.)

2. **Refutes F-10 (topology summary statistics on residual streams = matched-cov Gaussian null).** *Mechanism*: F-10 ran PH on **static point clouds** at a single layer/position and found PH ≈ Gaussian null (post-PC1 PH gap −0.074). DySIB recovers the *cylinder topology* of a dynamical system not from static PH descriptors but from **predictive MI across the temporal/across-layer trajectory** — the topology lives in the *flow*, not the instantaneous cloud. If the across-layer residual trajectory carries non-trivial recoverable latent topology (e.g. a closed loop / non-contractible coordinate) that a same-layer Gaussian-null PH test cannot see, F-10's generalization "residual-stream topology carries no signal" is too strong. *Falsifiable*: build a DySIB latent on the 28-layer per-problem trajectory, then run an intrinsic-dimension / loop-detection check on the learned latent; if the latent has non-trivial structure distinguishable from a Gaussian-trajectory null, the "no topology" verdict is layer/position-confined, not universal.

3. **Refutes F-9 (CoE-60 trajectory features are redundant with single-layer L19 DoM).** *Mechanism*: DySIB's `n_F`-saturation result shows a *second-order* system is unresolvable from a single frame — you need ≥2 frames to recover the "velocity" coordinate (Fig. 4). The analogue: if the correctness-predictive coordinate is "velocity-like" (a rate-of-change across layers/tokens), a single layer (n_F = 1) is *structurally incapable* of capturing it, and any single-layer probe (DoM at L19) is reading only the "position-like" component. F-9 concludes the trajectory adds nothing beyond one layer — DySIB predicts the opposite for any rate-like signal. *Falsifiable*: run the predictive-MI-to-correctness measurement at n_F = 1 vs n_F = 2 over the layer trajectory; if correctness MI jumps only at n_F ≥ 2, single-layer DoM is provably leaving signal on the table, contradicting F-9.

4. **Refines/refutes F-1 (dimensional "breathing" is a universal property).** *Mechanism*: F-1 reads per-layer participation-ratio / d_eff and calls its layer-to-layer variation "breathing." DySIB supplies a *principled, second-moment-independent* intrinsic-dimension estimator: the smallest `k_z` at which InfoNCE MI saturates under the data-processing inequality (Fig. 3), which is invariant to the eigenvalue *magnitudes* PR/d_eff are sensitive to. If the MI-saturation intrinsic dimension is approximately constant across layers where PR "breathes," then breathing is a covariance-spectrum (second-moment) artifact, not a change in true dynamical degrees of freedom — refuting F-1's mechanistic reading. *Falsifiable*: compute DySIB MI-saturation `k_z` per layer on cached activations and overlay on the PR/d_eff breathing curve; constant `k_z` under varying PR refutes the "breathing = changing DoF" interpretation.

## Methodologies extracted

- **DySIB / DVSIB objective** — `L = Ĩ_E(X;Z_X) + Ĩ_E(Y;Z_Y) − β·Ĩ_NCE(Z_X;Z_Y)` (Eq. 13): two variational Gaussian encoders compress past/future windows, InfoNCE lower-bounds the latent–latent MI, KL terms upper-bound the compression. *Replication cost*: small NNs (3-layer MLP width 256 encoder, width-64 δ-predictor) — trainable on CPU for our 500×1536 cache in ~1–2 hr. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — it's a tiny model and the inputs are cached activations, but the correctness-prediction variant is moot under refuted premises (see FEs).
- **δ-predictor (residual latent-dynamics critic)** — `r(z_y|z_x) = N(z_x + μ_δ(z_x), diag(exp(ℓ_δ(z_x))))` (Eq. 10): models the future latent as a small differential increment of the past, serving as the InfoNCE critic. *Replication cost*: trivial (one small MLP) once latents exist; ~20–40 min CPU. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly tests F-3 / H-926 prefill→final flow structure.
- **Tied (shared) past/future encoder** — same DNN encodes X and Y to enforce time-translation invariance; overlapping windows (n_s < n_F) still non-trivial because shared bottleneck encodes overlapping frames differently. *Replication cost*: a constraint, free. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes (it's the right inductive bias for a layer trajectory).
- **MI-saturation-under-DPI intrinsic-dimension selection** — take the smallest `k_z` at which InfoNCE MI increments fall inside the error bars; same criterion on `n_F` gives temporal order. *Replication cost*: a sweep over `k_z`/`n_F`, ~1–2 hr CPU total. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — a second-moment-free dimensionality estimator for F-1.
- **InfoNCE MI estimator with explicit error bars** — batch-contrastive `Ĩ_NCE` (Eq. 12) with bootstrap error bars [55,59] used as the saturation stopping rule. *Replication cost*: standard, included in DySIB train loop. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes.
- **Random-feature linear probe + tiny-MLP probe on frozen latent** — low-capacity readouts that check whether target variables are *already accessible* in the latent, not reconstructable only by a powerful model. *Replication cost*: minutes CPU. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — exactly our DoM/correctness probe protocol; transfers directly.
- **Overparameterized bottleneck for variance reduction** — choosing `k_z` larger than the intrinsic dimension reduces seed-to-seed variability without changing intrinsic dimensionality (Fig. S4). *Replication cost*: free design choice. *We'd plausibly run this*: yes.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Prediction in latent space, not data space" (JEPA-aligned).** The model never reconstructs observations; all information lives in the compressed latent. *Intersection*: our whole program reads *static* latent directions; DySIB reframes the question as "what predicts the *next* latent state," which is the temporal/across-layer axis F-3/F-9 sidestep.
- **Dynamics as differential increments (residual δ-flow).** Physics lives in small changes to state, encoded as a residual connection in latent space. *Intersection*: directly recasts F-3's prefill→final orthogonality and gives H-926 its estimator.
- **Self-consistent dimensionality via MI saturation (DPI bound).** Intrinsic dimension and temporal order are *read off the data*, not chosen by hand or by eigenvalue thresholds. *Intersection*: a magnitude-free alternative to PR/d_eff "breathing" (F-1) and to participation-ratio dimension estimates throughout the program.
- **Information-relevant ≠ reconstruction-relevant.** Autoencoders preserve all salient variation including dynamics-irrelevant variability; predictive IB keeps only what predicts the future. *Intersection*: mirrors our own finding that PH/cov descriptors integrate over and lose the load-bearing shape (F-10 sharpening); same "the readout determines what survives" logic.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Frame encoder Φ: 3-layer MLP, hidden width 256, ReLU. Heads Ψ_μ, Ψ_ℓ: parallel linear maps (linear heads "nearly perfect" for this simple problem; deeper heads needed for longer `n_F`/complex systems).
- δ-predictor: 3-layer MLP, hidden width 64, ReLU.
- Gaussian variational posteriors with reparameterization trick; standard-normal priors for the KL/compression terms.
- β fixed = 100 (objective primarily predictive; bottleneck dimension `k_z` is the *primary* regularizer, KL only weak regularization).
- Stride n_s = 1 (overlapping windows) in main analysis; non-overlapping windows give qualitatively similar results (App. C2).
- Per parameter value, report the run with **highest test MI across 10 seeds** (filters failed local optima); all MI/probe metrics on a 200-trajectory held-out test set.
- Sample efficiency: MI grows ~log₂(N); accurate θ recovery needs only ~4–8 trajectories, ω needs ~256 — predictive-MI latents are extremely data-cheap (relevant: our cache is only 500 problems).
- No code link surfaced in the extracted text; builds on DVSIB [47] (Abdelaleem et al.) whose implementation may be public.
