# Triage brief — 2604.20817 — Convergent Evolution: How Different Language Models Learn Similar Number Representations

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20817
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **F-1 (universality of dimensional breathing) — risk of "spectral-but-not-geometric" universality.**
   F-1 claims dimensional breathing is universal across architectures. This paper documents a precise mode in which surface universality dissociates from functional universality: LSTMs, classical word embeddings, and even the raw token-frequency distribution all reproduce the same Fourier spikes at T=2,5,10 as Transformers, yet only Transformers and linear RNNs achieve linearly separable mod-T classes (Cohen's κ=85.4 vs ~0). By analogy, our claim that breathing PR-traces appear in 1.5B / 7B Qwen / Llama / Mistral may be the LSTM-style of universality — present in spectrum, absent in function.
   *Mechanism*: a periodic feature contributes Tr(S_B) but condition-number of within-class scatter S_W can swallow the signal (Theorem 1, part ii).
   *Falsifiable test*: re-extract the breathing PR trace on an LSTM-only or Mamba-Vanilla model on MATH-500 and ask whether the trace is followed by a non-trivial L19 prefill DoM linear probe. If breathing PR is preserved but probe AUROC ≈ 0.5, F-1 universality is the spectral kind, not the functional kind, and should be re-scoped.

2. **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731) — between-class scatter could be a frequency artifact, not a learned correctness axis.**
   Theorem 1 part (ii) explicitly constructs embeddings with arbitrarily large Φ_T but no linear separability, governed by the eigenstructure of S_W^{-1} S_B. The converse risk is that an *apparently* good linear probe (AUROC 0.7731) is supported by a between-class direction whose alignment with within-class noise is unfavorable but happens to project onto the logistic decision boundary anyway.
   *Mechanism*: what we call "DoM = mean(correct) − mean(incorrect)" is, in our setting, the principal axis of class-mean dispersal — exactly the quantity the paper deconstructs. If cond(S_W) is large for L19 prefill activations, the discriminant ratio λ_max(S_W^{-1} S_B) may be much smaller than the AUROC suggests, indicating most lift is class-mean dispersion rather than class-discriminative geometry.
   *Falsifiable test*: compute Fisher's λ_max(S_W^{-1} S_B), Tr(S_B)/N, and cond(S_W) on the cached L19 prefill NPZs (correct vs incorrect). If λ_max bound from Theorem 1 collapses to chance while AUROC stays at 0.77, the probe is exploiting an artifact direction (e.g., problem length, problem topic) and F-2 is a confound.

3. **F-7 (D-bucket distinctive geometric signature) — could be class-mean dispersion without separable axis.**
   F-7 says D-bucket has a collective geometric signature distinct from A/B/C. The paper's Theorem 1 says visible structure in spectra (or in our case, in 2D projections of class means) does not imply linear separability when within-class scatter is anisotropic.
   *Mechanism*: D-bucket might just have a different mean μ_D in residual-stream coordinates while overlapping heavily with A/B/C in the relevant separability sense — exactly the LSTM regime.
   *Falsifiable test*: train a 4-class logistic probe on cached L19 features for A/B/C/D problems with stratified sampling. Report Cohen's κ (not accuracy or AUROC). If κ_D is low while μ-distance is high, D-bucket is a between-class scatter artifact and F-7 needs revision.

4. **Methodology-level refutation (concerns the whole probe-AUROC pipeline).**
   The paper's central methodological warning is that representation-level diagnostics can mistake training-distribution artifacts for learned structure. The structure-attribution recipe (Swap Numbers / Isolate-k / Unigram Replace) controls for this. We have not done the analog: a "Swap Labels" ablation (shuffle correct/incorrect labels but keep the question text distribution) that would tell us how much of L19 DoM AUROC survives without the actual correctness signal. Length-band, topic-band, and unigram-replace ablations are missing.
   *Mechanism*: linear probes on hidden states reflect any directional structure correlated with the label, including length, problem topic frequency, and prompt-token-frequency confounds.
   *Falsifiable test*: shuffle correct/incorrect labels within length-bands of MATH-500, refit the L19 prefill DoM linear probe. Repeat 30 times. If the AUROC distribution overlaps the original 0.7731, F-2 is partially a length-artifact. (This is the Swap-Numbers analog to our pipeline.)

## Methodologies extracted

- **Fisher's Linear Discriminant Analysis decomposition (λ_max(S_W^{-1} S_B), cond(S_W), Tr(S_B)).** Computes between-class scatter, within-class scatter, condition number, and the generalized eigenvalue that bounds linear separability. Directly applicable to L19 prefill cached NPZs.
  *Replication cost*: 20min CPU on cached NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — this is the cheapest sanity-check on F-2 and F-7 we could possibly run.
- **Cohen's κ instead of raw accuracy / AUROC.** Adjusts for chance-level baseline; useful for class-imbalanced correct/incorrect (1.5B is 48.6% correct, so AUROC 0.7731 ≈ κ ~0.5 territory).
  *Replication cost*: 5min CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — trivial sanity reformat of existing tables.
- **Controlled data-perturbation structure attribution (Swap-Numbers / Isolate-k / Unigram-Replace / ContextLength).** Generalizes to "Swap-Labels", "Length-band shuffle", "Topic-band shuffle" for our pipeline. Each ablation removes a specific confound while preserving others.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU per ablation on cached NPZs.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — this is the missing infrastructure for F-2.
- **Multiple probe families (linear logistic / MLP / RFM kernel — Radhakrishnan et al. 2024).** Paper trains all three to rule out probe-class artifacts; reports linear in main, others in appendix.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU for MLP, 1h for RFM kernel.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — adds RFM as a kernel-method probe to the L19 DoM evaluation.
- **30-run protocol (3 seeds × 10-fold CV).** Strict variance estimation for probe accuracy.
  *Replication cost*: ~10× current 5-fold OOF cost; feasible on CPU.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — cheap and reduces 0.7731 ± uncertainty.
- **Circular probes (project embeddings to unit circle).** Specific to mod-T modular arithmetic; not directly applicable to correctness binary classification.
  *Replication cost*: trivial.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — wrong geometry for our binary correctness task.
- **Fourier-spectrum analysis of token embeddings.** Discrete Fourier transform across token index; checks for periodic structure in number-token embeddings.
  *Replication cost*: 10min CPU but conceptually misaligned with our hidden-state pipeline.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — our regime is residual-stream activations across problems, not token embeddings across token-index.

## Approaches & framings

- **Spectral vs geometric convergence dichotomy** — separates "structure in spectrum" from "structure that supports linear classification". Directly portable to our context as "AUROC is high" vs "AUROC reflects functional structure". Bears on F-2, F-7, F-9.
- **Convergent evolution as a framing for universality findings** — universality of a representational pattern across architectures may indicate either (a) a shared training-data constraint that is reproduced even by trivial baselines (the spectral kind) or (b) a learned functional structure that only some architectures achieve. F-1's universality claim should be re-scoped against these two regimes.
- **Structure attribution via controlled data perturbation** — complementary to influence-function instance attribution. Frames the question "what causes representation X to emerge" as a series of ablations on the data-generating process. For our setting: what causes prefill DoM AUROC=0.7731 — actual correctness signal, length confound, topic confound, or token-frequency confound? Strongly recommended framing for any future selectivity claim about a probe.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- 300M-parameter models, 10B token FineWeb-Edu pretraining, Llama-3 tokenizer (single-token integers 0–999, so N=1000).
- Tied input/output embeddings (load-bearing for the multi-token-addition argument: output logits inherit input embedding spectrum).
- Probe metric: Cohen's κ = (acc − 1/T) / (1 − 1/T); 30 runs = 3 seeds × 10-fold CV.
- Probe types: linear logistic regression, MLP, RFM kernel (Radhakrishnan et al. 2024).
- Architectures benchmarked: Transformer, Gated DeltaNet, Mamba-2, 4-layer and 12-layer LSTM, GloVe, FastText, word2vec, PPMI.
- Optimizers: Muon (Jordan et al. 2024), AdamW; effects are architecture-dependent.
- Theorem 1 part (i) proof reduces to Fourier identity Tr(S_B) = Φ_T / N (Lemma 4 in Appendix A).
- Theorem 1 part (ii) constructive: e(n) = A·r + B·m where r = n mod T, m = ⌊n/T⌋; A controls S_B and B controls S_W independently (Fig. 3).
