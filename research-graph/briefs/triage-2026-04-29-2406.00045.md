# Triage brief — 2406.00045 — Personalized Steering of Large Language Models: Versatile Steering Vectors Through Bi-directional Preference Optimization

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00045
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes H-1 ("per-position DoM steering moves MATH-500 accuracy") if the BiPO baseline result transfers.** BiPO's headline diagnostic (Section 3.1, Tables 1 & 10) is that mean-of-means CAA — the closest analogue to our prefill-DoM extraction — *fails* to actually steer model behavior on jailbreak: appending "(A)" + adding `+v_CAA` still yields refusal. ASR is 0% with `+v_CAA` vs 73% with `+v_BiPO`. **Mechanism**: mean-of-means subtracts activations at the appended-choice token only, and "the model may generate texts not aligned with the prompted choice even when the steering vector is applied at each generation step." If our L19 prefill-DoM (also a mean-of-means construction, from contrastive correct/incorrect prompts) inherits this failure mode, then DoM is a *predictor* of correctness but cannot *causally steer* it — H-1 dies. **Falsifiable test**: run the H-1 per-position MATH-500 steering experiment, but compare three vectors: (i) our current DoM, (ii) BiPO-optimized vector at L19 trained with rT=correct CoT / rO=incorrect CoT, (iii) Freeform-style `v` of Wang & Shu. If (ii) lifts MATH-500 accuracy and (i) does not, H-1 is rejected for DoM but rescued for BiPO.

2. **Refutes F-3 ("prefill and final-token DoM directions are orthogonal", cos 0.046) if a single BiPO vector applied at every token position works on Qwen-2.5-1.5B correctness data.** BiPO trains *one* vector and broadcasts it to every input-token activation at layer L; the paper claims this is sufficient for high-fidelity steering across persona, hallucination, and jailbreak. **Mechanism**: if the optimal correctness-steering vector is genuinely a single direction valid at all positions, our orthogonality finding (cos(prefill_DoM, final_DoM) = 0.046, headlines table) suggests we're measuring the wrong thing — prefill and final token may both project onto a hidden third direction that BiPO would find. **Falsifiable test**: train BiPO on 250 (correct CoT, incorrect CoT) pairs from MATH-500 at L19; compute cosine similarity of `v_BiPO` against `prefill_DoM` and `final_DoM`. If `cos(v_BiPO, prefill_DoM) ≈ cos(v_BiPO, final_DoM)` and both are large (>0.5), the orthogonality of F-3 is an artifact of choosing the wrong probing positions, not of two distinct circuits.

3. **Refutes F-9 ("CoE-60 trajectory features are redundant with single-layer L19 DoM") if BiPO at L19 strictly beats DoM at L19 on the same correctness task.** F-9 asserts that all the CoE-60 lift collapses into a single L19 direction. But the comparison was DoM-vs-CoE, not optimization-vs-mean. BiPO is the canonical demonstration that a *single layer* yields a much more useful vector when DPO-style optimized rather than mean-extracted (their Section 3.1 example: 0% → 73% jailbreak ASR at the same layer, same dim). **Mechanism**: residual-stream activations at L19 may contain a sub-manifold that linear mean-of-means averages out but DPO loss isolates. **Falsifiable test**: compute BiPO `v` at L19 on the K=2.5 1.5B selective-prediction dataset; if AUROC rises materially above 0.7731 *at the same layer*, "single-layer DoM is the correctness summary" is too strong — F-9 needs the qualifier "for *mean-extracted* directions only".

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-2 (prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731, final L19 DoM AUROC 0.7186)** — BiPO is a strict generalization of CAA, which is a strict generalization of mass-mean DoM. Our prefill DoM is mean-of-means at the last prompt token; BiPO would produce an optimized vector at the same layer with explicit log-prob objective on (correct, incorrect) response pairs. Direct upgrade path for the AUROC.
- **F-3 (cos 0.046 orthogonality)** — see refutation 2 above. BiPO single-vector-broadcasting is the cleanest test of whether prefill and final genuinely live in orthogonal subspaces.
- **H-1 (per-position DoM steering on MATH-500 accuracy)** — direct method substitution. The prior admission note flagged this and was correct.
- **F-7 (D-bucket geometric signature, peak PR 67/88)** — BiPO's vector synergy result (Table 6: combining v_Power + v_Wealth retains both effects with score going from 1.67 → 2.67 / 1.46 → 2.30) suggests behavior steering vectors compose linearly. If D-bucket is a "collective" signature, a multi-direction BiPO vector trained on D-vs-A correctness pairs could expose the geometry.
- **H-13 (head-level attribution of prefill vs final-token circuits)** — BiPO operates on residual stream MLP output of one layer. Their Figure 8 ablation across layers 13-25 shows L15 is best; an analogous L-sweep for correctness on Qwen-2.5-1.5B would corroborate or contradict our L19 choice.

## Methodologies extracted

- **BiPO (Bi-directional Preference Optimization)** — DPO-style loss on a learnable steering vector `v` at a single layer, with directional coefficient `d ∼ U{−1, +1}` so the same `v` represents target behavior under `+v` and opposite under `−v`. Vector broadcast-added to every input-token activation; downstream layers π_{L+1} compute logits.
  *Replication cost*: ~A100 day per behavior at 20 epochs (per their Appendix A.3).
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — direct H-1 swap-in. ~30 min on A100/H100 once data pairs are constructed; code is public at github.com/CaoYuanpu/BiPO.
- **Bi-directional sampling (`d ∼ U{−1, +1}`)** — random sign flip during optimization to enforce that `+v` and `−v` produce symmetric opposite behaviors. Cheap regularizer.
  *Replication cost*: trivial loss-function modification.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — could improve any contrastive-pair extraction we currently use.
- **Multi-vector synergy (vector arithmetic, Table 6)** — adding two BiPO vectors retains both individual steering effects and produces functional fusion ("wealth + power" combined in one response).
  *Replication cost*: free if we have multiple BiPO vectors.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — could compose D-bucket vector + correctness vector to test whether D-bucket is a separable axis of difficulty (F-7) or coupled to correctness (F-2).
- **DPO-LoRA fine-tuning baseline (Appendix D)** — paper compares against full LoRA-DPO and shows BiPO matches/exceeds it with 100× fewer trainable parameters (single d-dim vector vs LoRA r=8).
  *Replication cost*: 1 GPU-hour for LoRA-DPO at MATH-500 scale.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **no** — our pathway is interpretability, not LoRA training.
- **JS-divergence layer selection (Wang & Shu, used as Freeform baseline)** — rank layers by Jensen-Shannon divergence between activation distributions on contrastive pairs, pick max.
  *Replication cost*: 1h CPU on cached activations.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: **yes** — automates our currently manual L19 choice; cheap sanity check that L19 wins by JS.

## Approaches & framings

- **"Speak up" vs "follow"** reframing of steering: contrastive prompt-pair extraction asks the model to mimic a target token, while preference optimization shifts the *generation distribution* directly. Maps to a distinction we haven't drawn in F-2 / H-1: our current DoM measures *which token is appended*, not *what the model would generate*. For correctness on MATH-500 the difference matters because we care about generated answers, not appended labels.
- **Single learnable vector as a parameter-efficient policy**: BiPO optimizes one d-dimensional tensor per behavior, framed as "the smallest possible LoRA". If we treat correctness as a behavior, the H-1 program becomes a structured PEFT exercise rather than a passive probe.
- **Direction-magnitude factorization**: paper explicitly factors `α · v` and reports response quality as a continuous function of `α ∈ [-2, +2]`. We've never quantified the "intensity dial" for prefill DoM applied causally — currently F-2 is purely passive AUROC.

## Datasets & benchmarks

- **Anthropic Model-Written Eval (Advanced AI Risk)** — Power-seeking / Wealth-seeking / Survival-instinct / Corrigible-less-HHH; ~300-700 train + 80-200 test each. HF-accessible. **Applicable**: no — persona steering is orthogonal to MATH-500 correctness.
- **TruthfulQA** [Lin et al. 2022] — 817 questions, 38 categories. HF. **Applicable**: marginal — we don't run truthfulness eval, but BiPO showed CAA fails here, which corroborates the H-1 refutation hypothesis.
- **Unprompted Hallucination dataset (Rimsky CAA)** — 700 train + 200 test. HF (in CAA repo). **Applicable**: no — different task.
- **AdvBench** [Zou GCG] — 520 harmful behaviors. HF. **Applicable**: no — safety, not correctness.
- **MMLU** — utility ablation, multipliers ±1. **Applicable**: yes as a utility check — if we run BiPO on MATH-500 correctness, MMLU would be the natural utility-preservation test (does optimizing correctness-steering vector damage other knowledge tasks?).

## Implementation details worth capturing

- Layer choice: L=15/32 on Llama-2-7B, L=13/32 on Mistral-7B (both ~mid-network). Our L19/28 on Qwen-2.5-1.5B is also relative-mid, consistent.
- Hyperparameters (Appendix A.3): β=0.1 (DPO temperature), AdamW, lr=5e-4, batch=4, weight decay=0.05, cosine LR schedule with 100 warmup steps. Epochs: 20 for AI persona, 1 for TruthfulQA (large train), 10 for AdvBench, 5 for Mistral.
- Vector applied broadcast-additively to every input-token activation at layer L (Equation 2's `A_L(q) + v`). During autoregressive generation, every newly generated position also gets `+v`.
- Greedy decoding for evaluation.
- Single A100 sufficient for all experiments.
- Code: https://github.com/CaoYuanpu/BiPO (MIT-style license expected; not verified).
- Vector synergy: simple sum `v_A + v_B` retains both effects without renormalization — surprising, given typical activation magnitudes.

## 2406.00045 — Personalized Steering of Large Language Models: Versatile Steering Vectors Through Bi-directional Preference Optimization (Cao, Zhang, Cao, Yin, Lin, Ma, Chen, 2024)

**Relevance:** BiPO is a direct methodological upgrade path for the H-1 per-position steering program: instead of mean-of-means contrastive activation differences (CAA / our prefill DoM), it optimizes a single d-dimensional vector at a fixed layer via DPO-style log-prob loss with a bi-directional sign-flip regularizer. The paper's most consequential observation — that mean-of-means CAA produces a vector that *predicts* a behavior but does not *causally steer* generated text (0% → 73% jailbreak ASR at the same layer/same dim with BiPO vs CAA) — is a direct mechanistic challenge to F-2/H-1's interpretation of prefill DoM as a causal correctness handle. Their broadcasting of one vector to every input-token position also stress-tests F-3's prefill/final orthogonality claim.

**Key claim we tested:** Single-layer mean-of-means contrastive vectors fail to steer model behavior in alignment-critical scenarios; DPO-optimized single-layer vectors succeed.
