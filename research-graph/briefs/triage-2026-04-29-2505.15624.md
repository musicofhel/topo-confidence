# Triage brief — 2505.15624 — Mechanistic Insights into Grokking from the Embedding Layer

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15624
Triaged: 2026-04-29

## Refutations

1. **Refutes H-4 (Breathing ↔ Sharpness Dimension / EoS correspondence).**
   The paper attributes delayed generalization to *bilinear coupling between
   embedding and downstream weights* plus *sparse weight-decay updates on rare
   tokens* — neither of which is an EoS / curvature signature. If grokking-style
   transitions on modular arithmetic are explained without invoking sharpness
   dynamics, the claim that breathing in residual streams is the same physical
   phenomenon as EoS sharpness becomes a non-unique mapping; the bilinear-coupling
   hypothesis is an alternative driver of representational dynamics that H-4 does
   not currently distinguish.
   **Mechanism:** the paper's effective-update ratio
   `||ΔE||/||ΔW|| ∝ (η_E σ_max(W) f_E)/(η_W σ_max(E) f_W)` predicts that breathing
   amplitude across architectures should track the σ-asymmetry of embedding vs.
   first attention/MLP weights, not the loss-Hessian top eigenvalue.
   **Falsifiable experiment:** for Qwen-2.5-1.5B compute σ_max(E_embed) /
   σ_max(W_layer1_q,k,v,o,gate,up,down). If H-4's EoS framing is right, this
   ratio should not predict per-layer participation-ratio breathing amplitude
   beyond what curvature already explains. If the embedding-coupling framing wins,
   the σ-ratio should outperform a sharpness proxy at predicting F-1 amplitude
   layer-by-layer. Cost: ~1h CPU on weight tensors only.

2. **Refutes F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC = 0.7731) via a token-rarity confound.**
   The paper's central mechanism is that *rare tokens stagnate* in the embedding
   layer because they receive sparse gradient updates and are continuously
   shrunk by weight decay; their embeddings remain close to initialization.
   MATH-500 prompts contain rare math tokens (LaTeX symbols, multi-digit
   numerals) at heterogeneous rates, and rare-token density correlates with
   problem difficulty (harder problems often have more LaTeX). If the prefill
   L19 representation carries any residual signature of how-stuck-in-init the
   embedding row is, then the 0.7731 prefill AUROC may be partially a token-
   rarity probe rather than a correctness probe.
   **Mechanism:** prefill L19 inherits from layer 0 via standard residual
   addition; if embedding norms scale inversely with token rarity, prefill
   activations carry a token-frequency signal that is correlated with MATH
   difficulty and hence with correctness.
   **Falsifiable experiment:** compute, per MATH-500 prompt, mean log-unigram-
   frequency of input tokens under Qwen tokenizer (or mean
   ‖E[token]‖₂). Refit the prefill DoM probe with this scalar partialled out
   (residualize labels and probe scores). If AUROC drops materially below
   0.7731, F-2 is partly token-rarity-confounded. Cost: 30min CPU on cached
   `pathway11_h100/prefill_gated_compute` activations.

3. **Refutes H-8 (Prefill direction stable across training checkpoints).**
   The paper directly characterizes how σ_max(E) and σ_max(W) co-evolve through
   training, with σ_max(W) shrinking and σ_max(E) growing in the grokking
   regime. Because the prefill DoM is a linear functional of the residual
   stream that depends on E·W^(0)·…·W^(19), its direction must rotate as the
   underlying singular subspaces rotate. H-8 (which presumes the prefill DoM is
   a stable feature recoverable across checkpoints) therefore predicts the
   opposite of what bilinear-coupling dynamics imply.
   **Mechanism:** if the singular vectors of E and the early weight matrices
   rotate during training (which they must during grokking-like phases), the
   direction in residual space that linearly separates correct/incorrect must
   rotate too.
   **Falsifiable experiment:** on an open-checkpoint LM with intermediate
   checkpoints (Pythia-1.4B or OLMo-1B) re-fit prefill DoM at each checkpoint
   and compute cos(DoM_t, DoM_t+1). H-8 predicts cos > 0.9 across late training;
   the embedding-coupling story predicts a measurable decay.
   Cost: 1 day H100 across ~10 checkpoints.

## Direct connections to F-N / H-N

- **F-1 (Dimensional breathing universal across architectures and scales).**
  Paper supplies a candidate mechanism — bilinear E↔W coupling — for why early-
  layer breathing should look similar across transformers (they all share the
  same embedding-then-block structure). Numerical anchor: F-1 reports
  cross-scale breathing across 1.5B and 7B; the σ_max(E)/σ_max(W) ratio is the
  paper's universal control variable.
- **F-2 (Prefill L19 DoM AUROC 0.7731).** See refutation #2 above — the paper's
  rare-token-stagnation mechanism is a candidate confound for the headline
  number.
- **H-4 (Breathing ↔ Sharpness / EoS).** Paper proposes a *competing* dynamical
  driver for the same phenomenology; see refutation #1.
- **H-8 (Prefill direction stable across training checkpoints).** Paper
  predicts singular-subspace rotation; see refutation #3.
- **H-10 (Breathing pattern changes after fine-tuning).** Fine-tuning is exactly
  the regime where σ_max(E) and σ_max(W) shift relative to each other. The
  paper's machinery gives a quantitative prediction for the direction and
  magnitude of breathing change after fine-tuning. Anchor: H-10 currently has
  no quantitative mechanism — this paper supplies one.

## Methodologies extracted

- **σ_max(E) / σ_max(W) diagnostic** — top singular value of the embedding
  matrix divided by top singular value of an early Transformer block weight,
  as a scalar diagnostic for bilinear-coupling regime.
  *Replication cost*: 30min CPU on cached Qwen-2.5 weights.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes — directly diagnoses whether F-1 breathing
  amplitude is predicted by training-dynamics singular-value asymmetry.

- **Effective update ratio formula** —
  `||ΔE||/||ΔW|| ∝ (η_E σ_max(W) f_E)/(η_W σ_max(E) f_W)`,
  used to predict relative parameter motion under Adam.
  *Replication cost*: trivial; needs gradient norms during fine-tuning.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: yes if H-10 is ever instantiated, no otherwise.

- **Frequency-aware sampling weights** —
  `p_i* ∝ exp(L_i² / γ)` where `L_i` upper-bounds the per-token gradient norm.
  Used to balance gradient variance across rare/common tokens.
  *Replication cost*: needs new fine-tuning run.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — out of scope; we don't fine-tune.

- **Embedding-specific learning rate (Adam-LR with c=10)** — set
  `η_E = c · η_W` where `c ≈ σ_max(E)/σ_max(W) · f_W/f_E`.
  *Replication cost*: needs fine-tuning run.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no — only relevant if we ever train.

- **Gradient-proportionality probes** — `∇E ∝ W^T ∇loss`, `∇W ∝ E^T ∇loss` as
  a sanity check for bilinear regime.
  *Replication cost*: 1h on cached gradients; we don't have Qwen training
  gradients.
  *We'd plausibly run this*: no.

## Approaches & framings

- **Embedding-as-driver framing.** Paper reframes grokking as a phenomenon of
  the embedding layer specifically (embeddings-free MLPs generalize
  immediately), not of network depth or residual-stream geometry per se. This
  intersects F-1 / F-5 (breathing is content-dependent) by suggesting the
  content-dependence is *embedding-layer content-dependence*, not arbitrary
  semantic content — a sharper, more falsifiable version of F-5.
- **Bilinear-coupling-as-saddle framing.** Treats the E·W product structure as
  the source of saddle points and initialization sensitivity. Provides a
  language to talk about why prefill and final-token DoMs are orthogonal
  (F-3, cos = 0.046): they may live in different singular subspaces of the
  effective E·W^(layers) operator, with different update rates.
- **σ-asymmetry as control variable.** The ratio σ_max(E)/σ_max(W) as a
  scalar that tracks training regime. Useful as a one-number summary of
  "where in the bilinear-coupling phase diagram are we right now" that we
  could compute on every Qwen layer.

## Datasets & benchmarks

- **Modular arithmetic (a+b, a×b, a÷b, a²+b²) mod P=97** — synthetic, trivially
  generated. Applicable? **no** — far from MATH-500 / 1024-tok regime; useful
  only as a controlled testbed if we wanted to validate the σ-ratio framework
  before running on Qwen.

## Implementation details worth capturing

- 2-layer MLP, hidden = 4 × embed_dim, embed_dim = 128, P = 97, ReLU.
- Adam optimizer, weight decay λ = 0.001.
- Train/test split 30/70, with 20% held out — note: this is a test-larger-than-
  train setup, deliberate for grokking.
- Empirical optimal learning-rate ratio: c = 10 (η_E = 10 η_W).
- Skewed sampling causes plateaus at sub-optimal accuracy with 100% train fit
  — relevant signature for diagnosing whether "stuck during fine-tuning" is
  embedding-stagnation.
- No transformer experiments — the authors explicitly call this a limitation
  (Section 6).

## Proposed PAPER_INDEX.md classification

## 2505.15624 — Mechanistic Insights into Grokking from the Embedding Layer (AlquBoj, AlQuabeh, Bojkovic, Nwadike, Inui, 2025)

**Relevance:** A mechanistic-interpretability study of training dynamics on
modular-arithmetic MLPs. Paper attributes grokking to embedding-layer dynamics:
sparse weight-decay-driven stagnation of rare-token rows, and bilinear E·W
coupling that introduces saddle points. Although no Transformer experiments
are reported (explicit limitation), the σ_max(E)/σ_max(W) ratio is a
generalizable diagnostic that we can compute directly on Qwen-2.5 weights.
Most importantly, the paper supplies a *candidate competing mechanism* for
H-4 (breathing ↔ EoS) and a *candidate confound* for F-2 (rare-token
stagnation could leak correctness-correlated signal into prefill DoM).

**Key claim we tested:** Embedding-layer bilinear coupling generates training-
dynamics signatures (rotated singular subspaces, saddle-point sensitivity)
that should propagate into residual-stream geometry of any Transformer that
shares the embedding-then-block structure.

**Our result:** **TO TEST.** Paper provides a falsifiable diagnostic
(σ-asymmetry table) that we can run in ~1h CPU on cached Qwen weights.
Outcomes determine whether H-4 needs to admit a competing mechanism and
whether F-2 needs a rare-token control.

**Related experiments:** P11-FE73 (σ-ratio diagnostic), P11-FE74 (token-
rarity confound on F-2), P11-FE75 (checkpoint-drift refutation of H-8),
H-73, H-74, H-75.

**Status:** TO TEST
